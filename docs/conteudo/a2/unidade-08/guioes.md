# A2.2 · Unidade 8 — Technology and problems · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções e personagens: ver os [guiões da unidade 1 do A2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2.1 e das unidades 6 e 7. Novo: o condicional zero (*If you press this button, the screen turns off.*); o 1.º condicional (*If the power goes off, we'll use the generator.*); *will / won't* para previsões, com *I think / I don't think* e *probably*; *phrasal verbs* da tecnologia, com o pronome no meio (*turn it off*); instruções por ordem (*first, next, then, after that, finally*).

**Não usar nesta unidade:** o 2.º condicional (*if* + passado, *would*); *could* (unidade 9); *unless*; a voz passiva; orações relativas; o *present perfect continuous*; o *past perfect*; *too / enough*.

**Personagens:** **Nuno**, **Mateus**, **Paulo**, **Sílvia**, **Ana**, **Rita** e **João** (Mussulo Energy), **Arjun** (de volta a Pune, ligado por videochamada), **Lerato** (em Luanda desde Fevereiro, unidade 7). Na 8F: **Sipho**, técnico do apoio da empresa da Thandi, em Joanesburgo (voz nova). Na 8E: quatro pessoas ligam ao apoio técnico da Kalandula Tech, onde atende o **Paulo**.

**A história:** Março de 2027. Na segunda-feira, 1 de Março, o sistema de pagamentos da Mussulo Energy começa a funcionar. Durante a semana há problemas: a luz falta, a rede falha, um portátil não liga.

## 8A — If you press this…

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 8        Aula: 8A — If you press this…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue explicar como funciona uma aplicação ou um equipamento com o
  condicional zero e instruções por ordem (If you click "Confirm", the system prints a receipt. First…,
  then…, finally…).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: condicional zero: if / when + present simple, present simple (factos e regras do
  sistema); imperativo (revisto) para instruções; first, next, then, after that, finally
Palavras-alvo: screen, button, click, press, type, enter, confirm, receipt, client number, amount,
  app, payment, system
Reciclagem: imperativo e direcções (A2, 6D); present simple (A1)
Pronúncia: if /ɪf/ curto; ritmo das instruções (FIRST, … THEN, …)
Contexto: segunda-feira, 1 de Março; o Nuno mostra à equipa do João o novo ecrã de pagamentos
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 8A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 8
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today we explain how things work — an app, a machine, a system. A new structure: "If you press this, this happens."

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o balcão de atendimento da Mussulo Energy; um ecrã novo com o logótipo do sistema de pagamentos; o Nuno, o João e duas colegas do atendimento]

**NUNO:** Good morning, everyone. Today the new payment system starts. It's easy. First, type the client number here.
**JOÃO:** And then?
**NUNO:** Then the screen shows the client's name and the amount. If the amount is correct, click "Confirm".
**COLEGA:** And if the client pays by card?
**NUNO:** If the client pays by card, the card machine turns on. The client enters the PIN. After that, the system prints a receipt.
**JOÃO:** And what happens if I click "Cancel"?
**NUNO:** If you click "Cancel", nothing happens. The system goes back to the first screen.
**COLEGA:** Good. And the receipt? Do we give it to the client?
**NUNO:** Yes. When the system prints the receipt, give it to the client. Finally, click "New client".

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**O condicional zero**

[ECRÃ: **If** you **click** "Cancel", nothing **happens**. · **If** the client **pays** by card, the card machine **turns** on. · **When** the system **prints** the receipt, …]

**PROF:** "If" plus the present simple, and the present simple again. We use it for things that are always true — how a system or a machine works. Every time you click "Cancel", nothing happens.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Se carregar em Cancelar, não acontece nada" → "If you click 'Cancel', nothing happens." Os dois verbos no presente.)

[ECRÃ: Nothing happens **if** you click "Cancel". (a frase também pode começar pelo resultado; sem vírgula)]

**PROF:** You can start with "if", or put "if" in the middle. With "if" at the start, use a comma.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***If* ou *when*?**

[ECRÃ: **If** the client pays by card… (talvez pague) · **When** the system prints the receipt… (vai acontecer sempre)]

**PROF:** "If" — maybe it happens. "When" — it always happens, sooner or later.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Instruções por ordem**

[ECRÃ: **First**, type the client number. **Then** check the amount. **Next**, click "Confirm". **After that**, give the receipt to the client. **Finally**, click "New client".]

**PROF:** For instructions, use the imperative and the order words: first, then, next, after that, finally.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "If" is short: if. If you click "Cancel", nothing happens.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** In instructions, the order words are strong, and there's a small pause after them: FIRST, … type the number. THEN, … check the amount.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Complete with the present simple.
[ECRÃ: If you … (press) this button, the screen … (turn) off.] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: If you press this button, the screen turns off.]
[ECRÃ: If you … (type) the wrong PIN three times, the card … (stop) working.] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: If you type the wrong PIN three times, the card stops working.]

**PROF:** Now explain your phone. What happens if you press the button on the side for five seconds?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: If I press it for five seconds, the phone turns off.]

**PROF:** Explain in three steps how to send a message on your phone. Use first, then and finally.
[PAUSA 15 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 7B):** Have you already used a payment app this week?
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Great! Now you can explain how things work. Next lesson: problems — "If the power goes off, we'll…"

## 8B — If the power goes off…

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 8        Aula: 8B — If the power goes off…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue falar de consequências prováveis e de planos para quando
  algo corre mal com o 1.º condicional (If the power goes off, we'll use the generator. If the system
  stops, I'll call Arjun.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: 1.º condicional: if + present simple, will / won't + verbo simples; perguntas (What
  will we do if…?); 'll contraído; ≠ condicional zero
Palavras-alvo: power cut, generator, battery, backup, plan B, internet, down, slow, call, fix
Reciclagem: going to e present continuous para combinações (A2, 3); have to (A2, 5)
Pronúncia: 'll (we'll, I'll, it'll) curto e ligado
Contexto: sexta-feira, 26 de Fevereiro, depois do teste final; o Mateus, a Rita e o Nuno preparam o
  plano B para segunda-feira
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 8B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Before a big day, good teams have a plan B. Today: "If this happens, we'll do that."

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a sala de reuniões da Mussulo Energy, sexta-feira à tarde; o Mateus, a Rita e o Nuno; o Arjun numa videochamada]

**RITA:** The final test went well. But on Monday the system starts with real clients. What will we do if something goes wrong?
**MATEUS:** OK, let's think. If the power goes off, we'll use the generator. It starts in ten seconds.
**RITA:** And if the internet goes down?
**NUNO:** If the internet goes down, the system will work offline for four hours. When the internet comes back, it'll send the payments.
**RITA:** And if the system stops?
**ARJUN:** If it stops, João will call me. I'll be online all day on Monday.
**RITA:** And if Arjun doesn't answer?
**NUNO:** Then I'll go to the office and restart the server. I won't go far — I'll be in the building.
**RITA:** Good. I feel better now.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**O 1.º condicional**

[ECRÃ: **If** the power **goes** off, we**'ll use** the generator. · **If** the system **stops**, João **will call** me. · **If** Arjun **doesn't answer**, I**'ll go** to the office.]

**PROF:** "If" plus the present simple, then "will" plus the simple verb. We use it for a possible situation in the future, and its result.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✔ If the power goes off, we'll… ✘ If the power will go off, we'll…]
**PROF:** After "if", the present simple — not "will". If the power goes off — not "if the power will go off".
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: Em português dizemos "se a luz for abaixo" — no inglês, depois de "if", o presente: "if the power goes off".)

**Perguntas**

[ECRÃ: **What will** we **do if** the internet **goes** down? · **What will happen if** the system **stops**?]

**PROF:** What will we do if…? What will happen if…?
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Zero ou 1.º?**

[ECRÃ: **If** you **click** "Cancel", nothing **happens**. (sempre, é como o sistema funciona) · **If** the system **stops** on Monday, João **will call** me. (uma situação possível, no futuro)]

**PROF:** Zero conditional: it's always true. First conditional: one possible situation, in the future.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "Will" is usually short: I'll, we'll, it'll. It'll send the payments. We'll use the generator.
[ECRÃ: I'll · we'll · it'll · João'll (na fala)]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: If the power goes off, we'll use the generator.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Complete the plan B.
[ECRÃ: If the printer … (stop), I … (use) the printer on the second floor.] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: If the printer stops, I'll use the printer on the second floor.]
[ECRÃ: If it … (rain) on Saturday, we … (not go) to the beach.] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: If it rains on Saturday, we won't go to the beach.]

**PROF:** Now you. You have an important presentation tomorrow. What will you do if your laptop doesn't work?
[PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: If my laptop doesn't work, I'll use my colleague's laptop. / I'll print the slides.]
**PROF:** What will you do if you're sick tomorrow?
[PAUSA 8 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 5B):** What do you have to do before an important day at work?
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! Now you have a plan B. Next lesson: predictions — "I think it'll be fine."

## 8C — I think it'll be fine

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 8        Aula: 8C — I think it'll be fine        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue fazer previsões com will / won't, I think / I don't think e
  probably (I think it'll be fine. It probably won't rain. I don't think the clients will notice.), e
  perguntar a opinião de outra pessoa sobre o futuro.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: will / won't para previsões; I think + will; I don't think + will (e não "I think …
  won't"); probably (depois de will, antes de won't); Do you think…? Will it…?
Palavras-alvo: probably, maybe, sure, I hope so, I hope not, busy, queue, notice, ready
Reciclagem: 1.º condicional (8B); going to e present continuous (A2, 3)
Pronúncia: won't /wəʊnt/ ≠ want /wɒnt/ (revisto da 3B)
Contexto: domingo à noite, 28 de Fevereiro; o Nuno e a Sílvia falam ao telefone sobre o dia seguinte
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 8C; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Nobody knows the future. But we make predictions all the time: "I think it'll be fine." Today: "will" for predictions.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: domingo à noite; o Nuno em casa ao telefone; a Sílvia em casa, com os filhos ao fundo]

**SÍLVIA:** So, tomorrow's the big day! Are you nervous?
**NUNO:** A bit. I think the system will work, but I'm worried about the first hour. There will probably be a long queue.
**SÍLVIA:** Do you think the clients will like it?
**NUNO:** I think so. It's much faster than the old system. People won't wait so long.
**SÍLVIA:** And the weather? Will it rain?
**NUNO:** I hope not! I don't think it'll rain. The forecast says it'll be sunny.
**SÍLVIA:** Good. I'm sure it'll be fine, Nuno. You've worked very hard.
**NUNO:** Thanks, Sílvia. I'll call you tomorrow and tell you everything.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Will* para previsões**

[ECRÃ: I think the system **will work**. · It**'ll be** sunny. · People **won't wait** so long.]

**PROF:** For predictions — what we think about the future — use "will" or "won't" plus the simple verb.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***I think / I don't think***

[ECRÃ: I think it**'ll** be fine. · **I don't think** it'll rain. (também se diz "I think it won't rain", mas é menos comum)]

**PROF:** For a negative prediction, people usually say "I don't think it'll rain".
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Probably***

[ECRÃ: There **will probably** be a long queue. · It **probably won't** rain.]

**PROF:** "Probably" goes after "will", but before "won't": it'll probably be busy. It probably won't rain.
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Responder**

[ECRÃ: Do you think the clients will like it? — **I think so.** / **I don't think so.** · Will it rain? — **I hope not!** · Will it work? — **I hope so!**]

**PROF:** Short answers: I think so. I don't think so. I hope so. I hope not.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Will* ou *going to*?**

[ECRÃ: I**'m going to** call you tomorrow. (já decidi) · I**'ll** call you tomorrow. (decido agora, ao falar — unidade 3) · I think it**'ll** be fine. (previsão)]

**PROF:** Remember Unit 3: "going to" for plans you decided before. "Will" for decisions now — and now, also for predictions.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Won't and want — again! I won't wait. I want to wait. Won't — like "don't".
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I don't think it'll rain. It probably won't rain.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Make a prediction. Use "I think" or "I don't think".
**PROF:** Will it rain tomorrow in your city?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I think it'll rain. / I don't think it'll rain.]
**PROF:** Will you have a lot of work next week?
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Who will win the next Girabola?
[PAUSA 5 s]

**PROF:** Now ask me about the future with "Do you think…?"
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: modelos — Do you think it'll rain this weekend? · Do you think the project will be ready on time?]

**PROF (reciclagem — 8B):** And what will you do if it rains this weekend?
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Now practise with your tutor: what will happen if…? Then: the verbs of technology — log in, set up, turn off.

## 8D — Log in, set up, turn off

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 8        Aula: 8D — Log in, set up, turn off        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar os phrasal verbs da tecnologia (log in / out, set up,
  turn on / off, plug in, back up, sign up, fill in), pôr o pronome no meio (turn it off), dizer palavras
  começadas por s + consoante sem vogal a mais, e evitar os falsos amigos library e push / pull.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 4
Gramática-alvo: phrasal verbs separáveis: turn off the laptop / turn the laptop off / turn it off (✘
  turn off it); phrasal verbs sem objecto: log in, sign up
Palavras-alvo: log in, log out, set up, turn on, turn off, plug in, back up, sign up, fill in,
  password, update, crash, freeze, charger, battery, screen, spreadsheet, Wi-Fi, error message
Reciclagem: 1.º condicional (8B); instruções (8A)
Pronúncia: grupos iniciais com s (screen, start, smartphone, spreadsheet, system, stop) sem vogal antes
Falsos amigos: library (≠ livraria); push / pull (≠ puxar)
Contexto: a lista de regras de informática da Kalandula Tech para quem começa
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 8D; pergunta 7 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Computers and phones have their own verbs: log in, turn off, back up. Today: these verbs — and some words that Portuguese speakers often say with an extra sound.

### 2. As regras de informática (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: "IT rules for new staff — Kalandula Tech", com ícones]

**PROF:** These are the IT rules for new staff at Kalandula Tech.
**PROF:** One: when you start work, turn on your laptop and log in with your password.
**PROF:** Two: never write your password on paper. If you forget it, call the IT team. They'll set up a new one.
**PROF:** Three: back up your files every Friday.
**PROF:** Four: when you finish work, log out and turn off your laptop.
**PROF:** Five: plug in your laptop at night. Don't leave it with no battery.
**PROF:** Six: if your computer freezes, don't turn it off and on many times. Call the IT team.
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: **log in / out** (entrar / sair) · **turn on / off** (ligar / desligar) · **set up** (configurar) · **plug in** (ligar à corrente) · **back up** (fazer cópia de segurança) · **sign up** (inscrever-se) · **fill in** (preencher) · **crash / freeze** (o sistema vai abaixo / bloqueia) · **update** (actualização)]

### 3. O pronome no meio (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: Turn **off the laptop**. ✔ · Turn **the laptop off**. ✔ · Turn **it** off. ✔ · ✘ Turn off it.]

**PROF:** With a noun, you can say "turn off the laptop" or "turn the laptop off". With a pronoun — it, them — the pronoun goes in the middle: turn it off. Never "turn off it".
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: plug **it** in · set **it** up · fill **it** in · back **them** up]
**PROF:** Plug it in. Set it up. Fill it in.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia: sem vogal a mais (2 min)

**PROF:** Portuguese speakers often add a small sound before "s" plus a consonant: "e-screen", "e-start". In English, start with the "s".
[ECRÃ: ✘ e-screen ✔ screen · ✘ e-start ✔ start · ✘ e-smartphone ✔ smartphone · ✘ e-spreadsheet ✔ spreadsheet · ✘ e-system… ✔ system]
**PROF:** Listen: screen. Start. Smartphone. Spreadsheet. Stop.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** A trick: say "s" for a long time, then the word. Ssss — screen. Ssss — start.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: Start the system and open the spreadsheet on the screen.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: ✘ **library** ≠ livraria · library = biblioteca · livraria = **bookshop**]
**PROF:** A "library" is a place where you borrow books — "biblioteca". A shop for books is a "bookshop".
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✘ **push** ≠ puxar · push = empurrar · **pull** = puxar]
**PROF:** On doors, be careful! "Push" sounds like "puxa", but it means empurrar. To open a door towards you — puxar — the word is "pull".
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 6. Prática e fecho (1 min)

**PROF:** Your colleague's laptop has no battery. What do you say?
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: Plug it in!]
**PROF:** Your manager asks you to turn off the projector. Answer with "it".
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: OK, I'll turn it off.]

**PROF (reciclagem — 8B):** What will you do if you forget your password?
[PAUSA 5 s]

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: four calls to the IT help desk.

## 8E — Real English: It doesn't work!

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 8        Aula: 8E — It doesn't work!        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue identificar, em quatro chamadas para o apoio técnico a
  velocidade natural, o problema de cada pessoa e a solução.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 4
Língua: A1, A2.1 e unidades 6–8, sem estruturas novas
Vozes: Paulo (no apoio técnico), Ana, Rita (portuguesa), Mr Okafor (nigeriano), Fátima
Velocidade: natural
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 8E; pergunta 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Apresentação (30 s)

**PROF:** Paulo works on the IT help desk this week. Four people call him with a problem. Listen for the problem — and the solution. You hear the calls three times.

### 2. Primeira escuta: a ideia geral (3 min)

**PROF:** First: which problem does Paulo not solve on the phone?

**CHAMADA 1 — ANA**
**ANA:** Paulo, the printer at reception isn't working. It says "paper jam".
**PAULO:** OK. Open the front door of the printer. Can you see the paper?
**ANA:** Yes… there it is. I've got it!
**PAULO:** Great. Close the door. If the light is green, it's ready.
**ANA:** It's green. Thanks!

**CHAMADA 2 — RITA**
**RITA:** Hi Paulo, it's Rita. I can't log in to the payment system. It says "wrong password", but I'm sure it's correct.
**PAULO:** Is "Caps Lock" on? If it's on, all the letters are capital letters.
**RITA:** Oh… yes, it is. Sorry! It works now.

**CHAMADA 3 — MR OKAFOR**
**MR OKAFOR:** Good morning. I'm in the meeting room at Kalandula Tech, and the Wi-Fi isn't working on my laptop.
**PAULO:** Have you tried the guest network? The name is "KT-Guest", and the password is on the wall.
**MR OKAFOR:** Let me see… Yes, I'm connected now. Thank you!

**CHAMADA 4 — FÁTIMA**
**FÁTIMA:** Hello, I'm calling from the bakery. Our new card machine doesn't turn on. I charged it all night.
**PAULO:** Hmm. Is the charger the right one? Is the light on the charger red or green?
**FÁTIMA:** There's no light.
**PAULO:** Then the charger is probably broken. I'll send a technician this afternoon with a new one.
**FÁTIMA:** Perfect. Thank you!

[ECRÃ: Ana — printer · Rita — password · Mr Okafor — Wi-Fi · Fátima — card machine]

### 3. Segunda escuta: os pormenores (3 min)

1. What does the printer say? (paper jam ✓ / no paper / no ink)
2. Why can't Rita log in? (Caps Lock is on. ✓ / Her password is old. / The system is down.)
3. What network does Mr Okafor use? (the guest network ✓ / his phone / the office network)
4. Where is the Wi-Fi password? (on the wall ✓ / in an email / on the laptop)
5. What's the problem with Fátima's card machine? (The charger is probably broken. ✓ / The battery is new. / The machine is very old.)
6. What will Paulo do for Fátima? (send a technician with a new charger ✓ / call her tomorrow / sell her a new machine)

*(Cada chamada repete-se; depois aparecem as perguntas, com 8 s para responder.)*

### 4. Terceira escuta: como disseram (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Listen and repeat.
**PROF:** The printer isn't working. It says "paper jam". [PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** I can't log in. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** If the light is green, it's ready. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** I'll send a technician this afternoon. [PAUSA 3 s]

**PROF:** Did you notice? To describe a problem, people say what the screen says: It says "paper jam". It says "wrong password". It's very useful on the phone.
(PT: Para explicar um problema, diga o que aparece no ecrã: "It says…".)

### 5. A sua vez (1 min)

**PROF:** Now you. Think of a problem with your phone or computer. Call me and explain it: what happened, and what the screen says.
[PAUSA 20 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Hello, my … isn't working. When I … , it … It says "…". What should I do?]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: Nuno has a problem, and he calls the help desk in Johannesburg.

## 8F — Work & Life: Help desk call

```

Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue ligar ao apoio técnico, descrever um problema (o que fez, o
```
