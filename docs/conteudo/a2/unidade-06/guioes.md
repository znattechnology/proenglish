# A2.2 · Unidade 6 — Travelling for work (7 aulas) · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções e personagens: ver os [guiões da unidade 1 do A2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1 e do A2.1 (unidades 1 a 5). Novo: o *present perfect* para experiências (*Have you ever been to Lagos? — Yes, I have. / No, I've never been there.*), com o particípio dos verbos mais comuns; a passagem da experiência aos pormenores com o *past simple* (*I've been to Lisbon. I went there in 2023.*); *been / gone*; preposições de movimento (*along, across, through, past, up, down, into, out of*); *What's the weather like?*; aeroporto, hotel e tempo.

**Não usar nesta unidade:** o *present perfect* com *just, already, yet, for, since* (unidade 7); *will* e *going to* para previsões; *could*; o condicional (*if*, *would* + verbo, excepto *would like*); *too / enough*; *used to*.

**Os 30 particípios irregulares da unidade** (o *past simple* de quase todos já é conhecido):

| 6A | 6B | 6E |
|---|---|---|
| be → was / were → **been** · go → went → **gone** · see → saw → **seen** · do → did → **done** · have → had → **had** · eat → ate → **eaten** · drink → drank → **drunk** · fly → flew → **flown** · take → took → **taken** · meet → met → **met** | lose → lost → **lost** · forget → forgot → **forgotten** · write → wrote → **written** · speak → spoke → **spoken** · buy → bought → **bought** · give → gave → **given** · leave → left → **left** · sleep → slept → **slept** · break → broke → **broken** · drive → drove → **driven** | make → made → **made** · get → got → **got** · come → came → **come** · read → read → **read** · swim → swam → **swum** · sing → sang → **sung** · win → won → **won** · sell → sold → **sold** · send → sent → **sent** · teach → taught → **taught** |

**Personagens:** **Nuno**, **Mateus**, **Sílvia**, **Paulo**, **Ana**, **Thandi** (ainda em Luanda até meados de Fevereiro, como disse na unidade 1). Em Joanesburgo: **Lerato Dlamini**, colega da Thandi (voz nova); um recepcionista de hotel (voz nova); um funcionário do aeroporto (voz nova). Na 6F: **Rita**, **Tunde** e **Fátima**.

## 6A — Have you ever been to Lagos?

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 6        Aula: 6A — Have you ever been to Lagos?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue perguntar e responder sobre experiências com o present
  perfect e ever / never (Have you ever been to Lagos? — Yes, I have. / No, I've never been there. I've
  never flown.), com o particípio de 10 verbos irregulares comuns.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: have / has + particípio; Have you ever…? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.; I've never…;
  been to + lugar; particípios regulares (-ed) e 10 irregulares (been, gone, seen, done, had, eaten,
  drunk, flown, taken, met)
Palavras-alvo: ever, never, abroad, plane, fly, training course, twice, three times, many times
Reciclagem: past simple (A2, 2); present continuous para combinações (A2, 3A)
Pronúncia: I've /aɪv/, you've, haven't; been na forma curta /bɪn/
Contexto: segunda-feira, 11 de Janeiro; o Mateus conta que vai a Joanesburgo com o Nuno
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 6A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 6
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello, and welcome to A2.2! Today: experiences in your life — places you've been to, things you've done. A new tense: the present perfect.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a copa da Kalandula Tech, segunda-feira de manhã; o Mateus, o Nuno e a Sílvia]

**MATEUS:** Good news, Nuno. Next Monday we're flying to Johannesburg for the training course. You and me, for five days.
**NUNO:** Johannesburg? Wow! Have you ever been there?
**MATEUS:** Yes, I have. I've been there twice. It's a great city.
**NUNO:** I've never been abroad. And I've never flown!
**SÍLVIA:** Never? Not even to Lubango?
**NUNO:** No, I always take the bus. Twelve hours!
**SÍLVIA:** Ha! I've flown many times, but I've never been to South Africa. Have you ever eaten bunny chow, Mateus?
**MATEUS:** Bunny chow? Yes, I have! It's bread with curry inside. It's delicious. Try it, Nuno!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**O *present perfect***

[ECRÃ: I **have been** to Johannesburg. → I**'ve been** · She **has flown** many times. → She**'s flown** · I **haven't been** abroad. · **Have** you **ever** **been** there? — Yes, I **have**. / No, I **haven't**.]

**PROF:** Have, or has, plus the past participle. We use it for experiences in your life — when we don't say when.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Já estiveste em Joanesburgo?" — uma experiência na vida, sem data: "Have you ever been to Johannesburg?")

***Ever* e *never***

[ECRÃ: **Have** you **ever** been to Lagos? (alguma vez) · I've **never** flown. (nunca) · She's been there **twice** / **three times** / **many times**.]

**PROF:** "Ever" — in questions: at any time in your life? "Never" — not one time.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✔ I've never been there. ✘ I haven't never been there.]
**PROF:** "I've never been" — not "I haven't never". Only one negative.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Been to***

[ECRÃ: Para uma visita: I've **been to** Johannesburg. (fui e voltei)]

**PROF:** For a visit, we say "been to": I've been to Lisbon.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**O particípio**

[ECRÃ: regulares: visit → visited → **visited** · travel → travelled → **travelled** · irregulares: be → was → **been** · go → went → **gone** · see → saw → **seen** · do → did → **done** · have → had → **had** · eat → ate → **eaten** · drink → drank → **drunk** · fly → flew → **flown** · take → took → **taken** · meet → met → **met**]

**PROF:** Regular verbs: the participle is the same as the past — visited, travelled. Irregular verbs have a third form. Listen and repeat: be, was, been.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** Go, went, gone. See, saw, seen. Do, did, done. Eat, ate, eaten. Fly, flew, flown.
[PAUSA 2 s depois de cada grupo]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In speaking: I've, you've, she's, haven't. I've been there. I've never flown.
[ECRÃ: I've /aɪv/ · haven't /ˈhævnt/]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Been — usually short: "bin". Have you ever been to Lagos?
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Answer about you. Use "Yes, I have" or "No, I haven't".
**PROF:** Have you ever been abroad?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, I have. I've been to … / No, I haven't. I've never been abroad.]
**PROF:** Have you ever flown?
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Have you ever eaten fish for breakfast?
[PAUSA 5 s]

**PROF:** Now ask me two questions with "Have you ever…?"
[PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: modelos — Have you ever been to Benguela? · Have you ever met a famous person? · Have you ever eaten kizaca?]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 3A):** And what are you doing next weekend?
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Great! Experiences with the present perfect. Next lesson: "When did you go?" — from the experience to the details.

## 6B — When did you go?

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 6        Aula: 6B — When did you go?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue passar de uma experiência (present perfect) para os
  pormenores com o past simple (I've lost my luggage once. It happened in Lisbon in 2019. I waited
  for three days.), e fazer as perguntas de seguimento (When did you go? What happened?).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: present perfect sem data / past simple com data ou pormenores (in 2019, last year,
  when I was…); perguntas de seguimento com did; 10 particípios: lost, forgotten, written, spoken,
  bought, given, left, slept, broken, driven
Palavras-alvo: luggage, suitcase, passport, miss a flight, delay, airport, once, happen
Reciclagem: present perfect com ever / never (6A); past simple e perguntas (A2, 2B)
Pronúncia: Have you /həv jə/ fraco nas perguntas; forte na resposta curta (Yes, I have.)
Contexto: segunda-feira, 18 de Janeiro, de madrugada; o Mateus, o Nuno e a Thandi à espera no
  aeroporto de Luanda
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 6B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! "Have you ever…?" opens a conversation. Then people want details: when, where, what happened. Today: from the present perfect to the past simple.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o aeroporto de Luanda, segunda-feira, 5h30; o Mateus, o Nuno e a Thandi sentados à porta de embarque]

**NUNO:** I'm a bit nervous. Have you ever lost your luggage, Thandi?
**THANDI:** Yes, I have — once. It happened in Lisbon, in 2019.
**NUNO:** Oh no. What did you do?
**THANDI:** I waited for three days. I bought new clothes, and the airline paid. In the end, the suitcase arrived on my last day!
**MATEUS:** I've never lost a suitcase, but I've missed a flight.
**NUNO:** Really? When?
**MATEUS:** Two years ago, in Cabinda. I was talking to a client on the phone, and I forgot the time. I slept at the airport!
**NUNO:** Have you ever forgotten your passport?
**THANDI AND MATEUS:** No, never!
**THANDI:** And today you have yours, right, Nuno?
**NUNO:** Yes, it's… It's in my bag. I think.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**A experiência e os pormenores**

[ECRÃ: duas colunas — **present perfect** (experiência, sem data): I've lost my luggage once. · I've missed a flight. | **past simple** (quando, onde, o que aconteceu): It happened in Lisbon in 2019. · I waited for three days. · I forgot the time.]

**PROF:** First the experience, with no time: I've lost my luggage once. Then the details, with the past simple: It happened in Lisbon. I waited for three days.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** When you say when — in 2019, two years ago, last month — use the past simple.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✔ I went to Lisbon in 2019. ✘ I've been to Lisbon in 2019.]
(PT: Com uma data ou "há…", é sempre o past simple: "I went to Lisbon in 2019", nunca "I've been to Lisbon in 2019".)

**Perguntas de seguimento**

[ECRÃ: Have you ever missed a flight? — Yes, I have. → **When did** it happen? · **Where were** you? · **What did** you do?]

**PROF:** After "Yes, I have", ask for details with "did": When did it happen? What did you do?
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Particípios novos**

[ECRÃ: lose → lost → **lost** · forget → forgot → **forgotten** · write → wrote → **written** · speak → spoke → **spoken** · buy → bought → **bought** · give → gave → **given** · leave → left → **left** · sleep → slept → **slept** · break → broke → **broken** · drive → drove → **driven**]

**PROF:** Lose, lost, lost. Forget, forgot, forgotten. Speak, spoke, spoken. Break, broke, broken. Drive, drove, driven.
[PAUSA 2 s depois de cada grupo]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In questions, "have you" is weak and fast: Have you ever lost your luggage?
[ECRÃ: Have you /həv jə/ ever…?]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** In the short answer, it's strong: Yes, I have.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Present perfect or past simple?
[ECRÃ: I … (be) to Benguela many times.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: I've been to Benguela many times.]
[ECRÃ: I … (go) there last year.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: I went there last year.]
[ECRÃ: … you ever … (lose) your phone?] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Have you ever lost your phone?]

**PROF:** Now a conversation. Have you ever had a problem on a trip?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. I've never…]
**PROF:** When did it happen? What did you do?
[PAUSA 12 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 2C):** What were you doing when the problem started?
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Experience, then details. Next lesson: "been" and "gone" — two words that change the meaning.

## 6C — She's gone to Lobito

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 6        Aula: 6C — She's gone to Lobito        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue distinguir been e gone (He's been to Lobito: foi e
  voltou. He's gone to Lobito: está lá agora.) e explicar ao telefone que um colega está fora em
  viagem de serviço e quando volta.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: has been to (foi e voltou) / has gone to (foi e ainda lá está); He's away; He's back
  on…; Can I take a message? (A1, 9)
Palavras-alvo: away, back, business trip, out of the office, return, message, client visit
Reciclagem: present continuous para combinações (A2, 3A); recados ao telefone (A1, 9)
Pronúncia: he's /hiːz/ = he has (he's gone) e he is (he's away)
Contexto: quarta-feira, 20 de Janeiro; a Ana atende telefonemas e visitas na Kalandula Tech
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 6C; pergunta 5 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today, two small words: "been" and "gone". They look the same, but they tell a different story.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a recepção da Kalandula Tech, quarta-feira; a Ana ao telefone; depois chega um visitante, o Sr. Okafor]

**ANA:** Kalandula Tech, good morning. … Mateus? I'm sorry, he's gone to Johannesburg. He's on a business trip. He's back on Monday. Can I take a message?
[A Ana desliga. O telefone toca outra vez.]
**ANA:** Kalandula Tech, good morning. … Paulo? I'm afraid he's out of the office. He's gone to Lobito — he's visiting a client. He's back tomorrow afternoon.
[Entra o Sr. Okafor.]
**MR OKAFOR:** Good morning, Ana. Is Mateus here?
**ANA:** Good morning, Mr Okafor. No, he's gone to Johannesburg with Nuno and Thandi.
**MR OKAFOR:** Johannesburg! I've been there many times. It's a great city.
**ANA:** Nuno is very happy. It's his first time abroad.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Been* e *gone***

[ECRÃ: duas imagens — Luanda → Johannesburg → Luanda (foi e voltou): **He's been to Johannesburg.** · Luanda → Johannesburg (ainda lá está): **He's gone to Johannesburg.**]

**PROF:** He's been to Johannesburg: he went, and he came back. It's an experience. He's gone to Johannesburg: he went, and he's there now.
[PAUSA 4 s]
(PT: "He's gone to Lobito" = foi para o Lobito e está lá. "He's been to Lobito" = já esteve no Lobito.)

[ECRÃ: Mr Okafor: "I've **been** there many times." (agora está em Luanda) · Ana: "Paulo's **gone** to Lobito." (agora está no Lobito)]

**PROF:** Mr Okafor has been to Johannesburg many times — he's here, in Luanda. Paulo has gone to Lobito — he isn't here.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Quem está fora**

[ECRÃ: He's **away**. · He's **out of the office**. · He's on a **business trip**. · He's **back** on Monday.]

**PROF:** To say someone isn't at work: he's away, he's out of the office, he's on a business trip. And when he returns: he's back on Monday.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***He's* = *he has* ou *he is***

[ECRÃ: He's gone. = He **has** gone. · He's away. = He **is** away.]

**PROF:** "He's" can be "he has" or "he is". Look at the next word: gone — he has. Away — he is.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Listen: He's gone to Lobito. He's back tomorrow. Same sound, different meaning.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I'm sorry, he's gone to Johannesburg. He's back on Monday.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Been or gone?
[ECRÃ: Sílvia isn't here. She's … to the bank.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: She's gone to the bank.]
[ECRÃ: I know Cabinda well. I've … there five times.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: I've been there five times.]
[ECRÃ: Where's Paulo? — He's … to lunch.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: He's gone to lunch.]

**PROF:** Now you're at reception. I call: "Can I speak to your manager?" Your manager is on a business trip in Benguela until Friday.
[PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: I'm sorry, she's gone to Benguela. She's on a business trip. She's back on Friday. Can I take a message?]

**PROF (reciclagem — A1, 9):** And now take my message: my name is Chris, and my number is 923 456 781.
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: OK, Chris, 923 456 781. I'll give her the message.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! Now practise with your tutor: "Have you ever…?" Then: how to find your way in an airport.

## 6D — Along the corridor, through the door

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 6        Aula: 6D — Along the corridor, through the door        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue pedir, dar e perceber direcções dentro de um aeroporto ou
  de um edifício com preposições de movimento (Go along this corridor, through the doors and up the
  stairs. The gate is past the shops.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 4
Gramática-alvo: along, across, through, past, up, down, into, out of, to, from; imperativo (revisto);
  Excuse me, how do I get to…?; Is it far?
Palavras-alvo: corridor, gate, stairs, lift, escalator, sign, passport control, exit, terminal,
  car park, shuttle bus
Reciclagem: direcções na rua (A1, 7); there is / are (A1, 7)
Pronúncia: acento de frase nas direcções (Go ALONG the CORridor, THROUGH the DOORS)
Contexto: segunda-feira, 18 de Janeiro; o aeroporto de Joanesburgo; o Nuno separa-se dos colegas e
  procura a saída e o autocarro do hotel
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 6D; pergunta 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! In A1 you gave directions in the street: turn left, go straight on. Today: directions inside a big building — like an airport.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o aeroporto de Joanesburgo, segunda-feira, 11h; o Nuno sozinho, com a mala, olha para os sinais]

**NUNO:** Excuse me, I'm looking for my colleagues. I went to the toilet, and now I can't find them. They're waiting at the exit for the shuttle bus to the hotel. How do I get there?
**FUNCIONÁRIA:** OK. First, go through passport control. Then go down the escalator and walk along the corridor.
**NUNO:** Down the escalator, along the corridor…
**FUNCIONÁRIA:** That's right. Go past the shops and the cafés. At the end of the corridor, go through the big glass doors. That's the exit.
**NUNO:** And the shuttle bus?
**FUNCIONÁRIA:** Go out of the building and across the road. The shuttle buses stop in front of the car park.
**NUNO:** Is it far?
**FUNCIONÁRIA:** No, five minutes on foot.
**NUNO:** Thank you very much!
[Cinco minutos depois]
**THANDI:** Nuno! There you are! Welcome to Johannesburg!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Preposições de movimento**

[ECRÃ: desenhos com uma seta para cada uma — **along** the corridor · **across** the road · **through** the doors · **past** the shops · **up / down** the escalator · **into / out of** the building]

**PROF:** Along — from one end to the other: along the corridor. Across — from one side to the other: across the road. Through — in one side, out the other: through the doors.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Past — you see it, but you don't stop: past the shops. Up and down — the stairs, the escalator. Into and out of — a building, a room.
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Pedir direcções num edifício**

[ECRÃ: **Excuse me, how do I get to** gate 12? · **Where's** passport control? · **Is it far?**]

**PROF:** Excuse me, how do I get to the exit? Is it far?
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Dar direcções: por ordem**

[ECRÃ: **First,** go through passport control. **Then** go down the escalator. **At the end of** the corridor, go through the doors.]

**PROF:** Give directions in order: first, then, at the end of. Use the imperative: go, walk, take.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In directions, the important words are strong: Go ALONG the CORridor, THROUGH the DOORS.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: Go past the shops and through the big glass doors.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a planta de um edifício de escritórios: a entrada, o corredor, as escadas, o elevador e a sala de reuniões no primeiro andar]

**PROF:** You're at the entrance. Tell me how to get to the meeting room.
[PAUSA 15 s]
[ECRÃ: Go through the doors and along the corridor. Go past the lift and up the stairs. The meeting room is on the left.]

**PROF:** Now think of your workplace. A visitor is at the entrance. How do they get to your office?
[PAUSA 15 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A1, 7):** Is there a bank near your office?
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: the words of airports and hotels — and the weather.

## 6E — At the airport and the hotel

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 6        Aula: 6E — At the airport and the hotel        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar as palavras do aeroporto, do hotel e do tempo
  (check-in, boarding pass, gate, delayed, luggage, reservation, single room, What's the weather
  like?), dizer as contracções do present perfect, e evitar os falsos amigos large e parents.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 4 (e 1)
Gramática-alvo: What's the weather like? — It's hot and humid.; 10 particípios: made, got, come, read,
  swum, sung, won, sold, sent, taught
Palavras-alvo: check in, check out, boarding pass, gate, departure, arrival, flight, delayed,
  cancelled, luggage, suitcase, hand luggage, passport, reservation, single room, double room, key
  card, breakfast included, hot, humid, cool, rainy season, storm
Reciclagem: present perfect (6A, 6B); comparativos (A2, 4A)
Pronúncia: contracções I've, she's (has), we've, haven't, hasn't; been /biːn/ e /bɪn/
Falsos amigos: large (≠ largo); parents (≠ parentes)
Contexto: um painel de partidas, o cartão de embarque e a confirmação de reserva do hotel do Nuno
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 6E; pergunta 7 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: the words you need at the airport and at the hotel — and how to talk about the weather.

### 2. No aeroporto (2 min)

[ECRÃ: o cartão de embarque do Nuno — "Passenger: KAPINGA/NUNO · Flight: LAD → JNB · Gate: 14 · Boarding: 07:20 · Departure: 08:00 · Seat: 23A"; um painel de partidas com um voo "DELAYED" e outro "CANCELLED"]

**PROF:** This is Nuno's boarding pass. You need it to get on the plane. His flight leaves from gate 14. Boarding is at seven twenty — that's when you get on the plane. The departure is at eight.
**PROF:** On the screen, one flight is delayed — it's late. Another flight is cancelled — there's no flight.
**PROF:** At check-in, you give your suitcase. Your luggage goes on the plane. Your hand luggage — a small bag — stays with you.
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: **check in** · **boarding pass** · **gate** · **boarding** · **departure / arrival** · **delayed** · **cancelled** · **luggage** (incontável) · **suitcase** · **hand luggage** · **passport**]

**PROF:** Luggage is uncountable, like information: my luggage, a lot of luggage. Not "luggages". One piece: a suitcase.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 3. No hotel (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a confirmação da reserva — "Jacaranda Park Hotel · Reservation for: Nuno Kapinga · Single room · Check-in: Mon 18 Jan · Check-out: Fri 22 Jan · Breakfast included"]

**PROF:** Nuno has a reservation: a single room — for one person. A double room is for two. Breakfast is included — he doesn't have to pay for it.
**PROF:** He checks in on Monday and checks out on Friday. At reception, they give him a key card to open the door.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. O tempo (1 min)

## 6F — Real English: Travel stories

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 6        Aula: 6F — Travel stories        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue identificar, em três relatos de viagens a velocidade
  natural, onde cada pessoa já esteve, quando foi e o que correu mal.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 2
Língua: A1, A2.1 e unidade 6, sem estruturas novas
Vozes: Rita (portuguesa), Tunde (nigeriano), Fátima (angolana)
Velocidade: natural
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 6F; pergunta 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Apresentação (30 s)

**PROF:** Three people talk about trips. Listen for three things: where they've been, when they went, and what went wrong. You hear them three times.

### 2. Primeira escuta: a ideia geral (3 min)

**PROF:** First: who has never been on a plane?

**RITA:** I travel a lot for work. I've been to Brazil, Mozambique, South Africa… I've been to São Paulo four times. The worst trip? Last year, my flight to São Paulo was cancelled because of a storm. I slept at the airport, on a chair. The next morning I had a meeting at nine, and I arrived at ten past nine — with no sleep!

**TUNDE:** I've driven all over Angola for work — Benguela, Huambo, Malanje, Uíge. I've never flown inside Angola. I like the roads! But once, in 2021, my car broke down near Huambo. It was night, and there was no phone signal. A farmer saw me and gave me a lift to the next town. I've never forgotten him.

**FÁTIMA:** I've never been on a plane! For the bakery, I go to markets in Benguela and Lubango by bus. Last month I went to a big food fair in Luanda. The bus left Huambo at four in the morning, and it arrived eleven hours later. I sold all my cakes on the first day! Next year I'm going to fly — maybe.

[ECRÃ: Rita — São Paulo · Tunde — Huambo · Fátima — Luanda]

### 3. Segunda escuta: os pormenores (3 min)

1. How many times has Rita been to São Paulo? (four times ✓ / twice / never)
2. Why was Rita's flight cancelled? (There was a storm. ✓ / The plane was broken. / She was late.)
3. Where did Tunde's car break down? (near Huambo ✓ / near Malanje / in Benguela)
4. Who helped Tunde? (a farmer ✓ / a police officer / a colleague)
5. How long was Fátima's bus trip to Luanda? (eleven hours ✓ / four hours / seven hours)
6. What happened at the food fair? (She sold all her cakes on the first day. ✓ / She missed the bus. / Nobody bought her cakes.)

*(Cada relato repete-se; depois aparecem as perguntas, com 8 s para responder.)*

### 4. Terceira escuta: como disseram (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Listen and repeat.
**PROF:** I've been to São Paulo four times. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** My car broke down near Huambo. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** A farmer gave me a lift. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** I've never forgotten him. [PAUSA 3 s]

**PROF:** Did you notice? Each person starts with the present perfect — I've been, I've driven, I've never been — and then tells the story in the past simple. That's what you learned in lesson B.
(PT: Primeiro a experiência, no present perfect; depois a história, no past simple.)
**PROF:** And "break down" means the car stops and doesn't work. "Give someone a lift": take someone in your car.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. A sua vez (1 min)

**PROF:** Now you. Tell me about a trip you've had — for work or for a holiday. Where have you been? When did you go? What happened?
[PAUSA 25 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I've been to … many times / once. The last time, in … , I … It was … In the end, …]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: Nuno checks in at the hotel — and there's a problem.

## 6G — Work & Life: There's a problem with my room

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 6        Aula: 6G — There's a problem with my room        Tipo: Work & Life
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue fazer o check-in num hotel, explicar com calma um problema
  com a reserva e chegar a uma solução; e explicar a um colega estrangeiro, em inglês, como apanhar um
  táxi no aeroporto de Luanda e quanto custa.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 4 (e 1, 2)
Gramática-alvo: toda a da unidade; I have a reservation for…; There's a problem with…; The reservation
  is for…; Can I have…?; What can we do?
Palavras-alvo: check in, reservation, confirmation, single / double room, nights, key card, upgrade,
  receptionist, taxi rank, fixed price, app
Reciclagem: will para ofertas e decisões (A2, 3B); can para pedidos (A1, 8); comparativos (A2, 4)
Pronúncia: datas e números ao balcão (four nights, from Monday the eighteenth)
Mediação: o Nuno explica à Lerato, em inglês, uma página em português sobre os táxis do aeroporto de
  Luanda
Contexto: segunda-feira, 18 de Janeiro, à tarde: a recepção do hotel em Joanesburgo; sexta-feira, 22:
  o último dia de formação
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 6G; conversa livre "Lost luggage"; pergunta 9 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: you arrive at your hotel, tired after the trip — and there's a problem with your reservation. How do you solve it, politely and clearly?

### 2. Situação 1: o check-in (3 min)

[ECRÃ: a recepção do Jacaranda Park Hotel, segunda-feira, 14h; o Nuno com a mala e o telemóvel na mão]

**RECEPCIONISTA:** Good afternoon. Welcome to the Jacaranda Park. How can I help you?
**NUNO:** Good afternoon. I have a reservation. My name is Nuno Kapinga — K, A, P, I, N, G, A.
**RECEPCIONISTA:** Let me check… Yes, Mr Kapinga. A single room for one night.
**NUNO:** One night? I'm sorry, there's a problem. The reservation is for four nights, from Monday to Friday. I have the confirmation here, on my phone.
**RECEPCIONISTA:** Can I see it, please? … Oh, you're right. I'm very sorry, it's our mistake.
**NUNO:** No problem. Can I have the room for four nights?
**RECEPCIONISTA:** I'm afraid we don't have any single rooms after tonight. The hotel is full for a conference.
**NUNO:** Oh no. What can we do?
**RECEPCIONISTA:** I can give you a double room for the same price. It's larger, and it has a view of the park.
**NUNO:** That's great, thank you. And is breakfast included?
**RECEPCIONISTA:** Yes, from six thirty to ten, on the first floor. Here's your key card. Room 412.
**NUNO:** Thank you very much.
**RECEPCIONISTA:** You're welcome. And again, I'm sorry about the mistake.

### 3. Como se resolve um problema no hotel (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: quatro passos]

| Passo | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| 1. Dizer que há um problema, com calma | I'm sorry, there's a problem. |
| 2. Explicar o que devia ser | The reservation is for four nights, from Monday to Friday. |
| 3. Mostrar a prova | I have the confirmation here, on my phone. |
| 4. Pedir uma solução e aceitar | Can I have the room for four nights? / What can we do? — That's great, thank you. |

**PROF:** Stay calm and polite. Say what's wrong, show the confirmation, and ask for a solution: What can we do?
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Situação 2: mediação (2 min)

[ECRÃ: sexta-feira, 22 de Janeiro, último dia de formação; a Lerato e o Nuno; no telemóvel do Nuno, uma página em português: "Aeroporto de Luanda — Táxis: praça de táxis à saída das chegadas. Táxis oficiais com preço fixo para o centro: 15 000 Kz. Também pode usar aplicações de transporte. Não aceite boleias de desconhecidos."]

**LERATO:** Nuno, I'm going to Luanda next month for the project. How do I get from the airport to the city?
**NUNO:** Let me see… This page is in Portuguese. It says the taxi rank is at the exit, after arrivals. The official taxis have a fixed price to the centre: fifteen thousand kwanzas. You can also use an app.
**LERATO:** Fifteen thousand kwanzas — how much is that in rand?
**NUNO:** I don't know exactly. Maybe about three hundred rand. And it says: don't accept a lift from strangers.
**LERATO:** OK. Taxi rank, official taxi, fixed price. Thanks, Nuno!
**NUNO:** And I'll send you my number. Call me when you arrive!

**PROF:** Mediation: Nuno doesn't translate everything. He gives Lerato what she needs: where, what, how much, and one safety tip.
(PT: Na mediação, diga onde, o quê, quanto custa e um conselho de segurança — não traduza tudo.)

### 5. A sua vez (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** I'm the receptionist. Good evening, welcome. Your name, please?
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** Let me check… A double room for two nights.
[PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: I'm sorry, there's a problem. The reservation is for a single room, for three nights. I have the confirmation here.]
**PROF:** Oh, you're right. I'm sorry. We have a single room for three nights, but on the ground floor, near the restaurant. It's a bit noisy.
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: That's OK, thank you. / Is there a quieter room? Can I see it?]

**PROF:** Now a foreign colleague asks you: "How do I get from the airport to your office?" Explain in three sentences.
[PAUSA 15 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Fantastic! Now go to the tutor: you arrive in Johannesburg — but your suitcase doesn't. Good luck!
