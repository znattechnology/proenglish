# A2.2 · Unidade 9 — Customer service · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções e personagens: ver os [guiões da unidade 1 do A2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2.1 e das unidades 6 a 8. Novo: *could* e *would* em pedidos (*Could you help me? Would you check this, please? Could I speak to the manager?*); *I'm afraid…* para recusar ou dar uma má notícia (já usado na unidade 3); reclamar e responder a uma reclamação; *still* (*It still doesn't work*); verbos seguidos de *to* (*need, want, try, decide, forget, hope, plan*) e de *-ing* (*stop, finish, enjoy, don't mind*).

**Não usar nesta unidade:** o 2.º condicional e *would* com valor hipotético (*I would buy…*); *Would you mind + -ing?*; *could* para capacidade no passado; a voz passiva; orações relativas; *too / enough*; o *past perfect*.

**Personagens:** **Sílvia**, **Paulo**, **Nuno**, **Mateus**, **Ana**, **Arjun** (em Pune, por videochamada), **João** (Mussulo Energy), **Mr Okafor** (cliente nigeriano da Kalandula Tech, A1). Na 9A: uma funcionária do banco (voz nova). Na 9B: um funcionário da LuandaNet, o fornecedor de internet (voz nova). Na 9E: **Tunde**, **Rita**, **Fátima**, **Emma** e quem as atende.

**A história:** Abril de 2027. A Sílvia, agora directora financeira, trata de um problema no banco; a internet da Kalandula Tech está lenta; o Nuno decide fazer o curso de inglês escrito; e um cliente da Mussulo Energy reclama de um pagamento cobrado duas vezes.

## 9A — Could you help me?

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9A — Could you help me?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue fazer pedidos formais com Could you…? / Would you…? / Could
  I…? e responder com cortesia, aceitando (Of course. / Sure.) ou recusando com uma alternativa (I'm
  afraid I can't, but…).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: Could you + verbo…, please? · Would you + verbo…, please? · Could I + verbo…? ·
  respostas: Of course. / Sure. / Certainly. / I'm afraid I can't, but I can… · can (informal) /
  could (mais educado)
Palavras-alvo: transfer, account, account number, bank statement, manager, department, check, print,
  sign, certainly
Reciclagem: can para pedidos (A1, 8); I'm afraid (A2, 3C)
Pronúncia: could you /kʊdʒu/ e would you /wʊdʒu/ em fala ligada; entoação de pedido (sobe no fim)
Contexto: segunda-feira, 5 de Abril; a Sílvia vai ao banco por causa de uma transferência atrasada
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 9A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 9
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! At a bank, a hotel or an office, you often ask for help. Today: polite requests — "Could you…?" — and polite answers.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o balcão de um banco em Luanda, segunda-feira de manhã; a Sílvia e uma funcionária]

**FUNCIONÁRIA:** Good morning. How can I help you?
**SÍLVIA:** Good morning. Could you help me, please? We sent a transfer to a supplier last Monday, but the supplier says the money hasn't arrived.
**FUNCIONÁRIA:** Of course. Could I have the account number, please?
**SÍLVIA:** Here it is. And would you check the date of the transfer, please?
**FUNCIONÁRIA:** Certainly… Yes, it left the account on the twenty-ninth of March. That's strange.
**SÍLVIA:** Could I speak to the manager?
**FUNCIONÁRIA:** I'm afraid the manager isn't here today, but I can call the transfer department now.
**SÍLVIA:** Thank you. And could you print a bank statement for March?
**FUNCIONÁRIA:** Sure. Could you sign here, please?
**SÍLVIA:** Of course. Thank you very much for your help.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Pedir com *could* e *would***

[ECRÃ: **Could you** help me, please? · **Would you** check the date, please? · **Could I** speak to the manager? · **Could I have** the account number, please?]

**PROF:** "Could you…?" and "Would you…?" ask another person to do something. "Could I…?" asks for permission, or for something you want. After "could" and "would", the simple verb.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✔ Could you help me? ✘ Could you to help me? ✘ Could you helping me?]

***Can* ou *could*?**

[ECRÃ: **Can** you help me? (informal, com colegas e amigos) · **Could** you help me? (mais educado, com clientes, no banco, com quem não conhece)]

**PROF:** "Can" is fine with friends and colleagues. "Could" and "would" are more polite — use them at a bank, with clients, with new people.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: Em português, a diferença é como entre "Pode ajudar-me?" e "Podia ajudar-me, por favor?".)

**Responder**

[ECRÃ: sim: **Of course. · Sure. · Certainly.** · não: **I'm afraid I can't, but I can…** · **I'm afraid the manager isn't here today, but…**]

**PROF:** To say yes: Of course. Sure. Certainly. To say no politely, use "I'm afraid", and offer something else: I'm afraid I can't, but I can call the department.
[PAUSA 4 s]
(PT: Uma boa recusa tem sempre uma alternativa: "but I can…".)

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In fast English, "could you" sounds like "cudja", and "would you" like "wudja". Could you help me? Would you check this?
[ECRÃ: could you /kʊdʒu/ · would you /wʊdʒu/]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** A polite request goes up at the end: Could you help me, please? ↗ Without the music, it sounds like an order.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Make a polite request.
[ECRÃ: Ask the receptionist to call a taxi.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Could you call a taxi, please?]
[ECRÃ: You want to use the meeting room.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Could I use the meeting room, please?]
[ECRÃ: Ask a colleague to send you the report.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Would you send me the report, please?]

**PROF:** Now I ask you. Say no politely, and offer something else. Could you work on Saturday?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: I'm afraid I can't, but I can stay late on Friday.]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 8B):** What will you do if the bank doesn't find the transfer?
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very polite! Next lesson: when there's a problem — how to make a complaint.

## 9B — I'd like to make a complaint

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9B — I'd like to make a complaint        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue fazer uma reclamação educada e clara: dizer porque liga, o
  problema, desde quando (present perfect com for / since), o que já fez, e o que quer (I'd like a
  technician / a refund / a discount).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: I'm calling about…; It still doesn't work. / It's still slow.; present perfect com
  for / since e com o número de vezes (I've called three times); I'd like + nome / I'd like to + verbo;
  Could you…? (9A)
Palavras-alvo: complaint, make a complaint, slow, still, contract, router, restart, technician,
  discount, customer number
Reciclagem: present perfect (A2, 7); could (9A)
Pronúncia: still /stɪl/ (sem vogal antes do s, A2, 8D); calma e firmeza na voz
Contexto: terça-feira, 6 de Abril; o Paulo liga para a LuandaNet, o fornecedor de internet da
  Kalandula Tech
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 9B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Sometimes a service doesn't work, and we need to complain. Today: how to make a complaint — clear, polite and firm.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o escritório da Kalandula Tech; o Paulo ao telefone, com o portátil aberto; no ecrã, um teste de velocidade da internet muito lento]

**FUNCIONÁRIO:** LuandaNet, good morning. How can I help you?
**PAULO:** Good morning. I'm calling about our internet. I'd like to make a complaint.
**FUNCIONÁRIO:** I'm sorry to hear that. What's the problem?
**PAULO:** Our internet has been very slow since last Thursday. We have a contract for one hundred megabits, but today it's only five.
**FUNCIONÁRIO:** I see. Have you restarted the router?
**PAULO:** Yes, I've restarted it three times. And I've called you twice this week, but it's still slow.
**FUNCIONÁRIO:** I'm very sorry. Could I have your customer number?
**PAULO:** It's seven, eight, oh, three, four. And could a technician come today, please? We can't work like this.
**FUNCIONÁRIO:** I'm afraid there's no technician free today. But one can come tomorrow at nine.
**PAULO:** OK. Tomorrow at nine. And I'd like a discount for this month.
**FUNCIONÁRIO:** That's fair. I'll send your request to the manager.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Uma reclamação em cinco partes**

[ECRÃ: cinco passos]

| Parte | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| 1. Porque liga | I'm calling about our internet. I'd like to make a complaint. |
| 2. O problema | It's very slow. We have a contract for 100 megabits, but today it's only five. |
| 3. Desde quando, e o que já fez | since last Thursday · I've restarted it three times. I've called you twice. |
| 4. *Still* | …but it's still slow. |
| 5. O que quer | I'd like a technician today. I'd like a discount. |

**PROF:** Five parts: why you're calling, the problem, since when and what you've done, "still", and what you want.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Since / for* e as vezes**

[ECRÃ: It's been slow **since** last Thursday. · I've called you **twice** this week. · I've restarted it **three times**.]

**PROF:** The present perfect from Unit 7 is perfect for complaints: since last Thursday, three times, twice this week.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Still***

[ECRÃ: It's **still** slow. · It **still** doesn't work.]

**PROF:** "Still" means the problem continues. It goes after "be" — it's still slow — and before other verbs — it still doesn't work.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Ainda está lento" → "It's still slow." "Ainda não funciona" → "It still doesn't work.")

***I'd like…***

[ECRÃ: **I'd like** a discount. (nome) · **I'd like to** speak to the manager. (verbo)]

**PROF:** "I'd like" plus a noun, or "I'd like to" plus a verb. It's firm, but polite.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Firme, mas educado**

[ECRÃ: ✘ This is terrible! You're useless! · ✔ I'm not happy with the service. I'd like a solution today.]

**PROF:** Be firm, not rude. Say what you want clearly — and stay calm.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "Still" starts with "s" — no extra vowel, like in Unit 8: still. It's still slow.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** In a complaint, your voice is calm and clear. Not loud. Repeat: I've called you twice, but it's still slow.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Your new phone doesn't charge. You bought it ten days ago. Call the shop. Use the five parts.
[PAUSA 25 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Hello, I'm calling about a phone. I'd like to make a complaint. I bought it ten days ago, and it doesn't charge. I've tried two chargers, but it still doesn't work. I'd like a replacement, please.]

**PROF:** The shop says: "I'm afraid we don't have any phones today." What do you say?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: Could you call me when you have one? / I'd like a refund, then.]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 8F):** And what does the screen say when you plug it in?
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent complaint! Next lesson: verbs with "to" and verbs with "-ing".

## 9C — I need to talk to someone

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9C — I need to talk to someone        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar os verbos mais comuns seguidos de to (need, want, try,
  decide, forget, hope, plan) e de -ing (stop, finish, enjoy, don't mind) para falar de planos,
  esforços e gostos (I've decided to do the course. I enjoy speaking, but I need to practise writing.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 4
Gramática-alvo: verbo + to + verbo: need to, want to, try to, decide to, forget to, hope to, plan to,
  would like to; verbo + -ing: enjoy, finish, stop, don't mind (e like / love / hate, do A1)
Palavras-alvo: course, improve, practise, writing, article, every day, someone, form, HR
Reciclagem: like / love / hate + -ing (A1, 9); present perfect (A2, 7)
Pronúncia: to fraco /tə/ (I need to go); o -ing sem vogal no fim (A2, 1A)
Contexto: quarta-feira, 7 de Abril; o Nuno conta à Sílvia que decidiu fazer o curso de inglês escrito
  (A2.1, revisão 1)
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 9C; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Some verbs take "to", and some take "-ing". "I need to go", but "I enjoy going". Today: the most common ones.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a copa, quarta-feira à tarde; a Sílvia e o Nuno com um café]

**SÍLVIA:** Nuno, you look busy. What are you reading?
**NUNO:** An article about networks. I've decided to do the English course. Mr Baptista told me about it in December.
**SÍLVIA:** Great! Why?
**NUNO:** I enjoy speaking English, but I need to practise writing. My reports take hours! So I'm trying to read one article every day.
**SÍLVIA:** Good idea. When is the course?
**NUNO:** In May. I'm planning to study on Saturday mornings. I don't mind working at the weekend.
**SÍLVIA:** And did you send the form to HR?
**NUNO:** Oh no! I forgot to send it. I need to talk to someone in HR today.
**SÍLVIA:** Ha! Stop worrying. Send it now, and finish reading your article later.
**NUNO:** You're right. I hope to start in May!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Verbo + *to***

[ECRÃ: I've **decided to do** the course. · I **need to practise** writing. · I'm **trying to read** an article every day. · I'm **planning to study** on Saturdays. · I **forgot to send** it. · I **hope to start** in May. · I **want to improve**. · I**'d like to learn**.]

**PROF:** These verbs take "to" plus a verb: need to, want to, try to, decide to, forget to, hope to, plan to — and would like to.
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Verbo + *-ing***

[ECRÃ: I **enjoy speaking** English. · I **don't mind working** at the weekend. · **Stop worrying**! · **Finish reading** your article. · I **like / love / hate** reading. (A1)]

**PROF:** These verbs take "-ing": enjoy, don't mind, stop, finish — and from A1, like, love, hate.
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: ✘ I enjoy to speak. ✔ I enjoy speaking. · ✘ I need practising. ✔ I need to practise.]
(PT: Em português, quase todos levam o infinitivo ("gosto de falar", "preciso de praticar"). Em inglês, há que decorar: "enjoy speaking", mas "need to practise".)

***Don't mind***

[ECRÃ: I **don't mind** working at the weekend. (não me importo)]

**PROF:** "I don't mind working at the weekend" — it's OK for me, it isn't a problem.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "To" is weak: I need to go — "I need tə go". I want to learn.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** And the "-ing": speaking, working, reading — stop at the "ng".
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** "To" or "-ing"?
[ECRÃ: I enjoy … (work) with people.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: I enjoy working with people.]
[ECRÃ: I need … (finish) the report today.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: I need to finish the report today.]
[ECRÃ: I forgot … (call) the client.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: I forgot to call the client.]
[ECRÃ: Stop … (talk), please!] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: Stop talking, please!]

**PROF:** Now you. What do you enjoy doing at work? And what do you need to improve?
[PAUSA 12 s]
[ECRÃ: I enjoy … , but I need to … I'm trying to …]
**PROF:** What are you planning to do this year?
[PAUSA 8 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 7C):** How long have you studied English?
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! Now practise with your tutor: requests at the bank. Then: the words of refunds and replacements.

## 9D — Refunds and replacements

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9D — Refunds and replacements        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar as palavras do serviço ao cliente (order number,
  refund, replacement, receipt, broken, damaged, delay, invoice, charge twice), distinguir pela entoação
  um pedido educado de uma ordem, e evitar os falsos amigos attend e exit.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2, 3
Gramática-alvo: get a refund / a replacement; It arrived damaged.; You charged me twice.; There's a
  delay.; I'd like to return it.
Palavras-alvo: order, order number, refund, replacement, return, receipt, invoice, broken, damaged,
  delay, charge, charge twice, customer service, apologise
Reciclagem: comparativos (A2, 4); past simple (A2, 2)
Pronúncia: entoação: pedido educado (sobe, suave) ≠ ordem (desce, seca)
Falsos amigos: attend (≠ atender); exit (≠ êxito)
Contexto: a página de ajuda de uma loja online, com perguntas de clientes
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 9D; pergunta 7 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! When you buy something and there's a problem, you need the right words: refund, replacement, receipt. Today: the words of customer service.

### 2. A página de ajuda (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: "Help — Your orders", uma página de uma loja online com quatro perguntas e respostas]

**PROF:** This is the help page of an online shop. Listen to the customers' problems.
**PROF:** One: "My order arrived damaged. The box was wet, and the screen is broken." Answer: "Send us a photo. We'll send you a replacement — a new one."
**PROF:** Two: "I don't want the shoes. They're small for me." Answer: "You can return them in thirty days. We'll give you a refund — your money back."
**PROF:** Three: "You charged me twice for one order." Answer: "We're very sorry. Please send us the invoice and the bank statement."
**PROF:** Four: "My order is late." Answer: "There's a delay with the delivery. It'll arrive on Friday."
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: **order** (encomenda) · **order number** · **damaged** (danificado) · **broken** (partido, avariado) · **replacement** (troca por um novo) · **refund** (reembolso) · **return** (devolver) · **receipt** (talão, recibo) · **invoice** (factura) · **charge twice** (cobrar duas vezes) · **delay** (atraso)]

**PROF:** A refund: you get your money back. A replacement: you get a new one. A receipt: the paper from the shop. An invoice: the bill for a company.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 3. A entoação: pedido ou ordem? (2 min)

**PROF:** The same words can be polite or rude. Listen to the music.
**PROF:** Could you send me a replacement, please? ↗ — polite.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Send me a replacement. ↘ — an order. Not good with a customer service agent!
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat the polite one: Could you send me a replacement, please?
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** And "receipt" — the "p" is silent: re-CEIPT.
[ECRÃ: rece**i**pt — o p não se diz]
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: ✘ **attend** ≠ atender · atender o telefone = **answer the phone** · atender um cliente = **serve / help a customer** · attend = assistir a (uma reunião, A2, 3D)]
**PROF:** In Unit 3 you learned "attend a meeting". "Atender o telefone" is "answer the phone". "Atender um cliente" is "serve" or "help a customer".
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✘ **exit** ≠ êxito · êxito = **success** · exit = saída]
**PROF:** "Exit" is the door out of a building — saída. "Êxito" is "success".
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "O projecto foi um êxito" → "The project was a success.")

### 5. Prática e fecho (2 min)

**PROF:** What do you want? Your new laptop doesn't turn on.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I'd like a replacement. / I'd like a refund.]
**PROF:** The bank charged you twice for one payment.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: You charged me twice. I'd like my money back, please.]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 4D):** Is a refund better than a discount, for you? Why?
[PAUSA 6 s]

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: four real complaints. Listen to what each customer wants.

## 9E — Real English: I'm not happy with…

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9E — I'm not happy with…        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue identificar, em quatro reclamações a velocidade natural, o
  problema, o que o cliente quer e a solução proposta.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2, 3
Língua: A1, A2.1 e unidades 6–9, sem estruturas novas
Vozes: Tunde (nigeriano) e uma funcionária de banco; Rita (portuguesa) e um funcionário de uma
  operadora; Fátima (angolana) e um fornecedor; Emma (britânica) e um empregado de restaurante
Velocidade: natural
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 9E; pergunta 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Apresentação (30 s)

**PROF:** Four complaints: at a bank, a phone company, a supplier and a restaurant. Listen for three things: the problem, what the customer wants, and the solution.

### 2. Primeira escuta: a ideia geral (3 min)

**PROF:** First: which customer doesn't get what they want today?

**RECLAMAÇÃO 1 — TUNDE, NO BANCO**
**TUNDE:** Good morning. My card doesn't work. I tried to pay at the supermarket this morning, and it said "card blocked".
**FUNCIONÁRIA:** I'm sorry about that. Could I see your passport, please? … Ah, I see. The system blocked the card because someone typed the wrong PIN three times.
**TUNDE:** That was me! I forgot my new PIN.
**FUNCIONÁRIA:** No problem. I'll unblock it now, and you can choose a new PIN.

**RECLAMAÇÃO 2 — RITA, AO TELEFONE**
**RITA:** Hello. I'm calling about my phone bill. You've charged me for international calls, but I haven't made any international calls this month.
**FUNCIONÁRIO:** I'm very sorry. Let me check… Yes, it's our mistake. We'll take it off your next bill.
**RITA:** Thank you. Could you send me an email to confirm?
**FUNCIONÁRIO:** Of course.

**RECLAMAÇÃO 3 — FÁTIMA, AO FORNECEDOR**
**FÁTIMA:** Good afternoon. I ordered fifty bags of flour last week, and you delivered thirty. I need the other twenty today — I have a big order for a wedding.
**FORNECEDOR:** I'm afraid we don't have any more flour today. The truck is arriving tomorrow morning.
**FÁTIMA:** Tomorrow? Then I'd like a discount on this order.
**FORNECEDOR:** OK. I can give you ten per cent.

**RECLAMAÇÃO 4 — EMMA, NO RESTAURANTE**
**EMMA:** Excuse me. I ordered the grilled fish, but this is chicken. And it's cold.
**EMPREGADO:** Oh, I'm so sorry. I'll bring you the fish right now.
**EMMA:** Thank you. Could you also bring some more water, please?
**EMPREGADO:** Of course. And the dessert is free today — our apologies.

[ECRÃ: Tunde — card · Rita — phone bill · Fátima — flour · Emma — wrong dish]

### 3. Segunda escuta: os pormenores (3 min)

1. Why is Tunde's card blocked? (He typed the wrong PIN three times. ✓ / The bank closed his account. / The card is old.)
2. What's the problem with Rita's bill? (international calls she didn't make ✓ / a very high internet bill / two bills in one month)
3. What does Rita ask for? (an email to confirm ✓ / a refund in cash / a new phone)
4. How many bags of flour did Fátima get? (thirty ✓ / fifty / twenty)
5. What does Fátima get? (a ten per cent discount ✓ / the flour today / a free truck)
6. What does the restaurant give Emma? (the right dish and a free dessert ✓ / a refund / a free bottle of wine)

*(Cada reclamação repete-se; depois aparecem as perguntas, com 8 s para responder.)*

### 4. Terceira escuta: como disseram (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Listen and repeat.
**PROF:** I'm calling about my phone bill. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** I haven't made any international calls this month. [PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Then I'd like a discount on this order. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Excuse me. I ordered the grilled fish, but this is chicken. [PAUSA 4 s]

**PROF:** Did you notice? The good answers to a complaint have three things: "I'm sorry", the reason, and a solution — "I'll bring you the fish right now."
(PT: Uma boa resposta a uma reclamação: pedir desculpa, explicar e propor uma solução.)

### 5. A sua vez (1 min)

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

### 2. Situação 1: a chamada (3 min)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 12 de Abril; o Nuno atende o telefone na secretária; do outro lado, o Mr Okafor, no escritório dele]

**NUNO:** I understand. Let me check… The last two laptops are in our warehouse. I'll send them to your office today. And we'll replace the damaged one.
**MR OKAFOR:** When?
**NUNO:** We'll bring the new one by Wednesday morning, and we'll take the damaged one back. If there's any problem, I'll call you myself.
**MR OKAFOR:** OK. That's better. Thank you, Nuno.
**NUNO:** Thank you for your patience, Mr Okafor. I'll send you an email with the details now.

### 3. Como se atende uma reclamação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: cinco passos]
