# A2.1 · Revisão 1 (unidades 1 a 5) · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções e personagens: ver os [guiões da unidade 1 do A2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que a revisão pode usar:** tudo do A1 e das unidades 1 a 5 do A2, e nada de novo, com uma excepção: duas estratégias de exame (*That's a good question. Well…* para ganhar tempo; *Do you mean…?* para confirmar). As estratégias do A1 mantêm-se (*Can you repeat that, please? Let me think. I don't know the word in English. It's a kind of…*).

**Não usar:** o *present perfect*; *will* e *going to* para previsões; *could*; o condicional (*if*, *would* + verbo, excepto *would like*); *too / enough*; *used to*.

**Personagens:** **Nuno** e o **Sr. Baptista** (R1A); **Lurdes Cassoma**, a aluna-modelo dos exames do A1 (contabilista num banco, de Benguela, casada, um filho e uma filha), agora no exame do A2.1 (R1B).

**Quando:** sexta-feira, 11 de Dezembro de 2026, no fim da primeira semana de instalação na Mussulo Energy.

## R1A — Review: my work, my story, my plans

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: Revisão 1        Aula: R1A — Review: my work, my story, my plans        Tipo: Revisão
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue falar durante 2 minutos sobre o seu trabalho (agora e neste
  período), contar um episódio do passado e dizer os planos, escolhendo entre os cinco tempos do A2.1,
  e corrigir os 10 erros mais comuns do A2.1.
Objectivos de unidade que revê: U1–U5, todos
Gramática revista: present simple / present continuous; past simple / past continuous; present
  continuous para combinações, going to e will (ofertas); comparativos e superlativos; should, have
  to, must
Situação nova: a conversa de fim de ano do Sr. Baptista com o Nuno
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: laboratórios R1-1 e R1-2; conversa de ensaio; questionário da revisão
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! You finished five units of A2. Today: no new grammar. We put it all together — your work, your story and your plans — in one conversation.

### 2. Situação: a conversa de fim de ano (3 min)

[ECRÃ: o gabinete do Sr. Baptista, sexta-feira, 11 de Dezembro; o Nuno senta-se. Título: "End-of-year review"]

**SR. BAPTISTA:** Come in, Nuno. So, it's the end of the year. Tell me: what do you usually do here, and what are you working on these days?
**NUNO:** I usually support the staff and fix computers. But this month I'm working on the Mussulo Energy installation. We started on Monday.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** How's it going?
**NUNO:** Well, it's going well, but we had a problem on Wednesday. I was checking the cables on the third floor when the power went off. The UPS didn't arrive on time, so we had to stop for two hours.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** And what happened in the end?
**NUNO:** In the end, João found a generator, and we finished the floor at nine p.m.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Nine p.m.! You should rest this weekend.
**NUNO:** I'm going to! On Saturday I'm visiting my aunt, and on Sunday I'm going to sleep all day.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Ha! And what's the most difficult part of your job?
**NUNO:** For me, the reports. Writing in English is much harder than speaking.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** I see. Next year there's a training course in English. Do you want to do it?
**NUNO:** I'd love to! I'll send you an email with my name today.

### 3. Cinco tempos lado a lado (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: cinco caixas, cada uma com a palavra-chave e uma frase do Nuno]

| Para… | Tempo | Frase do Nuno |
|---|---|---|
| o habitual (*usually, every day*) | *present simple* | I **usually support** the staff. |
| o agora e este período (*these days, this month*) | *present continuous* | This month I**'m working** on the installation. |
| o que aconteceu (*on Wednesday, in the end*) | *past simple* | The UPS **didn't arrive** on time. |
| o que estava a acontecer (*when…*) | *past continuous* | I **was checking** the cables **when** the power **went off**. |
| o que está combinado / o que tenciona / o que decide agora | *present continuous* / *going to* / *will* | On Saturday I**'m visiting** my aunt. I**'m going to** sleep all day. I**'ll send** you an email. |

**PROF:** Look at the time words again. They tell you the tense.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** And remember the three futures: an arrangement — I'm visiting my aunt. An intention — I'm going to sleep. A decision now — I'll send you an email.
[PAUSA 4 s]

**PROF:** Now you. Complete with your life.
[ECRÃ: I usually … at work.] [PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: These days I'm … ] [PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Last week I was … when … ] [PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: This weekend I'm … ] [PAUSA 6 s]

### 4. Os erros mais comuns do A2.1 (2 min)

**PROF:** Here are the ten most common mistakes in Units 1 to 5. Look at the wrong sentence, and say the right one.

[ECRÃ: um erro de cada vez; a frase certa aparece depois da pausa]

| Errado | Certo | Unidade |
|---|---|---|
| ~~I'm needing a new laptop.~~ | I need a new laptop. | 1 |
| ~~What does she like? (= como é ela?)~~ | What's she like? | 1 |
| ~~I born in Huambo.~~ | I was born in Huambo. | 2 |
| ~~What did happen?~~ | What happened? | 2 |
| ~~Would you like coming?~~ | Would you like to come? | 3 |
| ~~I'll to call him.~~ | I'll call him. | 3 |
| ~~It's very cheaper.~~ | It's much cheaper. | 4 |
| ~~I need some informations.~~ | I need some information. | 4 |
| ~~You should to rest.~~ | You should rest. | 5 |
| ~~You mustn't wear a tie. (= não é preciso)~~ | You don't have to wear a tie. | 5 |

*(Para cada linha: o professor lê a frase errada com tom de dúvida, [PAUSA 4 s], e diz a certa.)*

(PT: Os dois com nota entre parênteses não são erros de gramática: são erros de sentido. "What does she like?" pergunta do que ela gosta; "You mustn't wear a tie" quer dizer que é proibido.)

### 5. A sua vez: a sua conversa de fim de ano (1 min 30 s)

## R1B — Getting ready for the A2.1 speaking exam

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: Revisão 1        Aula: R1B — Getting ready for the A2.1 speaking exam        Tipo: Revisão
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue descrever as três partes do exame oral do A2.1, sabe o que é
  avaliado e usa duas estratégias novas: ganhar tempo (That's a good question. Well…) e confirmar
  (Do you mean…?).
Objectivos de unidade que revê: U1–U5, todos
Gramática revista: toda a do A2.1
Língua nova (blocos fixos): That's a good question. Well… · Do you mean…?
Situação: a Lurdes (dos exames do A1) faz o exame de demonstração do A2.1
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: conversa de ensaio; exame oral do A2.1
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Soon you take your A2.1 speaking exam. Today you see how it works, you learn two new strategies, and you practise every part.

### 2. Como é o exame (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: três blocos]

| Parte | O quê | Tempo |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Interview | O seu trabalho agora, as pessoas com quem trabalha e o seu percurso | cerca de 2 minutos |
| 2. A story | Uma situação do passado: o que aconteceu, o que estava a fazer, como acabou | cerca de 1 a 2 minutos |
| 3. Situation | Uma situação sorteada: mudar uma reunião, convidar e combinar, comparar e recomendar, ou dar conselhos a um colega doente | cerca de 2 a 3 minutos |

**PROF:** It's a conversation with the examiner, like in A1. It's a bit longer: about six or seven minutes.
(PT: Como no A1, é uma conversa com o examinador. É um pouco mais longa: seis a sete minutos.)

**PROF:** In A2, the examiner wants longer answers. Answer in two or three sentences, not one word.
(PT: No A2, responda com duas ou três frases, não só com uma palavra.)

**PROF:** The rules are the same as in A1: the recording is your choice, the exam opens when you finish most of the A2.1 lessons, and when you don't pass, you can try again a week later.
(PT: As regras são as do A1: a gravação é opcional (com ela, a pronúncia conta), o exame abre com 80% das aulas do A2.1 feitas, e pode repetir uma semana depois.)

### 3. O que é avaliado (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: cinco ícones]

| Competência | No A2.1, quer dizer… |
|---|---|
| Task — a tarefa | Responde com detalhe, conta a história com princípio, meio e fim, e resolve a situação. |
| Grammar — a gramática | Escolhe o tempo certo (os cinco do A2.1), *should / have to / must*, comparativos e superlativos. |
| Vocabulary — o vocabulário | As palavras das unidades 1 a 5: trabalho, pessoas, percurso, agenda, orçamentos, saúde e segurança. |
| Fluency — a fluência | Respostas de duas ou três frases ligadas (*and, but, because, so, when, then, in the end*). |
| Pronunciation — a pronúncia | Os outros percebem-no. (Só com a gravação.) |

**PROF:** In A1, you connected sentences. In A2, you tell a whole story, and you choose the right tense.
(PT: No A1, ligava frases; no A2, conta uma história completa e escolhe o tempo certo.)

### 4. Duas estratégias novas (2 min)

[ECRÃ: já conhece — **Can you repeat that, please?** · **Let me think.** · **I don't know the word in English. It's a kind of…** · novas — **That's a good question. Well…** · **Do you mean…?**]

**PROF:** You know three strategies. Here are two new ones.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** One: you need time to think. Say: That's a good question. Well… — and start.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: "What's the most difficult part of your job?" — "That's a good question. Well… I think the reports."]
**PROF:** Two: you're not sure what the question means. Ask: Do you mean…? — and say what you understood.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: "Tell me about your background." — "Do you mean my studies?" — "Yes, your studies and your work."]
**PROF:** Do you mean my studies? It's much better than a wrong answer.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Do you mean…?" = "Quer dizer…?" Confirmar mostra que está a ouvir, e o examinador explica.)

### 5. Um exame de demonstração (3 min)

**PROF:** Now watch Lurdes again. You know her from A1. Now she's at the end of A2.1.

[ECRÃ: "Practice exam A2.1 — Lurdes"; ícone do examinador]

**EXAMINADOR:** Hello, Lurdes. Welcome to your A2.1 speaking exam. Tell me about your job. What do you usually do, and what are you working on these days?
**LURDES:** I'm an accountant at a bank. I usually check the accounts and I deal with the big clients. These days I'm preparing the end-of-year report, so I'm working late.
**EXAMINADOR:** What's your manager like?
**LURDES:** She's very organised and quite patient, but a bit serious in meetings. She's tall, and she wears glasses.
**EXAMINADOR:** Tell me about your background.
**LURDES:** Do you mean my studies?
**EXAMINADOR:** Yes, your studies and your work.
**LURDES:** I was born in Benguela, and I grew up there. I studied economics in Luanda, and I got a job at the bank in 2014.
**EXAMINADOR:** Thank you. Now tell me about a difficult day at work. What happened?
**LURDES:** That's a good question. Well… Last year, I was presenting a report to the directors when my computer stopped. First, I was very nervous. Then I took my notes and I explained the numbers without the computer. In the end, the director said: "Good presentation!"
**EXAMINADOR:** Now a situation. I'm your manager. We need a new projector for the meeting room. Here are two options. Which one do you recommend?
[ECRÃ: Projector A: 400 000 Kz, very bright, one-year warranty · Projector B: 290 000 Kz, not as bright, three-year warranty]
**LURDES:** I recommend Projector B. It isn't as bright as Projector A, but it's much cheaper, and the warranty is longer — three years.
**EXAMINADOR:** But we have a lot of meetings with clients. Isn't Projector A better?
**LURDES:** Projector A is brighter, but our meeting room is small and quite dark. For us, the price and the warranty are more important.
**EXAMINADOR:** OK, thank you, Lurdes. The exam is finished. You can press the finish button now.

**PROF:** Look at what Lurdes does well.
[ECRÃ: lista com vistos]
**PROF:** She answers in two or three sentences. She uses the right tense: I usually check, these days I'm preparing, I was presenting when my computer stopped. She uses both new strategies: "Do you mean my studies?" and "That's a good question. Well…". And in the situation, she recommends, gives reasons, and answers the examiner's objection.
(PT: Responde com frases completas, escolhe o tempo certo, usa as duas estratégias novas e, na situação, recomenda, dá razões e responde à objecção.)

### 6. Pratique cada parte (1 min)

**PROF:** Your turn. Part one: What do you usually do at work, and what are you working on these days?
[PAUSA 12 s]
**PROF:** Part two: Tell me about a difficult day at work, or at school. What happened?
[PAUSA 20 s]
[ECRÃ: I was … when … First, … Then … In the end, …]
**PROF:** Part three: I'm your colleague. I have a terrible headache, and I have a lot of work. What should I do?
[PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: You should … You don't have to … Why don't you … ?]

### 7. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** You're ready! First, practise with your tutor. Then, when you're ready, start your exam. Good luck — and congratulations on finishing A2.1!
