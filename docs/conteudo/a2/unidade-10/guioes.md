# A2.2 · Unidade 10 — The next step · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções e personagens: ver os [guiões da unidade 1 do A2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2.1 e das unidades 6 a 9. Novo: *be good at + -ing*; verbo + *-ing / to* (revisto e alargado); advérbios de modo (*carefully, quickly, clearly, politely*; irregulares *well, fast, hard*); *good / well*; a posição do advérbio depois do objecto (*She speaks English well*); perguntas e respostas de entrevista, com todos os tempos do A2.

**Não usar nesta unidade:** o 2.º condicional e *would* hipotético; a voz passiva; orações relativas (também "a time when…"); *used to*; o *past perfect*; o *present perfect continuous*; *too / enough*.

## 10A — I'm good at solving problems

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10A — I'm good at solving problems        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue falar dos seus pontos fortes e do que gosta de fazer no
  trabalho, com be good at + -ing, enjoy + -ing e want / would like + to, e dar um exemplo (I'm good at
  solving problems. In March I fixed a network switch after a power cut.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: be good at + nome / -ing (good at English, good at solving problems); enjoy / like /
  love + -ing; want / would like / hope + to; not very good at…
Palavras-alvo: strengths, weaknesses, position, apply for, team, solve, organise, explain, patient,
  example
Reciclagem: verbo + to / -ing (A2, 9C); past simple para um exemplo (A2, 2)
Pronúncia: good at /gʊd ət/ ligado; at fraco
Contexto: segunda-feira, 24 de Maio; o Mateus mostra ao Nuno o anúncio do lugar e pergunta-lhe pelos
  pontos fortes
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 10A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 10
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! This is the last unit of A2. Today: your strengths — what you're good at, what you enjoy — with an example.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o gabinete do Mateus; no ecrã do portátil, um anúncio: "Joint Support Team — Project Support Technician. English required."]

**MATEUS:** Nuno, look at this. Our new joint team with Thandi's company needs a project support technician. Do you want to apply?
**NUNO:** Me? I'd love to! But the interview is in English.
**MATEUS:** Your English is much better now. Let's practise. What are your strengths?
**NUNO:** Well, I'm good at solving problems. In March I fixed a network switch at Mussulo Energy after a power cut — on the phone with Johannesburg!
**MATEUS:** True. What else?
**NUNO:** I enjoy working with clients, and I'm good at explaining technical things in a simple way.
**MATEUS:** And your weaknesses?
**NUNO:** Hmm. I'm not very good at writing reports. But I'm doing a course on Saturdays, and I want to improve.
**MATEUS:** Perfect answer. Send your application by Friday.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Good at***

[ECRÃ: I'm **good at** solving problems. · I'm **good at** English. · I'm **not very good at** writing reports.]

**PROF:** "Good at" plus a noun — good at English — or plus a verb with "-ing" — good at solving problems.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✘ I'm good in solving problems. ✘ I'm good at solve problems. ✔ I'm good at solving problems.]
**PROF:** Not "good in". Good at. And after "at", "-ing".
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Sou bom a resolver problemas" → "I'm good at solving problems.")

**Gostos e planos**

[ECRÃ: I **enjoy working** with clients. (-ing) · I **want to improve**. · I**'d love to** apply. · I **hope to get** the job. (to)]

**PROF:** From Unit 9: enjoy plus "-ing"; want, would like and hope plus "to".
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Um ponto forte com um exemplo**

[ECRÃ: I'm good at solving problems. → **For example,** in March I fixed a network switch after a power cut.]

**PROF:** In an interview, a strength without an example is weak. Say the strength, then an example in the past simple.
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Pontos fracos**

[ECRÃ: I'm not very good at writing reports, **but** I'm doing a course, and I want to improve.]

**PROF:** For a weakness: be honest, then say what you're doing about it.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "Good at" — join the words: good‿at. I'm good at solving problems.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I'm not very good at writing reports, but I want to improve.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Answer about you.
**PROF:** What are you good at?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: I'm good at … -ing / I'm good at …]
**PROF:** Give me an example.
[PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: For example, last … I …]
**PROF:** What do you enjoy doing at work?
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** And what are you not very good at? What are you doing about it?
[PAUSA 12 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 8F):** How did Nuno fix the switch? Do you remember the steps?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: He turned the switch off, unplugged the cable, plugged it in again, and turned the switch on.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Great! Strengths with examples. Next lesson: how you do things — quickly, carefully, well.

## 10B — She speaks English well

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10B — She speaks English well        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer como uma pessoa faz as coisas com advérbios de modo
  (He works carefully. She learns quickly.), distinguir adjectivo de advérbio (She's a good speaker. She
  speaks well.) e pôr o advérbio depois do objecto (She speaks English well).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: advérbios em -ly (carefully, quickly, clearly, politely, slowly, badly); irregulares
  (well, fast, hard); good (adjectivo) / well (advérbio); posição: verbo + objecto + advérbio
Palavras-alvo: careful, carefully, quick, quickly, clear, clearly, polite, politely, hard, fast,
  reference, colleague
Reciclagem: adjectivos de personalidade (A2, 1C); comparativos (A2, 4)
Pronúncia: -ly fraco (carefully /ˈkeəfəli/); acento no adjectivo (CAREful → CAREfully)
Contexto: terça-feira, 25 de Maio; a Sílvia escreve uma referência para o Nuno e fala com o Paulo
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 10B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: not what people do, but how they do it — carefully, quickly, well.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a sala da Sílvia; a Sílvia ao computador; o Paulo entra]

**PAULO:** What are you writing?
**SÍLVIA:** A reference for Nuno. Lerato asked me for one. What can I say about him?
**PAULO:** Well, he works hard, and he learns very quickly. In October he needed help with everything; now he solves problems alone.
**SÍLVIA:** True. And he speaks to clients politely and clearly.
**PAULO:** And he's a careful worker. He checks everything carefully.
**SÍLVIA:** His English is very good now, too. He speaks English well — much better than in October.
**PAULO:** The only thing… he writes slowly.
**SÍLVIA:** Ha! Yes, but he's doing a course. I'll write that too.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Adjectivo e advérbio**

[ECRÃ: adjectivo (como é) → advérbio (como faz): careful → careful**ly** · quick → quick**ly** · clear → clear**ly** · polite → polite**ly** · slow → slow**ly** · bad → bad**ly**]

**PROF:** An adjective describes a person or a thing: a careful worker. An adverb describes how someone does something: he works carefully. Most adverbs are adjective plus "-ly".
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Os irregulares**

[ECRÃ: good → **well** · fast → **fast** · hard → **hard**]

**PROF:** Three irregulars: good becomes well. Fast stays fast. Hard stays hard.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✔ He works hard. (trabalha muito) · ✘ He works hardly.]
**PROF:** He works hard — he works a lot. "Hardly" is a different word: don't use it here.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Good* ou *well*?**

[ECRÃ: She's a **good** speaker. (adjectivo, com um nome) · She speaks **well**. (advérbio, com um verbo) · ✘ She speaks good.]

**PROF:** Good — with a noun: a good speaker. Well — with a verb: she speaks well.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Onde fica o advérbio**

[ECRÃ: ✔ He speaks **English well**. ✘ He speaks well English. · ✔ He checks **everything carefully**.]

**PROF:** The adverb goes after the object: he speaks English well — not "speaks well English".
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: Em português dizemos "fala bem inglês"; em inglês, "speaks English well".)

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** The "-ly" is short and weak, and the stress stays on the adjective: CAREful — CAREfully. QUICK — QUICKly. CLEAR — CLEARly.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: He checks everything carefully. He speaks English well.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Make the adverb.
[ECRÃ: She's a careful driver. → She drives …] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: She drives carefully.]
[ECRÃ: He's a good speaker. → He speaks …] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: He speaks well.]
[ECRÃ: They're fast workers. → They work …] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: They work fast.]

**PROF:** Now describe a colleague or a friend: how does he or she work? Three sentences, with adverbs.
[PAUSA 15 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — She works hard. She speaks to clients politely. She explains things clearly.]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 4A):** Who speaks English better: you now, or you one year ago?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I speak English much better now!]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! Now practise with your tutor: your strengths, with examples. Then: the big interview questions — past, present and future.

## 10C — What have you done? What will you do?

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10C — What have you done? What will you do?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue responder às perguntas mais comuns de uma entrevista,
  escolhendo o tempo certo: o que faz agora (present simple / continuous), o que já fez (present
  perfect), quando e como (past simple), e o que quer ou vai fazer (want to, going to, will).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: revisão activa dos tempos do A2: present simple / continuous, past simple / continuous,
  present perfect (experience, for / since, already / yet), going to, will (previsões e promessas)
Palavras-alvo: experience, career, in five years, goal, responsible for, since, mock interview
Reciclagem: todas as unidades do A2
Pronúncia: pausas curtas entre ideias (… since October. / Before that, …)
Contexto: quarta-feira, 26 de Maio; o Paulo faz ao Nuno uma entrevista de treino
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 10C; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! In an interview, every question has its tense. Today: we practise the big questions — and the right tense for each one.

### 2. Situação (2 min)

[ECRÃ: a sala de reuniões, quarta-feira; o Paulo faz de entrevistador, com uma lista de perguntas; o Nuno à frente dele]

**PAULO:** OK, mock interview. First question: what do you do at the moment?
**NUNO:** I'm a junior IT technician at Kalandula Tech. I support the staff, and these days I'm working on the network for Mussulo Energy's new building.
**PAULO:** How long have you worked there?
**NUNO:** I've worked at Kalandula Tech since October — about seven months. Before that, I did an internship there for three months.
**PAULO:** What have you learned in that time?
**NUNO:** I've learned a lot. I've installed networks, I've worked with clients from three countries, and I've already given a project update in English.
**PAULO:** Tell me about a difficult situation. What happened?
**NUNO:** In March, I was working at Mussulo Energy when the power went off. When it came back, a network switch didn't work. I called the help desk in Johannesburg, and in the end we fixed it in ten minutes.
**PAULO:** Where do you see yourself in five years?
**NUNO:** I want to be a network engineer. I'm going to finish my English course this year, and I think I'll study for a network certificate next year.
**PAULO:** Great. You're ready!

### 3. Língua (3 min 30 s)

**Cada pergunta, o seu tempo**

[ECRÃ: seis perguntas e o tempo de cada uma]

| Pergunta | Tempo | Exemplo do Nuno |
|---|---|---|
| What do you do at the moment? | present simple / continuous | I support the staff. These days I'm working on… |
| How long have you worked there? | present perfect + for / since | I've worked there since October. |
| What have you learned? | present perfect (sem data) | I've installed networks. I've already given an update in English. |
| Tell me about a difficult situation. | past simple / continuous | I was working at Mussulo Energy when the power went off. |
| Where do you see yourself in five years? | want to / going to / will | I want to be… I'm going to finish… I think I'll study… |
| Before that? | past simple + data | Before that, I did an internship. |

**PROF:** Listen to the question — it often tells you the tense. "How long have you…?" — answer with "I've…". "Tell me about a situation" — past simple and past continuous. "In five years" — the future.
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Ligar as ideias**

[ECRÃ: **Before that,** … · **Since then,** … · **At the moment,** … · **In the future,** …]

**PROF:** Use these words to move between times: Before that, I did an internship. At the moment, I'm working on a new network.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** When you change time, make a short pause: I've worked there since October. — Before that, I did an internship.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I want to be a network engineer. I think I'll study for a certificate next year.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** I'm the interviewer. Answer with the right tense.
**PROF:** What do you do at the moment?
[PAUSA 8 s]
**PROF:** How long have you worked or studied there?
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** What have you learned in your job or your studies?
[PAUSA 10 s]
**PROF:** Where do you see yourself in five years?
[PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I want to … I'm going to … I think I'll …]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 2G):** And where did you grow up?
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! All your A2 tenses in one interview. Next lesson: the words for skills and qualities.

## 10D — Skills and qualities

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10D — Skills and qualities        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar as palavras de competências, qualidades e candidaturas
  (communication, teamwork, problem-solving, leadership, CV, application, position, salary, training),
  dizer com o acento certo as palavras em -tion, -ity e -ive, e evitar os falsos amigos realise e
  sensible.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: skills (communication skills); experience in / with + nome (experience in IT support,
  experience with clients); good at (10A)
Palavras-alvo: skill, communication, teamwork, problem-solving, leadership, responsibility,
  experience, CV, application, apply for, position, salary, training, interview, candidate, creative,
  reliable, flexible
Reciclagem: adjectivos de personalidade (A2, 1C); good at (10A)
Pronúncia: acento em -tion (communiCAtion, appliCAtion), -ity (responsiBIlity, aBIlity) e -ive
  (creAtive, posItive)
Falsos amigos: realise (≠ realizar); sensible (≠ sensível)
Contexto: o anúncio do lugar e a candidatura do Nuno
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 10D; pergunta 7 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Job ads and CVs have their own words: skills, experience, responsibility. Today: these words — and their stress.

### 2. O anúncio (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o anúncio completo: "Joint Support Team — Project Support Technician · Luanda · Skills: communication, teamwork, problem-solving · Experience: one year in IT support · Languages: Portuguese and English · Training: yes, in Johannesburg · Salary: we'll talk about it at the interview · Send your CV and application by Friday 28 May."]

**PROF:** This is the job ad. The position is project support technician.
**PROF:** The skills: communication — talking and writing clearly; teamwork — working well with other people; problem-solving — finding solutions.
**PROF:** The experience: one year in IT support. Languages: Portuguese and English.
**PROF:** There's training in Johannesburg, and they'll talk about the salary at the interview.
**PROF:** To apply for the position, send your CV and an application by Friday.
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: **skill** (competência) · **communication** · **teamwork** (trabalho de equipa) · **problem-solving** · **leadership** (liderança) · **responsibility** · **experience** · **CV** · **application** (candidatura) · **position** (lugar, cargo) · **salary** (salário) · **training** (formação) · **candidate** · **creative · reliable · flexible**]

**PROF:** A position is a job in a company. An application is your letter or form to ask for the job. A candidate is a person in an interview for a job.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 3. Pronúncia: o acento dos sufixos (2 min)

**PROF:** These endings move the stress. Listen.
[ECRÃ: -tion: communi**CA**tion · appli**CA**tion · presen**TA**tion | -ity: responsi**BI**lity · a**BI**lity | -ive: cre**A**tive · **POS**itive]
**PROF:** With "-tion", the stress is just before it: communiCAtion. appliCAtion. presenTAtion.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** With "-ity", also just before it: responsiBIlity. aBIlity.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I have good communication skills and a lot of responsibility.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 4. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: ✘ **realise** ≠ realizar · realizar = **do, carry out, make** · realise = perceber, dar-se conta]
**PROF:** "Realise" means "understand suddenly": I realised I was late. "Realizar" in Portuguese is "do" or "carry out": we carried out the project.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Realizámos o projecto em seis meses" → "We did the project in six months.")
[ECRÃ: ✘ **sensible** ≠ sensível · sensível = **sensitive** · sensible = sensato, com bom senso]
**PROF:** "Sensible" means you make good decisions — "sensato". "Sensível" is "sensitive".
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Prática e fecho (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Answer: What are your three best skills?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — My best skills are communication, teamwork and problem-solving.]
**PROF:** Do you have experience with clients?
[PAUSA 6 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — 10A):** Which of these skills are you not very good at?
[PAUSA 6 s]

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: three real people answer "Why do you want this job?"

## 10E — Real English: Why do you want this job?

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10E — Why do you want this job?        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue identificar, em três respostas de entrevista a velocidade

Vozes: Esperança (angolana), Kevin (escocês), Patrick (congolês)

```

**PROF:** You know these three people from A1. Now they're in job interviews. Each one answers: "Why do you want this job?" Listen for their experience, their qualities, and their goal.

### 2. Primeira escuta: a ideia geral (3 min)

**PROF:** First: who wants a job for family reasons?

**ESPERANÇA (entrevista para formadora de inglês numa empresa):** I've taught English at a secondary school in Malanje for twelve years, and I love teaching. But I'd like to work with adults now. I've already given some evening classes to bank staff, and I enjoyed it very much. I'm patient, and I'm good at explaining grammar in a simple way. My goal is to help professionals speak English with confidence.

**KEVIN (entrevista para engenheiro em terra, em Luanda):** I've worked on an oil platform near Soyo for six years. I work four weeks there and then I go home to Scotland for four weeks. It's a good job, but last year my wife and I had a baby, and I want to be at home every night. I'm a reliable engineer, and I work well in a team. I want to stay in Angola — my wife is Angolan, and we're going to live in Luanda.

**PATRICK (entrevista para responsável de logística):** I speak French, Lingala and English, and I'm learning Portuguese. I've worked in logistics in Kinshasa and in Luanda since 2015. Your company works a lot with clients in the Congo, so I think my languages will be very useful. I'm good at organising things, and I always deliver on time. In the future, I'd like to manage a team.

[ECRÃ: Esperança — English trainer · Kevin — engineer · Patrick — logistics]

1. How long has Esperança taught in Malanje? (for twelve years ✓ / since twelve years / for two years)
2. Who has she already taught in the evenings? (bank staff ✓ / children / engineers)
3. Why does Kevin want a new job? (He wants to be at home every night. ✓ / The money is bad. / He doesn't like engineering.)
4. Where is Kevin going to live? (in Luanda ✓ / in Scotland / in Soyo)
5. Which languages does Patrick speak? (French, Lingala and English ✓ / French, Portuguese and Spanish / only English)
6. What's Patrick's goal for the future? (to manage a team ✓ / to move to the Congo / to learn Lingala)

*(Cada resposta repete-se; depois aparecem as perguntas, com 8 s para responder.)*

### 4. Terceira escuta: como disseram (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Listen and repeat.
**PROF:** I've already given some evening classes. [PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** I'm good at explaining grammar in a simple way. [PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** I work well in a team. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** I always deliver on time. [PAUSA 3 s]

**PROF:** Did you notice? All three answers have the same parts: experience (with the present perfect), a quality (I'm good at…, I'm reliable), and a goal (I'd like to…, I want to…).
(PT: As três respostas têm experiência, uma qualidade e um objectivo.)

### 5. A sua vez (1 min)

**PROF:** Now you. Imagine an interview for your dream job. Why do you want this job? Use the three parts.
[PAUSA 25 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I've worked as … for / since … I'm good at … , and I'm … My goal is to …]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: Nuno's real interview.

## 10F — Work & Life: Job interview

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10F — Job interview        Tipo: Work & Life
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue fazer uma entrevista simples para um lugar: apresentar-se,

Gramática-alvo: toda a do A2; Could you tell me more about…? (pedidos educados da unidade 9); When will I

Reciclagem: todo o A2
Pronúncia: falar com confiança: frases completas, pausas curtas, voz clara

  Baptista escreveu em português
Contexto: quinta-feira, 3 de Junho; a entrevista do Nuno com a Lerato e o Mateus
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 10F; conversa livre "Job interview"; pergunta 9 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! This is the big day: Nuno's job interview, in English. Watch how he uses everything from A2 — and how he asks questions at the end.

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

### 3. Como se faz uma entrevista (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: cinco momentos]

| Momento | O que fazer | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Tell me about yourself | origem, estudos, experiência, agora (A2, 2G) | I was born in… I've worked at… since… These days I'm… |
| 2. Why this position? | o que gosta + o que quer | I enjoy… I'd like to… |
| 3. Strengths | ponto forte + exemplo | I'm good at… For example, in March I… |
| 4. Weakness | honestidade + o que está a fazer | My writing is slower… But I've started a course. |
| 5. Questions | duas perguntas educadas | Could you tell me more about…? When will I know…? |

**PROF:** Five moments. Prepare an example for each strength, be honest about a weakness, and always ask two questions at the end.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**PROF:** And the mediation: Nuno doesn't read the letter in Portuguese. He gives the important points in English, with "He says…".

**PROF:** In an interview, speak in complete sentences, with short pauses — not very fast. Calm and clear.
