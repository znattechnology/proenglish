# A2.1 · Unidade 5 — Health and safety · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções e personagens: ver os [guiões da unidade 1 do A2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1 e das unidades 1 a 4 do A2. Do A1 (aula 8F, na farmácia) o aluno já sabe dizer o que sente (*I have a headache. My back hurts. I don't feel well.*) e perceber instruções no imperativo (*Take one tablet twice a day. Don't wait.*). Novo: *should / shouldn't* para conselhos (*You should see a doctor.*); *have to / don't have to / had to* para obrigação e o que não é necessário; *must / mustn't* para regras e avisos; *Why don't you…?* (A2, 3C) também para conselho; palavras de saúde, estilo de vida e segurança no trabalho.

**Não usar nesta unidade:** o *present perfect*; *will* e *going to* para previsões; *could*; o condicional (*if*, *would* + verbo, excepto *would like*); *too / enough*; *used to*.

**Personagens:** **Nuno**, **Mateus**, **Sílvia**, **Paulo**, **Thandi**, **João**, **Ana**. Na 5C: o **Sr. Cardoso**, chefe de segurança da Mussulo Energy (voz nova). Na 5E: **Rita**, **Tunde**, **Fátima** e **Emma**. Na 5F: o **Sr. Domingos**, encarregado da obra na Mussulo Energy (voz nova, fala português).

**A história continua:** semana de 30 de Novembro a 4 de Dezembro de 2026, a última antes da instalação na Mussulo Energy (7 de Dezembro). Toda a gente está cansada; o Nuno fica doente na quarta-feira (5F).

## 5A — You should rest

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5A — You should rest        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer como se sente e dar e pedir conselhos com should /
  shouldn't e Why don't you…? (You should go to bed early. You shouldn't drink coffee at night. What
  should I do?).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: should / shouldn't + verbo simples; What should I do?; Should I…?; Why don't you…?
  (revisto da 3C, agora como conselho); I think you should…
Palavras-alvo: tired, exhausted, stressed, sleep, rest, take a break, go for a walk, drink water,
  coffee, at night, early
Reciclagem: I have a headache / I don't feel well (A1, 8F); past continuous (A2, 2C)
Pronúncia: should /ʃʊd/ (o l não se diz); shouldn't /ˈʃʊdnt/
Contexto: segunda-feira, dia 30; o Paulo chega ao escritório exausto e a Sílvia dá-lhe conselhos
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 5A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 5
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! This week, everybody at Kalandula Tech is very tired. Today: how to say how you feel, and how to give advice — "You should rest!"

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a copa, segunda-feira, 8h00; o Paulo com ar exausto e um café grande]

**SÍLVIA:** Paulo, are you OK? You look exhausted.
**PAULO:** I am. I only slept four hours. I was working on the Mussulo Energy plans until two a.m.
**SÍLVIA:** Two a.m.? You shouldn't work so late. And you shouldn't drink so much coffee.
**PAULO:** I know, I know. But I have a headache, and I'm very stressed. What should I do?
**SÍLVIA:** I think you should take a break now. Why don't you go for a walk at lunchtime?
**PAULO:** A walk? In this heat?
**SÍLVIA:** OK, then drink a lot of water. And tonight you should go to bed early. No computer!
**PAULO:** Should I talk to Mateus about my hours?
**SÍLVIA:** Yes, you should. He's a good manager. He understands.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Should* para conselhos**

[ECRÃ: You **should** take a break. · You **shouldn't** work so late. · **What should** I do? · **Should** I talk to Mateus? — Yes, you **should**. / No, you **shouldn't**.]

**PROF:** "Should" gives advice: it's a good idea. "Shouldn't" — it's a bad idea. After "should", the simple verb.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✔ You should rest. ✘ You should to rest. ✘ You should resting.]
**PROF:** You should rest — not "should to rest".
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Devias descansar" → "You should rest." "Não devias trabalhar até tão tarde" → "You shouldn't work so late.")

**Mais suave: *I think you should…***

[ECRÃ: **I think** you should take a break. · **Why don't you** go for a walk?]

**PROF:** "I think you should…" is softer and more polite. And from Unit 3: "Why don't you…?" — also good for advice.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Pedir um conselho**

[ECRÃ: **What should I do?** · **Should I** see a doctor?]

**PROF:** To ask for advice: What should I do? Should I see a doctor?
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Should — the "l" is silent. Should. Shouldn't.
[ECRÃ: shou**l**d /ʃʊd/ — o l não se diz · shouldn't /ˈʃʊdnt/]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: You should rest. You shouldn't work so late.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Give me advice. Use "should" or "shouldn't".
**PROF:** I can't sleep at night.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: You shouldn't drink coffee at night. / You should go to bed at the same time every day.]
**PROF:** I have a terrible headache.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: You should take a break and drink some water.]
**PROF:** I'm very stressed at work.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Why don't you talk to your manager? / I think you should go for a walk after work.]

**PROF:** Now ask me for advice about a real problem. Start with "What should I do?" or "Should I…?"
[PAUSA 8 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 2C):** Paulo was working at two a.m. What were you doing at two a.m. last night?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I was sleeping!]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Good advice! Next lesson: things you have to do — and things you don't have to do.

## 5B — I have to finish this today

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5B — I have to finish this today        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer o que é obrigatório e o que não é necessário com
  have to / don't have to, no presente e no passado (had to), e perguntar (Do you have to work on
  Saturday?).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: have to / has to + verbo; don't / doesn't have to (não é necessário); Do you have to…?;
  had to / didn't have to
Palavras-alvo: finish, deadline, check, sign, report, come in, on Saturday, wear a tie, dress code
Reciclagem: present continuous para combinações (A2, 3A); past simple (A2, 2)
Pronúncia: have to /ˈhæftə/ e has to /ˈhæstə/ — o v soa como f
Contexto: segunda-feira; a Thandi pergunta ao Nuno o que ele tem de fazer esta semana
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 5B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! At work, some things are necessary, and some are not. Today: "have to" and "don't have to".

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a sala do projecto, segunda-feira à tarde; a Thandi e o Nuno com o plano da instalação]

**THANDI:** Nuno, you look busy. What do you have to do this week?
**NUNO:** A lot! I have to check all the cables on the third floor, and I have to finish the report by Thursday. That's the deadline.
**THANDI:** Do you have to work on Saturday too?
**NUNO:** No, I don't have to — Mateus says it isn't necessary. But last Saturday I had to come in. We had a problem with the server.
**THANDI:** Oh no. And on Monday the seventh? Do we have to be at Mussulo Energy early?
**NUNO:** Yes, we have to be there at seven. Rita has to sign the papers before we start.
**THANDI:** Seven! OK. And do I have to wear a tie? There's a big meeting.
**NUNO:** Ha! No, you don't have to. There's no dress code for engineers.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Have to*: é obrigatório, é necessário**

[ECRÃ: I **have to** check the cables. · Rita **has to** sign the papers. · We **have to** be there at seven.]

**PROF:** "Have to" — it's necessary, there's no choice. With he, she, it: "has to". After it, the simple verb.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Tenho de acabar o relatório" → "I have to finish the report.")

***Don't have to*: não é necessário**

[ECRÃ: I **don't have to** work on Saturday. (não é preciso — mas posso) · You **don't have to** wear a tie.]

**PROF:** "Don't have to" means it isn't necessary. You can, but you don't need to.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Não tenho de trabalhar ao sábado" = "não é preciso". Não quer dizer "é proibido".)

**Perguntar**

[ECRÃ: **Do you have to** work on Saturday? — No, I **don't**. · **Does** she **have to** sign? — Yes, she **does**.]

**PROF:** Questions with "do" and "does": Do you have to…? Does she have to…?
[PAUSA 3 s]

**No passado: *had to***

[ECRÃ: Last Saturday I **had to** come in. · I **didn't have to** work on Sunday.]

**PROF:** In the past: had to, didn't have to.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Should* ou *have to*?**

[ECRÃ: You **should** go to bed early. (conselho: é boa ideia) · You **have to** finish the report by Thursday. (obrigação: não há escolha)]

**PROF:** Should — advice, a good idea. Have to — necessary, no choice.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In "have to", the "v" sounds like "f": haf-ta. I have to go. And "has to" sounds like "has-ta".
[ECRÃ: have to /ˈhæftə/ · has to /ˈhæstə/]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I have to finish the report. She has to sign the papers.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Answer about your job or your studies.
**PROF:** What do you have to do every day at work?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I have to answer a lot of emails. I have to be at work at eight.]
**PROF:** What don't you have to do?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I don't have to wear a suit. I don't have to work on Sundays.]
**PROF:** Did you have to work late last week?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, I had to work late on Tuesday. / No, I didn't have to.]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 3A):** And this week? What are you doing on Friday?
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! Next lesson: rules and signs — "must" and "mustn't". And the difference between "mustn't" and "don't have to".

## 5C — You mustn't smoke here

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5C — You mustn't smoke here        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue explicar e perceber regras e avisos com must / mustn't e o
  imperativo (You must wear your badge. You mustn't eat in the server room.), e distinguir mustn't
  (é proibido) de don't have to (não é necessário).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: must / mustn't + verbo simples (regras, avisos, sinais); imperativo (revisto: Wear…,
  Don't…); mustn't ≠ don't have to
Palavras-alvo: rule, sign, badge, visitor, server room, fire exit, emergency, smoke, lift, stairs,
  sign in, allowed
Reciclagem: have to (5B); imperativo (A1, 7 e 8)
Pronúncia: mustn't /ˈmʌsnt/ — o primeiro t não se diz
Contexto: quinta-feira, dia 3; o Sr. Cardoso, chefe de segurança da Mussulo Energy, explica as regras
  à equipa da Kalandula Tech
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 5C; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Every building has rules. Today: how to explain rules and signs in English — "must" and "mustn't".

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a recepção da Mussulo Energy, quinta-feira; o Sr. Cardoso, de uniforme, com a equipa: o Mateus, o Nuno, o Paulo e a Thandi]

**SR. CARDOSO:** Good morning, everyone. On Monday you're starting work in our building, so here are the safety rules. First, you must wear your badge at all times.
**THANDI:** Do we have to sign in every day?
**SR. CARDOSO:** Yes. You must sign in at reception every morning. You don't have to sign out — the system does it.
**NUNO:** And in the server room?
**SR. CARDOSO:** In the server room, you mustn't eat or drink. Not even water. And you mustn't open the door to people without a badge.
**PAULO:** Can we smoke outside?
**SR. CARDOSO:** Only in the smoking area, behind the car park. You mustn't smoke near the building.
**MATEUS:** And in an emergency?
**SR. CARDOSO:** Use the stairs and the fire exits. Don't use the lift. The meeting point is in the car park.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Must* e *mustn't***

[ECRÃ: You **must** wear your badge. · You **must** sign in. · You **mustn't** eat in the server room. · You **mustn't** smoke near the building.]

**PROF:** "Must" — a rule: you have no choice. "Mustn't" — it's prohibited, not allowed. After them, the simple verb.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Mustn't" = "é proibido". "You mustn't smoke here" = "É proibido fumar aqui.")

**Nos sinais**

[ECRÃ: três sinais — "Visitors must sign in at reception" · "No food or drink" · "In case of fire, do not use the lift"]

**PROF:** Signs often use "must" or the imperative: Do not use the lift. No food or drink.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Mustn't* ≠ *don't have to***

[ECRÃ: You **mustn't** smoke here. (é proibido) · You **don't have to** sign out. (não é necessário — o sistema faz isso)]

**PROF:** Careful! These two are very different. Mustn't: you can't, it's prohibited. Don't have to: it isn't necessary — you can do it, but you don't need to.
[PAUSA 4 s]
(PT: Em português, "não tens de" e "não podes" são muito diferentes, e em inglês também: "don't have to" ≠ "mustn't".)

***Must* ou *have to*?**

[ECRÃ: **must** — regras escritas, sinais, avisos fortes · **have to** — obrigações do dia-a-dia, na fala]

**PROF:** In speaking, people often use "have to" for normal obligations. "Must" is common in rules and signs.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Mustn't — the first "t" is silent. Must-n't: "musn't".
[ECRÃ: mus**t**n't /ˈmʌsnt/ — o primeiro t não se diz]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: You mustn't smoke here. You mustn't eat in the server room.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Mustn't or don't have to?
[ECRÃ: You … use your phone in the server room. (proibido)] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: You mustn't use your phone in the server room.]
[ECRÃ: You … wear a tie. (não é necessário)] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: You don't have to wear a tie.]
[ECRÃ: Visitors … park in the director's place. (proibido)] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: Visitors mustn't park in the director's place.]

**PROF:** Now tell me three rules in your workplace: two with "must" or "mustn't", one with "don't have to".
[PAUSA 15 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — 5B):** What did you have to do last week?
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Now practise with your tutor: you don't feel well, and you need advice. Then: words for body and mind.

## 5D — Body and mind

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5D — Body and mind        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue falar de saúde, sono, stress e exercício (I'm exhausted. I
  have a check-up next week. I'm on sick leave.), dizer palavras com letras que não se pronunciam, e
  evitar os falsos amigos constipated e injury.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 4
Gramática-alvo: be + adjectivo (I'm stressed / exhausted); have + nome (have a check-up, have an
  injury); be on sick leave; get + adjectivo (get tired, get sick)
Palavras-alvo: healthy, unhealthy, stressed, exhausted, sleep, exercise, diet, check-up, blood
  pressure, sick leave, injury, back pain, doctor's note, get tired / stressed / sick
Reciclagem: sintomas e farmácia (A1, 8F); should (5A)
Pronúncia: letras mudas: should, would, listen, half, talk, walk, mustn't, Wednesday
Falsos amigos: constipated (≠ constipado); injury (≠ injúria)
Contexto: um folheto de saúde para trabalhadores, "Healthy at work"
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 5D; pergunta 7 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: words for health, sleep and stress — and some English letters you don't say.

### 2. Um folheto de saúde (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: um folheto "Healthy at work", com fotografias]

**PROF:** This is a health leaflet for workers. Listen.
**PROF:** Sleep: you should sleep seven or eight hours. When you don't sleep, you get tired and stressed.
**PROF:** Exercise: you should walk for thirty minutes every day. Take the stairs, not the lift.
**PROF:** Diet: you should eat fruit and vegetables, and drink a lot of water. A healthy diet is good for your blood pressure.
**PROF:** Check-ups: you should have a check-up with your doctor every year.
**PROF:** And when you're sick, you shouldn't come to work. Stay at home, see a doctor, and send your manager a doctor's note. Then you're on sick leave.
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: **healthy / unhealthy** · **stressed** · **exhausted** (muito cansado) · **exercise** · **diet** (alimentação) · **check-up** · **blood pressure** (tensão arterial) · **sick leave** (baixa médica) · **doctor's note** (atestado) · **back pain** · **an injury** (um ferimento)]

**PROF:** Exhausted — very, very tired. Sick leave — when you're sick and you don't go to work. A doctor's note — the paper from the doctor.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 3. *Get* + adjectivo (1 min)

[ECRÃ: I **get tired** in the afternoon. · She **gets stressed** before meetings. · Don't **get sick**!]

**PROF:** "Get" plus an adjective is a change: you start to be tired, stressed, sick.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia: letras mudas (2 min)

**PROF:** In some English words, you don't say every letter. Listen.
[ECRÃ: shou**l**d · wou**l**d · lis**t**en · ha**l**f · ta**l**k · wa**l**k · mus**t**n't · We**d**nesday]
**PROF:** should.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** would — as in "would like".
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** listen.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** half.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** talk. walk.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Wednesday.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: You should walk for half an hour on Wednesday.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: ✘ **constipated** ≠ constipado · constipado = **I have a cold** · constipated = com prisão de ventre]
**PROF:** Careful! "Constipated" is not "constipado". In English, it's a problem with your stomach — you can't go to the toilet. When you have a cold, say "I have a cold".
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Estou constipado" → "I have a cold." Nunca "I'm constipated", a não ser que seja mesmo isso!)

[ECRÃ: ✘ **injury** ≠ injúria · injury = ferimento, lesão · injúria = **insult**]
**PROF:** An "injury" is when you hurt your body — at work, in sport. "Injúria" in Portuguese is an insult.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 6. Prática e fecho (1 min)

**PROF:** Answer: How many hours do you usually sleep?
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Is your diet healthy? Why, or why not?
[PAUSA 6 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A1, 8F):** You have a cold. What do you say at the pharmacy?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I have a cold. Can I have something for it, please?]

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: real people talk about how they stay healthy.

## 5E — Real English: How do you stay healthy?

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5E — How do you stay healthy?        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue identificar, em quatro conversas a velocidade natural, o
  problema de cada pessoa e o conselho que recebe ou que dá.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 2
Língua: A1 e unidades 1–5 do A2, sem estruturas novas
Vozes: Rita (portuguesa), Tunde (nigeriano), Fátima (angolana), Emma (britânica)
Velocidade: natural
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 5E; pergunta 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Apresentação (30 s)

**PROF:** Four people talk about health. Each person has a problem — and there's some advice. Listen three times.

### 2. Primeira escuta: a ideia geral (3 min)

**PROF:** First: who has a problem with sleep?

**RITA:** These days I don't sleep well. I get home at eight, and then I answer emails until midnight. My doctor says I shouldn't use my phone in bed. So now I leave it in the kitchen. It's hard, but I sleep better.

**TUNDE:** I drive a lot for work — three or four hours a day — and I have back pain. Last month I had to stop work for a week. The doctor says I have to stop the car every hour and walk for five minutes. I do it now, and my back is much better.

**FÁTIMA:** At the bakery, I have to start at four in the morning, so I'm always tired in the afternoon. My sister says I should sleep after lunch, but I can't — I have to look after the shop. So I go to bed at eight. My friends think I'm crazy!

**EMMA:** Luanda is very hot for me. In my first month I got sick, because I didn't drink much… — sorry — I didn't drink any water at work! Now I always carry a big bottle. My advice for visitors? You must drink water, all day!

[ECRÃ: Rita — sleep · Tunde — back · Fátima — tired · Emma — heat]

### 3. Segunda escuta: os pormenores (3 min)

1. What does Rita's doctor say? (She shouldn't use her phone in bed. ✓ / She has to work less. / She should drink less coffee.)
2. Where does Rita leave her phone now? (in the kitchen ✓ / in her bag / at the office)
3. What does Tunde have to do? (stop the car every hour and walk ✓ / stop driving / go to the doctor every week)
4. Why can't Fátima sleep after lunch? (She has to look after the shop. ✓ / Her sister is there. / It's very hot.)
5. Why did Emma get sick? (She didn't drink any water at work. ✓ / She ate bad food. / She worked a lot.)
6. What's Emma's advice for visitors? (Drink water all day. ✓ / Don't go out at lunchtime. / Sleep after lunch.)

*(Cada conversa repete-se; depois aparecem as perguntas, com 8 s para responder.)*

### 4. Terceira escuta: como disseram (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Listen and repeat.
**PROF:** My doctor says I shouldn't use my phone in bed. [PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** I had to stop work for a week. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** My friends think I'm crazy! [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** You must drink water, all day! [PAUSA 3 s]

**PROF:** Did you notice? Emma corrects herself: "I didn't drink much… — sorry — I didn't drink any water at work!" People do this all the time. It's normal!
(PT: Corrigir-se a meio da frase é normal, também em inglês.)

### 5. A sua vez (1 min)

**PROF:** Now you. What do you do to stay healthy? And what's your advice for a colleague who works a lot?
[PAUSA 20 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I … every day. I don't … My advice? You should … , and you shouldn't …]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: you're sick, and you have to call your manager.

## 5F — Work & Life: I can't come in today

