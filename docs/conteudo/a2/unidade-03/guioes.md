# A2.1 · Unidade 3 — Plans and arrangements · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções e personagens: ver os [guiões da unidade 1 do A2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1 e das unidades 1 e 2 do A2. Do A1 (aula 5G) o aluno já sabe marcar uma reunião (*Can we meet on…? How about…? I'm busy on… I'm free on… See you on…*). Novo: o *present continuous* para o que está combinado (*I'm meeting the client on Thursday*); *going to* (revisto) para intenções; *will / won't* para ofertas, decisões no momento e promessas (*I'll call him now. I won't forget.*); *Shall I…?*; convites (*Would you like to…? I'd love to. I'm afraid I can't.*); sugestões (*Let's…, How about + -ing…?, Why don't we…?*); mudar, adiar e cancelar (*move, postpone, cancel, confirm*).

**Não usar nesta unidade:** o *present perfect*; *will* para previsões (*I think it'll rain* — unidade 8); *should / have to / must*; o condicional (*if*); *could* em pedidos (unidade 9); *used to*; o *past perfect*.

**Personagens:** as da [unidade 1](../unidade-01/guioes.md) e da [unidade 2](../unidade-02/guioes.md): **Nuno**, **Mateus**, **Sílvia** (adora dançar kizomba, A1), **Paulo**, **Thandi**, **Rita** e **João** (Mussulo Energy), **Ana** (recepção).

**Calendário desta unidade:** Novembro de 2026. Segunda-feira, dia 16; quinta-feira, dia 19; sexta-feira, dia 20; sábado, dia 21; segunda-feira, dia 23.

## 3A — I'm meeting the client on Thursday

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 3        Aula: 3A — I'm meeting the client on Thursday        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer e perguntar o que está combinado, com o present
  continuous e dia e hora (I'm meeting Rita on Thursday at ten. What are you doing on Friday?), e
  distinguir do que só tenciona fazer, com going to (I'm going to visit my parents one day.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: present continuous para combinações (com hora, lugar ou outra pessoa); going to para
  intenções; What are you doing on…? Are you doing anything on…?
Palavras-alvo: arrangement, diary, plan, on Thursday, at ten, next week, delivery, training session
Reciclagem: present continuous para agora (A2, 1A); going to (A1, 10); in / on / at (A1, 5)
Pronúncia: What are you doing /wɒtəjə ˈduːɪŋ/ em fala ligada
Contexto: segunda-feira, dia 16; o Mateus e o Nuno vêem a agenda da semana
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 3A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 3
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! In A1 you learned to make a meeting: "How about Wednesday?" Today: how to talk about your plans for the week — the things you arranged with other people.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o gabinete do Mateus, segunda-feira, dia 16; a agenda da semana no ecrã do portátil]

**MATEUS:** OK, Nuno, let's look at the week. What are you doing on Tuesday?
**NUNO:** On Tuesday morning I'm checking the cables with João. And in the afternoon I'm having a training session with Thandi.
**MATEUS:** Good. And Thursday is important: we're meeting Rita at ten, at Mussulo Energy. We're presenting the new network.
**NUNO:** Are we going in your car?
**MATEUS:** Yes, I'm driving. We're leaving the office at nine fifteen.
**NUNO:** OK. And on Wednesday the new equipment is arriving from Johannesburg, right?
**MATEUS:** That's right. Thandi is waiting for the delivery at the airport. Are you doing anything on Sunday?
**NUNO:** Nothing special. I'm going to clean my flat one day… but not on Sunday!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**O que está combinado**

[ECRÃ: a agenda do Nuno — **Tue** checking cables with João · training session with Thandi · **Wed** delivery · **Thu** 10:00 meeting with Rita]

**PROF:** When you have an arrangement — a time, a place, other people — use the present continuous. I'm meeting Rita on Thursday. We're leaving at nine fifteen. It's in your diary.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: É como "amanhã vou ter com o cliente": em inglês, para o que está marcado, usa-se o present continuous com uma palavra de futuro — "I'm meeting the client tomorrow".)

[ECRÃ: ✔ I**'m meeting** Rita **on Thursday**. (futuro: tem dia) · ✔ I**'m checking** the cables **now**. (agora)]

**PROF:** The same form for now and for the future. The time word tells you: now, or on Thursday.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Going to*: uma intenção**

[ECRÃ: I**'m going to** clean my flat one day. (decidi, mas não marquei) · I**'m meeting** Rita on Thursday at ten. (está marcado)]

**PROF:** "Going to" is your intention — you decided, but there's no time in your diary. "I'm going to clean my flat one day." The present continuous is an arrangement: "I'm meeting Rita on Thursday at ten."
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** For many plans, both are correct. But for meetings with a day and a time, people usually say: I'm meeting… I'm seeing… I'm having…
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Perguntas**

[ECRÃ: **What are you doing** on Friday? · **Are you doing anything** on Saturday? — Nothing special. / Yes, I'm visiting my parents.]

**PROF:** What are you doing on Friday? Are you doing anything on Saturday? These questions ask about your plans.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Listen: What are you doing on Friday? Fast, it sounds like "whatcha doing".
[ECRÃ: What are you doing /wɒtəjə ˈduːɪŋ/]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: What are you doing on Friday?
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Are you doing anything on Saturday?
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Look at Nuno's diary. What's he doing?
[ECRÃ: Tue — training session with Thandi] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: On Tuesday he's having a training session with Thandi.]
[ECRÃ: Thu 10:00 — meeting with Rita] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: On Thursday at ten he's meeting Rita.]

**PROF:** Now you. Tell me two things in your diary this week.
[PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — On Wednesday I'm meeting … / On Friday evening I'm having dinner with …]
**PROF:** Are you doing anything this weekend?
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** And one intention — something you're going to do one day, with no date.
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I'm going to learn to swim one day.]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 2C):** What were you doing at nine o'clock this morning?
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Great! Your arrangements, in the present continuous. Next lesson: offers and quick decisions — "I'll do it!"

## 3B — I'll do it

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 3        Aula: 3B — I'll do it        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue oferecer ajuda, decidir no momento e prometer com will /
  won't (I'll call the technician. I'll send it today — I won't forget.), oferecer com Shall I…?, e
  distinguir will (decidido agora) de going to (decidido antes).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: will / won't (ofertas, decisões no momento, promessas); Shall I…?; will (agora) vs
  going to (antes)
Palavras-alvo: printer, paper, technician, carry, boxes, help, forget, promise, offer
Reciclagem: present continuous para combinações (3A); past simple (A2, 2)
Pronúncia: I'll /aɪl/, we'll /wiːl/, won't /wəʊnt/ ≠ want /wɒnt/
Contexto: terça-feira; a impressora avaria e os portáteis novos chegam mais cedo
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 3B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Someone needs help. You decide now, at this moment: "I'll help you!" Today: "will" for offers, decisions and promises.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o escritório, terça-feira; a Sílvia em frente à impressora, com uma luz vermelha a piscar]

**SÍLVIA:** Oh no, the printer isn't working again! And I need these documents for the bank.
**NUNO:** Let me see… There's no paper. I'll get some.
**SÍLVIA:** Thanks, Nuno. And the bank closes at three!
**NUNO:** Don't worry. I'll print them for you. Shall I put them in an envelope?
**SÍLVIA:** Yes, please. You're very helpful.
[O telefone toca]
**ANA:** Nuno? The new laptops for the office are at reception — six boxes! They arrived early.
**NUNO:** Six boxes? OK, I'll come down now. Paulo, can you help me?
**PAULO:** Sure, I'll carry the big ones.
**NUNO:** Sílvia, your documents are in the envelope. I'll take them to the bank after lunch.
**SÍLVIA:** Are you sure? You're very busy.
**NUNO:** It's on my way. I promise. I won't forget!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Will* para ofertas e decisões**

[ECRÃ: There's no paper. — I**'ll get** some. · The boxes are here. — I**'ll come** down now. · I**'ll carry** the big ones.]

**PROF:** You decide at this moment, when you speak: I'll get some paper. I'll come down now. "I'll" is "I will". After "will", the simple verb.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: Em português dizemos "eu trago papel!" ou "eu vou buscar!". Em inglês, para uma oferta ou uma decisão no momento: "I'll get some.")

[ECRÃ: ✔ I'll help you. ✘ I'll to help you. ✘ I'll helping you.]

**Promessas**

[ECRÃ: I**'ll take** them to the bank after lunch. I promise. I **won't** forget!]

**PROF:** Will not is "won't". I won't forget. I won't be late.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Shall I…?***

[ECRÃ: **Shall I** put them in an envelope? — Yes, please. / No, thanks, it's OK.]

**PROF:** "Shall I…?" is an offer as a question: Shall I help you? Shall I open the window?
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Will* ou *going to*?**

[ECRÃ: "Nuno, there's no paper!" — "I**'ll get** some." (decide agora) · "Why are you taking your car?" — "I**'m going to get** paper for the office." (decidiu antes)]

**PROF:** "Will": you decide now, when you speak. "Going to": you decided before.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** I'll. We'll. She'll. Short — one sound.
[ECRÃ: I'll /aɪl/ · we'll /wiːl/]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Careful: won't and want. I won't forget. I want a coffee. Won't — like "don't". Want — like "on".
[ECRÃ: won't /wəʊnt/ ≠ want /wɒnt/]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I won't forget. I want to help.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Offer to help. Use "I'll".
**PROF:** I'm cold.
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: I'll close the window.]
**PROF:** I don't understand this email. It's in Portuguese.
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: I'll help you. / I'll explain it.]
**PROF:** The phone's ringing!
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: I'll answer it.]

**PROF:** Now offer with "Shall I…?". Your manager is carrying a lot of boxes.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Shall I help you? / Shall I carry some?]

**PROF:** Make a promise to your manager about a report. Use "won't".
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: I'll send it today. I won't forget.]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 2B):** Who called Nuno about the boxes?
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: Ana called him.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very helpful! Next lesson: invitations — how to say yes, and how to say no politely.

## 3C — Would you like to come?

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 3        Aula: 3C — Would you like to come?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue convidar (Would you like to…? Do you want to…?), aceitar
  (I'd love to.) e recusar com cortesia e uma razão (I'm afraid I can't. I'm visiting my parents.),
  e fazer sugestões (Let's…, How about + -ing?, Why don't we…?).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: Would you like to + verbo?; Do you want to + verbo? (informal); I'd love to. / That
  sounds great.; I'm afraid I can't. / Sorry, I can't + razão no present continuous; Let's + verbo;
  How about + -ing / nome?; Why don't we + verbo?
Palavras-alvo: invite, invitation, kizomba night, dance class, sounds great, maybe another time,
  pick you up
Reciclagem: present continuous para combinações (3A); like + -ing (A1, 9)
Pronúncia: entoação: um convite sobe no fim; uma recusa educada desce e é mais lenta
Contexto: quinta-feira; a Sílvia convida a Thandi e o Nuno para uma noite de kizomba no sábado
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 3C; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: invitations. How to invite, how to say yes, and how to say no — without being rude.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a copa, quinta-feira à tarde; a Sílvia, a Thandi e o Nuno]

**SÍLVIA:** Thandi, would you like to come to a kizomba night on Saturday? My dance class is having a party.
**THANDI:** Kizomba? I'd love to! But I can't dance it.
**SÍLVIA:** Don't worry, I'll teach you. It's easy! Nuno, do you want to come too?
**NUNO:** I'm afraid I can't. I'm visiting my aunt in Cacuaco on Saturday. It's her birthday.
**SÍLVIA:** Oh, that's a pity. Maybe another time.
**NUNO:** Yes, definitely. Next time!
**THANDI:** So, what time does it start?
**SÍLVIA:** At eight. Why don't we meet at my house first? It's near the dance school.
**THANDI:** That sounds great. How about having dinner before the party?
**SÍLVIA:** Good idea. Let's have dinner at my house at seven. I'll pick you up at your hotel at half past six.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Convidar**

[ECRÃ: **Would you like to** come to a party? (formal, educado) · **Do you want to** come too? (informal, entre amigos)]

**PROF:** Would you like to come? — polite, good at work. Do you want to come? — friendly, with people you know well.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✔ Would you like **to come**? ✘ Would you like coming?]

**Aceitar**

[ECRÃ: **I'd love to.** · **That sounds great.** · **Yes, why not?**]

**PROF:** I'd love to. That sounds great. Say it with energy!
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Recusar com cortesia**

[ECRÃ: **I'm afraid I can't.** + razão: I'm visiting my aunt. · **Sorry, I can't.** I'm working late. · **Maybe another time.**]

**PROF:** To say no politely: I'm afraid I can't — plus a reason. The reason is often an arrangement: I'm visiting my aunt. I'm working late. Then: Maybe another time.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "I'm afraid" aqui não é "tenho medo": é "lamento, mas…". "I'm afraid I can't" = "Lamento, mas não posso".)

[ECRÃ: ✘ No. ✘ I don't want. ✔ I'm afraid I can't. I'm visiting my aunt. Maybe another time.]

**Sugerir**

[ECRÃ: **Let's** have dinner at seven. · **Why don't we** meet at my house? · **How about having** dinner first? · **How about** seven o'clock?]

**PROF:** Let's, plus the simple verb. Why don't we, plus the simple verb. How about, plus "-ing" — or a time, a place.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** An invitation goes up at the end: Would you like to come? ↗
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** A polite "no" goes down, and it's slower: I'm afraid I can't… ↘ I'm visiting my aunt.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I'd love to! — I'm afraid I can't.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** I invite you. Say yes.
**PROF:** Would you like to have lunch with me on Friday?
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: I'd love to. / That sounds great.]
**PROF:** Now say no, politely, with a reason. Would you like to come to the cinema on Saturday?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: I'm afraid I can't. I'm … on Saturday. Maybe another time.]

**PROF:** Now invite me to something. Use "Would you like to…?"
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** And make a suggestion for the weekend: "Let's…" or "How about…?"
[PAUSA 6 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A1, 9):** Do you like dancing?
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Lovely! Now practise with your tutor: your plans for the week, and an invitation. Then: the words for your diary.

## 3D — My diary

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 3        Aula: 3D — My diary        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar as palavras da agenda e das reuniões (appointment,
  available, move, postpone, cancel, confirm, remind), dizer I'll / we'll / won't com clareza, e evitar
  os falsos amigos agenda e assist.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 4
Gramática-alvo: move / postpone + to (move the meeting to Monday); cancel; confirm; remind; available
  (= free)
Palavras-alvo: diary, appointment, available, busy, move, postpone, cancel, confirm, remind,
  reminder, attend, agenda (de uma reunião), launch, workshop
Reciclagem: in / on / at (A1, 5); will (3B)
Pronúncia: o acento em post-PONE, con-FIRM, CAN-cel, a-VAIL-able, ap-POINT-ment
Falsos amigos: agenda (≠ agenda pessoal); assist (≠ assistir)
Contexto: a agenda da semana do Mateus, com mudanças
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 3D; pergunta 7 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Plans change. Today: the words to move, cancel and confirm meetings.

### 2. A agenda do Mateus (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a agenda do Mateus, com riscos e setas]

**PROF:** Look at Mateus's diary. Next Monday he has an appointment with the dentist at eight.
**PROF:** The meeting with Rita is on Thursday at ten. But the equipment is late, so they're going to move the meeting to Monday.
**PROF:** On Wednesday there's a workshop. Mateus isn't available, so he's going to cancel his place.
**PROF:** On Friday there's a product launch at a hotel on the Marginal. Mateus is going to attend.
**PROF:** And Ana sends a reminder: "Please confirm your place at the launch."
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: **an appointment** (médico, dentista, banco) · **a meeting** (trabalho) · **available** = free · **move / postpone** a meeting **to** Monday · **cancel** · **confirm** · **remind** · **a reminder** · **attend** (= go to) · **a launch** · **a workshop**]

**PROF:** An appointment — with a doctor, a dentist, the bank. A meeting — at work.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** Move a meeting to another day. Postpone: move it later. Cancel: there's no meeting.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Confirm: say "yes, it's OK". Remind: help someone remember.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 3. Pronúncia: acento e contracções (2 min)

[ECRÃ: ap**POINT**ment · a**VAIL**able · post**PONE** · con**FIRM** · **CAN**cel · re**MIND**]
**PROF:** appointment.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** available.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** postpone.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** confirm.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** cancel.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** remind.
[PAUSA 2 s]

**PROF:** Now I'll, we'll and won't. Say them clearly in phone calls.
**PROF:** I'll confirm by email.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** We'll move it to Monday.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** I won't be there, I'm afraid.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: ✘ **agenda** ≠ a sua agenda pessoal · agenda pessoal = **diary** (ou **calendar**, no telemóvel) · **agenda** = a lista de temas de uma reunião]
**PROF:** In English, your personal agenda is your diary, or the calendar on your phone. An "agenda" is the list of topics for a meeting.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Vou ver na minha agenda" → "I'll check my diary." "A agenda da reunião" → "the agenda of the meeting".)

[ECRÃ: ✘ **assist** ≠ assistir · assistir a uma reunião = **attend** a meeting · assist = ajudar]
**PROF:** "Assist" means help. To go to a meeting is "attend".
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Vou assistir à reunião" → "I'm going to attend the meeting.")

### 5. Prática e fecho (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Answer.
**PROF:** Are you available on Thursday afternoon?
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Do you have any appointments this week?
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Your manager asks you to move a meeting. Say it: "We're going to move the meeting to…"
[PAUSA 5 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 3B):** You can't find the agenda of the meeting. Your colleague says: "I'll send it to you." What do you say?
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: Thank you! / Thanks, that's very helpful.]

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: real phone calls and voice messages. Listen carefully: things change!

## 3E — Real English: Are you free on Friday?

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 3        Aula: 3E — Are you free on Friday?        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue identificar, em quatro chamadas e mensagens de voz a
  velocidade natural, o dia, a hora e o lugar combinados, e o que mudou.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 4
Língua: A1 e unidades 1–3 do A2, sem estruturas novas
Vozes: recepcionista de uma clínica (angolana), Thandi (sul-africana), João (angolano), Tunde
  (nigeriano)
Velocidade: natural
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 3E; pergunta 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Apresentação (30 s)

**PROF:** Four phone messages. In every message, something is arranged — or something changes. Listen for the day, the time, the place — and the change. You hear them three times.

### 2. Primeira escuta: a ideia geral (3 min)

**PROF:** First: which message cancels something?

[ECRÃ: um telemóvel com quatro mensagens de voz]

**MENSAGEM 1 — RECEPCIONISTA:** Good morning, this is a message for Mateus from Clínica Dentária Kianda. It's a reminder about your appointment with the dentist on Monday the twenty-third at eight a.m. Please call us to confirm. Thank you.

**MENSAGEM 2 — THANDI:** Hi Nuno, it's Thandi. Bad news about the delivery: the equipment isn't arriving today. It's arriving on Friday, at four. So I'm going to the airport on Friday at three thirty. I'll call you when I have the boxes. Bye!

**MENSAGEM 3 — JOÃO:** Hello Nuno, João here. About the cables on Tuesday: I'm going to the bank at nine, so I'm not in the office early. Let's meet at eleven, on the second floor. I'll wait for you at the lift. See you then.

**MENSAGEM 4 — TUNDE:** Hey, it's Tunde. I'm really sorry, but I can't come to the football match on Sunday. My daughter is sick, so I'm staying at home with her. Let's play next week, OK? And please say sorry to the team for me.

[ECRÃ: 1 — dentist, Monday · 2 — delivery, Friday · 3 — cables, Tuesday · 4 — football, Sunday]

### 3. Segunda escuta: os pormenores (3 min)

1. When is Mateus's appointment with the dentist? (Monday the 23rd at 8 a.m. ✓ / Monday the 13th at 8 a.m. / Thursday the 23rd at 8 p.m.)
2. What changed in Thandi's message? (The equipment is arriving on Friday, not today. ✓ / The delivery is cancelled. / The boxes are at the office.)
3. What time is Thandi going to the airport? (at 3:30 ✓ / at 2:00 / at 4:00)
4. When and where is João meeting Nuno? (at eleven, at the lift on the second floor ✓ / at nine, at the bank / at eleven, at reception)
5. Why can't Tunde play football? (His daughter is sick. ✓ / He's working. / He's travelling.)

*(Cada mensagem repete-se; depois aparecem as perguntas, com 8 s para responder.)*

### 4. Terceira escuta: como disseram (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Listen and repeat.
**PROF:** It's a reminder about your appointment. [PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Please call us to confirm. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** I'll call you when I have the boxes. [PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** I'll wait for you at the lift. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** I'm really sorry, but I can't come. [PAUSA 3 s]

**PROF:** Did you notice? In voice messages, people say their name first: "João here." "It's Tunde."
(PT: Numa mensagem de voz, o nome vem logo no princípio: "It's Thandi", "João here".)

### 5. A sua vez (1 min)

**PROF:** Leave a voice message for a colleague. You can't come to a meeting tomorrow. Say who you are, why you can't come, and suggest another day.
[PAUSA 20 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Hi, it's … I'm really sorry, but I can't come to the meeting tomorrow. I'm … How about … ? I'll call you later.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent listening! Next lesson: you call a client to move a meeting.

## 3F — Work & Life: Can we move the meeting?

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 3        Aula: 3F — Can we move the meeting?        Tipo: Work & Life
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue ligar a um cliente para mudar uma reunião: pedir
  desculpa, dar a razão, propor duas alternativas, combinar uma e confirmar por escrito.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 4 (e 1, 2)
Gramática-alvo: toda a da unidade; I'm calling about…; I'm afraid we need to move…; Are you
  available on…? / How about…?; I'll send you an email to confirm.
Palavras-alvo: I'm calling about, move, available, confirm, the equipment is late, as we agreed
Reciclagem: past simple (A2, 2); dias e datas (A1, 10)
Pronúncia: dizer datas ao telefone com clareza (Monday the twenty-third, at three p.m.)
Mediação: o Nuno passa à Thandi, em inglês, um recado que o Mateus lhe deixou em português
Contexto: quarta-feira, dia 18; o Nuno liga à Rita para mudar a reunião de quinta-feira
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 3F; conversa livre "A busy week"; pergunta 9 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today a real task: your client meeting is tomorrow, but there's a problem. You call the client to move it.

### 2. Situação (3 min)

[ECRÃ: quarta-feira, dia 18; o Nuno ao telefone na secretária; uma nota em português do Mateus colada no ecrã]

**PROF:** Mateus is visiting a client in Cabinda today. He asks Nuno to call Rita.

**RITA:** Rita Fonseca, good morning.
**NUNO:** Good morning, Rita. It's Nuno, from Kalandula Tech. I'm calling about our meeting tomorrow at ten.
**RITA:** Yes, the presentation of the new network.
**NUNO:** That's right. I'm afraid we need to move it. The new equipment is late — it's arriving on Friday, not today. So we can't show it to you tomorrow.
**RITA:** Oh, I see. When would you like to meet, then?
**NUNO:** Are you available on Monday morning? Or how about Tuesday afternoon?
**RITA:** Monday morning I'm afraid I'm busy — I'm visiting our office in Soyo. But Monday afternoon is fine. How about three o'clock?
**NUNO:** Monday the twenty-third at three. Perfect. In your office?
**RITA:** In the big meeting room, on the fourth floor.
**NUNO:** Great. I'll send you an email to confirm. And I'm sorry about the change.
**RITA:** No problem, Nuno. Thanks for calling.

[ECRÃ: o e-mail do Nuno]

> **Subject:** New date: network presentation
>
> Dear Rita,
>
> Thank you for your time on the phone today. As we agreed, we're moving the presentation of the new network to **Monday 23 November at 3 p.m.**, in the big meeting room on the fourth floor.
>
> I'm sorry again for the change. See you on Monday.
>
> Best regards,
> Nuno Kapinga
> Kalandula Tech

**Mediação**

[ECRÃ: a nota do Mateus: "Nuno, diz à Thandi que a reunião com a Rita passou para segunda às 15h e que ela também deve ir. Obrigado!"]

**THANDI:** (ao telefone) Hi Nuno! Any news about the meeting?
**NUNO:** Yes. Mateus says the meeting with Rita is now on Monday at three. And he says: please come too.
**THANDI:** Monday at three. OK, I'll be there!

### 3. Como se muda uma reunião (2 min)

[ECRÃ: cinco passos]

| Passo | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| 1. Porque liga | I'm calling about our meeting tomorrow at ten. |
| 2. Desculpa e razão | I'm afraid we need to move it. The new equipment is late. |
| 3. Duas alternativas | Are you available on Monday morning? Or how about Tuesday afternoon? |
| 4. Repetir e combinar | Monday the twenty-third at three. Perfect. |
| 5. Confirmar | I'll send you an email to confirm. |

**PROF:** Five steps. And always repeat the new day and time — on the phone, it's easy to make a mistake.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**PROF:** In the email, "As we agreed" means "como combinámos". It's very useful for confirmations.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**PROF:** And the mediation: Mateus wrote a note in Portuguese. Nuno tells Thandi the important part, in simple English: the new day, the time, and "please come too".
(PT: Na mediação, diga só o que a outra pessoa precisa de saber.)

### 4. Pronúncia (30 s)

**PROF:** Dates on the phone: slowly and clearly. Monday the twenty-third, at three p.m.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. A sua vez (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** I'm your client. You're calling to move our meeting on Thursday. Start.
[PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: Good morning. It's … from … I'm calling about our meeting on … I'm afraid we need to move it. …]
**PROF:** Oh, I see. Why?
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** OK. When are you available?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: Are you available on … ? Or how about … ?]
**PROF:** Tuesday at eleven is fine for me.
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Tuesday at eleven. Perfect. I'll send you an email to confirm.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Fantastic! Now go to the tutor: a busy week, and you need to find a time to meet. Good luck!
