# A2.2 · Unidade 7 — News and updates (7 aulas) · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções e personagens: ver os [guiões da unidade 1 do A2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2.1 e da unidade 6. Novo: o *present perfect* com *just* (novidades recentes), *already / yet* (feito / ainda não), *How long…? / for / since* (duração até agora, com *since* + um momento: *since 2020, since October, since Monday*); reacções a novidades (*Congratulations! That's great news! Oh no, I'm sorry to hear that. Really? What happened?*); adjectivos de sentimentos e o par *-ed / -ing*; palavras de projectos.

**Não usar nesta unidade:** o *present perfect continuous* (*I've been working*); *since* + oração (*since I was a child*); *will* e *going to* para previsões; *could*; o condicional (*if*, *would* + verbo, excepto *would like*); *too / enough*; o *past perfect*.

## 7A — I've just heard the news

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 7        Aula: 7A — I've just heard the news        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dar uma novidade recente com o present perfect e just (I've
  just got a promotion! The client has just called.) e reagir com a expressão certa para uma boa
  notícia, uma má notícia ou uma surpresa.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: have / has + just + particípio (posição de just: entre have e o particípio); reacções:
  Congratulations! That's great news! / Oh no, I'm sorry to hear that. / Really? / What happened?
Palavras-alvo: news (incontável), promotion, finance manager, contract, leave (Luanda), break down,
  congratulations, sorry to hear that, so soon
Reciclagem: present perfect para experiências (A2, 6A); present continuous para combinações (A2, 3A)
Pronúncia: just /dʒʌst/ forte; reacções com entoação (Really? ↗ Great! ↘)
Contexto: segunda-feira, 1 de Fevereiro; a copa da Kalandula Tech
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 7A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 7
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! At work, there's always news: good news, bad news, surprises. Today: how to give news — "I've just…" — and how to react.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a copa, segunda-feira, 8h30; a Sílvia entra a sorrir; o Paulo, o Nuno e a Ana]

**SÍLVIA:** Good morning, everyone! I've just heard the news — I've got the promotion! I'm the new finance manager.
**PAULO:** Really? Congratulations, Sílvia! That's great news!
**NUNO:** Well done! You really deserve it.
**ANA:** And I have some news too. Thandi has just called. She's leaving on the twelfth.
**NUNO:** The twelfth? So soon? Oh, that's a pity.
**ANA:** Yes, her time in Luanda is almost finished.
**PAULO:** And my news isn't so good. My car has broken down again.
**SÍLVIA:** Oh no, I'm sorry to hear that. What happened?
**PAULO:** I don't know. It stopped on the road this morning. I came by candongueiro!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Just*: uma novidade recente**

[ECRÃ: I**'ve just heard** the news. · Thandi **has just called**. · The client **has just sent** an email.]

**PROF:** "Just" means a short time ago — a few minutes, a few hours. It goes between "have" and the participle: I've just heard. She has just called.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Acabei de ouvir a notícia" → "I've just heard the news." "Acabei de" + verbo = have just + particípio.)
[ECRÃ: ✔ I've just heard. ✘ I just have heard. ✘ I've heard just.]

***Present perfect* para novidades**

[ECRÃ: I**'ve got** the promotion! · My car **has broken down** again.]

**PROF:** For news — something that happened recently and is important now — we often use the present perfect, with or without "just".
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** But when you tell the details — when, where — use the past simple, like in Unit 6: It stopped on the road this morning.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Reagir**

[ECRÃ: boa notícia: **Congratulations! That's great news! Well done!** · má notícia: **Oh no, I'm sorry to hear that. That's a pity.** · surpresa: **Really? So soon? What happened?**]

**PROF:** Good news: Congratulations! That's great news! Bad news: Oh no, I'm sorry to hear that. A surprise: Really? What happened?
[PAUSA 4 s]

***News***

[ECRÃ: ✔ **The news is** good. ✔ **some** news · ✘ a news ✘ the news are]

**PROF:** "News" is uncountable, like "information": the news is good, I have some news. Not "a news".
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In news, "just" is strong: I've JUST heard the news.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Reactions need the right music. Really? — up. That's great news! — down, with energy. I'm sorry to hear that — down, slow and soft.
[ECRÃ: Really? ↗ · That's great news! ↘ · I'm sorry to hear that. ↘ (devagar)]
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** React to my news.
**PROF:** I've just got a new job!
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: Congratulations! That's great news!]
**PROF:** My laptop has just stopped working, and I have a meeting in ten minutes.
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: Oh no, I'm sorry to hear that.]
**PROF:** The director has just resigned.
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: Really? What happened?]

**PROF:** Now give me some news — real or invented — with "I've just…".
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: modelos — I've just finished a big report. · I've just booked a holiday. · My sister has just had a baby!]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 3A):** And what are you doing this weekend?
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Great! News with "just", and the right reaction. Next lesson: "already" and "yet" — what's done, and what isn't.

## 7B — Have you finished yet?

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 7        Aula: 7B — Have you finished yet?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer o que já está feito e o que ainda não está com
  already / yet (We've already installed the network, but we haven't trained the staff yet.) e
  perguntar (Have you tested the system yet?).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: already (afirmativa, entre have e o particípio); yet (negativa e pergunta, no fim);
  Have you … yet? — Yes, we've already… / No, not yet.
Palavras-alvo: checklist, test, train, staff, install, finish, on track, behind schedule, go live
Reciclagem: just (7A); comparativos (A2, 4) e have to (A2, 5)
Pronúncia: already /ɔːlˈredi/ e yet /jet/ (≠ jet)
Contexto: quarta-feira, 3 de Fevereiro; o Mateus e o Nuno vêem a lista de tarefas do projecto
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 7B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! A project has a list of tasks. Some are done, some aren't. Today: "already" and "yet".

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o gabinete do Mateus; uma lista no ecrã: "Mussulo Energy — go live: Monday 1 March"]

**MATEUS:** OK, Nuno, let's check the list. Have you installed the network on the fourth floor yet?
**NUNO:** Yes, we've already installed it. Paulo and I finished on Friday.
**MATEUS:** Great. And the payment system? Has Arjun tested it yet?
**NUNO:** Not yet. He's arriving in Luanda on Saturday, and on Monday he's going to test it with João.
**MATEUS:** And the training for the staff?
**NUNO:** We haven't started the training yet. The training room isn't ready.
**MATEUS:** Hmm. So we're a bit behind schedule.
**NUNO:** A bit. But we've already done the most difficult part.
**MATEUS:** True. Have you sent the update to Rita yet?
**NUNO:** No, not yet. I'll send it this afternoon.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Already*: já está feito**

[ECRÃ: We**'ve already installed** it. · We**'ve already done** the most difficult part.]

**PROF:** "Already" — it's done, maybe earlier than you think. It goes between "have" and the participle.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Yet*: ainda não / já?**

[ECRÃ: We **haven't started** the training **yet**. (ainda não) · **Has** Arjun **tested** it **yet**? (já?) — **Not yet.**]

**PROF:** "Yet" — in negatives and questions, at the end of the sentence. We haven't started yet. Have you finished yet?
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Ainda não começámos" → "We haven't started yet." "Já testaste?" → "Have you tested it yet?")

[ECRÃ: ✔ Have you finished yet? — Yes, I've already finished. / No, not yet. · ✘ Have you finished already yet?]

**Palavras de projecto**

[ECRÃ: **on track** (dentro do prazo) · **behind schedule** (atrasado) · **go live** (começar a funcionar) · **a checklist**]

**PROF:** On track — the project is on time. Behind schedule — it's late. Go live — the system starts to work for real.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Already: all-READ-y. Yet: like "yes", with a "t". Not "jet" — yet.
[ECRÃ: al**READ**y · **y**et (como em "yes") ≠ jet]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: We've already installed it, but we haven't trained the staff yet.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a lista de tarefas de uma colega — ✔ write the report · ✔ call the supplier · ✘ send the invoice · ✘ book the meeting room]

**PROF:** Look at the list. What has she already done? What hasn't she done yet?
[PAUSA 15 s]
[ECRÃ: She's already written the report and called the supplier. She hasn't sent the invoice yet, and she hasn't booked the meeting room yet.]

**PROF:** Now you. What have you already done today? What haven't you done yet?
[PAUSA 12 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 5B):** What do you have to finish this week?
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! Next lesson: how long — "for" and "since".

## 7C — How long have you worked here?

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 7        Aula: 7C — How long have you worked here?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue perguntar e dizer há quanto tempo com o present perfect,
  for e since (I've worked here for twelve years. I've lived in Luanda since 2010.), e distinguir de
  quando começou, com o past simple e ago (I started four months ago.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: How long have you + particípio?; for + período (for three months, for ten years);
  since + momento (since October, since 2005, since Monday); present perfect para algo que começou no
  passado e continua; ≠ past simple + ago
Palavras-alvo: How long, for, since, farewell lunch, colleague, miss (sentir a falta), almost
Reciclagem: ago e past simple (A1, 10; A2, 2); present perfect (6A)
Pronúncia: for /fə/ fraco; since /sɪns/
Contexto: sexta-feira, 12 de Fevereiro; o almoço de despedida da Thandi
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 7C; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! "How long have you worked here?" — a very common question. Today: "for" and "since".

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: um restaurante na Ilha, sexta-feira; o almoço de despedida da Thandi; à mesa, o Sr. Baptista, a Ana, o Nuno, o Mateus e a Thandi]

**THANDI:** I'm going to miss you all. Mr Baptista, how long have you worked at Kalandula Tech?
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Oh, a long time. I've worked here since 2005 — I started the company with two friends.
**THANDI:** And you, Ana?
**ANA:** I've been the receptionist for twelve years. I know everybody!
**THANDI:** And Nuno is the new one.
**NUNO:** That's right. I've only worked here since October. I started four months ago — but I've learned a lot.
**MATEUS:** And you, Thandi? How long have you been in Luanda?
**THANDI:** Since the beginning of November. More than three months. And I've loved every day.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Thank you for everything, Thandi. Have a good trip home.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***How long…?***

[ECRÃ: **How long have** you **worked** here? · **How long have** you **been** in Luanda?]

**PROF:** "How long have you…?" asks about something that started in the past and continues now.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Há quanto tempo trabalha aqui?" → "How long have you worked here?" Em português usa-se o presente; em inglês, o present perfect.)
[ECRÃ: ✘ How long do you work here? ✘ I work here since 2005. ✔ I've worked here since 2005.]
**PROF:** Not "I work here since 2005". In English: I've worked here since 2005.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***For* e *since***

[ECRÃ: **for** + um período: for twelve years · for three months · for a week | **since** + um momento: since 2005 · since October · since Monday · since the beginning of November]

**PROF:** "For" — a period of time: for twelve years. "Since" — the moment it started: since 2005.
[PAUSA 4 s]

***Ago*: quando começou**

[ECRÃ: I've worked here **since October**. = I've worked here **for four months**. = I **started** four months **ago**.]

**PROF:** Three ways to say the same thing. With "ago", the past simple: I started four months ago.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "For" is weak: for twelve years — "fə twelve years". "Since" — clear: since October.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I've worked here for twelve years. I've lived in Luanda since 2010.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** For or since?
[ECRÃ: … 2018] [PAUSA 3 s] [ECRÃ: since 2018]
[ECRÃ: … six months] [PAUSA 3 s] [ECRÃ: for six months]
[ECRÃ: … last Monday] [PAUSA 3 s] [ECRÃ: since last Monday]

**PROF:** Now answer.
**PROF:** How long have you worked or studied in your job or school?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: I've worked at … for … / since … I started … ago.]
**PROF:** How long have you lived in your house or flat?
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** How long have you known your best friend?
[PAUSA 6 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 6A):** Have you ever been to South Africa?
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Now practise with your tutor: any news? Then: small talk — how to have a friendly conversation for two minutes.

## 7D — Small talk

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 7        Aula: 7D — Small talk        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue manter uma conversa de circunstância de cerca de 2
  minutos com um visitante — novidades, tempo, viagem, fim-de-semana — fazendo perguntas de
  seguimento (Really? How was it? What happened next?) e respondendo com mais do que uma frase.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 3
Gramática-alvo: perguntas de abertura (How was your trip? Is this your first time in Angola? How long
  have you been here?); perguntas de seguimento; respostas com um pormenor e uma pergunta de volta
  (And you?)
Palavras-alvo: small talk, first time, trip, flight, for a start, How about you?
Reciclagem: present perfect para experiências (A2, 6); What's the weather like? (A2, 6E); for /
  since (7C)
Pronúncia: entoação de interesse nas perguntas de seguimento (Really? How was it?)
Contexto: segunda-feira, 8 de Fevereiro; o Nuno vai buscar o Arjun ao hotel e conversam no carro
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 7D; pergunta 7 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Before a meeting, in a car, at a coffee break — people make small talk: a short, friendly conversation. Today you learn how to keep it going.

### 2. Situação (2 min)

[ECRÃ: o carro da empresa, segunda-feira, 8h; o Nuno conduz; o Arjun ao lado, a olhar pela janela]

**NUNO:** Good morning, Arjun! Welcome to Luanda. How was your flight?
**ARJUN:** Long! I flew from Mumbai to Dubai, and then to Luanda. Twenty hours.
**NUNO:** Twenty hours? Wow. Is this your first time in Angola?
**ARJUN:** Yes, it is. I've worked on this project for six months, but I've never been here. It's very different from Pune!
**NUNO:** Really? How is it different?
**ARJUN:** The sea, for a start! And it's very hot. What's the weather like in February?
**NUNO:** Hot and humid — it's the rainy season. How long have you been here?
**ARJUN:** Only since Saturday. On Sunday I walked on the Marginal. It was beautiful.
**NUNO:** Did you try the food?
**ARJUN:** Yes! I had grilled fish at a restaurant on the Ilha. Delicious. How about you? Have you ever been to India?
**NUNO:** No, never. I've only been to South Africa — last month, for the training. Maybe India next!
**ARJUN:** Ha! You're welcome in Pune any time.

### 3. Como se mantém uma conversa (3 min)

[ECRÃ: três regras]

| Regra | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| 1. Abra com uma pergunta fácil | How was your flight? · Is this your first time in Angola? |
| 2. Responda com um pormenor — não só "yes" | Yes, it is. I've worked on this project for six months, but I've never been here. |
| 3. Faça uma pergunta de seguimento ou devolva a pergunta | Really? How is it different? · How about you? · And you? |

**PROF:** One: open with an easy question. Two: answer with a detail, not only "yes" or "no". Three: ask a follow-up question, or ask the same question back.
[PAUSA 4 s]

[ECRÃ: perguntas de seguimento — **Really? How was it?** · **What happened next?** · **Why?** · **What was the best part?** · **How about you? / And you?**]

**PROF:** Follow-up questions show you're interested: Really? How was it? What happened next?
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: temas seguros — a viagem, o tempo, a comida, o fim-de-semana, a cidade · temas a evitar com quem não conhece bem — dinheiro, política, religião]

**PROF:** Safe topics: the trip, the weather, food, the weekend. With new people, don't talk about money or politics.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (30 s)

**PROF:** Show interest with your voice: Really? ↗ How was it? ↘
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** I'm a visitor in your city. Ask me an opening question.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Yes, it's my first time here. I arrived on Saturday.
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Really? How was your flight? / How long have you been here? — Since Saturday… / What do you think of the city?]
**PROF:** I went to the beach yesterday, but it rained!
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Oh no! What happened next? / What did you do?]
**PROF:** And you? What did you do at the weekend?
[PAUSA 10 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 4B):** What's the best place to eat fish in your city?
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Lovely small talk! Next lesson: words for feelings — excited, worried, relieved — and how to react with your voice.

## 7E — Good news, bad news

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 7        Aula: 7E — Good news, bad news        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer como se sente com uma novidade (I'm excited. I was
  worried, but now I'm relieved.), distinguir os adjectivos em -ed e -ing (bored / boring), reagir com a
  entoação certa, e evitar os falsos amigos eventually e notice.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: -ed (como a pessoa se sente) / -ing (como é a coisa ou a pessoa que causa o
  sentimento): bored / boring, tired / tiring, interested / interesting, surprised / surprising
Palavras-alvo: excited, worried, nervous, disappointed, relieved, proud, surprised, bored, boring,
  tiring, interesting, news, promotion, new baby, engaged, resign
Reciclagem: just e reacções (7A); be + adjectivo (A2, 5D)
Pronúncia: entoação ascendente e descendente nas reacções; o -ed de adjectivos (bored /d/, excited
  /ɪd/, relieved /d/, surprised /d/)
Falsos amigos: eventually (≠ eventualmente); notice (≠ notícia)
Contexto: cinco pessoas, cinco novidades, cinco sentimentos
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 7E; pergunta 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! News makes us feel something: excited, worried, disappointed. Today: words for feelings, and how to say them with the right voice.

### 2. Cinco novidades, cinco sentimentos (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: cinco fotografias com legenda]

**PROF:** Sílvia has got a promotion. She's excited — and proud.
**PROF:** Paulo's car has broken down. He was worried about the cost, but the mechanic says it's cheap. Now he's relieved.
**PROF:** Thandi is leaving Luanda. Ana is disappointed. She wanted more time with Thandi.
**PROF:** Nuno has a presentation tomorrow. He's nervous.
**PROF:** Joana, Paulo's sister, has just had some news: she's engaged! Everybody is surprised.
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: **excited** (entusiasmado) · **proud** (orgulhoso) · **worried** (preocupado) · **relieved** (aliviado) · **disappointed** (desiludido) · **nervous** (nervoso) · **surprised** (surpreendido)]

**PROF:** Relieved: you were worried, and now the problem is gone. Disappointed: you wanted something, and it didn't happen.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 3. *-ed* ou *-ing* (2 min)

[ECRÃ: The meeting was **boring**. → I was **bored**. · The trip was **tiring**. → We were **tired**. · The news is **surprising**. → I'm **surprised**. · The project is **interesting**. → I'm **interested**.]

**PROF:** "-ed" is how a person feels: I was bored. "-ing" is the thing that makes the feeling: the meeting was boring.
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: ✘ I'm boring. (= sou chato!) ✔ I'm bored. (= estou aborrecido)]
**PROF:** Careful! "I'm boring" means people find you boring. You mean "I'm bored".
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia: a voz das reacções (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Listen to the music of reactions.
**PROF:** Really? ↗ — surprised, the voice goes up.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** That's great! ↘ — happy, the voice goes down, with energy.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Oh no… ↘ — sad, the voice goes down, slowly.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** And the "-ed": bored, relieved, surprised — just a "d" sound. But excited, disappointed — an extra syllable, because of the "t".
[ECRÃ: bored /d/ · relieved /d/ · surprised /d/ · excited /ɪd/ · disappointed /ɪd/]
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: ✘ **eventually** ≠ eventualmente · eventually = finalmente, depois de muito tempo · eventualmente = **maybe, possibly**]
**PROF:** "Eventually" means "in the end, after a long time": Eventually, the train arrived. "Eventualmente" in Portuguese is "maybe".
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Eventualmente vou a Joanesburgo" → "Maybe I'm going to Johannesburg." Não "Eventually I'm going…".)

[ECRÃ: ✘ **notice** ≠ notícia · notícia = **news** · notice = aviso (um papel na parede) ou reparar]
**PROF:** "News" is "notícia". A "notice" is a short message on a wall: There's a notice on the door. And "to notice" means to see something: I noticed the mistake.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 6. Prática e fecho (1 min)

**PROF:** How do you feel? Your best friend has just got a new job.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Your flight is cancelled.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** You had a test, and you passed.
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: I'm happy for him / her! · I'm disappointed. · I'm relieved!]

**PROF (reciclagem — 7A):** Tell me some good news with "I've just…".
[PAUSA 5 s]

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: four conversations in the corridor. What's new?

## 7F — Real English: What's new?

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 7        Aula: 7F — What's new?        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue identificar, em quatro conversas curtas a velocidade
  natural, a novidade de cada pessoa e a reacção de quem ouve.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 3
Língua: A1, A2.1 e unidades 6–7, sem estruturas novas
Vozes: Paulo e Joana (angolanos), Rita (portuguesa), Tunde (nigeriano), Lerato (sul-africana)
Velocidade: natural
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 7F; pergunta 9 do questionário
```

### 1. Apresentação (30 s)

**PROF:** Four short conversations. In each one, someone has some news. Listen for the news — and the reaction. You hear them three times.

### 2. Primeira escuta: a ideia geral (3 min)

**PROF:** First: which news is bad?

**CONVERSA 1 — JOANA E PAULO**
**JOANA:** Paulo! I have some news. Mário and I are getting married!
**PAULO:** What? Really? Congratulations! When did he ask you?
**JOANA:** Last night, at dinner. We've been together for four years, so… it was time!

**CONVERSA 2 — RITA**
**RITA:** Good news for Mussulo Energy: we've just signed a contract with a big company in Benguela. So we're opening a new office there in June.
**NUNO:** That's great news! Are you moving to Benguela?
**RITA:** No, no. I'm staying in Luanda — but I'm going to visit a lot!

**CONVERSA 3 — TUNDE**
**TUNDE:** You know my daughter, the small one? She's started nursery school this week! She was nervous on the first day, but now she loves it.
**AMIGO:** Ah, that's lovely. How old is she now?
**TUNDE:** Two and a half. Time goes fast!

**CONVERSA 4 — LERATO**
**LERATO:** Hi Nuno, it's Lerato. I've just arrived in Luanda — but my suitcase hasn't. It's still in Johannesburg!
**NUNO:** Oh no, I'm sorry to hear that! The same thing happened to Thandi once. What did the airline say?
**LERATO:** It's arriving on tomorrow's flight. So tonight I need a toothbrush!

[ECRÃ: Joana — engaged · Rita — new contract · Tunde — school · Lerato — suitcase]

### 3. Segunda escuta: os pormenores (3 min)

1. How long have Joana and Mário been together? (for four years ✓ / since last night / for four months)
2. When did Mário ask Joana? (last night, at dinner ✓ / last week / this morning)
3. What is Mussulo Energy opening in June? (a new office in Benguela ✓ / a new office in Luanda / a bakery)
4. How did Tunde's daughter feel on the first day of nursery school? (nervous ✓ / bored / excited)
5. Where is Lerato's suitcase? (in Johannesburg ✓ / at the hotel / in Luanda)
6. How does Nuno react to Lerato's news? (Oh no, I'm sorry to hear that! ✓ / Congratulations! / Really? Great!)

*(Cada conversa repete-se; depois aparecem as perguntas, com 8 s para responder.)*

### 4. Terceira escuta: como disseram (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Listen and repeat.
**PROF:** I have some news. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** We've just signed a contract. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** She's started school this week! [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Time goes fast! [PAUSA 3 s]

**PROF:** Did you notice? In one sentence, Rita uses three tenses: "we've just signed" — news; "we're opening" — an arrangement; "I'm going to visit" — an intention.
(PT: Numa só fala, a Rita usa o present perfect para a novidade, o present continuous para o que está combinado e going to para a intenção.)

### 5. A sua vez (1 min)

**PROF:** Now you. Tell me some news — from your work, your family or your city. Then imagine my reaction.
[PAUSA 20 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I have some news! I've just … / We've … since … / Last week, … ]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: the most important update of the project — for the manager and the client.

## 7G — Work & Life: Project update

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: 7        Aula: 7G — Project update        Tipo: Work & Life
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dar o ponto de situação de um projecto à chefia e a um
  cliente: o que já está feito, o que ainda não está e porquê, um problema e os próximos passos.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 4 (e 2, 3)
Gramática-alvo: toda a da unidade; We've already…; We haven't … yet, because…; Since Monday, we've…;
  Next week we're…; We're on track / a bit behind schedule.
Palavras-alvo: update, on track, behind schedule, go live, next steps, final test, train
Reciclagem: present continuous para combinações e going to (A2, 3); because / so (A2, 2D)
Pronúncia: pausas entre as partes do ponto de situação (Done. / Not yet. / Next steps.)
Mediação: o Nuno passa ao Arjun, em inglês, uma mensagem do João escrita em português
Contexto: quarta-feira, 10 de Fevereiro; videochamada semanal com a Rita, o Mateus e o Arjun
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 7G; conversa livre "Project update"; pergunta 9 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Every week, projects have an update meeting: what's done, what isn't, and what's next. Today you give one.

### 2. Situação (3 min)

[ECRÃ: uma videochamada; a Rita, o Mateus, o Nuno e o Arjun; no ecrã do Nuno, uma lista]

**RITA:** Good morning, everyone. Nuno, can you give us the update?
**NUNO:** Of course. So, we're on track for the first of March. First, what's done: we've already installed the network on all four floors, and since Monday, Arjun and João have tested the payment system with fifty clients.
**RITA:** Fifty? That's great. Any problems?
**NUNO:** Just one. The system is slow in the afternoons, when a lot of clients pay. Arjun is working on it.
**ARJUN:** Yes, I've found the problem. I'm going to fix it this week.
**RITA:** Good. What haven't you done yet?
**NUNO:** We haven't trained the staff yet, because the training room wasn't ready. But it's ready now. So the next steps: we're training the staff next week, on Monday and Tuesday, and on Friday the twenty-sixth we're doing a final test.
**RITA:** Perfect. Thank you, Nuno. Very clear.

**Mediação**

[ECRÃ: uma mensagem do João no telemóvel do Nuno: "Nuno, diz ao Arjun que hoje à tarde não posso fazer os testes: tenho uma reunião com o banco. Podemos fazer amanhã às 9h?"]

**ARJUN:** Nuno, João sent you a message? Is it about the tests?
**NUNO:** Yes. He says he can't do the tests this afternoon — he has a meeting with the bank. He asks: can you do them tomorrow at nine?
**ARJUN:** Tomorrow at nine is fine. Thanks!

### 3. Como se dá um ponto de situação (2 min)

[ECRÃ: quatro partes]

| Parte | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| 1. A situação geral | We're on track for the first of March. / We're a bit behind schedule. |
| 2. O que está feito | We've already installed the network. Since Monday, we've tested the system with fifty clients. |
| 3. O que não está, e porquê | We haven't trained the staff yet, because the training room wasn't ready. |
| 4. Os próximos passos | Next week we're training the staff. On Friday we're doing a final test. |

**PROF:** Start with the big picture: on track, or behind schedule. Then what's done, what isn't and why, and what's next.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Use the right tense for each part: present perfect for done and not done, past simple for the reason, and present continuous or "going to" for the next steps.
[PAUSA 4 s]

**PROF:** And the mediation: João wrote in Portuguese. Nuno gives Arjun the three facts he needs: no tests this afternoon, why, and the new time.
(PT: Na mediação, passe o essencial: o quê, porquê e a nova proposta.)

### 4. Pronúncia (30 s)

**PROF:** In an update, make short pauses between the parts. What's done. — What isn't. — What's next.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. A sua vez (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o projecto de um escritório novo — ✔ choose the furniture · ✔ install the internet · ✘ paint the walls (the painter is sick) · next: paint next Monday, move in on the 15th]

**PROF:** I'm your manager. Give me the update. Use the four parts.
[PAUSA 30 s]
[ECRÃ: We're … We've already … We haven't … yet, because … Next week we're …]
**PROF:** Thank you. Any problems?
[PAUSA 8 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Fantastic! Now go to the tutor: a client wants an update on your project. Good luck!
