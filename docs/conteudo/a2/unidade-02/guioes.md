# A2.1 · Unidade 2 — Life stories (7 aulas) · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções e personagens: ver os [guiões da unidade 1 do A2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1 e da unidade 1 do A2. Novo: mais 25 verbos irregulares no *past simple*; perguntas sobre o passado com e sem *did* (*Where did you study? Who told you? What happened?*); o *past continuous* (*I was working*); *when* e *while*; *first, then, after that, later, in the end*; *I was born*; *in* + ano, *from … to*, *… ago*; os marcos da vida e do percurso profissional.

**Não usar nesta unidade:** o *present perfect*, *will*, *should / have to / must*, o condicional (*if*), o *present continuous* com valor de futuro, *used to* e o *past perfect*.

**Os 25 irregulares novos** (os do A1 continuam: *go, have, do, see, make, get, come, buy, eat, take, drive, meet, say, give, write, swim, sing, leave, sleep, put, read, think, tell*):

| 2A | 2C | 2D | 2E |
|---|---|---|---|
| become → became · choose → chose · grow → grew · know → knew · spend → spent · teach → taught · understand → understood | ring → rang · stand → stood · run → ran · hear → heard · wake → woke · wear → wore | lose → lost · find → found · feel → felt · pay → paid · bring → brought · send → sent · forget → forgot | begin → began · keep → kept · sell → sold · win → won · mean → meant |

**Personagens desta unidade:** **Mateus** (34 anos, de Huambo, engenheiro, casado com a advogada **Luísa**, uma filha bebé, **Ana Clara**, todos do A1); **Nuno**, **Sílvia**, **Paulo**, **Thandi**, **Sr. Baptista**. Na 2F: **Fátima** (angolana, do Huambo; A1, unidade 4), **Tunde** (nigeriano, de Lagos; A1, unidade 4) e **Emma** (britânica, de Manchester; A1, unidade 2).

## 2A — I grew up in Huambo

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 2        Aula: 2A — I grew up in Huambo        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue contar os marcos da sua vida por ordem, com o past simple
  e datas (I was born in Huambo in 1992. I grew up there. In 2010 I moved to Luanda.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: past simple (revisto) e 7 irregulares novos: became, chose, grew, knew, spent, taught,
  understood; I was born (não "I born"); in + ano; from 2010 to 2015; … years ago
Palavras-alvo: be born, grow up, primary / secondary school, university, engineering, graduate, get a
  job, move to, get married, daughter, team leader
Reciclagem: past simple e was / were (A1, 10); present continuous para o período actual (A2, 1A)
Pronúncia: anos (nineteen ninety-two, twenty ten); grew /gruː/, chose /tʃəʊz/
Contexto: almoço na cantina; o Nuno pergunta ao Mateus como chegou a chefe de equipa
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 2A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 2
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! In this unit, you tell stories: your life, your studies, your career. Today: the big moments of your life, in the right order.

### 2. Situação (2 min)

[ECRÃ: a cantina da Kalandula Tech; o Mateus e o Nuno almoçam]

**NUNO:** Mateus, can I ask you something? How did you become a team leader?
**MATEUS:** Ha! It's a long story. I was born in Huambo in 1992, and I grew up there. I went to primary and secondary school in Huambo.
**NUNO:** And university?
**MATEUS:** In 2010 I moved to Luanda. I chose engineering because I loved computers and machines. I studied from 2010 to 2015.
**NUNO:** Was it difficult?
**MATEUS:** Yes! In the first year I didn't understand anything. But a great teacher taught me a lot, and I understood. I graduated in 2015, and I got a job here at Kalandula Tech a year later.
**NUNO:** In Cabinda, right?
**MATEUS:** Yes. I spent ten years on projects in Cabinda. I met Luísa there, and we got married four years ago.
**NUNO:** And now you're in Luanda.
**MATEUS:** Yes. Our daughter was born in May, so I asked for a job in Luanda. And in September I became a team leader. And now I'm talking about my life and my lunch is cold!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Os marcos da vida, por ordem**

[ECRÃ: uma linha do tempo do Mateus: 1992 born, Huambo · grew up in Huambo · 2010 moved to Luanda · 2010–2015 university · 2015 graduated · 2016 got a job · 2016–2026 Cabinda · 2022 got married · 2026 daughter born · September 2026 team leader]

**PROF:** Look at Mateus's life. The big moments are in the past simple, in order.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***I was born***

[ECRÃ: ✔ I **was born** in Huambo. ✘ I born in Huambo. ✘ I am born in Huambo.]

**PROF:** I was born. Always "was born" — for one person, "was"; "we were born". When were you born? — I was born in 1992.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Nasci" diz-se "I was born", com "was". É um erro muito comum dizer "I born".)

**Verbos irregulares novos**

[ECRÃ: become → **became** · choose → **chose** · grow → **grew** · know → **knew** · spend → **spent** · teach → **taught** · understand → **understood**]

**PROF:** Listen and repeat. Become, became.
[PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** Choose, chose. Grow, grew. Know, knew. Spend, spent. Teach, taught. Understand, understood.
[PAUSA 2 s depois de cada par]

**Quando**

[ECRÃ: **in** 1992 · **in** May · **from** 2010 **to** 2015 · four years **ago** · a year **later**]

**PROF:** In 1992. From 2010 to 2015. Four years ago — "ago" after the time, as in A1. A year later — one year after that.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Years: nineteen ninety-two. Twenty ten. Twenty fifteen. Twenty twenty-six.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Grew. Chose. Taught — the "gh" is silent.
[ECRÃ: grew /gruː/ · chose /tʃəʊz/ · taught /tɔːt/]
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min)

**PROF:** Say the past.
[ECRÃ: I … (grow up) in Viana.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: I grew up in Viana.]
[ECRÃ: She … (choose) medicine.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: She chose medicine.]
[ECRÃ: We … (spend) two years in Benguela.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: We spent two years in Benguela.]

**PROF:** Now your life. Answer.
**PROF:** Where were you born, and where did you grow up?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: I was born in … in … I grew up in …]
**PROF:** When did you finish school? What did you do after that?
[PAUSA 8 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 1A):** And these days? What are you doing?
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Great! Now you can tell the big moments of your life. Next lesson: how to ask other people about their past.

## 2B — Who told you?

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 2        Aula: 2B — Who told you?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue fazer perguntas sobre o passado com did (Where did you
  study? Why did you choose IT?) e perguntas sobre o sujeito, sem did (Who told you? What happened?
  Who opened the door?), e responder.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: Wh- + did + sujeito + verbo (What did you see?); perguntas de sujeito: Who / What +
  verbo no passado (Who saw you? What happened?); contraste Who did you call? / Who called you?
Palavras-alvo: happen, open, close, server room, key, check, equipment, early
Reciclagem: perguntas com did (A1, 10); verbos de estado (A2, 1B)
Pronúncia: did you /dɪdʒu/ em fala ligada; entoação descendente nas perguntas wh-
Contexto: segunda-feira, 7h50; a porta da sala dos servidores está aberta e o Paulo quer saber porquê
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 2B; pergunta 3 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: questions about the past. Some questions have "did", and some don't. Let's see why.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o corredor da Kalandula Tech, 7h50; a porta da sala dos servidores aberta; o Paulo ao telefone com a Ana]

**PAULO:** Ana, the server room door is open! What happened? Who opened it?
**ANA:** Thandi opened it. She came early, at seven.
**PAULO:** Thandi? Who gave her the key?
**ANA:** Mateus gave it to her on Friday.
**PAULO:** OK… And what did she do in there?
**ANA:** She checked the new equipment. Then she went to Mussulo Energy.
**PAULO:** Did she close the door?
**ANA:** Ha! No, she didn't. That's why you're calling me!
**PAULO:** Who told you about the key?
**ANA:** Mateus did. He tells me everything!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Perguntas com *did***

[ECRÃ: **What did** she **do**? · **Where did** you **study**? · **Why did** you **choose** IT? · **Who did** you **call**?]

**PROF:** Most questions in the past use "did": question word, did, the person, and the simple verb. What did she do? Where did you study?
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Perguntas sobre o sujeito: sem *did***

[ECRÃ: **Who opened** the door? — Thandi opened it. · **Who gave** her the key? — Mateus. · **What happened**? — The door was open.]

**PROF:** But when "who" or "what" is the person or thing that did the action, there's no "did". Who opened the door? Thandi opened it. Thandi is the answer — she did it. The verb is in the past: opened, gave, happened.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: Quando a resposta é quem fez a acção, não há "did": "Who opened the door?" — "Quem abriu a porta?")

[ECRÃ: ✔ What happened? ✘ What did happen? · ✔ Who told you? ✘ Who did tell you?]

**Duas perguntas diferentes**

[ECRÃ: **Who did you call?** — I called Thandi. (você ligou) · **Who called you?** — Thandi called me. (a Thandi ligou)]

**PROF:** Who did you call? — You called someone. Who called you? — Someone called you. Different questions!
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In fast English, "did you" sounds like "didja". What did you do? Where did you go?
[ECRÃ: did you /dɪdʒu/]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Wh- questions go down at the end: What happened? Who opened it?
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Make the question. Use "did" — or not.
[ECRÃ: Someone sent the email. → Who …?] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Who sent the email?]
[ECRÃ: You saw someone at the bank. → Who …?] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Who did you see at the bank?]
[ECRÃ: Something happened in the meeting. → What …?] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: What happened in the meeting?]

**PROF:** Now answer my questions.
**PROF:** Who taught you English at school?
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** Where did you study after secondary school?
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** Now ask me two questions about my past.
[PAUSA 12 s]
[ECRÃ: modelos — Where did you grow up? · Why did you choose this job? · Who taught you…?]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 1B):** Do you remember the name of your first teacher?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, I remember. Her name was … / No, I don't remember.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! Questions with "did", and questions without. Next lesson: what were you doing when…?

## 2C — I was working when…

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 2        Aula: 2C — I was working when…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue contar o que estava a acontecer e o que interrompeu, com o
  past continuous e o past simple, when e while (I was preparing the accounts when the power went off.
  While I was driving, my phone rang.), e perguntar (What were you doing at three o'clock?).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: past continuous: was / were + -ing (+, −, ?); past continuous (acção longa) + when +
  past simple (acção curta); while + past continuous; 6 irregulares: rang, stood, ran, heard, woke, wore
Palavras-alvo: power cut, the power went off, the lights came on, generator, lift, stairs, luckily,
  suit, noise, phone rang
Reciclagem: past simple (2A); present continuous (A2, 1A)
Pronúncia: was /wəz/ e were /wə/ fracos antes do -ing; wasn't e weren't fortes
Contexto: a Sílvia conta o corte de energia de sexta-feira
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 2C; perguntas 4 e 5 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today we tell what was happening at a moment in the past — and what happened suddenly.

### 2. Situação (2 min)

[ECRÃ: o escritório às escuras; a Sílvia conta ao Nuno, na segunda-feira]

**NUNO:** Sílvia, what happened on Friday? I wasn't here.
**SÍLVIA:** Oh, Friday! At three o'clock I was preparing the annual accounts when the power went off.
**NUNO:** No! Did you lose your work?
**SÍLVIA:** No, I didn't, luckily. But it was crazy. Paulo was talking to a client on a video call, and Mateus and Thandi were standing in the lift!
**NUNO:** In the lift? What did they do?
**SÍLVIA:** They waited for twenty minutes. Mateus was wearing his new suit — and it was very hot!
**NUNO:** And the generator?
**SÍLVIA:** It didn't start. While we were waiting, Paulo ran down the stairs and called the technician. Then we heard a noise, and the lights came on.
**NUNO:** And Thandi?
**SÍLVIA:** She laughed and said: "In Johannesburg we have power cuts too!"

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**O *past continuous***

[ECRÃ: I **was preparing** · Paulo **was talking** · Mateus and Thandi **were standing** · ? **Were** you **working**? — Yes, I **was**. · − I **wasn't** working.]

**PROF:** Was or were, plus the verb with "-ing". It's an action in progress at a moment in the past. At three o'clock, I was preparing the accounts.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: É o "estava a preparar". "Às três da tarde estava a preparar as contas" → "At three o'clock I was preparing the accounts.")

**Acção longa e acção curta**

[ECRÃ: uma linha do tempo: uma barra comprida "was preparing the accounts" cortada por um raio "the power went off"]

**PROF:** The long action: past continuous — I was preparing the accounts. The short action that stops it: past simple — the power went off. We join them with "when".
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: I **was preparing** the accounts **when** the power **went off**. · **While** we **were waiting**, Paulo **ran** down the stairs.]

**PROF:** "While" goes with the long action: while we were waiting. "When" goes with the short action: when the power went off.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Verbos de estado, outra vez**

[ECRÃ: ✔ I **knew** the answer. ✘ I was knowing the answer.]

**PROF:** Remember: know, need, want — no "-ing", also in the past.
[PAUSA 2 s]

**Irregulares novos**

[ECRÃ: ring → **rang** · stand → **stood** · run → **ran** · hear → **heard** · wake → **woke** · wear → **wore**]

**PROF:** Ring, rang. Stand, stood. Run, ran. Hear, heard. Wake, woke. Wear, wore.
[PAUSA 2 s depois de cada par]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In the sentence, "was" and "were" are weak: I was working. They were standing.
[ECRÃ: I was /wəz/ working · they were /wə/ standing]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** In negatives, strong: I wasn't working. They weren't waiting.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min)

**PROF:** Join the two actions with "when".
[ECRÃ: I / drive to work — my phone / ring] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: I was driving to work when my phone rang.]
[ECRÃ: We / have lunch — the client / arrive] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: We were having lunch when the client arrived.]

**PROF:** Now answer.
**PROF:** What were you doing yesterday at eight p.m.?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: At eight p.m. I was … / I was having dinner.]
**PROF:** Tell me about a power cut. What were you doing when the power went off?
[PAUSA 10 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — 2A):** Where were you born?
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! The long action and the short action. Now practise with your tutor — tell your life story, and what you were doing when something happened. Then: how to tell a whole story — beginning, middle and end.

## 2D — In the end

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 2        Aula: 2D — In the end        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue contar uma pequena história com princípio, meio e fim,
  ligada com first, then, after that, later, in the end, because e so, misturando past simple e past
  continuous.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 4
Gramática-alvo: sequenciadores: first, then, after that, later, in the end (≠ at the end of); because /
  so; 7 irregulares: lost, found, felt, paid, brought, sent, forgot
Palavras-alvo: taxi driver, hotel, disaster, knock on the door, worried, relieved, story
Reciclagem: past continuous e when (2C); was / were (A1, 10)
Pronúncia: as pausas entre as partes de uma história; brought /brɔːt/ e bought /bɔːt/
Contexto: a Thandi conta o primeiro dia em Luanda, há duas semanas
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 2D; pergunta 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Everyone loves a good story. Today you tell one — in the right order, with a beginning, a middle and an end.

### 2. Situação (2 min)

[ECRÃ: a esplanada do café Kiandas; a Thandi conta à Sílvia e ao Nuno]

**THANDI:** Do you want to hear about my first day in Luanda? It was a disaster!
**SÍLVIA:** Yes, please!
**THANDI:** First, I arrived at the airport at night. I was very tired. Then I took a taxi to the hotel and I paid the driver. After that, I went to my room and I felt something was wrong. My phone wasn't in my bag!
**NUNO:** Oh no! Did you lose it in the taxi?
**THANDI:** Yes. I was worried, because all my work was on that phone. So I sent an email to my company from the hotel computer. Later, I was having a shower when someone knocked on the door.
**SÍLVIA:** Who was it?
**THANDI:** The taxi driver! He found my phone in his car and he brought it back to the hotel.
**NUNO:** Wow! Did you give him something?
**THANDI:** Of course. In the end, I felt really relieved — and I forgot my bad day!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**A ordem da história**

[ECRÃ: **First,** … · **Then** … · **After that,** … · **Later,** … · **In the end,** …]

**PROF:** First — the beginning. Then, after that, later — the middle. In the end — how the story finishes.
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: ✔ **In the end**, I felt relieved. (= finalmente, no fim da história) · ✔ **At the end of** the day, I was tired. (= no fim do dia)]

**PROF:** In the end: how everything finished. At the end of — the end of a time or a place: at the end of the day, at the end of the street.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***Because* e *so***

[ECRÃ: I was worried **because** my work was on the phone. (razão) · My phone wasn't there, **so** I sent an email. (resultado)]

**PROF:** Because gives the reason. So gives the result.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Os dois passados na história**

[ECRÃ: I **was having** a shower **when** someone **knocked** on the door.]

**PROF:** The story uses the past simple for the events, one after the other, and the past continuous for the background.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Irregulares novos**

[ECRÃ: lose → **lost** · find → **found** · feel → **felt** · pay → **paid** · bring → **brought** · send → **sent** · forget → **forgot**]

**PROF:** Lose, lost. Find, found. Feel, felt. Pay, paid. Bring, brought. Send, sent. Forget, forgot.
[PAUSA 2 s depois de cada par]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Brought and bought: they sound almost the same. Brought, from "bring". Bought, from "buy". Listen: brought. Bought.
[ECRÃ: brought /brɔːt/ (bring) · bought /bɔːt/ (buy)]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** In a story, make a short pause after "First", "Then" and "In the end". It helps people follow you.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min)

**PROF:** Put the story in order. Say it.
[ECRÃ: in the end, I found them in my car · first, I lost my keys · then, I looked in the office] [PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: First, I lost my keys. Then I looked in the office. In the end, I found them in my car.]

**PROF:** Now tell me a short story: a day when something went wrong. Use first, then and in the end.
[PAUSA 20 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — First, I … Then … I was … when … So I … In the end, …]

**PROF (reciclagem — A1, 10):** How was your weekend?
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Brilliant storytelling! Next lesson: words for the big events of life, and how to join words when you speak.

## 2E — Life events

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 2        Aula: 2E — Life events        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer os marcos da vida e do percurso de estudos e de
  trabalho (be born, grow up, graduate, get a job, get a promotion, move, get married, retire), ligar
  as palavras ao falar (lived‿in, got‿a job) e evitar os falsos amigos college e pretend.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: get + nome (get a job, get a promotion, get married); 5 irregulares: began, kept, sold,
  won, meant
Palavras-alvo: be born, grow up, go to school, primary / secondary school, university, degree, course,
  graduate, apply for, get a job, get a promotion, leave a job, move, get married, have children,
  retire, prize, business
Reciclagem: past simple (2A); perguntas (2B)
Pronúncia: ligação consoante-vogal (lived‿in, got‿a job, began‿a course, worked‿at); o -ed (revisto do A1)
Falsos amigos: college (≠ colégio); pretend (≠ pretender)
Contexto: a vida da Graça, mãe do Paulo, numa linha do tempo
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 2E; pergunta 7 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: the words for the big events of life — and how to join words to sound more natural.

### 2. A vida da Graça (3 min)

[ECRÃ: a linha do tempo da Graça Mendes, mãe do Paulo, com fotografias]

**PROF:** This is Graça, Paulo's mother. Listen to her life.
**PROF:** Graça was born in Malanje in 1970. She grew up in a big family.
**PROF:** She went to primary school and secondary school in Malanje.
**PROF:** In 1989 she moved to Luanda and began a course to become a teacher.
**PROF:** She graduated in 1992 and got a job at a school in Viana.
**PROF:** She got married to Alberto in 1995. They had three children: Paulo, Joana and Nelson.
**PROF:** In 2010 she got a promotion: she became the head teacher of her school.
**PROF:** She won a prize for her work in 2018.
**PROF:** She's going to retire next year.
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: as expressões a negrito — **was born · grew up · went to school · moved to · began a course · graduated · got a job · got married · had children · got a promotion · won a prize · retire**]

### 3. Expressões com *get* (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: **get a job** — arranjar emprego · **get a promotion** — ser promovido · **get married** — casar-se]

**PROF:** Get a job. Get a promotion. Get married — "to" a person: She got married to Alberto.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✔ She got married **to** Alberto. ✘ She married with Alberto.]
**PROF:** Apply for a job: you send your CV. Leave a job: you stop working there.
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: irregulares — begin → **began** · keep → **kept** · sell → **sold** · win → **won** · mean → **meant**]
**PROF:** Begin, began. Keep, kept. Sell, sold. Win, won. Mean, meant.
[PAUSA 2 s depois de cada par]

### 4. Pronúncia: ligar as palavras (2 min)

**PROF:** When a word ends in a consonant and the next word starts with a vowel, join them.
[ECRÃ: lived‿in · got‿a job · began‿a course · worked‿at a bank · won‿a prize]
**PROF:** She lived‿in Malanje.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** She got‿a job.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** I worked‿at a bank.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** And remember the "-ed" from A1: moved — one syllable. Graduated — "-ed" is a syllable, because of the "t".
[ECRÃ: moved /muːvd/ · graduated /ˈgrædʒueɪtɪd/]
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Falsos amigos (1 min)

[ECRÃ: ✘ **college** ≠ colégio · colégio (escola privada) = **private school** · college = escola superior ou curso técnico]

[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Pretendo mudar de emprego" → "I want to change jobs." Nunca "I pretend to change jobs".)

## 2G — Work & Life: Tell me about yourself

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 2        Aula: 2G — Tell me about yourself        Tipo: Work & Life
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue contar à nova chefia, em cerca de 1 minuto, o seu
  percurso de estudos e de trabalho por ordem, terminar no presente, e responder a duas perguntas
  de seguimento.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 2, 4
Gramática-alvo: toda a da unidade, mais o present simple e continuous (A2, 1) para terminar no
  presente
Palavras-alvo: internship, experience, degree in, career, junior technician

Pronúncia: pausas nas mudanças de tempo (… in 2025. / Now I'm…)

  Técnico")

Verificação: Lab 2G; conversa livre "Tell me about yourself"; pergunta 9 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! "Tell me about yourself." You hear this with a new manager, in an interview, or with a new client. Today you prepare a one-minute answer.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o gabinete do Sr. Baptista; o Nuno senta-se]

**SR. BAPTISTA:** Nuno, welcome. We didn't really talk on your first day. Tell me about yourself.
**NUNO:** Of course. I was born in Lubango in 2003, and I grew up there with my three sisters. After secondary school, I began a degree in computer science at university in Lubango.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** And how did you come to Kalandula Tech?
**NUNO:** In my last year, I applied for an internship here. I spent three months with the IT team — I learned a lot from Paulo and Mateus. Then I went back to Lubango and finished my degree in July. In October, there was a place in the IT team, and I got the job.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** And what are you doing now?
**NUNO:** I support the staff, and this month I'm working on the Mussulo Energy project. I'm responsible for the cables and computers on the second floor.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Very good. Why did you choose IT?
**NUNO:** When I was a child, my father's computer was always broken — he has a small shop. So I began to fix it. I was twelve!
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Ha! And what was the most difficult moment of your internship?
**NUNO:** The trip to Benguela. I was speaking English with a new client all day, and I was very nervous. But in the end it was fine.

### 3. Como se organiza a resposta (2 min)

[ECRÃ: quatro partes, numa linha do tempo]

| Parte | Tempo verbal | Exemplo do Nuno |
|---|---|---|
| 1. De onde é | past simple | I was born in Lubango in 2003, and I grew up there. |
| 2. Estudos | past simple | After secondary school, I began a degree in computer science. |
| 3. Experiência | past simple (e past continuous para contar um episódio) | I applied for an internship here. I spent three months with the IT team. |
| 4. Agora | present simple e continuous | I support the staff, and this month I'm working on the Mussulo Energy project. |

**PROF:** Four parts, in order: where you're from, your studies, your experience, and now. About one minute. Finish in the present — that's what the manager wants to know.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Don't tell everything. Choose three or four facts, and add one detail people remember: "I was twelve!"
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Mediação: os nomes dos cursos**

**PROF:** Your manager is from another country. How do you explain an Angolan course? Don't translate word for word. Explain it.

**PROF:** I did a technical secondary school course in IT. I have a degree in management.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (30 s)

**PROF:** When you change from past to present, make a small pause: "In October, I got a job here." Pause. "Now I'm working on…"
[PAUSA 4 s]

[PAUSA 15 s]
