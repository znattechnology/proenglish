# A2.1 · Unidade 4 — Comparing and choosing · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções e personagens: ver os [guiões da unidade 1 do A2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1 e das unidades 1 a 3 do A2. Do A1 o aluno já sabe comparar duas coisas (*cheaper, bigger, better, more expensive … than* — aula 8C), dizer o mais barato e o melhor (*the cheapest, the best*), e falar de quantidades com *some / any, How much / How many, a lot of* (unidade 6). Novo: *much / a bit* + comparativo (*much cheaper, a bit faster*); *less*; *(not) as … as*; superlativos com *in* e *of* (*the best in Luanda, the cheapest of the three*); *the worst*; *much / many* na negativa e na pergunta; *a few / a little*; *a / the* / sem artigo; recomendar (*I recommend…, The only disadvantage is…*).

**Não usar nesta unidade:** o *present perfect*; *will* e *going to* para previsões; *should / have to / must*; *could*; o condicional (*if*, *would* + verbo, excepto *would like*); *too / enough*; *used to*.

**Personagens:** **Nuno**, **Mateus**, **Sílvia**, **Paulo**, **Thandi**, **Rita**, **Ana**. Na 4E: **Paulo** e a irmã **Joana** (enfermeira; A1, unidade 4), **Fátima** (A2, unidade 2) e **Emma** (A2, unidade 2).

**A história continua:** semana de 23 a 27 de Novembro de 2026. Depois do corte de energia da unidade 2, o Sr. Baptista quer um gerador novo para o escritório (4A). Na segunda-feira, dia 23, a Kalandula Tech apresenta a rede nova à Rita; a instalação na Mussulo Energy começa a 7 de Dezembro (4C), e a Rita pede duas UPS (baterias que mantêm os servidores ligados quando a luz falta) para a sala dos servidores (4F).

## 4A — Faster, cheaper, better

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 4        Aula: 4A — Faster, cheaper, better        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue comparar duas opções com comparativos (revistos, com much
  e a bit), less e (not) as … as, e dar uma razão (Model B is much cheaper, but it isn't as quiet as
  Model A.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: comparativos (revistos: -er, more, irregulares better / worse); much / a bit +
  comparativo; less + adjectivo; as … as; not as … as
Palavras-alvo: generator, model, quiet, noisy, heavy, powerful, fuel, price
Reciclagem: comparativos (A1, 8C); past simple (A2, 2) — o corte de energia
Pronúncia: than /ðən/ e as /əz/ fracos: as quiet as /əz ˈkwaɪət əz/
Contexto: a Sílvia e o Mateus escolhem um gerador para o escritório da Kalandula Tech
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 4A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 4
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! In A1 you compared two things: cheaper, bigger, better. Today we go further: much cheaper, less expensive, and "as good as".

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a sala da Sílvia; dois catálogos de geradores, Model A e Model B, lado a lado]

**SÍLVIA:** Mateus, after the power cut, Mr Baptista wants a new generator for the office. Look: Model A and Model B.
**MATEUS:** OK. Which one is cheaper?
**SÍLVIA:** Model B. It's much cheaper — two million kwanzas less.
**MATEUS:** And is it as powerful as Model A?
**SÍLVIA:** Yes, it's as powerful as Model A. But it's a bit noisier, and it's heavier.
**MATEUS:** Noisier? That's a problem. The generator is next to the meeting room.
**SÍLVIA:** True. And Model A uses less fuel. So it's more expensive to buy, but it's less expensive every month.
**MATEUS:** So Model B isn't as good as Model A.
**SÍLVIA:** Not for us. I think Model A is better for the office.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Comparativos: revisão**

[ECRÃ: cheap → cheap**er** · noisy → nois**ier** · heavy → heav**ier** · powerful → **more** powerful · good → **better** · bad → **worse**]

**PROF:** You know this from A1. Short adjectives: -er. Adjectives with "y": -ier. Long adjectives: more. And two irregulars: better, worse.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Quanto mais: *much* e *a bit***

[ECRÃ: Model B is **much** cheaper. (muito mais barato) · It's **a bit** noisier. (um pouco mais barulhento)]

**PROF:** Much cheaper: a big difference. A bit noisier: a small difference.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✔ much cheaper ✘ very cheaper]
**PROF:** Not "very cheaper". Much cheaper.
[PAUSA 2 s]

***Less***

[ECRÃ: Model A uses **less** fuel. · It's **less expensive** every month. (= cheaper)]

**PROF:** Less is the opposite of more. Less expensive means cheaper.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***As … as***

[ECRÃ: Model B is **as powerful as** Model A. (=) · Model B **isn't as good as** Model A. (A é melhor)]

**PROF:** As powerful as: the same. Not as good as: Model A is better.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Tão potente como" → "as powerful as". "Não é tão bom como" → "isn't as good as".)

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In a sentence, "than" and "as" are short and weak. Listen: It's cheaper than Model A. It's as powerful as Model A.
[ECRÃ: than /ðən/ · as /əz/]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: Model B isn't as good as Model A.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: dois telemóveis — Phone X: 120 000 Kz, câmara boa, bateria de um dia · Phone Y: 85 000 Kz, câmara boa, bateria de dois dias]

**PROF:** Compare the two phones. Is Phone Y cheaper?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, it's much cheaper.]
**PROF:** Is the camera of Phone Y as good as the camera of Phone X?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, it's as good as Phone X's camera.]
**PROF:** And the battery?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: The battery of Phone Y is better. / Phone X's battery isn't as good.]

**PROF:** Now compare two things in your life: two cars, two shops, two jobs. Three sentences.
[PAUSA 15 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 2C):** What were you doing when the power went off last time?
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Great! Much cheaper, less expensive, as good as. Next lesson: the best of all.

## 4B — The best in town

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 4        Aula: 4B — The best in town        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue escolher e recomendar o melhor de um grupo com superlativos
  (the cheapest, the most beautiful, the best, the worst) com in e of (the best restaurant in Luanda,
  the cheapest of the three), e perguntar (What's the best place to…?).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: the + -est / the most + adjectivo longo; the best / the worst; in + lugar ou grupo
  (in Luanda, in the team); of + número (of the three); What's the best… ?
Palavras-alvo: view, beach, market, souvenir, fortress, crowded, beautiful, old, amazing, recommend
Reciclagem: present continuous para combinações (A2, 3A); comparativos (4A)
Pronúncia: the antes de vogal /ði/ (the oldest) e antes de consoante /ðə/ (the best)
Contexto: a Thandi quer conhecer Luanda no fim-de-semana e pede conselhos à Ana e ao Nuno
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 4B; pergunta 3 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: the best, the cheapest, the most beautiful. How to choose — and recommend — the best of a group.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a recepção da Kalandula Tech; a Thandi com um mapa de Luanda; a Ana e o Nuno]

**THANDI:** Ana, Nuno — I'm free this weekend. What's the best place to visit in Luanda?
**ANA:** For me, the most beautiful place is the Miradouro da Lua. The view is amazing — it's the best view near Luanda.
**NUNO:** And the Fortaleza de São Miguel. It's one of the oldest buildings in the city.
**THANDI:** Great. And the best beach?
**NUNO:** Mussulo. But on Sundays it's the most crowded beach in Luanda! Go on Saturday morning.
**THANDI:** And where can I buy souvenirs? I don't want the most expensive shop.
**ANA:** There are three big markets. Benfica is the cheapest of the three, and the most interesting.
**THANDI:** Perfect. And what's the worst thing about Luanda?
**ANA AND NUNO:** The traffic!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Superlativos**

[ECRÃ: cheap → **the** cheap**est** · old → **the** old**est** · crowded → **the most** crowded · beautiful → **the most** beautiful · good → **the best** · bad → **the worst**]

**PROF:** To compare three or more: "the" plus "-est", or "the most". And the irregulars: the best, the worst.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✔ the most beautiful ✘ the beautifulest ✘ the more beautiful]

***In* e *of***

[ECRÃ: the most crowded beach **in** Luanda · the oldest building **in** the city · the cheapest **of** the three markets]

**PROF:** In — a place or a group: in Luanda, in the city, in the team. Of — a number: of the three, of all.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "A praia mais cheia de Luanda" → "the most crowded beach **in** Luanda", e não "of Luanda".)

***One of the…***

[ECRÃ: It's **one of the oldest buildings** in the city.]

**PROF:** One of the oldest buildings — "buildings", plural.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Perguntar e recomendar**

[ECRÃ: **What's the best place to** visit? · **What's the best way to** get there? · **I recommend** the Miradouro da Lua.]

**PROF:** What's the best place to visit? I recommend the Miradouro da Lua.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Listen to "the". Before a consonant: the best, the cheapest. Before a vowel sound, it changes: the oldest, the interesting one.
[ECRÃ: the best /ðə/ · the oldest /ði/]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: It's the oldest building in the city.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Answer about your city.
**PROF:** What's the best restaurant in your city?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — The best restaurant in my city is … because …]
**PROF:** What's the most beautiful place in Angola, for you?
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** And what's the worst thing about your city?
[PAUSA 6 s]

**PROF:** Think of three colleagues. Who's the most organised of the three?
[PAUSA 6 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A2, 3A):** And you? What are you doing this weekend?
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: how much time, how many problems — quantities.

## 4C — How much time do we have?

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 4        Aula: 4C — How much time do we have?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue falar de quantidades com much / many / a lot of / a few /
  a little, com contáveis e incontáveis (We don't have much time. There are a few problems.), e usar
  a / the / sem artigo nos casos mais comuns.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: much + incontável e many + contável (perguntas e negativas); a lot of (afirmativas,
  com os dois); a few + contável / a little + incontável; a / an (primeira vez), the (já se sabe qual),
  sem artigo (em geral: Cables are expensive.)
Palavras-alvo: time, money, budget, cable, plug, problem, information, equipment, advice, left
Reciclagem: contáveis e incontáveis e How much / How many (A1, 6); past simple (A2, 2)
Pronúncia: a lot of /əlɒtəv/; a few /əˈfjuː/
Contexto: segunda-feira, dia 23, de manhã; o Mateus e o Nuno vêem o material para a instalação na Mussulo Energy
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 4C; perguntas 4 e 5 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! How much? How many? A few, a little, a lot. Today: talking about quantities at work.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o armazém da Kalandula Tech, segunda-feira de manhã; caixas abertas; o Mateus com uma lista]

**MATEUS:** OK, Nuno. The installation at Mussulo Energy starts on the seventh of December. We don't have much time. How many cables do we have?
**NUNO:** A lot of cables — about two hundred. But we only have a few plugs. Maybe thirty.
**MATEUS:** Thirty? We need a lot more. How much money is left in the budget?
**NUNO:** Not much. Sílvia says there's a little money for small things — about fifty thousand kwanzas.
**MATEUS:** OK, that's fine for the plugs. And the equipment from Johannesburg?
**NUNO:** It arrived on Friday. But there's a problem: the boxes don't have much information in English. The instructions are only in Chinese!
**MATEUS:** Ha! Thandi knows this equipment very well. She can help — she's always full of good advice.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Much* e *many***

[ECRÃ: **How many** cables? — contáveis (one cable, two cables) · **How much** money? — incontáveis (money, time, information, equipment, advice)]

**PROF:** Many with things you can count: cables, plugs, problems. Much with things you can't count: time, money, information.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Much and many are common in questions and negatives: How much time? We don't have much time.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***A lot of***

[ECRÃ: We have **a lot of** cables. · There's **a lot of** work.]

**PROF:** In positive sentences, people usually say "a lot of" — with both.
[PAUSA 3 s]

***A few* e *a little***

[ECRÃ: **a few** plugs (contável: alguns, poucos) · **a little** money (incontável: um pouco)]

**PROF:** A few plugs — count. A little money — don't count.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Palavras que não se contam em inglês**

[ECRÃ: ✘ informations ✘ equipments ✘ advices · ✔ a lot of information · ✔ some equipment · ✔ good advice · ✔ **a piece of** advice]

**PROF:** In Portuguese you say "informações", "equipamentos", "conselhos". In English, these are uncountable: information, equipment, advice. No "s"!
[PAUSA 4 s]
(PT: É um dos erros mais frequentes de quem fala português: "informations" e "equipments" não existem.)

***A / the* / sem artigo**

[ECRÃ: **a** problem (um, pela primeira vez) · **the** problem (já sabemos qual) · Cables are expensive. (em geral, sem artigo)]

**PROF:** "A" — the first time, one of many. "The" — we know which one. No article — things in general.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: Em português diz-se "Os cabos são caros"; em inglês, em geral, sem "the": "Cables are expensive.")

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** A lot of — fast and connected: "a lotta". A lot of cables.
[ECRÃ: a lot of /əlɒtəv/]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** A few. A little. A few plugs. A little money.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Much or many?
[ECRÃ: How … emails do you get every day?] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: How many emails…]
[ECRÃ: How … time do you spend in traffic?] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: How much time…]

**PROF:** Correct the mistake.
[ECRÃ: I need some informations.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: I need some information.]
[ECRÃ: The new equipments arrived.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: The new equipment arrived.]

**PROF:** Now answer.
**PROF:** How many emails do you get every day?
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** How much free time do you have this week?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Not much. / A lot. / A little, on Sunday.]

**PROF (reciclagem — A1, 6):** What do you usually have for breakfast? Use "some" or "a little".
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! Now practise with your tutor: compare two cities. Then: the words for prices and quality.

## 4D — Prices and quality

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 4        Aula: 4D — Prices and quality        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar as palavras de um orçamento (quote, price, discount,
  delivery time, warranty, after-sales service, reliable, good value), dizer os comparativos com /ə/
  fraco, e evitar os falsos amigos costume e exquisite.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 4
Gramática-alvo: a quote for + produto; a discount of + percentagem; the delivery time is + tempo; the
  warranty is + tempo
Palavras-alvo: supplier, quote, price, total, discount, delivery time, warranty, after-sales service,
  quality, reliable, good value (for money), budget, invoice
Reciclagem: comparativos e superlativos (4A, 4B); números grandes e kwanzas (A1, 6)
Pronúncia: /ə/ fraco em -er, than, as, of (cheaper than, as good as, a lot of)
Falsos amigos: costume (≠ costume); exquisite (≠ esquisito)
Contexto: um orçamento da PowerSafe Angola para duas UPS
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 4D; pergunta 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! When you buy for your company, you get quotes. Today: the words of a quote — and how to say that something is good value.

### 2. Um orçamento (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: um orçamento — "Supplier: PowerSafe Angola · Product: UPS 10 kVA (x2) · Price: 1 800 000 Kz each · Discount: 5% · Total: 3 420 000 Kz · Delivery time: 2 weeks · Warranty: 2 years · After-sales service: yes, in Luanda"]

**PROF:** This is a quote. A supplier sends a quote with the price before you buy.
**PROF:** Two UPS units — a UPS keeps the computers on when the power goes off. The price is one million, eight hundred thousand kwanzas each.
**PROF:** There's a discount of five per cent. The total is three million, four hundred and twenty thousand kwanzas.
**PROF:** The delivery time is two weeks. The warranty is two years: when it breaks in the first two years, they fix it for free.
**PROF:** And there's after-sales service in Luanda: a technician can come to the office.
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: **supplier** · **quote** · **price** · **discount** · **total** · **delivery time** · **warranty** · **after-sales service** · **invoice** (factura, depois de comprar)]

**PROF:** A quote is before you buy. An invoice is after you buy — you pay it.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 3. Qualidade e valor (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: **good quality** · **reliable** (não avaria) · **good value (for money)** (o preço é justo para a qualidade) · **within budget** (dentro do orçamento)]

**PROF:** Reliable: it works, it doesn't break. Good value: the price is right for the quality. It isn't always the cheapest!
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** And the budget is the money you have for something. Within budget: not more than that money.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia: /ə/ fraco (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** In comparisons, many small words are weak — a short, weak sound, like "uh".
[ECRÃ: cheap**er** th**a**n · **a**s good **a**s · a lot **of** · more reli**a**ble]
**PROF:** PowerSafe is cheaper than the other supplier.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** It's as good as the other one.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** It's good value for money.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: ✘ **costume** ≠ costume · costume (hábito) = **habit**, **custom** · costume = traje, fantasia]
**PROF:** A "costume" is special clothes — for a party, for a play. "Costume" in Portuguese is a habit.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "É costume pagarmos a 30 dias" → "We usually pay in 30 days." ou "It's our custom to pay in 30 days.")

[ECRÃ: ✘ **exquisite** ≠ esquisito · esquisito = **strange**, **weird** · exquisite = belíssimo, requintado]
**PROF:** "Exquisite" means very beautiful, very fine. "Esquisito" is "strange".
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Este orçamento é esquisito" → "This quote is strange.")

### 6. Prática e fecho (1 min)

**PROF:** Answer: In your company, who asks for quotes?
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** What's more important for you: the cheapest price, or good value? Why?
[PAUSA 8 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — 4B):** What's the most reliable car in Angola, for you?
[PAUSA 5 s]

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: real people compare phones, cars and cities.

## 4E — Real English: Which one is better?

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 4        Aula: 4E — Which one is better?        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue identificar, em três conversas a velocidade natural, a
  opção que cada pessoa escolhe e as razões.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 2
Língua: A1 e unidades 1–4 do A2, sem estruturas novas
Vozes: Paulo e Joana (angolanos), Fátima (angolana), Emma (britânica) e Thandi (sul-africana)
Velocidade: natural
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 4E; pergunta 7 do questionário
```

### 1. Apresentação (30 s)

**PROF:** Three conversations. In each one, people choose between two things. Listen for the choice — and the reasons. You hear them three times.

### 2. Primeira escuta: a ideia geral (3 min)

**PROF:** First: what is each person choosing?

**CONVERSA 1 — PAULO E JOANA**
**JOANA:** Paulo, I need a new phone. This one or that one?
**PAULO:** The black one is much cheaper. But the camera isn't as good.
**JOANA:** I don't take many photos. I need a good battery — I work nights at the hospital.
**PAULO:** Then the black one. The battery is better, too.
**JOANA:** Great. The black one, then.

**CONVERSA 2 — FÁTIMA**
**FÁTIMA:** For the bakery, I need a van for deliveries. I looked at two. The new one is more comfortable, and it uses less fuel, but it's three times more expensive. The second one is older, but the mechanic says it's very reliable. In the end, I chose the older one. I saved a lot of money, so I bought a new oven too!

**CONVERSA 3 — EMMA E THANDI**
**EMMA:** So, Thandi, Johannesburg or Luanda? Which city is better?
**THANDI:** Hmm. Luanda is more expensive — restaurants, flats, everything. Johannesburg is bigger, and the traffic is as bad as here!
**EMMA:** Ha! And the people?
**THANDI:** The people are friendly in both cities. But the beaches… Luanda is much better. Johannesburg doesn't have a beach!

[ECRÃ: Joana — phone · Fátima — van · Thandi — cities]

### 3. Segunda escuta: os pormenores (3 min)

1. Why does Joana need a good battery? (She works nights at the hospital. ✓ / She takes a lot of photos. / Her old phone is broken.)
2. Which phone does Joana choose? (the black one ✓ / the most expensive one / the one with the best camera)
3. Which van does Fátima choose? (the older one ✓ / the new one / neither)
4. What else did Fátima buy? (a new oven ✓ / a new phone / a second van)
5. What does Thandi say about the traffic? (It's as bad as in Johannesburg. ✓ / It's better than in Johannesburg. / There isn't much traffic.)
6. What's better in Luanda, for Thandi? (the beaches ✓ / the prices / the traffic)

*(Cada conversa repete-se; depois aparecem as perguntas, com 8 s para responder.)*

### 4. Terceira escuta: como disseram (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Listen and repeat.
**PROF:** The black one is much cheaper. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** The camera isn't as good. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** It's three times more expensive. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** The traffic is as bad as here! [PAUSA 3 s]

**PROF:** Did you notice? Joana says "This one or that one?" — in a shop, people point. You don't need the name of the product.
(PT: "This one / that one" evita repetir o nome do produto.)

### 5. A sua vez (1 min)

**PROF:** Now you. Compare two cities in Angola you know. Which one is better for you, and why?
[PAUSA 20 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — … is bigger than … , but … is much quieter. … isn't as expensive as … For me, … is better because …]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: you compare two quotes and recommend one to your manager.

## 4F — Work & Life: Two quotes

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 4        Aula: 4F — Two quotes        Tipo: Work & Life
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue comparar dois orçamentos de fornecedores e recomendar um à
  chefia, com duas razões e uma desvantagem (I recommend PowerSafe. It's a bit more expensive, but the
  warranty is longer and the delivery is faster.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 4 (e 1, 2, 3)
Gramática-alvo: toda a da unidade; I recommend…; There are two big advantages. First,…; The only
  disadvantage is…; It's within budget.
Palavras-alvo: advantage, disadvantage, recommend, recommendation, within budget
Reciclagem: past simple (A2, 2); present continuous para combinações (A2, 3)
```
