# A2.1 · Unidade 1 — People at work · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. As convenções são as do A1 ([guiões da unidade 1 do A1](../../a1/unidade-01/guioes.md)): **PROF:** o que o professor diz; **(PT: …)** nota em português no ecrã, não dita; **[ECRÃ: …]**; **[PAUSA n s]**; nomes em maiúsculas são personagens com voz própria.

**No A2, o professor explica em inglês simples.** As notas em português ficam só para os contrastes com o português, os falsos amigos e os erros típicos. As legendas do vídeo são em inglês, com português disponível.

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1 (unidades 1 a 10). Novo: o contraste *present simple / present continuous* com *these days, this month, at the moment, currently*; os verbos de estado; *What's … like? / What does … look like?*; adjectivos de personalidade e de aparência; *be responsible for, be in charge of, report to, deal with, look after*; *'s* e *s'*.

**Não usar nesta unidade:** o *present perfect* (*I've worked*), o *past continuous*, *will*, *should*, *have to* e *must* (vêm nas unidades 2, 3, 5, 6 e 7 do A2).

**Personagens**

- **Nuno Kapinga**, 23 anos, de Lubango. Foi estagiário no A1 e acabou a universidade em Julho; agora é **técnico júnior de informática** da Kalandula Tech.
- **Mateus**, engenheiro da Kalandula Tech (A1), agora **chefe de equipa** do projecto novo.
- **Sílvia Neto**, contabilista; **Paulo Mendes**, técnico de informática; **Sr. Baptista**, director (todos do A1).
- **Thandi Mokoena**, engenheira sul-africana de Joanesburgo (A1, unidade 2). A empresa dela fornece o equipamento de rede do projecto; está três meses em Luanda.
- **Rita Fonseca**, portuguesa, de Lisboa, directora de informática da Mussulo Energy (a empresa cliente, A1).
- **João Lumbo**, da Mussulo Energy (A1, unidade 9).
- **Arjun Mehta**, programador indiano, de Pune, trabalha no projecto à distância (só na 1E).

**O projecto:** a Kalandula Tech está a instalar uma rede nova e um sistema de pagamentos na Mussulo Energy. Começa nesta unidade e acompanha o A2.1.

## 1A — What do you do? What are you doing?

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 1        Aula: 1A — What do you do? What are you doing?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue descrever o seu trabalho habitual com o present simple e o
  trabalho deste período com o present continuous (I usually fix computers, but this month I'm working
  on a big project.), escolhendo o tempo pelas palavras de tempo, e perguntar a outra pessoa.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: present simple (hábitos, factos, trabalho permanente) / present continuous (agora e
  período temporário); these days, this month, this week, at the moment, currently; usually, every
  day, always; posição dos advérbios de frequência (antes do verbo, depois de be) e das expressões
  de tempo (no fim ou no princípio)
Palavras-alvo: junior, project, network, install, fix, support, prepare, accounts, annual, currently,
  these days, still
Reciclagem: present simple e frequência (A1, 3 e 5); present continuous (A1, 9); past simple (A1, 10)
Pronúncia: I'm working / we're installing (contracções); -ing sem vogal a mais no fim
Contexto: a copa da Kalandula Tech; primeiro dia do Nuno como funcionário
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 1A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 1
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello, and welcome to A2! You know a lot of English now. In this level, you speak more: longer answers, more details. Today: your job — what you usually do, and what you're doing these days.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a copa da Kalandula Tech, 8h15. A Sílvia faz café; o Nuno entra com um crachá novo. Título: "Nuno — first day as an employee"]

**SÍLVIA:** Nuno! Welcome back! So you're working here now?
**NUNO:** Yes! I'm a junior IT technician. I started today.
**SÍLVIA:** Congratulations! And what do you do, exactly?
**NUNO:** I support the staff on the phone, and I fix computers. That's my normal job. But this month I'm working on a big project with Mateus. We're installing a new network at Mussulo Energy.
**SÍLVIA:** Mussulo Energy? They're a big client!
**NUNO:** I know! And you? Are you still in accounts?
**SÍLVIA:** Yes, I am. I usually check the payments and pay the suppliers. But these days I'm preparing the annual accounts, so I'm working late every day.
**NUNO:** Every day? That's hard.
**SÍLVIA:** It's only for two weeks. In December I'm going to take a holiday!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Dois presentes**

[ECRÃ: duas colunas]

| Present simple: always, usually, every day | Present continuous: now, these days, this month |
|---|---|
| I **support** the staff on the phone. | This month I**'m working** on a big project. |
| I usually **check** the payments. | These days I**'m preparing** the annual accounts. |
| Kalandula Tech **installs** networks. | We**'re installing** a new network at Mussulo Energy. |

**PROF:** The present simple is for your normal work: what you usually do, every day, every week. The present continuous is for now, and for this period: this week, this month, these days. It's temporary — it stops.
[PAUSA 2 s]
(PT: Em português pode dizer-se "este mês trabalho num projecto grande", no presente simples. Em inglês, o que é temporário — esta semana, este mês — vai sempre para o present continuous: "This month I'm working on a big project.")

[ECRÃ: palavras de tempo — present simple: **usually, always, every day, on Mondays** · present continuous: **now, at the moment, today, this week, this month, these days, currently**]

**PROF:** Look at the time words. They help you choose.

**Perguntas**

[ECRÃ: **What do you do?** — o seu trabalho (profissão, tarefas) · **What are you doing?** — agora · **What are you working on this month?** — o projecto deste período]

**PROF:** What do you do? — I'm an IT technician. I fix computers. What are you working on this month? — I'm working on a new network.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**A ordem das palavras**

[ECRÃ: I **usually** check the payments. · She's **always** busy. · I work late **every day**. · **These days** I'm working late.]

**PROF:** Usually, always, never: before the verb, but after "be". Every day, these days: at the end, or at the start.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Listen: I'm working. We're installing. She's preparing. When we speak, we usually say "I'm", not "I am".
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** And the end of "-ing": working — not "workingue". Stop at the "ng" sound.
[ECRÃ: work**ing** /ŋ/ — sem vogal no fim]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: This month I'm working on a big project.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Choose the tense. Say the full sentence.
[ECRÃ: Nuno usually … (fix) computers.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Nuno usually fixes computers.]
[ECRÃ: This month he … (work) on a big project.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: This month he's working on a big project.]
[ECRÃ: Sílvia … (prepare) the annual accounts these days.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Sílvia is preparing the annual accounts these days.]

**PROF:** Now you. Answer in two sentences: one normal, one temporary.
**PROF:** What do you do at work?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I'm a … I usually … / I don't work at the moment. I'm studying …]
**PROF:** And what are you working on these days?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — These days I'm … / This month I'm preparing …]

**PROF (reciclagem — A1, unidade 10):** When did you start your job?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I started in 2022. / I started two years ago.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Great start! Two presents: what you usually do, and what you're doing now. Next: some verbs never take "-ing". Which ones?

## 1B — I know, I need, I want

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 1        Aula: 1B — I know, I need, I want        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar os verbos de estado mais comuns (know, understand,
  need, want, like, love, hate, remember, mean, think = opinião, have = possuir) só no present simple,
  mesmo quando falam de agora (I need the password now.), e corrigir "I'm knowing" e "I'm needing".
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: verbos de estado no present simple; I don't understand / Do you know…?; think
  (opinião: I think it's good) e have (possuir: I have a car) no simple; have nas expressões de acção
  no continuous (I'm having lunch / a meeting)
Palavras-alvo: password, access, remember, mean, understand, plan, experience, laptop, server room
Reciclagem: present continuous (1A); can para pedidos (A1, 8)
Pronúncia: know /nəʊ/ (o k não se diz); I don't know /aɪ dəʊnəʊ/ em fala ligada
Contexto: o gabinete do Mateus; ele explica ao Nuno a primeira tarefa do projecto
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 1B; pergunta 3 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Last lesson: two presents. Today, a group of verbs that don't use "-ing" — not even now.

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o gabinete do Mateus; um mapa da rede da Mussulo Energy no ecrã do portátil]

**MATEUS:** OK, Nuno. This is the plan for Mussulo Energy. Do you understand it?
**NUNO:** Yes, I understand. But I need access to the server room.
**MATEUS:** Right. You need a card. Ask Ana at reception. Do you know Thandi Mokoena?
**NUNO:** No, I don't know her. Who is she?
**MATEUS:** She's an engineer from Johannesburg. Her company sells us the network equipment. She's working with us on this project. She has a lot of experience.
**NUNO:** Great. And what do I do first?
**MATEUS:** Check the cables on the second floor. Here's the password for the laptop. Do you remember the old password?
**NUNO:** No, I don't remember it, sorry.
**MATEUS:** No problem. It's on the paper. And Nuno — I think you're going to like this project.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Verbos de estado**

[ECRÃ: **know · understand · remember · mean · think** (= opinião) · **want · need** · **like · love · hate** · **have** (= possuir)]

**PROF:** These verbs talk about what you know, what you want, what you like, what you have. They are states, not actions. We use them in the present simple — even now, at this moment.
[PAUSA 2 s]

[ECRÃ: ✔ I **need** access now. ✘ I'm needing access. · ✔ I **don't understand**. ✘ I'm not understanding. · ✔ Do you **know** Thandi? ✘ Are you knowing Thandi?]

**PROF:** I need access now — not "I'm needing". I don't understand — not "I'm not understanding".
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: Em português "estou a perceber" é normal; em inglês, "I understand".)

**Duas excepções úteis**

[ECRÃ: **think** — I **think** it's a good plan. (opinião → simple) · I'm **thinking** about the problem. (acção na cabeça → continuous)]
[ECRÃ: **have** — She **has** a lot of experience. I **have** a laptop. (possuir → simple) · I'm **having** lunch / a meeting. (acção → continuous)]

[ECRÃ: **like** — ✔ I **like** my job. ✘ I'm liking my job. · ✔ I like work**ing** with people. (A1: like + -ing)]

**PROF:** Careful with "like". "I'm liking my job" is wrong. But "I like working with people" is right — the "-ing" is on the second verb, as in A1.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**PROF:** "I think it's good" is your opinion: simple. "I'm having lunch" is an action: you can use the continuous. But "I have a car": simple.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Perguntas e respostas**

[ECRÃ: Do you know her? — No, I don't know her. · What does "password" mean? — It means "palavra-passe". · Do you remember the password? — No, I don't remember it.]

**PROF:** What does it mean? — It means… This question is very useful in meetings.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Know: the "k" is silent. I know. I don't know.
[ECRÃ: **k**now /nəʊ/ — o k não se diz]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Fast: I don't know. I don't know.
[ECRÃ: I don't know /aɪ dəʊnəʊ/ — o t quase desaparece]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I don't know her. What does it mean? I don't remember it.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Correct the sentence. Say it right.
[ECRÃ: I'm needing a new laptop.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: I need a new laptop.]
[ECRÃ: Are you understanding the plan?] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Do you understand the plan?]
[ECRÃ: She's having two children.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: She has two children.]
[ECRÃ: I'm thinking it's a good idea.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: I think it's a good idea.]

**PROF:** Now answer.
**PROF:** What do you need for your work this week?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: I need a new password / more time / a printer…]
**PROF:** Do you know anyone in another country? Who?
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** What do you think of your job? Say two sentences.
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: I think it's interesting, but I'm very busy these days.]

**PROF (reciclagem — A1, unidade 8):** You need the password. Ask your manager politely.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Can I have the password, please? / Can you give me the password, please?]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! I know, I need, I want, I like — not "I'm knowing", not "I'm liking". Next lesson: describing people. What's your manager like?

## 1C — What's she like?

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 1        Aula: 1C — What's she like?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue descrever um colega pela personalidade e pela aparência
  e distinguir as perguntas What's she like? (personalidade) e What does she look like? (aparência).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: What's he / she like? — He's friendly and organised.; What does he / she look like?
  — She's tall. She has short hair. She wears glasses.; adjectivo + nome (a helpful colleague); very, quite,
  a bit (a bit só com coisas negativas); wear glasses; 's e s' (my manager's office, my colleagues' names)
Palavras-alvo: friendly, helpful, hard-working, organised, patient, reliable, confident, shy, serious,
  funny, quiet, tall, short, slim, beard, wear glasses, long / short / curly hair, in his / her forties
Reciclagem: have / has (A1, 4); be no passado (A1, 10); verbos de estado (1B)
Pronúncia: acento em adjectivos de duas e três sílabas (FRIENDly, HELPful, ORganised, reLIable,
  CONfident)
Contexto: o Nuno espera a Thandi na recepção da Mussulo Energy e não a conhece; liga à Sílvia
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 1C; perguntas 4, 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today we describe people: what they look like, and what they're like. Two different questions!

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a recepção da Mussulo Energy, 9h15; o Nuno com um papel "THANDI MOKOENA" e o telemóvel ao ouvido]

**NUNO:** Sílvia, I'm at reception at Mussulo Energy. I'm waiting for Thandi, but I don't know her. What does she look like?
**SÍLVIA:** She's tall and slim. She has short black hair, and she wears glasses. She's in her forties.
**NUNO:** OK… And what's she like? Is she serious?
**SÍLVIA:** In meetings, a bit. But she's very friendly and really helpful. And she's very organised — she always arrives early.
**NUNO:** Early? It's nine fifteen, and she isn't here!
**SÍLVIA:** Ha! Then it's the Luanda traffic. Oh, and she's quite funny. You're going to like her.
**NUNO:** Wait… I think that's her. Tall, glasses… and she's smiling at my paper!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Duas perguntas**

[ECRÃ: **What does she look like?** → aparência: She's tall. She wears glasses. · **What's she like?** → personalidade: She's friendly and organised.]

**PROF:** What does she look like? — the body, the face, the clothes. What's she like? — the person: friendly, serious, helpful.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: Em português, "como é ela?" serve para as duas. Em inglês, "What's she like?" é a pessoa por dentro; "What does she look like?" é o aspecto.)

**Personalidade**

[ECRÃ: palavras com imagem — friendly · helpful · hard-working · organised · patient · reliable (= you can trust her) · confident · shy · serious · funny · quiet]

**PROF:** Reliable: you can trust this person. She does what she says.
[PAUSA 2 s]

**Aparência**

[ECRÃ: She's **tall / short / slim**. · He has **a beard**. · She **wears glasses**. · He has **short / long / curly hair**. · She's **in her forties** (40–49).]

**PROF:** Be: tall, short, slim. Have: a beard, short hair. Wear: glasses.
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Quanto**

[ECRÃ: **very** friendly · **really** helpful · **quite** funny · **a bit** serious]

**PROF:** A bit — only for negative things: a bit serious, a bit shy. Not "a bit friendly".
[PAUSA 3 s]

***'s* e *s'***

[ECRÃ: my **manager's** office (um chefe) · my **colleagues'** names (vários colegas) · **Thandi's** company]

**PROF:** One person: 's. Many people, with "s": just the apostrophe. You hear the same sound.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Listen to the stress: FRIENDly. HELPful. ORganised. reLIable. CONfident.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: She's very friendly and really helpful.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Listen to the question. Is it about personality or appearance?
**PROF:** What does your manager look like?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — He's tall. He has a beard and short hair.]
**PROF:** And what's your manager like?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — He's very hard-working and quite patient, but a bit serious.]

**PROF:** Describe your best colleague in three sentences: one about appearance, two about personality.
[PAUSA 15 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — A1, unidade 4):** Does your colleague have children?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, she has two children. / No, he doesn't.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Now you can describe people. Practise with your tutor — tell them about your job these days. Then: words for jobs and responsibilities.

## 1D — Jobs and responsibilities

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 1        Aula: 1D — Jobs and responsibilities        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer de que é responsável, com quem trabalha e a quem
  responde (I'm responsible for the network. I report to Mateus. I deal with the clients.), dizer com
  o acento certo os cognatos mais comuns do trabalho e evitar os falsos amigos sympathetic e actually.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: be responsible for + nome / -ing; be in charge of; report to; deal with; look after;
  work with / for
Palavras-alvo: manager, team leader, staff, department, sales, finance, human resources (HR),
  customer service, responsible, in charge, report, deal with, look after, train, organise, develop,
  technology, engineer, analyst, administrator
Reciclagem: present simple (A1, 3); possessivos (A1, 4)
Pronúncia: acento de palavra em cognatos (reSPONsible, techNOLogy, deVELopment, engiNEER,
  ANalyst, adMINistrator, MANager)
Falsos amigos: sympathetic (≠ simpático); actually (≠ actualmente)
Contexto: o organograma da Kalandula Tech
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 1D; perguntas 7, 8 e 9 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: how to explain your job in a meeting — what you're responsible for, who you work with, and who your boss is.

### 2. O organograma (2 min)

[ECRÃ: o organograma da Kalandula Tech: Sr. Baptista (director) → Mateus (team leader, projects) → Paulo, Nuno; Sílvia (finance); Ana (reception)]

**PROF:** Look at Kalandula Tech. Mr Baptista is the director. He's in charge of the company.
**PROF:** Mateus is a team leader. He's responsible for the projects. Paulo and Nuno report to Mateus.
**PROF:** Sílvia works in finance. She deals with the payments.
**PROF:** Ana works at reception. She looks after the visitors.
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: departamentos — **sales · finance · human resources (HR) · customer service · IT**]

### 3. Expressões (3 min)

| Expressão | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| **be responsible for** | I'm responsible for the network. / … for training new staff. |
| **be in charge of** | She's in charge of the finance department. |
| **report to** | I report to Mateus. (= Mateus é o meu chefe) |
| **deal with** | I deal with the clients' problems. |
| **look after** | She looks after the visitors. |
| **work with / for** | I work with Paulo. I work for Kalandula Tech. |

**PROF:** After "for", a noun or "-ing": responsible for the network, responsible for training.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Work for a company. Work with people.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia: o acento dos cognatos (2 min)

**PROF:** Many work words look like Portuguese. But the stress is often in a different place.
[ECRÃ: responsável → re**SPON**sible · tecnologia → tech**NOL**ogy · desenvolvimento → de**VEL**opment · engenheiro → engi**NEER** · analista → **AN**alyst · administrador → ad**MIN**istrator · gestor → **MAN**ager]
**PROF:** Listen and repeat. reSPONsible.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** techNOLogy.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** deVELopment.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** engiNEER.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** ANalyst.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** adMINistrator.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** MANager.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Falsos amigos (1 min)

[ECRÃ: ✘ **sympathetic** ≠ simpático · ✔ simpático = **friendly, nice**]
**PROF:** Sympathetic is not "simpático". Sympathetic means you understand a person's problem. Simpático is "friendly" or "nice".
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: ✘ **actually** ≠ actualmente · ✔ actualmente = **at the moment, currently, these days** · actually = na verdade]
**PROF:** Actually means "in fact". "Actualmente" is "at the moment" or "currently".
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: "Actualmente trabalho num projecto" → "Currently, I'm working on a project.")

### 6. Prática (1 min)

**PROF:** Answer.
**PROF:** Who do you report to?
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** What are you responsible for?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I'm responsible for… / I deal with… / I look after…]

**PROF (reciclagem — 1A):** And currently? What are you working on?
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 7. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! Next: real people introduce themselves in an international meeting. Listen to their accents.

## 1E — Real English: My new team

```
Curso: A2.1        Unidade: 1        Aula: 1E — My new team        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue identificar, em quatro apresentações numa videochamada
  internacional a velocidade natural, a função, uma responsabilidade e uma característica de cada
  pessoa, com sotaques diferentes.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 2, 3
Língua: A1 e unidade 1 do A2, sem estruturas novas
Vozes: Rita Fonseca (portuguesa), Thandi Mokoena (sul-africana), Arjun Mehta (indiano), João Lumbo
  (angolano); o Mateus abre a chamada
Velocidade: natural
Duração: 9 min
Verificação: Lab 1E; pergunta 10 do questionário
```

### 1. Apresentação (30 s)

**PROF:** It's the first video call of the Mussulo Energy project. Four people introduce themselves. They're from four countries — listen to the different accents. You hear it three times.

### 2. Primeira escuta: a ideia geral (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** First: who works for Mussulo Energy?

[ECRÃ: uma videochamada com cinco janelas]

**MATEUS:** Good morning, everyone. Let's start with a quick round of introductions. Rita, can you start?

**RITA:** Sure. Hi, everyone. I'm Rita Fonseca. I'm Portuguese, from Lisbon, but I live in Luanda now. I'm the IT manager at Mussulo Energy, so I'm in charge of all the systems there. These days I'm spending a lot of time on this project. People say I'm very organised… and a bit impatient!
