# A2.2 · Revisão 2 (unidades 6 a 10) · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções e personagens: ver os [guiões da unidade 1 do A2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que a revisão pode usar:** tudo do A1 e do A2, e nada de novo, com uma excepção: uma estratégia de exame, corrigir-se a si próprio (*Sorry, I mean…*). As estratégias anteriores mantêm-se (*Can you repeat that, please? Let me think. It's a kind of… That's a good question. Well… Do you mean…?*).

**Não usar:** o 2.º condicional e *would* hipotético; a voz passiva; orações relativas (com ou sem pronome: *a job you like*); *used to*; o *past perfect*; o *present perfect continuous*; *too / enough*.

**Personagens:** **Nuno** e **Thandi** (R2A); **Lurdes Cassoma**, a aluna-modelo dos exames (contabilista num banco, de Benguela, casada, um filho e uma filha), agora no exame do A2.2 (R2B).

**Quando:** sexta-feira, 11 de Junho de 2027, o dia em que a Lerato liga aos candidatos (10F).

## R2A — Review: experiences, news and problems

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: Revisão 2        Aula: R2A — Review: experiences, news and problems        Tipo: Revisão
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dar e pedir novidades e falar de experiências numa conversa
  nova, escolhendo entre o past simple e o present perfect (ever, just, already / yet, for / since) e
  entre will, going to e o present continuous, e corrigir os 10 erros mais comuns do A2.2.
Objectivos de unidade que revê: U6–U10, todos
Gramática revista: present perfect (experiência, just, already / yet, for / since) e past simple;
  will (previsões e decisões), going to, present continuous (combinações); 1.º condicional;
  could / would em pedidos; verbo + to / -ing; good at; advérbios de modo
Situação nova: a Thandi liga ao Nuno quando sabe que ele ficou com o lugar
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: laboratórios R2-1 e R2-2; conversa de ensaio; questionário da revisão
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! You've finished all the units of A2. Today: no new grammar. We put it together — experiences, news, plans and problems — in one conversation.

### 2. Situação: a boa notícia (3 min)

[ECRÃ: sexta-feira, 11 de Junho, ao fim da tarde; videochamada: o Nuno em casa, em Luanda; a Thandi no escritório, em Joanesburgo]

**THANDI:** Nuno! I've just heard the news from Lerato. Congratulations!
**NUNO:** Thank you! She called me this morning. I'm starting on the first of July.
**THANDI:** Fantastic. How long have you worked at Kalandula Tech now?
**NUNO:** Since October — about eight months. And now a new team!
**THANDI:** Have you told your family yet?
**NUNO:** Yes, I've already called my mother in Lubango. She cried! But I haven't told Paulo yet. He's on holiday this week.
**THANDI:** Ha! And the training here in Johannesburg? Have you booked your flight yet?
**NUNO:** Not yet. Lerato's going to send me the details on Monday. I think I'll fly on the fifth of July.
**THANDI:** Have you ever been to Johannesburg in winter?
**NUNO:** No, never. I went there in January, and it was summer — thirty degrees!
**THANDI:** Well, in July it's very cold. I think it'll be about five degrees in the morning.
**NUNO:** Five degrees! OK, I'll buy a warm coat this weekend.
**THANDI:** Good idea. And if there's a problem at the hotel, call me.
**NUNO:** I will. Thanks, Thandi!

### 3. Os tempos do A2.2 lado a lado (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: seis caixas, cada uma com as palavras-chave e uma frase da conversa]

| Para… | Língua | Frase da conversa |
|---|---|---|
| uma novidade (*just*) | *present perfect* | I**'ve just heard** the news. |
| o que já está feito ou ainda não (*already / yet*) | *present perfect* | I**'ve already called** my mother. I **haven't told** Paulo **yet**. |
| há quanto tempo (*How long…? for / since*) | *present perfect* | How long **have** you **worked** here? — Since October. |
| uma experiência, sem data (*ever / never*) | *present perfect* | **Have** you **ever been** to Johannesburg in winter? — No, never. |
| o quando e o como (*in January, last week*) | *past simple* | I **went** there in January, and it **was** summer. |
| o futuro | *present continuous* (combinação) · *going to* (intenção) · *will* (previsão e decisão) · *if* + presente, *will* | I**'m starting** on the first of July. Lerato**'s going to send** me the details. I think it**'ll be** five degrees. I**'ll buy** a coat. **If** there**'s** a problem, call me. |

**PROF:** Look at the time words. "Just", "already", "yet", "since", "ever": present perfect. "In January", "last week": past simple.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** And the future: an arrangement — I'm starting on the first of July. A plan — Lerato's going to send the details. A prediction — I think it'll be cold. A decision now — I'll buy a coat.
[PAUSA 4 s]

**PROF:** Now you. Complete with your life.
[ECRÃ: I've just … ] [PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: I've already … , but I haven't … yet.] [PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: I've worked / studied … since … ] [PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: I've never been to … ] [PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Next month I think I'll … ] [PAUSA 6 s]

### 4. Os erros mais comuns do A2.2 (2 min)

**PROF:** Here are the ten most common mistakes in Units 6 to 10. Look at the wrong sentence, and say the right one.

[ECRÃ: um erro de cada vez; a frase certa aparece depois da pausa]

| Errado | Certo | Unidade |
|---|---|---|
| ~~I've been to Lubango last year.~~ | I went to Lubango last year. | 6 |
| ~~Have you ever went to Lagos?~~ | Have you ever been to Lagos? | 6 |
| ~~I work here since 2020.~~ | I've worked here since 2020. | 7 |
| ~~I haven't finished yet it.~~ | I haven't finished it yet. | 7 |
| ~~If it will rain, we'll stay at home.~~ | If it rains, we'll stay at home. | 8 |
| ~~It's not work.~~ | It doesn't work. | 8 |
| ~~Could you to help me?~~ | Could you help me? | 9 |

*(Para cada linha: o professor lê a frase errada com tom de dúvida, [PAUSA 4 s], e diz a certa.)*

(PT: Os três primeiros são os mais frequentes: com uma data no passado ("last year"), o *past simple*; com "since" ou "for" até agora, o *present perfect*.)

### 5. A sua vez: as suas novidades (1 min 30 s)

[PAUSA 8 s]
**PROF:** Have you ever travelled for work? Where did you go?

[PAUSA 12 s]

## R2B — Getting ready for the A2.2 speaking exam

```
Curso: A2.2        Unidade: Revisão 2        Aula: R2B — Getting ready for the A2.2 speaking exam        Tipo: Revisão
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue descrever as três partes do exame oral do A2.2, sabe o que é
  avaliado e usa uma estratégia nova: corrigir-se a si próprio (Sorry, I mean…).
Objectivos de unidade que revê: U6–U10, todos
Gramática revista: toda a do A2
Língua nova (bloco fixo): Sorry, I mean…
Situação: a Lurdes faz o exame de demonstração do A2.2
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: conversa de ensaio; exame oral do A2.2
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Soon you take your A2.2 speaking exam — the last exam of A2. Today you see how it works, you learn one new strategy, and you practise every part.

### 2. Como é o exame (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: três blocos]

| Parte | O quê | Tempo |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Interview | Experiências, novidades e há quanto tempo (*Have you ever…? What's new at work? How long have you…?*) | cerca de 2 minutos |
| 2. Explain | Como funciona uma coisa do seu trabalho, o que faz quando não funciona, e o ponto de situação de um trabalho | cerca de 2 minutos |
| 3. Situation | Uma situação sorteada: um problema no hotel, uma mala perdida, uma chamada para o apoio técnico, uma reclamação, ou um cliente insatisfeito | cerca de 3 minutos |

**PROF:** It's a conversation with the examiner, like in A2.1. It's a bit longer: about seven or eight minutes.
(PT: Como no A2.1, é uma conversa com o examinador. É um pouco mais longa: sete a oito minutos.)

**PROF:** In the situation, the examiner has a problem with your first answer. Stay calm, and give a reason or a new solution.
(PT: Na situação, o examinador põe uma dificuldade à primeira resposta. Mantenha a calma e dê uma razão ou outra solução.)

**PROF:** The rules are the same as before: the recording is your choice, the exam opens when you finish most of the A2.2 lessons, and when you don't pass, you can try again a week later.
(PT: As regras mantêm-se: a gravação é opcional (com ela, a pronúncia conta), o exame abre com 80% das aulas do A2.2 feitas, e pode repetir uma semana depois.)

### 3. O que é avaliado (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: cinco ícones]

| Competência | No A2.2, quer dizer… |
|---|---|
| Task — a tarefa | Responde com detalhe, explica com passos claros, e resolve a situação, incluindo a dificuldade do examinador. |
| Grammar — a gramática | Escolhe entre o *past simple* e o *present perfect*; usa *will, going to*, o 1.º condicional e pedidos com *could*. |
| Vocabulary — o vocabulário | As palavras das unidades 6 a 10: viagens, novidades, tecnologia, serviço ao cliente, competências. |
| Fluency — a fluência | Respostas ligadas (*first, then, if, so, because, but*), sem parar muito. |
| Pronunciation — a pronúncia | Os outros percebem-no. (Só com a gravação.) |

**PROF:** In A2.1, you told a story. In A2.2, you also explain, and you solve problems with other people.
(PT: No A2.1, contava uma história; no A2.2, também explica e resolve problemas com outra pessoa.)

### 4. Uma estratégia nova: corrigir-se (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: já conhece — **Can you repeat that, please?** · **Let me think.** · **It's a kind of…** · **That's a good question. Well…** · **Do you mean…?** · nova — **Sorry, I mean…**]

**PROF:** You know five strategies. Here's a new one: you make a mistake, and you hear it. Say: Sorry, I mean… — and say it again, correctly.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: "I've been to Lisbon last year — sorry, I mean, I went to Lisbon last year."]
**PROF:** I've been to Lisbon last year — sorry, I mean, I went to Lisbon last year.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: "The meeting is on Tuesday — sorry, I mean, on Thursday."]
**PROF:** It works for facts too: the meeting is on Tuesday — sorry, I mean, on Thursday.
[PAUSA 3 s]
(PT: Corrigir-se não baixa a nota. Mostra que conhece a regra.)

### 5. Um exame de demonstração (3 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Now watch Lurdes again. Now she's at the end of A2.

[ECRÃ: "Practice exam A2.2 — Lurdes"; ícone do examinador]

**EXAMINADOR:** Hello, Lurdes. Welcome to your A2.2 speaking exam. Have you ever travelled for work?
**LURDES:** Yes, I have. I've been to Lisbon twice. The first time was in 2019, for a training course, and the second time was last year, for a conference.
**EXAMINADOR:** What's new at work?
**LURDES:** We've just moved to a new office in Talatona. And I've finished the half-year report — sorry, I mean, I've almost finished it. I haven't checked the last page yet.
**EXAMINADOR:** How long have you worked at the bank?
**LURDES:** I've worked there since 2014, so for thirteen years.
**EXAMINADOR:** Thank you. Now please explain something. Tell me about a program or a machine at your work. What is it for, and how does it work?
**LURDES:** We use a program for the clients' payments. First, you log in with your card. Then you choose the client and check the amount. If the amount is over one million kwanzas, the system sends the payment to my manager.
**EXAMINADOR:** What happens if it doesn't work?
**LURDES:** If it stops, we call the IT help desk. If they can't fix it quickly, we'll use the old system, on paper.
**EXAMINADOR:** Now a situation. I'm a client of your bank. I ordered a new card three weeks ago, and it hasn't arrived yet!
**LURDES:** I'm very sorry to hear that. Could I have your account number, please?
**EXAMINADOR:** It's four, four, eight, one.
**LURDES:** Thank you. So, you ordered the card three weeks ago, and it hasn't arrived. Is that right?
**EXAMINADOR:** Yes. And I need it!
**LURDES:** I see there's a delay. I'll order a new card today, and you can collect it here by Friday.
**EXAMINADOR:** Friday? But I'm travelling to Namibia on Thursday!
**LURDES:** Then I'll give you a temporary card today. You can use it in Namibia, too. And I'll call you when the new card arrives.
**EXAMINADOR:** OK. Thank you, Lurdes. The exam is finished. You can press the finish button now.

**PROF:** Look at what Lurdes does well.
[ECRÃ: lista com vistos]
**PROF:** She chooses the right tense: I've been to Lisbon twice, but the first time was in 2019. She explains in steps: first, then, if. She corrects herself: "sorry, I mean". And in the situation, she says sorry, asks for the details, repeats them, and when the examiner says "Friday? But I'm travelling!", she finds a new solution.
(PT: Escolhe o tempo certo, explica por passos, corrige-se e, na situação, pede desculpa, pede os pormenores, repete-os e, perante a dificuldade, encontra outra solução.)

### 6. Pratique cada parte (1 min)

**PROF:** Your turn. Part one: What's new at work, or in your studies?
[PAUSA 12 s]
**PROF:** Part two: Tell me about a program or a machine at your work. How does it work?
[PAUSA 20 s]
[ECRÃ: First, you … Then you … If you … , it …]
**PROF:** Part three: I'm a shop assistant. You bought a phone here last week, and it doesn't charge. "Good morning. How can I help you?"
[PAUSA 15 s]
[ECRÃ: I'm calling about… / I bought it last week, and it doesn't charge. I've tried two chargers, but it still doesn't work. I'd like a replacement, please.]

### 7. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** You're ready! First, practise with your tutor. Then, when you're ready, start your exam. Good luck — and congratulations on finishing A2!
