# B2.2 · Unidade 8 — Reviews and reactions · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções: as dos [guiões da unidade 1 do B2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2, do B1, do B2.1 e das unidades 6 e 7 do B2. Novo: adjectivos graduáveis e não graduáveis com os intensificadores certos (*very / extremely / quite good* e *absolutely / completely / quite amazing*; *really* com os dois); advérbios que comentam a frase (*surprisingly, frankly, honestly, obviously, apparently, luckily, unfortunately, hopefully*; *admittedly*, da unidade 7); a ordem dos adjectivos (*a lovely old wooden door*); reacções informais (*Really? No way! Fair enough. I'm not that bothered. Tell me about it. Good for you!*); mudar de registo.

**Não usar nesta unidade:** verbos de relato como *suggest, recommend that, insist, deny, warn, advise* e a passiva de relato (unidade 9); *wish / if only*, *would* para hábitos do passado, o *past perfect continuous* e os condicionais mistos (unidade 10).

**Nomes de empresas e lugares:** a Kalandula Tech, a Mopane Systems e a Morro Azul Energia são fictícias; o "Festival Mar e Deserto", a cantora Mariana Kambundo, o filme "The Long Road to Benguela", o Hotel Duna Velha e o restaurante da 8B são fictícios. A cidade do Namibe (Moçâmedes) e a sua baía são reais.

**Personagens:** **Nuno**; **Paulo Mendes**; **Elsa Chilombo**; **Sílvia**; **Ingrid Solberg** (Morro Azul). Novos e fictícios: na 8E, uma repórter de rádio (**Joana Tavares**, angolana) e quatro pessoas à saída do festival: **Bento** (angolano, estudante), **Fatou** (senegalesa, professora), **Mr Clarke** (britânico, reformado) e **Kiala** (angolano, 16 anos).

**A história:** Maio de 2029. A Morro Azul patrocina o "Festival Mar e Deserto", na cidade do Namibe, no sábado, 19, e no domingo, 20; a Kalandula Tech monta e gere a "zona solar": carregamento gratuito de telemóveis e internet sem fios, alimentados por painéis solares, uma demonstração para o público do que vai ser a central do Tômbwa. Na segunda, 7, a Elsa conta o concerto e o filme do fim-de-semana (8A). Na quarta, 9, o Nuno e a Sílvia lêem críticas de hotéis e restaurantes no Namibe (8B). Na sexta, 11, a equipa fala do fim-de-semana do festival (8C). No sábado, 19, uma rádio entrevista pessoas à saída do festival (8E). Na segunda, 21, o Nuno faz o balanço da zona solar para a equipa e para a Ingrid (8F).

**Os números da zona solar (iguais em todas as aulas em que aparecem):** cerca de 3 200 visitantes em dois dias; 1 450 telemóveis carregados; 2 100 pessoas ligadas à internet; a internet caiu no sábado das 20:00 às 20:45, durante o concerto principal, por excesso de utilizadores; filas de até 40 minutos para carregar no sábado à noite. O inquérito (8F, em português): 312 respostas; 81% satisfeitos ou muito satisfeitos; o melhor: carregar o telemóvel de graça (64%) e a sombra e as cadeiras (22%); a melhorar: a internet durante o concerto (47% das queixas) e as filas (31%); nove em cada dez querem a zona no próximo ano.

## 8A — Absolutely brilliant

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 8        Aula: 8A — Absolutely brilliant        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue avaliar um evento com adjectivos graduáveis e não graduáveis e
  os intensificadores certos (very good, absolutely brilliant, not very fantastic).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 2
Gramática-alvo: adjectivos graduáveis (good, bad, big, tired, funny, interesting) com very, extremely, a
  bit, quite (= razoavelmente), fairly, rather; adjectivos não graduáveis ou extremos (brilliant, fantastic,
  awful, terrible, huge, exhausted, hilarious, fascinating, perfect) com absolutely, completely, totally,
  quite (= completamente); really com os dois
Palavras-alvo: concert, film, crowded, sound, goosebumps, trailer, ending, plot, acting, brilliant, awful,
  hilarious, exhausted, disappointing
Reciclagem: even though (B2, 7B)
Pronúncia: acento forte no intensificador (absolutely brilliant), e voz animada
Contexto: segunda-feira, 7 de Maio de 2029, 13:00; a cantina; a Elsa conta ao Nuno e ao Paulo o concerto de
  sexta e o filme de domingo
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 8A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 8
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! This unit is about opinions: reviews, reactions, and how to say the same thing to a friend and to a client. Today: how to make your adjectives stronger — correctly.

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 7 de Maio, 13:00; a cantina; a Elsa com o telemóvel, a mostrar fotografias de um palco; o Nuno e o Paulo]

**PAULO:** Elsa, you look exhausted. Good weekend?
**ELSA:** Absolutely brilliant. On Friday I went to see Mariana Kambundo at an open-air venue on the Ilha. She was fantastic.
**NUNO:** Was it very crowded?
**ELSA:** It was completely full. Two thousand people, and the sound was perfect. Her voice is incredibly powerful — I had goosebumps.
**PAULO:** Really? I've heard her songs on the radio. They're quite good, but a bit slow for me.
**ELSA:** Live, they're absolutely amazing. Even though the concert was three hours long, nobody left.
**NUNO:** And on Sunday?
**ELSA:** On Sunday, the opposite. I saw "The Long Road to Benguela". Everyone said it was hilarious.
**PAULO:** And?
**ELSA:** It was quite funny at the start, but the ending was awful. Really disappointing. Two hours of my life!
**NUNO:** Ha! I saw the trailer. The acting looked rather bad.
**ELSA:** The acting was fine. The plot was the problem — completely impossible.
**PAULO:** So, one perfect night and one terrible one.
**ELSA:** Exactly. And now I'm totally exhausted, and it's only Monday.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Graduáveis e não graduáveis**

[ECRÃ:]

| Graduáveis (pode haver mais ou menos) | Não graduáveis (já são o máximo) |
|---|---|
| good, bad, funny, tired, big, interesting | brilliant, fantastic, awful, hilarious, exhausted, huge, fascinating, perfect |
| **very / extremely / a bit / fairly** good | **absolutely / completely / totally** brilliant |
| ✘ absolutely good | ✘ very fantastic |

**PROF:** Some adjectives can be more or less: a film can be a bit funny or very funny. Others are already extreme: "hilarious" means "extremely funny", so "very hilarious" doesn't work. Use "absolutely" or "completely" with them.
[PAUSA 6 s]

***Really* e *quite***

[ECRÃ: **really** good · **really** brilliant (funciona com os dois) · **quite** good (= razoavelmente, não muito) · **quite** amazing (= completamente)]

**PROF:** "Really" works with both groups — it's the safest word in conversation. But "quite" changes meaning: with "good", it means "fairly"; with "amazing", it means "completely".
[PAUSA 6 s]

***Rather***

[ECRÃ: The acting looked **rather** bad. (bastante — muitas vezes para coisas negativas ou surpreendentes)]
[PAUSA 4 s]

[ECRÃ: Em português: "muito fantástico" soa normal. Em inglês: ✘ very fantastic → ✔ absolutely fantastic / really fantastic.]
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Put the stress on the intensifier, and let your voice show the feeling. Listen: It was absolutely brilliant. [voz animada] It was really disappointing. [voz desanimada]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat both.
[PAUSA 8 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Choose the right intensifier.
[ECRÃ: The hotel was … huge. (very / absolutely)] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: absolutely huge]
[ECRÃ: The food was … good. (extremely / completely)] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: extremely good]
[ECRÃ: I was … exhausted after the trip. (very / totally)] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: totally exhausted]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Tell me about the best and the worst event, film or concert of your last year. Use at least three graded adjectives and three extreme ones, with the right words. About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — The best was my cousin's wedding in Lubango: absolutely fantastic. The food was extremely good, and the band was brilliant. The worst was a film I saw in March. Everyone said it was fascinating, but it was quite boring and really long. By the end, I was completely exhausted.]

**PROF (opinião):** Do you trust what other people say about a film or a restaurant, or do you prefer to decide for yourself? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B2, 7B):** Elsa says the concert was three hours long, and nobody left. Join the ideas with "even though".
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Even though the concert was three hours long, nobody left.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! "Very good", "absolutely brilliant" — never "very fantastic". And "really" works with both. Next lesson: words that comment on a whole sentence — "surprisingly", "frankly" — and the order of adjectives.

## 8B — Surprisingly good

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 8        Aula: 8B — Surprisingly good        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue comentar com advérbios de frase (surprisingly, frankly,
  apparently, luckily, unfortunately, obviously, hopefully) e pôr vários adjectivos pela ordem certa.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: advérbios de comentário no início da frase, com vírgula (Surprisingly, the food was
  cheap.) ou antes do adjectivo (surprisingly good); a ordem dos adjectivos: opinião, tamanho, idade, forma,
  cor, origem, material, finalidade (a lovely old colonial building; a small blue wooden boat)
Palavras-alvo: review, stars, room, staff, seafront, chef, terrace, harbour, overpriced, value for money,
  spacious, colonial, recommend (com nome: I recommend the fish)
Reciclagem: admittedly (B2, 7A); números aproximados (B2, 2E)
Pronúncia: o advérbio de comentário com uma pausa e o tom da atitude (surpresa, franqueza, pena)
Contexto: quarta-feira, 9 de Maio, 16:00; o escritório; o Nuno e a Sílvia lêem críticas na internet para
  escolher o hotel e o restaurante da equipa no fim-de-semana do festival
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 8B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Online reviews are full of little words that show the writer's attitude: "surprisingly", "frankly", "unfortunately". Today: those words — and how to put several adjectives in the right order.

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: quarta-feira, 9 de Maio, 16:00; o escritório; o Nuno e a Sílvia diante de um portátil com críticas de hotéis: estrelas, fotografias, comentários]

**SÍLVIA:** OK, eight people, two nights in Namibe. What have you found?
**NUNO:** Two options. The Hotel Duna Velha: four stars, a lovely old colonial building on the seafront. Listen to this review: "Surprisingly good value. The rooms are small, but clean, and the staff are absolutely lovely."
**SÍLVIA:** And the other one?
**NUNO:** A big modern hotel near the market. "Frankly, overpriced. The rooms are spacious, but the restaurant was a disaster. Apparently, the chef left last month."
**SÍLVIA:** Ha! That's clear. And the price of the Duna Velha?
**NUNO:** Forty-five thousand kwanzas a night. Unfortunately, they only have six rooms free.
**SÍLVIA:** Six is fine. The four technicians can share two rooms. Luckily, they're friends.
**NUNO:** And for dinner on Saturday, people recommend a small Portuguese fish restaurant by the harbour. "Obviously, the fish is the reason to go. Hopefully, you'll get a table on the terrace."
**SÍLVIA:** Any bad reviews?
**NUNO:** One. "Admittedly, the fish was excellent, but we waited an hour." Only one out of about two hundred.
**SÍLVIA:** I can live with that. Book both. And, Nuno — book the restaurant for nine, after the concert. Hopefully, the internet in the solar zone won't need you.
**NUNO:** It won't. We've tested it for three thousand users.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Advérbios que comentam**

[ECRÃ:]

| Advérbio | A atitude | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| surprisingly | surpresa | **Surprisingly**, the food was cheap. · **surprisingly** good value |
| frankly / honestly | franqueza | **Frankly**, it's overpriced. |
| apparently | ouvi dizer | **Apparently**, the chef left last month. |
| luckily / fortunately | sorte | **Luckily**, they're friends. |
| unfortunately | pena | **Unfortunately**, they only have six rooms. |
| obviously | é evidente | **Obviously**, the fish is the reason to go. |
| hopefully | espero que | **Hopefully**, you'll get a table. |

**PROF:** These adverbs comment on the whole sentence. At the start, they take a comma — and in speech, a little pause.
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ: "Aparentemente" ≠ apparently? — Em inglês, "apparently" quer quase sempre dizer "ouvi dizer que…", não "à primeira vista".]
[PAUSA 4 s]

**A ordem dos adjectivos**

[ECRÃ: opinião → tamanho → idade → forma → cor → origem → material → finalidade
a **lovely old colonial** building · a **small Portuguese fish** restaurant · a **beautiful little blue wooden** boat]

**PROF:** When you use two or three adjectives, opinion comes first, then size, then age, then colour, then origin. Native speakers never think about it — but "an old lovely building" sounds wrong to them.
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ a colonial old lovely building · ✔ a lovely old colonial building · ✘ a Portuguese small restaurant · ✔ a small Portuguese restaurant]
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** The comment adverb carries the attitude. Listen: Surprisingly, [pausa, voz surpreendida] the food was cheap. Unfortunately, [pausa, voz de pena] they only have six rooms.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat both.
[PAUSA 8 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Put the adjectives in order.
[ECRÃ: a (wooden / old / beautiful) table] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: a beautiful old wooden table]
[ECRÃ: a (Angolan / delicious / spicy) dish] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: a delicious spicy Angolan dish]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Write a review out loud of a hotel, a restaurant or a shop you know: say what kind of place it is (with two or three adjectives in order), use three comment adverbs, and give your rating out of five. About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last month I tried a lovely little Cape Verdean restaurant in Maianga. Surprisingly, it's very cheap for the area. The cachupa was absolutely delicious. Unfortunately, it's tiny — only six tables — so you have to book. Apparently, it's full every Friday. Hopefully, it won't get too famous! Four stars out of five.]

**PROF (opinião):** Do you write online reviews? Should people only write when something goes wrong? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B2, 2E):** Nuno says there was one bad review "out of about two hundred". Say it as a fraction or a percentage.
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: That's about half a percent of the reviews.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! "Surprisingly", "frankly", "apparently" — with a comma and a pause. And adjectives in order: opinion, size, age, origin. Next lesson: how to react when a friend tells you something.

## 8C — I'm not that bothered

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 8        Aula: 8C — I'm not that bothered        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue reagir às opiniões e às notícias dos outros, no registo
  informal, mostrando interesse, surpresa, simpatia, acordo ou indiferença.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: reacções curtas: interesse (Really? Oh, did you? How was it?), surpresa (No way! You're
  joking! Seriously?), simpatia (Oh no, poor you! That's a shame.), acordo (Tell me about it! That's so true!
  Absolutely.), aceitar (Fair enough.), indiferença (I'm not that bothered. I don't mind either way. Whatever
  you prefer.), alegria pelo outro (Good for you! Lucky you!); perguntas-eco (She's leaving. — Is she?)
Palavras-alvo: weekend, football, final, beach, lie-in, bothered, mind, joking, lucky, legendary
Reciclagem: question tags (B1)
Pronúncia: entoação de atitude: entusiasmo (voz alta e larga), surpresa (subida forte), indiferença (voz
  plana) — e a ironia de "Great." dito sem entusiasmo
Contexto: sexta-feira, 11 de Maio, 17:30; o escritório, ao fim do dia; o Nuno, o Paulo e a Elsa falam do
  fim-de-semana e do festival
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 8C; conversa guiada "What did you think of it?"; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! In a conversation, listening isn't silent. You react: "Really?", "No way!", "Fair enough". Today: the short reactions that make you sound natural — and the tone that gives them meaning.

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: sexta-feira, 11 de Maio, 17:30; o escritório, quase vazio; o Paulo a arrumar a mochila; a Elsa e o Nuno à secretária]

**PAULO:** Right, I'm off. Big weekend: my team are playing in the cup final on Sunday!
**ELSA:** Oh, are they? Good for you. I'm not that bothered about football, to be honest.
**PAULO:** What? It's the final! Seriously?
**ELSA:** Seriously. I'm going to the beach and having a lie-in.
**NUNO:** Lucky you. I'm working on the festival plan all Saturday.
**ELSA:** Oh no, poor you! On a Saturday?
**NUNO:** Tell me about it. But the festival's next week, so…
**PAULO:** Fair enough. By the way, did you hear? Mariana Kambundo is singing at the festival on Saturday night.
**ELSA:** No way! Are you joking? I saw her last week — she's absolutely brilliant.
**NUNO:** I know. That's why we need the internet to work: thousands of people filming her at the same time.
**PAULO:** So, Nuno, do you want to watch the final at my place on Sunday?
**NUNO:** I don't mind either way. Whatever you prefer — your place or a café.
**PAULO:** My place. My mother's cooking.
**ELSA:** That's so true — her food is legendary. Paulo, take a photo of the food for me.
**PAULO:** Ha! Not a photo of the match?
**ELSA:** Not really. Enjoy the match!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Reacções curtas**

[ECRÃ:]

| Para mostrar… | Diga |
|---|---|
| interesse | Really? · Oh, are they? · How was it? |
| surpresa | No way! · Are you joking? · Seriously? |
| simpatia | Oh no, poor you! · That's a shame. |
| acordo | Tell me about it! · That's so true! · Absolutely. |
| aceitação | Fair enough. |
| indiferença | I'm not that bothered. · I don't mind either way. · Whatever you prefer. |
| alegria pelo outro | Good for you! · Lucky you! |

**PROF:** These phrases are short, but they do a lot of work: they show the other person that you're listening — and how you feel.
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Perguntas-eco**

[ECRÃ: My team are playing in the final. — **Are they?** · She's leaving. — **Is she?** · I saw her last week. — **Did you?**]

**PROF:** An echo question repeats the auxiliary: "Are they?", "Did you?". It means "Really? Tell me more."
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Cuidado com o registo**

[ECRÃ: "I'm not that bothered" e "Whatever you prefer" são amigáveis entre colegas. A um cliente, diga: "I'm happy with either option." · "Whatever." sozinho soa mal-educado.]

**PROF:** Careful: "Whatever" alone, with a flat voice, sounds rude. "Whatever you prefer" is friendly.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** The tone is the message. Listen to "Great" three times. Great! [entusiasmo] — Great? [surpresa] — Great. [plano, irónico: quer dizer "que mau"]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Now "Really?" with interest, then with surprise.
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** And: I'm not that bothered. [voz plana, mas simpática]
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** React.
[ECRÃ: "I've just got a new job in Lisbon!"] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: No way! Good for you!]
[ECRÃ: "My car broke down on the way here."] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: Oh no, poor you!]
[ECRÃ: "Traffic in this city is getting worse."] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: Tell me about it!]
[ECRÃ: "Shall we eat Chinese or Italian?" (não se importa)] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: I don't mind either way.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. I'll tell you my news. React to each one with a short phrase and a follow-up question.
**PROF:** I went to a fantastic concert last night.
[PAUSA 8 s]
**PROF:** But my phone was stolen at the end.
[PAUSA 8 s]
**PROF:** Luckily, I'd backed it up, so I didn't lose my photos.
[PAUSA 8 s]
**PROF:** And next week, I'm going to Cape Verde for a holiday.
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: modelos — Really? Who was playing? · Oh no, poor you! Did you call the police? · That's lucky! · Lucky you! How long are you going for?]

**PROF (opinião):** In your culture, is it rude to say "I'm not interested" about something a friend loves? How do you say it politely?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1):** Add a question tag: "The festival's next week, …?"
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: The festival's next week, isn't it?]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! "Really?", "No way!", "Fair enough", "I'm not that bothered" — and the tone does half the work. Try the tutor's conversation "What did you think of it?". Next lesson: the words of culture and reviews.

## 8D — Culture and reviews

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 8        Aula: 8D — Culture and reviews        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar o vocabulário de cultura e de crítica (concert,
  exhibition, line-up, venue, audience, overrated, underrated, a must-see), mostrar atitude com a
  entoação, e evitar os falsos amigos romance e spectacle.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 4
Gramática-alvo: colocações (give / put on a performance, get good / bad reviews, live up to expectations,
  a sell-out show, sold out, a standing ovation); adjectivos de crítica (overrated, underrated, a must-see,
  predictable, moving, gripping, catchy)
Palavras-alvo: concert, gig, exhibition, festival, line-up, venue, stage, audience, crowd, performance,
  review, sold out, overrated, underrated, must-see, catchy, moving
Reciclagem: graduáveis e não graduáveis (8A); advérbios de comentário (8B)
Pronúncia: entoação para entusiasmo, ironia e indiferença na mesma frase
Falsos amigos: romance (≠ romance → novel; romance = caso amoroso, história de amor); spectacle (≠
  espectáculo → show, performance; spectacle = cena impressionante; spectacles = óculos)
Contexto: ecrãs com expressões e exemplos da cultura em Angola (artistas e eventos fictícios)
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 8D; perguntas 7 e 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: the words people use to talk about music, art and events — and two false friends you'll want to avoid at the theatre.

### 2. Cultura e eventos (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ:]

| Palavra | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| gig | We went to a gig on Friday. (concerto, informal) |
| exhibition | There's a photography exhibition in Ilha. (exposição) |
| line-up | The festival line-up is fantastic. (os artistas do cartaz) |
| venue | The venue was too small for the crowd. (o local) |
| audience / crowd | The audience loved it. · The crowd was huge. (público / multidão) |
| stage | She came on stage at ten. (palco) |
| sold out | The concert was sold out. (esgotado) |
| standing ovation | She got a standing ovation. (ovação de pé) |

**PROF:** Repeat: gig. Exhibition. Line-up. Venue. Audience. Sold out.
[PAUSA 8 s]

### 3. Linguagem de crítica (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: **overrated** (sobrevalorizado) · **underrated** (subvalorizado) · **a must-see** (imperdível) · **predictable** (previsível) · **moving** (comovente) · **gripping** (que prende) · **catchy** (que fica no ouvido)]

**PROF:** "Overrated" means everyone says it's great, but it isn't. "Underrated" is the opposite. "A must-see" is the strongest recommendation.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: **get** good reviews · **live up to** expectations (corresponder às expectativas) · **put on** a show · **give** a performance]

[ECRÃ: The film didn't **live up to** the expectations. · The festival **got** excellent reviews. · The students **put on** a show for their parents.]
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 4. Pronúncia: atitude (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** The same sentence can mean three things. Listen: "It was interesting." [entusiasmo: voz alta, larga] — "It was interesting." [ironia: voz arrastada — quer dizer "estranho"] — "It was interesting." [indiferença: plana]
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** In English, "interesting" said slowly and flatly is often a polite way to say "I didn't like it".
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Repeat it with enthusiasm.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: **romance** ≠ romance (livro)]

**PROF:** In English, a "romance" is a love affair or a love story: "a summer romance".
(PT: Um "romance" — o livro — é "a novel": "li um romance de Pepetela" → "I read a novel by Pepetela".)
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: **spectacle** ≠ espectáculo]

**PROF:** A "spectacle" is an impressive sight: "The fireworks were quite a spectacle." And "spectacles" are glasses!
(PT: Um "espectáculo" de música ou teatro é "a show" ou "a performance": "o espectáculo começa às nove" → "the show starts at nine".)
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 6. Prática e fecho (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Complete.
[ECRÃ: The tickets went in one hour. The show was … out.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: sold]
[ECRÃ: Everyone loved the book, but for me it was … .] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: overrated]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Review an event, a restaurant or a product you tried recently. Say what it was, what was good and bad (with the right intensifiers and one comment adverb), whether it lived up to your expectations, and whether you'd recommend it. About one and a half minutes.
[PAUSA 90 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last month I went to a jazz gig at a small venue in Ilha. The line-up was quite good, and the last band was absolutely brilliant — they got a standing ovation. Unfortunately, the sound was rather bad at the back, and the drinks were overpriced. It didn't completely live up to my expectations, but I'd still recommend it — especially the last band. A must-see if they play again.]

**PROF (opinião):** Is there a famous film, book or band that you think is overrated? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF:** Well done! Next lesson: the festival — and four people with four very different opinions.

## 8E — Real English: The festival

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 8        Aula: 8E — The festival        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue seguir quatro opiniões curtas à saída de um festival, com
  barulho de fundo, e identificar a opinião e o tom de cada pessoa (entusiasmo, ironia, indiferença, crítica).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 3
Língua: A1, A2, B1, B2.1, as unidades 6 e 7 e a unidade 8 até à 8D, sem estruturas novas
Vozes: Joana Tavares (repórter), Bento (angolano, entusiasmado), Fatou (senegalesa, crítica mas justa),
  Mr Clarke (britânico, irónico), Kiala (angolano, 16 anos, indiferente); música e multidão ao fundo
Velocidade: natural, com risos e barulho
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 8E
```

### 1. Apresentação (30 s)

**PROF:** On Saturday night, at the end of the Mar e Deserto festival, a radio reporter stops four people at the exit. Listen for two things: each person's opinion, and their tone — enthusiastic, ironic, indifferent or critical.

### 2. Primeira escuta: as quatro opiniões (5 min)

[ECRÃ: sábado, 19 de Maio, 23:30; a saída do festival, na marginal do Namibe; luzes, música ao fundo; a repórter com um microfone]

**PROF:** First: who loved it, and who didn't?

**JOANA:** Good evening, I'm Joana Tavares for Rádio Costa. We're at the exit of the Mar e Deserto festival. Excuse me — what did you think?
**BENTO:** Absolutely incredible! Mariana Kambundo was the best thing I've ever seen. When she sang "Mar de Areia", the whole crowd was singing. I had tears in my eyes. Ten out of ten!
**JOANA:** And the rest of the line-up?
**BENTO:** Honestly, I only came for her. But the young band before her was surprisingly good.
**JOANA:** Thank you! And you, madam?
**FATOU:** It was very good, but not perfect. The music was excellent — really excellent. Unfortunately, the organisation was rather poor. We queued forty minutes for food, and there were only four toilets for thousands of people.
**JOANA:** Would you come back next year?
**FATOU:** Yes, if they fix the queues. The music deserves a better festival.
**JOANA:** Sir — did you enjoy it?
**MR CLARKE:** Oh, enormously. I particularly enjoyed standing behind a very tall man for three hours. [ri-se] No, seriously — the music was lovely, the sea breeze was perfect. But the internet died just when everybody wanted to film the main concert. Typical!
**JOANA:** So, did it live up to your expectations?
**MR CLARKE:** Apparently, my expectations were too high. Seven out of ten — and two of those points are for the sunset.
**JOANA:** And you? You look very relaxed.
**KIALA:** It was OK. I'm not that bothered about the big stars. I came with my friends. The solar zone was cool, though — free charging, and you could sit in the shade.
**JOANA:** Would you come next year?
**KIALA:** Probably. If my friends come. Whatever.
**JOANA:** Four people, four opinions. From the Mar e Deserto festival, this is Joana Tavares.

[ECRÃ: Bento — entusiasmado: "absolutely incredible", 10/10 · Fatou — crítica mas justa: música excelente, organização fraca (filas, casas de banho) · Mr Clarke — irónico: música "lovely", mas a internet caiu no concerto principal; 7/10 · Kiala — indiferente: "It was OK", mas gostou da zona solar]

### 3. Segunda escuta: os pormenores (3 min)

1. What happened when Mariana Kambundo sang "Mar de Areia"? (The whole crowd was singing. ✓ / The sound stopped. / She cried.)
2. How long did Fatou queue for food? (forty minutes ✓ / fourteen minutes / four hours)
3. What does Fatou want the festival to fix? (the queues ✓ / the music / the stage)
4. What is Mr Clarke being ironic about? (standing behind a very tall man ✓ / the sea breeze / the sunset)
5. What went wrong during the main concert, according to Mr Clarke? (The internet stopped working. ✓ / It rained. / The singer was late.)
6. What did Kiala like? (the solar zone: free charging and shade ✓ / the big stars / the food)

*(Cada resposta repete-se; depois aparecem as perguntas, com 8 s para responder.)*

### 4. Terceira escuta: como disseram (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Listen and repeat — with the same tone.
**PROF:** Absolutely incredible! [entusiasmo] [PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Unfortunately, the organisation was rather poor. [crítica calma] [PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Oh, enormously. [ironia] [PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** It was OK. I'm not that bothered. [indiferença] [PAUSA 4 s]

**PROF:** Did you notice? Mr Clarke's "Oh, enormously" means the opposite — you know from the next sentence and his laugh. That's typical British irony. And Kiala's "Whatever" at the end sounds a bit rude — but he's sixteen!
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 5. A sua vez (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Now you. Tell a colleague what each of the four people thought, and how they said it. Which opinion is most useful for the organisers? About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Bento was enthusiastic: he thought Mariana Kambundo was absolutely incredible. Fatou liked the music, but she was critical of the organisation — long queues and too few toilets. Mr Clarke was ironic: the music was lovely, but the internet died during the main concert. Kiala didn't care much, but he liked the solar zone. Fatou's opinion is the most useful, because it's specific.]

**PROF (opinião):** When you give your opinion about something a friend organised, do you say what you really think? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent listening! Next lesson: the feedback — Nuno talks about the solar zone to his team, and then to the sponsor.

## 8F — Work & Life: Event feedback

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 8        Aula: 8F — Event feedback        Tipo: Work & Life
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue fazer o balanço de um evento para dois públicos — a equipa, de
  forma informal, e o patrocinador, de forma formal — e resumir em inglês as respostas de um inquérito em
  português.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 2, 3, 4
Gramática-alvo: toda a da unidade: intensificadores, advérbios de comentário, reacções; mudar de registo
  (informal: It went really well! The Wi-Fi died, though. · formal: Overall, the zone was very well
  received. Unfortunately, the connection was interrupted…)
Palavras-alvo: feedback, survey, responses, satisfied, complaint, queue, capacity, sponsor, well received,
  overall, next year
Reciclagem: a passiva (B2, 4A); números aproximados e percentagens (B2, 2E); past perfect (B1)
Pronúncia: o mesmo facto dito com o tom de uma conversa entre colegas e com o tom de uma reunião formal
Mediação: o Nuno resume à Ingrid, em inglês, as respostas do inquérito aos visitantes, escrito em português
Contexto: segunda-feira, 21 de Maio, 9:00 (a equipa, no escritório) e 11:00 (videochamada com a Ingrid)
Duração: 13 min
Verificação: Lab 8F; conversa livre "Same opinion, two audiences"
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today, Nuno gives the same feedback twice: first to his team, as a friend; then to the sponsor, as a professional. Watch what changes — and what doesn't.

### 2. Situação (5 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 21 de Maio, 9:00; o escritório; o Nuno, o Paulo e a Elsa com cafés; fotografias do festival num ecrã]

**NUNO:** Morning, heroes! So, how was it for you?
**ELSA:** Absolutely exhausting — but brilliant. The kids loved the solar demo.
**PAULO:** Tell me about it. My feet still hurt.
**NUNO:** Ha! Honestly, you were amazing. One thousand four hundred and fifty phones charged. That's crazy.
**ELSA:** And the Wi-Fi? Be honest.
**NUNO:** OK — the Wi-Fi died during Mariana's concert. Forty-five minutes. Everyone was filming at the same time, and it just couldn't cope.
**PAULO:** We tested it for three thousand users…
**NUNO:** I know. Apparently, half of them were streaming video. That's our lesson for next year. But overall? A really good weekend. Thank you, guys.
**ELSA:** Fair enough. Can we have Friday off?
**NUNO:** Ha! I'll ask Lerato.
[ECRÃ: 11:00; videochamada; o Nuno no escritório; a Ingrid na sede da Morro Azul]
**INGRID:** Good morning, Nuno. I'd like your assessment of the solar zone before I report to the board.
**NUNO:** Good morning, Ingrid. Overall, the zone was very well received. Over the two days, about three thousand two hundred people visited it, one thousand four hundred and fifty phones were charged, and two thousand one hundred people used the internet.
**INGRID:** Impressive. And the problems?
**NUNO:** There were two. Unfortunately, the connection was interrupted on Saturday from eight to eight forty-five, during the main concert, because of the very high number of users. And on Saturday evening, visitors queued for up to forty minutes to charge their phones.
**INGRID:** I heard about the internet on the radio. A British gentleman wasn't impressed.
**NUNO:** I heard him too. Frankly, he was right. We had planned for three thousand users, but we hadn't expected so much video. We'll double the capacity next year.
**INGRID:** Good. What did the visitors say?
**NUNO:** We did a short survey. The answers are in Portuguese — let me summarise them for you.

[ECRÃ: o inquérito, em português: "Inquérito aos visitantes da zona solar — 312 respostas. Satisfação: 81% satisfeitos ou muito satisfeitos. O que mais gostaram: carregar o telemóvel de graça (64%); a sombra e as cadeiras (22%). O que deve melhorar: a internet durante o concerto principal (47% das queixas); as filas para carregar (31% das queixas). 9 em cada 10 querem a zona solar no próximo ano. Comentário frequente: 'Devia haver mais tomadas.'"]

**NUNO:** We had three hundred and twelve responses. Eighty-one percent were satisfied or very satisfied. What people liked most was free charging — almost two thirds mentioned it — and, surprisingly, the shade and the chairs: about a fifth. The main complaints were the internet during the concert — almost half of the complaints — and the queues, about a third. And nine out of ten want the zone again next year. The most common comment was simply: "There should be more sockets."
**INGRID:** Very clear, thank you. So, your recommendation?
**NUNO:** Repeat it next year, with twice the internet capacity and twice the charging points. And, hopefully, with your sponsorship again.
**INGRID:** I'll put that to the board. Well done, Nuno — and thank your team.
**NUNO:** I will. They were absolutely brilliant.

### 3. O mesmo balanço, dois registos (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o que mudou entre as 9:00 e as 11:00]

| Para a equipa (informal) | Para a patrocinadora (formal) |
|---|---|
| Morning, heroes! | Good morning, Ingrid. |
| One thousand four hundred and fifty phones. That's crazy. | One thousand four hundred and fifty phones were charged. |
| The Wi-Fi died during Mariana's concert. | Unfortunately, the connection was interrupted during the main concert. |
| It just couldn't cope. | …because of the very high number of users. |
| But overall? A really good weekend. | Overall, the zone was very well received. |
| Thank you, guys. | We'll double the capacity next year. |

**PROF:** The facts are exactly the same. What changes is the register: in the formal version, Nuno uses the passive, "unfortunately" instead of "died", full sentences, and no jokes. And he takes responsibility: "Frankly, he was right."
[PAUSA 6 s]

**A mediação**

**PROF:** Nuno turns percentages into words people remember: sixty-four percent becomes "almost two thirds", twenty-two percent "about a fifth", forty-seven percent "almost half". And he keeps one real comment — "There should be more sockets" — because it's short and it says everything.
(PT: "Satisfeitos ou muito satisfeitos" → "satisfied or very satisfied"; "queixas" → "complaints"; "tomadas" → "sockets"; "9 em cada 10" → "nine out of ten".)
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Same fact, two tones. Listen: The Wi-Fi died during the concert! [entre colegas, rápido, com humor] — Unfortunately, the connection was interrupted during the main concert. [formal, calmo, a descer]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat both.
[PAUSA 10 s]

### 5. A sua vez (3 min)

**PROF:** Now you. Give feedback on an event you organised or attended — a party, a training day, a conference, a wedding — twice: first to a friend, informally, with reactions and strong adjectives; then to your manager or a sponsor, formally, with one problem and a recommendation. About one and a half minutes.
[PAUSA 90 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — To a friend: "The training day? Absolutely brilliant! The speaker was hilarious, and the lunch was amazing. The room was boiling, though — the air conditioning died at eleven!" — To my manager: "Overall, the training day was very well received. Unfortunately, the air conditioning stopped working in the late morning, which made the room uncomfortable. For the next session, I recommend a room with a backup system."]

**PROF:** Now the mediation. Summarise these results in English, with words instead of percentages where you can.
[ECRÃ: "Inquérito ao jantar de fim de ano (86 respostas): 92% gostaram da comida; 54% acharam a música alta demais; 26% gostariam de mudar de restaurante no próximo ano."]
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — We had eighty-six responses. Almost everyone liked the food. Just over half thought the music was too loud, and about a quarter would like a different restaurant next year.]

**PROF (opinião):** Is it more difficult to give negative feedback to a friend or to a client? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Fantastic! You can now give precise opinions, react naturally, and say the same thing to a friend and to a client. Go to the tutor for "Same opinion, two audiences". Next unit: Mediation at work.
