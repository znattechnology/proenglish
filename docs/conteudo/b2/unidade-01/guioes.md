# B2.1 · Unidade 1 — Leading a team · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções: as dos [guiões da unidade 1 do B1](../../b1/unidade-01/guioes.md): tudo em inglês, e o português só nos falsos amigos e na mediação. No B2, o professor fala à velocidade natural e as práticas pedem turnos de 1 a 2 minutos.

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2 e do B1. Novo: orações relativas explicativas (*Elsa, who joined in May, …*; *the plant, which…*; *Sipho, whose team…*; *Tômbwa, where…*), *which* a comentar uma frase inteira (*…, which is great news*), relativas com preposição (*the client we work for*; formal, só para reconhecer: *the client for whom…*); dar a palavra, convidar quem não falou, cortar uma digressão e retomar; delegar.

**Não usar nesta unidade (fica para unidades seguintes do B2):** o *future perfect* (*will have done*, unidade 2); *provided that, as long as, supposing* (unidade 3); a passiva contínua, no *present perfect* e com modais, e *have something done* (unidade 4); *be bound to*, *be likely to* e o *future perfect continuous* (unidade 5); *needn't have*, *was supposed to* (unidade 6); *whereas, admittedly, even if, nevertheless* (unidade 7); verbos de relato como *suggest, insist, deny, warn* (unidade 9); *wish / if only*, *would* para hábitos do passado, o *past perfect continuous* e os condicionais mistos (unidade 10).

**Nomes de empresas e lugares:** a Kalandula Tech, a Mopane Systems e a Morro Azul Energia são fictícias; a central do Tômbwa também (B1 9F).

**Personagens:** **Nuno** (lidera pela primeira vez uma equipa: a proposta para a central do Tômbwa); **Lerato** (coordenadora; modelo de quem conduz uma reunião, na 1C); **Paulo Mendes** (apoio técnico; entrou na Kalandula Tech pouco antes do Nuno, em 2026, e foi quem o recebeu; é mais velho do que ele, e não quis liderar a equipa porque começou um mestrado à noite); **Sílvia** (directora financeira); **Thandi** (Mopane, Joanesburgo; conduz a reunião da 1E); **Sipho** (Mopane; responsável pela parte do satélite); **Hannah** e **Femi Adeyemi** (Morro Azul; o Femi é o director financeiro, B1 3F). Novos e fictícios: **Elsa Chilombo** (engenheira electrotécnica júnior, de Benguela, na Kalandula Tech desde Maio de 2028; tímida nas reuniões); na 1E, **Bongani** e **Lindiwe** (engenheiros da Mopane) e **Rajesh** (analista da Mopane, de Durban).

**A história:** Setembro de 2028. A Morro Azul convidou a Kalandula Tech e a Mopane a apresentar uma proposta para o sistema de rede e de monitorização da nova central do Tômbwa; as obras começam em Outubro (B1 9F). A Lerato pôs o Nuno a liderar a equipa da proposta. Na segunda, 4, o Nuno apresenta a equipa à Hannah (1A); na terça, 5, fala com o Paulo, que tem mais anos de casa do que ele (1B); na quarta, 6, observa a Lerato a conduzir a reunião semanal (1C); na quinta, 7, assiste por vídeo à reunião rápida da equipa da Thandi (1E); na segunda, 11, conduz a primeira reunião da sua equipa (1F).

## 1A — Elsa, who joined in May

```
Curso: B2.1        Unidade: 1        Aula: 1A — Elsa, who joined in May        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dar informação extra sobre pessoas, coisas e lugares com
  orações relativas explicativas (Elsa, who joined in May, will lead the testing.), com as vírgulas na
  escrita e as pausas na voz.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: relativas explicativas com who, which, whose, where; nunca that; a relativa entre
  vírgulas pode sair sem mudar a frase principal; contraste com as relativas de definição (B1, 5C)
Palavras-alvo: team, join, lead, look after, in charge of, testing, sharp, quiet, nervous, satellite
  backup, proposal, board
Reciclagem: relativas de definição (B1, 5C); present perfect continuous (B1, 2B)
Pronúncia: pausa antes e depois da relativa explicativa, e a voz um pouco mais baixa dentro dela
Contexto: segunda-feira, 4 de Setembro de 2028, 10:00; videochamada; o Nuno, em Luanda, apresenta a nova
  equipa à Hannah, na central do Namibe
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 1A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 1
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello, and welcome to B2! This unit is about leading people: introducing a team, giving everyone a voice, and sharing the work. Today: how to add extra information about people, things and places — "Elsa, who joined in May, …".

### 2. Situação (3 min)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 4 de Setembro, 10:00; videochamada; o Nuno no escritório, em Luanda; a Hannah na sala de controlo do Namibe]

**HANNAH:** Morning, Nuno! Lerato tells me you're leading the Tômbwa proposal. Congratulations.
**NUNO:** Thanks, Hannah. It's my first time leading a team, so I'm a bit nervous — but excited. Can I introduce the team?
**HANNAH:** Please. I'd like to know who I'll be talking to.
**NUNO:** First, Paulo, who you know from the antennas in Namibe. He's been working on your network since the start, so he'll look after the installation plan.
**HANNAH:** Great. Paulo knows our plant better than some of our own engineers.
**NUNO:** Then Elsa Chilombo, who joined us in May. She's an electrical engineer from Benguela, and she'll be in charge of testing. She's quiet, but she's very sharp.
**HANNAH:** Good to hear. And from Mopane?
**NUNO:** Sipho, whose team worked with us on the satellite backup at your plant. He'll do the satellite part again, from Johannesburg.
**HANNAH:** Perfect. That backup has saved us more than once.
**NUNO:** And the plant itself — Tômbwa, where the work starts in October — is quite different from Namibe. It's even windier, and closer to the desert, so we'll need stronger antennas.
**HANNAH:** True. And the proposal, which our board wants to see in October, needs to be clear about costs. And Femi will read every number.
**NUNO:** Understood — I know Femi from the antennas project. We'll present it on the twelfth of October, if that still works for the board.
**HANNAH:** It does. I'm looking forward to it.

### 3. Língua (4 min 30 s)

**Relativas explicativas: informação extra**

[ECRÃ:
Paulo, **who you know from Namibe**, will look after the installation.
Elsa Chilombo, **who joined us in May**, will be in charge of testing.
Sipho, **whose team worked on the satellite backup**, will do the satellite part.
Tômbwa, **where the work starts in October**, is different from Namibe.
The proposal, **which the board wants in October**, needs clear costs.]

**PROF:** These clauses add extra information. The sentence is complete without them: "Elsa Chilombo will be in charge of testing." The extra part goes between commas — and in speech, between short pauses.
[PAUSA 6 s]

**Definição ou informação extra?**

[ECRÃ:
definição (B1) — The engineer **who** designed it is here. (qual engenheiro? este)
informação extra (B2) — Elsa, **who** joined in May, is here. (já sabemos quem é a Elsa)]

**PROF:** You learned the first type in B1: it tells us which person or thing. The second type doesn't identify — we already know who Elsa is; it only adds information.
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Quatro regras**

[ECRÃ:
1. vírgulas (e pausas) — sempre
2. **who** (pessoas), **which** (coisas), **whose** (de quem), **where** (lugares)
3. nunca **that**: ✘ Elsa, that joined in May, … · ✔ Elsa, who joined in May, …
4. o pronome nunca sai: ✘ The proposal, the board wants in October, … · ✔ The proposal, which the board wants in October, …]

**PROF:** Four rules. Always commas. "Who", "which", "whose", "where" — never "that". And you can't leave the pronoun out, as you can in "the report I sent you".
[PAUSA 6 s]

***Whose***

[ECRÃ: Sipho, **whose** team worked on the backup, … (= a equipa do Sipho) · ✘ Sipho, who his team worked on…]

**PROF:** "Whose" means "his", "her" or "their" in the middle of the sentence. Don't add "his" again.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In speech, the commas become pauses, and the extra information is said a little lower and faster. Listen: Elsa Chilombo, [pausa] who joined us in May, [pausa] will be in charge of testing.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: Sipho, whose team worked on the backup, will do the satellite part.
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** Tômbwa, where the work starts in October, is quite different.
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Join the two sentences with extra information.
[ECRÃ: My manager is very patient. She has worked here for twenty years.] [PAUSA 7 s] [ECRÃ: My manager, who has worked here for twenty years, is very patient.]
[ECRÃ: Lobito is a busy port city. My brother lives there.] [PAUSA 7 s] [ECRÃ: Lobito, where my brother lives, is a busy port city.]
[ECRÃ: Kagiso is very reliable. His company makes our sensors.] [PAUSA 7 s] [ECRÃ: Kagiso, whose company makes our sensors, is very reliable.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Introduce three people from your work, your studies or your family to a new colleague. For each person, add extra information with "who", "whose" or "where". About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Let me introduce my team. Ana, who's been with us for fifteen years, knows every client by name. Then there's Mário, whose father started the company, so he knows its whole history. And our office is in Talatona, where most of our clients are.]

**PROF (opinião):** In your opinion, is it harder to lead people who are older or more experienced than you? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 5C):** Defining or extra? "The technician who works at night is Lourenço." Does it need commas?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: No commas: it tells us which technician.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! Commas and pauses, "who, which, whose, where" — and never "that" for extra information. Next lesson: "which" for a whole sentence, and clauses with prepositions.

## 1B — Which is why we need you

```
Curso: B2.1        Unidade: 1        Aula: 1B — Which is why we need you        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue comentar uma frase inteira com which (…, which is why I need
  you.) e usar relativas com preposição (the client we work for, the people I report to).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 3
Gramática-alvo: which a referir uma frase inteira (…, which is great / which means… / which is why…);
  preposição no fim da relativa (the people we work with); no registo formal, preposição antes de
  whom / which (só para reconhecer: the client for whom…)
Palavras-alvo: awkward, upset, appreciate, rely on, report to, in charge of, the kind of person, hardly,
  honest, overtime
Reciclagem: 1A; opiniões e acordo (B1, 3B)
Pronúncia: which is why… dito sem pausa longa antes; preposições fracas no fim da frase (who I work with)
Contexto: terça-feira, 5 de Setembro, 8:00; a cantina, antes do trabalho; o Nuno fala com o Paulo, que tem
  mais anos de casa do que ele
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 1B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Sometimes we want to comment on a whole situation, not just one word. Today: "which" for a whole sentence — and clauses that end with a preposition.

### 2. Situação (3 min)

[ECRÃ: terça-feira, 5 de Setembro, 8:00; a cantina; o Paulo com um café; o Nuno senta-se ao lado dele]

**NUNO:** Paulo, have you got a minute? There's something I'd like to talk about.
**PAULO:** Sure. Is it about the Tômbwa team?
**NUNO:** Yes. Lerato asked me to lead it, which you probably know. And honestly, it feels a bit awkward. You're older than me, and you were here before me.
**PAULO:** Ha! That's true. When you started, you used to ask me where the printer cables were.
**NUNO:** Exactly — which is why I wanted to talk to you first. You're the person I learned most from. I don't want you to feel I'm giving you orders.
**PAULO:** Nuno, I'm not upset. I didn't want to lead it — I've just started a master's degree in the evenings, which means I can't travel to Tômbwa every month. But I appreciate the conversation.
**NUNO:** Thank you. So, what I'd like is this: you're in charge of the installation plan, and I'll rely on you for anything technical. You're the one I'll come to with difficult questions.
**PAULO:** That works. And Elsa? She's the one I'm a bit worried about. She hardly speaks in meetings.
**NUNO:** I've noticed. She's the kind of person you need to ask directly. I'll give her the testing, which she's very good at.
**PAULO:** Good idea. Just one thing: the people we work with at Morro Azul like short meetings. Don't make the kick-off two hours long.
**NUNO:** Ha! Noted. One hour, maximum.

### 3. Língua (4 min 30 s)

***Which* sobre uma frase inteira**

[ECRÃ:
Lerato asked me to lead it, **which** you probably know. (= você sabe isso)
You're the person I learned most from — **which is why** I wanted to talk to you. (= é por isso que)
I've started a master's degree, **which means** I can't travel every month. (= o que quer dizer que)]

**PROF:** Here, "which" doesn't refer to one noun. It refers to the whole idea before it. It's very common in conversation: "which is great", "which means…", "which is why…".
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ: "…, o que é óptimo." → ✔ …, **which** is great. · ✘ …, what is great. · ✘ …, that is great.]

**PROF:** Careful: the Portuguese "o que" here is "which", not "what".
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Relativas com preposição**

[ECRÃ:
the person **I learned most from** · the people **we work with** · the one **I'll come to** with difficult questions · the testing, **which she's very good at**]

**PROF:** In conversation, the preposition goes to the end of the clause: "the people we work with", "the person I report to".
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: muito formal (só para reconhecer): the client **for whom** we work · the company **with which** we signed]

**PROF:** In very formal English — contracts, official letters — the preposition can come before "whom" or "which". Recognise it, but in meetings, say "the client we work for".
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ the people with who we work · ✔ the people we work with · ✔ the people with whom we work (formal)]
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "Which is why" follows the first idea without a long pause. And a preposition at the end is short and weak. Listen: You're the person I learned most from, which is why I wanted to talk to you.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I've started a master's degree, which means I can't travel every month.
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** The people we work with like short meetings.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Comment on the whole idea with "which".
[ECRÃ: The client signed the contract. (great news)] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: The client signed the contract, which is great news.]
[ECRÃ: The bridge near our office is closed for repairs in October. (we need to plan the trips carefully)] [PAUSA 7 s] [ECRÃ: The bridge is closed for repairs in October, which means we need to plan the trips carefully.]
[ECRÃ: She speaks Portuguese and Chinese. (she's in charge of the clients from Macau)] [PAUSA 7 s] [ECRÃ: She speaks Portuguese and Chinese, which is why she's in charge of the clients from Macau.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Think of a difficult conversation you had at work or with your family — or one you need to have. Explain the situation, how you started the conversation, and what happened. Use "which" for a whole idea at least twice. About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last year I had to tell my team that we couldn't take holidays in December, which nobody wanted to hear. I started by explaining the reason: our biggest client needed us, which meant overtime for everyone. I was honest, which helped a lot. In the end, we agreed on two days off each in January.]

**PROF (opinião):** Should a new team leader talk to each person alone before the first meeting? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — 1A):** Add extra information about Paulo: he has worked on Morro Azul's network since the start.
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Paulo, who has worked on Morro Azul's network since the start, is in charge of the installation plan.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! "Which" for a whole idea — "which is why", "which means" — and the preposition at the end: "the people we work with". Next lesson: how to give everyone a voice in a meeting.

## 1C — What's your take on this?

```
Curso: B2.1        Unidade: 1        Aula: 1C — What's your take on this?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue conduzir a conversa num grupo: dar a palavra, convidar quem
  não falou, cortar uma digressão com cortesia, retomar e resumir.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2, 4
Gramática-alvo: perguntas para dar a palavra (What's your take on this? How do you see it? Would you
  like to add anything?); Let's hear from…; imperativos suaves (Let's come back to… Let's park that.);
  resumir (So, where we are is… — expressão fixa)
Palavras-alvo: take (opinião), view, floor, park (um assunto), get back on track, item, timeline,
  the bottom line, sum up
Reciclagem: interromper e retomar (B1, 3C); 1A e 1B
Pronúncia: entoação convidativa a subir (Elsa, what's your take?), e firme e cortês a descer ao cortar uma
  digressão (Let's park that for now.)
Contexto: quarta-feira, 6 de Setembro, 9:00; a reunião semanal de coordenação; a Lerato preside; o Nuno,
  o Paulo, a Sílvia e a Elsa
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 1C; conversa guiada "Who's who in your team?"; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! A good leader doesn't talk the most — she makes sure the right people talk. Today, watch Lerato chair a meeting, and learn her phrases.

### 2. Situação (3 min)

[ECRÃ: quarta-feira, 6 de Setembro, 9:00; a sala de reuniões; a Lerato à cabeceira; no quadro: "1. Tômbwa proposal — timeline. 2. Training budget."]

**LERATO:** Morning, everyone. Two items today, and we finish by ten. First, the Tômbwa timeline. Nuno, would you like to start?
**NUNO:** Sure. We present to the Morro Azul board on the twelfth of October, which gives us five weeks. The first draft should be ready by the end of September.
**LERATO:** Thank you. Paulo, how do you see it from the technical side?
**PAULO:** It's tight but possible. Although — you know, when we did Namibe, the crane company called me at midnight, and the driver had —
**LERATO:** Sorry, Paulo — great story, but let's park that for now, or we won't finish by ten. Coming back to the timeline: is five weeks enough for the installation plan?
**PAULO:** Ha, fair enough. Yes, if Mopane sends the satellite part by the twentieth.
**LERATO:** Good. Elsa, we haven't heard from you yet. What's your take on the testing?
**ELSA:** Oh… well, I think we need two weeks for testing, not one. When I checked the Lobito sensors in June, two of them only failed in the second week.
**LERATO:** That's an important point. Thank you, Elsa. Nuno, can we find another week?
**NUNO:** Yes — if the draft is ready by the twenty-second, we have time.
**LERATO:** Great. Sílvia, would you like to add anything from the finance side?
**SÍLVIA:** Only that I need the cost table by the twenty-ninth, please.
**LERATO:** Noted. So, where we are is this: draft by the twenty-second, two weeks of testing, costs to Sílvia by the twenty-ninth. Let's move on to the training budget.

### 3. Língua (4 min 30 s)

**Dar a palavra**

[ECRÃ: Nuno, **would you like to start?** · Paulo, **how do you see it** from the technical side? · Elsa, **what's your take on** the testing? · Sílvia, **would you like to add anything?** · **Let's hear from** Elsa.]

**PROF:** Use the person's name, and a specific question. "What's your take on…?" means "What's your opinion?" — it's friendly and professional.
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Convidar quem não falou**

[ECRÃ: Elsa, **we haven't heard from you yet**. · I'd like to **hear your view** on this, Elsa. · **Anyone else?** · **Does anyone see it differently?**]

**PROF:** Quiet people often have the best ideas. Invite them by name, and ask an easy, specific question first.
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Cortar uma digressão com cortesia**

[ECRÃ: Sorry, Paulo — **great story, but let's park that for now**. · **Let's come back to** that later. · **Coming back to** the timeline… · **Let's get back on track.**]

**PROF:** When someone goes off the topic, be kind but clear: say something positive, then "let's park that". "Park" means "leave it for later".
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Resumir**

[ECRÃ: **So, where we are is this:** … · **To sum up so far:** … · **The bottom line is…**]

**PROF:** Before the next item, sum up the decisions — short, with numbers and dates.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** An invitation goes up, to sound open. Listen: Elsa, what's your take on the testing? [voz a subir]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Cutting a digression is firm and friendly, with the voice going down. Listen: Great story, but let's park that for now. [voz a descer]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat both: Elsa, what's your take on the testing? — Great story, but let's park that for now.
[PAUSA 8 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** What do you say?
[ECRÃ: Um colega ainda não falou: o Mário.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Mário, we haven't heard from you yet. What's your take on this?]
[ECRÃ: Uma colega conta uma história longa que não tem a ver com o assunto.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Sorry, Ana — interesting, but let's park that for now. Coming back to the budget…]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. You're chairing a meeting about the office move. Open it, give the floor to two people by name, cut one digression politely, invite a quiet colleague, and sum up. Say it as if you were in the room. About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Morning, everyone. One item today: the office move. João, would you like to start with the dates? … Thank you. Ana, how do you see it from the IT side? … Sorry, Ana, great story, but let's park that for now. Rui, we haven't heard from you yet — what's your take? … So, where we are is this: we move on the fifteenth, and IT needs two days.]

**PROF (opinião):** In your culture, is it normal to ask quiet colleagues for their opinion in a meeting? Is it a good idea? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 3C):** Lerato interrupts you to say something important. How do you go back to your point afterwards?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Anyway, as I was saying… / Going back to the timeline…]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! Give the floor by name, invite the quiet ones, park the digressions, and sum up. Now talk to the tutor: "Who's who in your team?". Next lesson: the words of teams and leadership.

## 1D — Teams and leadership

```
Curso: B2.1        Unidade: 1        Aula: 1D — Teams and leadership        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar palavras e colocações de equipas e liderança (delegate,
  take on, step up, morale, accountable), phrasal verbs de trabalho em equipa (take over, back up, follow
  through), dizer as relativas explicativas com pausas, e evitar os falsos amigos support e eventual.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: colocações (delegate a task, take on responsibility, boost morale, set priorities, give
  feedback, lead by example); phrasal verbs com mais de um sentido (take over, back up, follow through,
  step up, step down)
Palavras-alvo: delegate, take on, step up, step down, take over, back up, follow through, morale,
  accountable, responsibility, priority, workload, strengths, mentor, lead by example
Reciclagem: relativas explicativas (1A); which sobre uma frase (1B)
Pronúncia: pausas das relativas explicativas numa frase longa
Falsos amigos: support (≠ suportar → put up with, stand; support = apoiar); eventual (≠ eventual →
  possible; eventual = final, que acaba por acontecer)
Contexto: ecrãs com expressões e exemplos da história
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 1D; perguntas 7 e 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: the words of leadership — delegating, motivating, taking responsibility — and some phrasal verbs that every team leader uses.

### 2. Liderança e equipas (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ:]

| Palavra | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| delegate | Nuno delegates the testing to Elsa. (delegar) |
| take on | Elsa is ready to take on more responsibility. (assumir, aceitar) |
| workload | Paulo's workload is very heavy this month. (volume de trabalho) |
| priority | Our first priority is the proposal. |
| strengths | Elsa's strengths are testing and detail. (pontos fortes) |
| morale | Team morale is high after the Namibe project. (moral da equipa) |
| accountable | The team leader is accountable for the deadline. (responsável, que responde por) |
| mentor | Paulo was Nuno's mentor when he started. |

**PROF:** Repeat: delegate. Take on. Workload. Priority. Strengths. Morale. Accountable.
[PAUSA 8 s]

[ECRÃ: **morale** (o moral da equipa) ≠ **moral** (a lição de uma história; a moral)]

**PROF:** Notice: "morale", with an "e" at the end and the stress on "rale", is how a team feels. "Moral" is about right and wrong.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 3. Colocações e *phrasal verbs* (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: **delegate** a task · **take on** responsibility · **boost** morale · **set** priorities · **give** feedback · **lead** by example]
[PAUSA 4 s]

[ECRÃ:]

| *Phrasal verb* | Sentido 1 | Sentido 2 |
|---|---|---|
| take over | assumir (uma tarefa, de outra pessoa): Elsa took over the testing. | comprar (uma empresa): A bigger company took over Mopane's rival. |
| back up | apoiar: Paulo always backs me up in meetings. | fazer cópia de segurança: Back up the files every day. |
| follow through | levar até ao fim: A good leader follows through on promises. | — |
| step up | chegar-se à frente: When Lerato was ill, Nuno stepped up. | — |
| step down | sair de um cargo: The director stepped down in June. | — |

**PROF:** Many phrasal verbs have two meanings. "Back up" — support a person, or copy your files. "Take over" — take a task from someone, or buy a company. The context tells you which.
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 4. Pronúncia: pausas numa frase longa (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: Paulo, / who was my mentor when I started, / backs me up in every meeting, / which helps a lot.]

**PROF:** In a long sentence with extra information, make short pauses where the commas are. Listen: Paulo, [pausa] who was my mentor when I started, [pausa] backs me up in every meeting, [pausa] which helps a lot.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat.
[PAUSA 8 s]

### 5. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: **support** ≠ suportar]

**PROF:** "Support" means to help or encourage: "The team supports Nuno."
(PT: "Suportar" no sentido de aguentar diz-se "put up with" ou "stand": "Não suporto o barulho" → "I can't stand the noise".)
[PAUSA 4 s]

[ECRÃ: **eventual** ≠ eventual]

**PROF:** "Eventual" means final — what happens in the end: "the eventual winner".
(PT: "Eventual" no sentido de possível diz-se "possible": "um eventual atraso" → "a possible delay". Lembre-se do *eventually* do B1: "por fim".)
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 6. Prática e fecho (2 min)

**PROF:** Complete.
[ECRÃ: When the manager left, Elsa … the project.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: took over]
[ECRÃ: Remember to … your files before the update.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: back up]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Describe the best leader you've worked or studied with. What did this person do — delegate, give feedback, lead by example? Use at least one phrasal verb and one clause with "who" or "which". About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — The best leader I've had was my first manager, who's now retired. She delegated a lot, which scared me at first, but she always backed us up when something went wrong. She gave honest feedback every Friday, and she followed through on every promise. She really led by example.]

## 1F — Work & Life: The kick-off meeting

```
Curso: B2.1        Unidade: 1        Aula: 1F — The kick-off meeting        Tipo: Work & Life
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue conduzir a primeira reunião de uma equipa: apresentar as pessoas,
  dar a palavra, cortar uma digressão, delegar com razões e fechar com os próximos passos; e passar à equipa,
  em inglês, instruções da direcção escritas em português.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 2, 3, 4
Gramática-alvo: toda a da unidade: relativas explicativas; which sobre uma frase; dar a palavra; delegar (I'd like you to…,
  Who'd like to…?, Can you take that on?)
Palavras-alvo: kick-off, objective, role, milestone, draft, deadline, next steps, check-in
Reciclagem: future continuous (B1, 8C); going to (A2); 2.º condicional (B1, 4A)
Pronúncia: um fecho de reunião com pausas entre as tarefas e a voz a descer no fim
Mediação: o Nuno passa à equipa, em inglês, as instruções do Sr. Baptista, escritas em português
Contexto: segunda-feira, 11 de Setembro, 10:00; a sala de reuniões; o Nuno, o Paulo e a Elsa em Luanda; o
  Sipho e o Rajesh por videochamada, de Joanesburgo
Duração: 13 min
Verificação: Lab 1F; conversa livre "The kick-off meeting"
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today Nuno chairs his first meeting as a team leader. Watch how he opens, gives the floor, deals with a long story, delegates, and closes — and how he passes on his director's instructions.

### 2. Situação (5 min)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 11 de Setembro, 10:00; a sala de reuniões; o Paulo e a Elsa à mesa; o Sipho e o Rajesh no ecrã; no quadro: "Tômbwa proposal — kick-off. 1. Objective. 2. Roles. 3. Timeline. 4. Next steps."]

**NUNO:** Good morning, everyone, and welcome to our kick-off. Four items, and we'll finish by eleven. First, the objective. On the twelfth of October, we present our proposal to the Morro Azul board, which means we have exactly one month. Our objective is simple: to win the contract.
**NUNO:** Before we start, Mr Baptista sent me a short note this morning. It's in Portuguese, so let me give you the main points.

[ECRÃ: a nota, em português: "Nuno, para a equipa: 1) A proposta tem de incluir uma opção mais barata, sem satélite, porque o Femi, o director financeiro da Morro Azul, vai perguntar por ela. 2) Nada de promessas de prazos sem confirmar com o Paulo. 3) Quero ver o primeiro rascunho no dia 22. Boa sorte a todos. — J. Baptista"]

**NUNO:** Mr Baptista says three things. First, the proposal must include a cheaper option, without the satellite, because Femi, Morro Azul's finance director, will ask for one. Second, no promises about deadlines without checking with Paulo. And third, he wants to see the first draft on the twenty-second. He also wishes us good luck.
**PAULO:** I like rule number two!
**NUNO:** Ha — I thought you would. Second item: roles. Paulo, who knows Morro Azul's plants better than anyone, is in charge of the installation plan. Elsa, who did such good work on the Lobito sensors in June, will be in charge of testing. Sipho, whose team worked on the Namibe backup, takes the satellite design — he'll be working from Johannesburg. And Rajesh, who's doing the costs, will work closely with Sílvia.
**RAJESH:** Sounds good. Can I ask about the cheaper option? Without the satellite, the plant has no backup when the radio link goes down, which —
**PAULO:** Exactly, and in Namibe we lost thirty hours a month because of that! I remember one night when the —
**NUNO:** Sorry, Paulo — I know that story, and it's a good one, but let's keep it for the proposal itself: it's the perfect example for the board. Rajesh, please finish your point.
**RAJESH:** Thanks. I was saying: if we took out the satellite, the price would drop by about fifteen percent, but the risk would go up.
**NUNO:** Good point — we'll show both options, with the risk for each. Elsa, we haven't heard from you yet. How do you see the testing?
**ELSA:** I'd like to test both options at the Namibe plant, in the two weeks after the draft. It's very windy there at this time of year, which makes it the closest we can get to Tômbwa.
**NUNO:** That's a great idea. I'd like you to take that on with Paulo, if that's OK.
**ELSA:** Yes, I'd be happy to.
**NUNO:** And who'd like to write the one-page summary for the board? It needs someone who writes clearly.
**RAJESH:** I'll do it — I wrote the one for Durban.
**NUNO:** Third item, the timeline. Sipho, how long do you need for the satellite design?
**SIPHO:** About ten working days. I started on Friday, when the wind data came in — thanks, Nuno — so I can send it by the twentieth.
**NUNO:** Great. So, the next steps. Sipho: the satellite design by the twentieth. Paulo: the installation plan by the twenty-second. Paulo and Elsa: the tests in Namibe in the two weeks after that. Rajesh: the costs for both options by the twenty-ninth, to Sílvia, and the summary for the board. And I'm going to put the first draft together for Mr Baptista on the twenty-second. We'll have a check-in every Monday at ten. Anything I've missed?
**PAULO:** Only the coffee.
**NUNO:** Ha! I'll bring it next Monday. Thank you, everyone. Let's win this one.

### 3. Como se conduz uma primeira reunião (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o que o Nuno fez]

| Momento | Língua | Exemplo do Nuno |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Abrir com o objectivo e o tempo | Four items, and we'll finish by… · Our objective is… | Four items, and we'll finish by eleven. Our objective is simple: to win the contract. |
| 2. Passar a nota da direcção (mediação) | … says three things. First… | Mr Baptista says three things. First, the proposal must include a cheaper option… |
| 3. Apresentar os papéis | relativas explicativas | Elsa, who did such good work on the Lobito sensors in June, will be in charge of testing. |
| 4. Gerir a conversa | Sorry, … let's keep it for… · …, please finish your point. · We haven't heard from you yet. | Sorry, Paulo — let's keep it for the proposal itself. |
| 5. Delegar com uma razão | I'd like you to… · Who'd like to…? · …, which… | I'd like you to take that on with Paulo. Who'd like to write the summary for the board? |
| 6. Fechar | So, the next steps… · Anything I've missed? | Sipho: the satellite design by the twentieth… |

**PROF:** Look at how Nuno cuts Paulo's story: he says something positive — "it's a good one" — and he finds a use for it: "the perfect example for the board". Paulo doesn't feel ignored. And notice the close: each person, each task, each date — then "Anything I've missed?".
[PAUSA 6 s]

**A mediação**

**PROF:** Mr Baptista's note is written to Nuno, in Portuguese, with numbers. Nuno passes it on in the order of the note, with "First, Second, Third", and he keeps the reason in the first point ("because… will ask for one"). He keeps the warm ending: "He also wishes us good luck."
(PT: Ao passar instruções da direcção a uma equipa, mantenha a ordem e o tom — firme, mas humano — e diga quem escreveu. "Nada de promessas" fica mais natural como "no promises", não como "nothing of promises".)
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** When you close a meeting, say each person's name, a short pause, then the task and the date. Listen.
**PROF:** Sipho: [pausa] the satellite design by the twentieth. [pausa] Rajesh: [pausa] the costs by the twenty-ninth.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Now you. Close a meeting with two tasks for two people.
[PAUSA 10 s]

### 5. A sua vez (3 min 30 s)

**PROF:** You're starting a new project — at work, at school, or in your community. Chair the kick-off: give the objective and the time, introduce three people with extra information and their roles, and close with the next steps. About one and a half minutes.
[PAUSA 90 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Good morning, and welcome. Three items, and we'll finish by ten. Our objective is to open the new branch in Huambo by March. Ana, who managed the Benguela opening, will lead the project. Rui, whose team does our IT, will set up the systems. And Marta, who knows the local suppliers, will handle the furniture. So, the next steps: Ana, the timeline by Friday; Rui, the IT plan by the twentieth; Marta, three quotes by the end of the month. Anything I've missed?]

**PROF:** Now the mediation. Pass on this note from your director to your team, in English.
[ECRÃ: "Para a equipa: 1) As despesas de viagem passam a ser aprovadas por mim, antes da viagem. 2) Quem trabalhar no sábado tem um dia de folga na semana seguinte. Obrigada pelo esforço. — A directora"]
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Our director says two things. First, travel expenses now need her approval before the trip. Second, if you work on a Saturday, you get a day off the next week. She also thanks everyone for the hard work.]

**PROF (opinião):** In your opinion, what's the most important thing a new team leader should do in the first meeting? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Fantastic! You can now introduce a team with extra information, give everyone a voice, delegate with reasons, and close with clear next steps. Go to the tutor for the conversation "The kick-off meeting". Next unit: Presentations.
