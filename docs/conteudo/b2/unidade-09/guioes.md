# B2.2 · Unidade 9 — Mediation at work · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções: as dos [guiões da unidade 1 do B2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2, do B1, do B2.1 e das unidades 6 a 8 do B2. Novo: verbos de relato e os seus padrões — *suggest, recommend* (+ *-ing* ou + *that*), *insist on* (+ *-ing*) e *insist that*, *admit, deny* (+ *-ing* ou + *that*), *warn, advise, urge, remind, persuade* (+ pessoa + *to*), *warn someone not to*, e os do B1 revistos (*tell, ask, promise, agree, refuse, offer*); sintetizar (escolher, agrupar, reformular); avaliar ao passar a informação com expressões fixas (*What worries me is… What concerns me is… The good news is… It's worth noting that… The key point, in my view, is…*); a passiva de relato só para reconhecer (*The builders are said to be behind. It is reported that…*).

**Não usar nesta unidade:** *wish / if only*, *would* para hábitos do passado, o *past perfect continuous* e os condicionais mistos (unidade 10). As orações clivadas, como estrutura de ênfase ensinada e variada (*It was Paulo who…, All I want is…*), ficam para o C1; aqui entram só as expressões fixas da 9D.

**Nomes de empresas e lugares:** a Kalandula Tech, a Mopane Systems, a Morro Azul Energia, a Kuvango Construções e a central do Tômbwa são fictícias. As actas da 9G foram escritas pela ProEnglish.

**Personagens:** **Nuno**; **Paulo Mendes**; **Elsa Chilombo**; **Lerato**; **Thandi** (Mopane); **Hannah** (Morro Azul, no Tômbwa); **Ruth Mwangi** (segurança da Morro Azul, B2 6); **Kgosi** (Mopane, B2 6E); **Celeste Kiala**, **Ingrid Solberg** e **Femi Adeyemi** (administração da Morro Azul); os técnicos **Celso** e **Matilde**. Novos e fictícios: **Eng. Benvindo Sapalo** (angolano, director da obra da Kuvango; fala português e inglês) e **Eng. Helena Teixeira** (angolana, engenheira da Morro Azul na obra; fala português e inglês).

**A história:** fim de Maio e início de Junho de 2029. A sala de controlo do Tômbwa devia estar pronta em Julho (B2 2C), e os testes da rede deviam começar em Junho. Na segunda, 28 de Maio, o Nuno relata à Lerato uma chamada difícil (9A). Na quarta, 30, passa aos técnicos os avisos da Ruth depois da visita de segurança (9B). Na sexta, 1 de Junho, a Lerato ensina-o a resumir o relatório de testes da Elsa (9C). Na segunda, 4, o Nuno e o Paulo avaliam os primeiros resultados (9D). Na terça, 5, há uma reunião de coordenação em inglês na obra (9F). Na quarta, 6, uma reunião de obra em português decide o que fazer (9G). Na quinta, 7, o Nuno faz a síntese para a administração da Morro Azul (9G).

**O atraso da sala de controlo (igual em todas as aulas em que aparece):**
- A sala de controlo devia ficar pronta no fim de Julho; a Kuvango diz agora que só fica pronta a 31 de Agosto.
- A Kuvango nega ter causado o atraso: diz que o cimento chegou com três semanas de atraso (B2 6B) e que choveu dois dias em Maio. Admite que a equipa do telhado começou tarde.
- O Paulo propõe uma sala de controlo provisória num contentor climatizado, para os testes começarem a 18 de Junho.
- A decisão (9G): a Kuvango trabalha aos sábados a partir de 9 de Junho; o contentor é aceite, e o custo (cerca de 6 000 dólares) é dividido a meias entre a Kuvango e a Kalandula; a Eng. Helena Teixeira exige um técnico de segurança na obra aos sábados; nova data da sala de controlo: 31 de Agosto, com uma reunião de ponto de situação todas as quartas.
- A avaliação do Nuno: a abertura da central (fim de 2029) não está em risco, desde que o contentor funcione; o que o preocupa é o período de testes, que fica com menos margem.

## 9A — She suggested waiting

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9A — She suggested waiting        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue relatar o que outros disseram com suggest, recommend, insist
  on, admit e deny, com os padrões certos (+ -ing ou + that).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: suggest + -ing / suggest that + sujeito + verbo (She suggested waiting. She suggested that
  we wait.); recommend + -ing / that; insist on + -ing / insist that; admit + -ing / that; deny + -ing /
  that; ✘ suggest someone to; revisão do B1: tell / ask someone to, said that
Palavras-alvo: delay, control room, roof, cement, rain, booked, container, honest, object, arguing, call
Reciclagem: discurso indirecto (B1, 7A–7C); was supposed to (B2, 6B)
Pronúncia: o acento no verbo de relato (She suggested… He denied…) e o -ing ligado
Contexto: segunda-feira, 28 de Maio de 2029, 17:00; o gabinete da Lerato; o Nuno relata uma chamada da
  manhã com a Hannah, a Thandi e o Eng. Sapalo
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 9A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 9
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! This unit is about mediation: passing information from one person to another — and from one language to another. Today: how to report what people said with precise verbs — not just "said".

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 28 de Maio, 17:00; o gabinete da Lerato; o Nuno sentado, com o caderno; a Lerato com um café]

**LERATO:** So, how was the call this morning? I heard it was tense.
**NUNO:** It was. Mr Sapalo from Kuvango told us that the control room won't be ready until the end of August, not July.
**LERATO:** August! Did he say why?
**NUNO:** He denied causing the delay. He insisted that the cement had arrived three weeks late, and that it had rained for two days in May.
**LERATO:** Both true, I suppose.
**NUNO:** Yes. But then Hannah asked about the roof, and he admitted starting the roof late. His team was working on the road.
**LERATO:** Good — at least he was honest. And Hannah?
**NUNO:** Hannah suggested moving the tests to September. Thandi didn't like that at all. She insisted on keeping June, because Mopane's engineers are booked for July and August.
**LERATO:** And what did you say?
**NUNO:** I didn't say much, to be honest. But Paulo suggested that we use a temporary control room — a container with air conditioning — so the tests can start on time.
**LERATO:** That's clever. Did anyone object?
**NUNO:** Mr Sapalo recommended waiting until Wednesday's site meeting before deciding. Everybody agreed to that.
**LERATO:** OK. Next time, though, don't stay quiet. You're the project leader.
**NUNO:** You're right. I admit I was a bit lost in the middle of four people arguing.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Verbos de relato e os seus padrões**

[ECRÃ:]

| Verbo | + -ing | + that | O que NÃO se diz |
|---|---|---|---|
| suggest | She **suggested moving** the tests. | He **suggested that** we **use** a container. | ✘ She suggested us to move… |
| recommend | He **recommended waiting**. | He **recommended that** we **wait**. | ✘ He recommended us to wait… |
| insist | She **insisted on keeping** June. | He **insisted that** the cement **had arrived** late. | ✘ She insisted to keep… |
| admit | He **admitted starting** the roof late. | He **admitted that** they **had started** late. | |
| deny | He **denied causing** the delay. | He **denied that** it **was** their fault. | ✘ He denied to cause… |

**PROF:** These verbs say more than "said": they show the speaker's intention — a proposal, a demand, a confession, a refusal. Each one has its pattern. The most common mistake for Portuguese speakers: "She suggested us to…". In English, "suggest" never takes a person plus "to".
[PAUSA 7 s]

[ECRÃ: "Ela sugeriu-nos que esperássemos." → ✔ She suggested that we wait. / She suggested waiting. · ✘ She suggested us to wait.]
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Com o que já sabe (B1)**

[ECRÃ: He **told** us **that**… · She **asked** me **to**… · They **agreed to**… · He **promised to**…]

**PROF:** "Told" and "asked" are still useful. But in a report to a manager, "admitted", "denied" or "insisted" tell the whole story in one word.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Stress the reporting verb — it carries the meaning. Listen: He denied causing the delay. She insisted on keeping June.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: He admitted starting the roof late.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Paulo suggested that we use a temporary control room.
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Report it with the verb in brackets.
[ECRÃ: Ana: "Let's take a taxi." (suggest)] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: Ana suggested taking a taxi.]
[ECRÃ: Rui: "I didn't break the printer!" (deny)] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: Rui denied breaking the printer.]
[ECRÃ: Marta: "Yes, I forgot to send the invoice." (admit)] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: Marta admitted forgetting to send the invoice.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Think of a recent discussion — at work, at school, in your family. Report it to someone who wasn't there, using at least four different reporting verbs: who suggested what, who insisted on something, who admitted or denied something. About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last Sunday, my family discussed where to spend Christmas. My mother suggested going to Huambo, to my grandmother's house. My brother insisted on staying in Luanda, because of his work. My father admitted that he didn't want to drive twelve hours. In the end, my sister recommended inviting my grandmother here — and everyone agreed.]

**PROF (opinião):** In a meeting, is it better to report exactly what people said, or just the decisions? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B2, 6B):** When was the control room supposed to be ready?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: It was supposed to be ready at the end of July.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! "Suggested waiting", "insisted on keeping", "admitted starting", "denied causing" — and never "suggested us to". Next lesson: "warn", "advise", "remind" — verbs with a person.

## 9B — He warned us not to…

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9B — He warned us not to…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue relatar avisos, conselhos e pedidos com warn, advise, urge,
  remind e persuade + pessoa + to, também na negativa.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: verbo + pessoa + (not) to + infinitivo: warn / advise / urge / remind / persuade / encourage
  someone (not) to do something; warn someone that…; revisão: tell / ask someone (not) to (B1, 7C);
  contraste com suggest (sem pessoa + to)
Palavras-alvo: safety visit, harness, heat, water, break, shade, supervisor, height, lunch break, check,
  report
Reciclagem: tell / ask someone to (B1, 7C)
Pronúncia: "not to" ligado e com o acento no "not" (warned us not to)
Contexto: quarta-feira, 30 de Maio, 7:00; a obra do Tômbwa; o Nuno passa à equipa os avisos da Ruth, depois
  da visita de segurança da semana anterior
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 9B; conversa guiada "What did they say?"; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! When you pass on a warning or advice, the reporting verb matters: "warned", "advised", "reminded" — each one tells the listener how serious it is. Today: verbs with a person and "to".

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: quarta-feira, 30 de Maio, 7:00; a obra; o Nuno, o Kgosi, o Celso, a Matilde e outros técnicos em círculo; sol forte; garrafas de água]

**NUNO:** Morning, everyone. Ruth visited last week and sent her report yesterday. Let me give you the main points.
**KGOSI:** Good or bad?
**NUNO:** Mostly good. She said the new exclusion zone works well. But she warned us not to get relaxed — near misses usually happen when people think everything is fine.
**CELSO:** Fair enough.
**NUNO:** She also reminded us to drink water every hour. Two people felt sick last week in the heat. She advised everyone to take a ten-minute break in the shade every two hours.
**MATILDE:** Every two hours? The supervisors won't like that.
**NUNO:** I know. But Ruth urged the supervisors to respect it. And Kgosi, she asked you to check the harnesses every morning, not every week.
**KGOSI:** OK. That's fair. Anything else?
**NUNO:** One more thing. Ruth warned that the afternoon wind is getting stronger in June. So she encouraged us to finish the high work before two o'clock.
**KGOSI:** That's what I've been telling my team for weeks!
**NUNO:** Then you were right. And she persuaded the builders to move their lunch break, so the crane isn't working when your team is on the masts.
**KGOSI:** Now that's good news.
**NUNO:** So: water every hour, a break every two hours, harnesses every morning, high work before two. Questions?
**MATILDE:** No. But thank you for explaining it. Reading the report in English would take me an hour.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Verbo + pessoa + *to***

[ECRÃ:]

| Verbo | Exemplo | Sentido |
|---|---|---|
| warn | She **warned us not to get** relaxed. | avisar de um perigo |
| remind | She **reminded us to drink** water. | lembrar |
| advise | She **advised everyone to take** a break. | aconselhar |
| urge | She **urged the supervisors to respect** it. | pedir com insistência |
| persuade | She **persuaded the builders to move** their lunch. | convencer (e conseguiu) |
| encourage | She **encouraged us to finish** before two. | incentivar |

**PROF:** These verbs all take a person, then "to". For the negative, put "not" before "to": "warned us not to get relaxed".
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ: She warned us not to get relaxed. ("to not get" também se ouve, mas "not to" é a forma mais segura) · ✘ She advised to us to take a break. · ✔ She advised us to take a break.]
[PAUSA 4 s]

***Warn that***

[ECRÃ: She **warned that** the wind is getting stronger. (aviso sem pessoa)]
[PAUSA 4 s]

***Suggest* não funciona assim**

[ECRÃ: ✔ She advised us to take a break. · ✘ She suggested us to take a break. → ✔ She suggested taking a break.]

**PROF:** Remember 9A: "suggest" and "recommend" don't take a person plus "to". "Advise" does. They mean almost the same, but the grammar is different.
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In "not to", stress the "not" — it's the most important word in a warning. Listen: She warned us not to get relaxed.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: She warned us not to get relaxed.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** She reminded us to drink water every hour.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Report it with the verb in brackets.
[ECRÃ: The doctor: "Don't eat so much salt." (warn)] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: The doctor warned me not to eat so much salt.]
[ECRÃ: My manager: "Don't forget the meeting at nine." (remind)] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: My manager reminded me about the meeting at nine. / reminded me not to forget the meeting.]
[ECRÃ: My friend: "You should apply for the job." (encourage)] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: My friend encouraged me to apply for the job.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Pass on the advice someone gave you — a doctor, a teacher, a manager, a parent. Use at least four of these verbs: warn, advise, remind, urge, persuade, encourage. About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — When I started my first job, my manager gave me great advice. She warned me not to promise dates I couldn't keep. She advised me to write everything down after meetings, and she reminded me to back up my files every Friday. She also encouraged me to ask questions — and she persuaded me to take an English course!]

**PROF (opinião):** Is it easier to follow advice from a manager or from a friend? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 7C):** Ruth's report: "Kgosi, please check the harnesses every morning." Report it with "ask".
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: She asked Kgosi to check the harnesses every morning.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! "Warned us not to", "reminded us to", "advised everyone to" — verb, person, to. Try the tutor's conversation "What did they say?". Next lesson: how to summarise a long document.

## 9C — The key point is…

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9C — The key point is…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue sintetizar um documento ou uma reunião: escolher o essencial,
  agrupar ideias e reformular por palavras próprias, para um público diferente.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: marcadores de síntese (In short… The key point is… Basically… In other words… To put it
  simply… The report covers three areas… Overall…); agrupar (Most of… The rest… Apart from…); reformular
  (paraphrase); a passiva para dar relevo aos resultados
Palavras-alvo: summary, key point, area, pre-test, technical, paraphrase, in short, in other words,
  basically, signal, delay, sensor, packet loss
Reciclagem: números aproximados (B2, 2E)
Pronúncia: a voz a descer no fim de cada ponto de uma síntese
Contexto: sexta-feira, 1 de Junho, 15:00; o gabinete da Lerato; o Nuno tem o relatório de testes da Elsa (12
  páginas) e tem de o explicar à Hannah em dois minutos
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 9C; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Summarising isn't making a text shorter — it's choosing what matters for your listener. Today: how to turn twelve pages into three points.

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: sexta-feira, 1 de Junho, 15:00; o gabinete da Lerato; em cima da mesa, um relatório de 12 páginas com gráficos: "Pre-tests — radio link and satellite — Tômbwa — Elsa Chilombo"]

**NUNO:** Elsa's pre-test report. Twelve pages. I have to explain it to Hannah on Monday, and she has two minutes.
**LERATO:** OK. First question: what does Hannah need to know?
**NUNO:** Everything?
**LERATO:** No. Hannah needs to know if the network will work, and what could go wrong. Not how the antennas were aligned.
**NUNO:** Fair enough. Let me try. The report covers three areas: the radio link, the satellite and the sensors.
**LERATO:** Good start. Now, for each area, one sentence.
**NUNO:** The radio link: the signal is strong — about ninety-nine percent of the time it's perfect. The satellite: it works, but there's a short delay when it switches on, about four seconds. The sensors: most of them are fine, apart from two, which send data late.
**LERATO:** Better. Now say it like Hannah would: no "packet loss", no "latency".
**NUNO:** In other words: the network works, the backup works with a tiny delay, and two sensors need fixing.
**LERATO:** Perfect. That's your summary. And the key point?
**NUNO:** The key point is that nothing in the report stops us from starting the tests on the eighteenth.
**LERATO:** Excellent. Now you can give her the twelve pages — after the two minutes.
**NUNO:** Basically: choose, group, rephrase.
**LERATO:** In short, yes!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Três passos**

[ECRÃ:]

| Passo | Pergunta | Língua |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Escolher | O que precisa o ouvinte de saber? | The key point is… · What matters here is… |
| 2. Agrupar | Que ideias vão juntas? | The report covers three areas… · Most of… apart from… |
| 3. Reformular | Como digo isto por palavras simples? | In other words… · To put it simply… · Basically… |

**PROF:** The listener decides what goes into a summary. The same report becomes three different summaries for an engineer, a manager and a client.
[PAUSA 6 s]

**Reformular: do técnico ao simples**

[ECRÃ: "packet loss below 1%" → **the signal is almost always perfect** · "4-second switchover latency" → **a tiny delay when the backup starts** · "2 nodes with delayed telemetry" → **two sensors send their data late**]

**PROF:** Paraphrasing is the heart of mediation. Don't translate words; translate meaning.
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Fechar a síntese**

[ECRÃ: **In short,** … · **Overall,** … · **So, basically,** …]
[PAUSA 4 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ Resuming, … (= recomeçando) · ✔ In short, … / To sum up, …]
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In a summary, each point ends with the voice going down — it sounds sure and complete. Listen: The network works. [a descer] The backup works, with a tiny delay. [a descer] Two sensors need fixing. [a descer]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat.
[PAUSA 9 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Paraphrase in simple words.
[ECRÃ: "The project is experiencing significant budgetary constraints."] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: In other words, the project doesn't have enough money.]
[ECRÃ: "Attendance has decreased by approximately 40% year on year."] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: To put it simply, almost half the people who came last year didn't come this year.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Summarise a meeting or a class you had this week for someone who wasn't there: the main areas, one sentence for each, and the key point. Use "in other words" once. About one and a half minutes.
[PAUSA 90 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — On Tuesday we had our monthly sales meeting. It covered three areas: the results, the new product and the holidays. The results were good — about ten percent above the target. The new product launches in July, but the boxes haven't arrived yet; in other words, the date could change. And the holidays: everyone has to choose their dates by Friday. The key point is that July will be busy, so nobody should plan a holiday then.]

**PROF (opinião):** Is it harder to summarise something you know very well, or something you know little about? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B2, 2E):** "The radio link works ninety-nine point two percent of the time." Say it approximately.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: It works almost all the time — about ninety-nine percent.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! Choose, group, rephrase — and say the key point. Next lesson: how to add your own view when you pass on information.

## 9D — What worries me is…

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9D — What worries me is…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue avaliar e comentar ao passar informação, separando os factos da
  sua opinião (What worries me is… The good news is… It's worth noting that… In my view, the key point is…).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: expressões fixas de avaliação: What worries / concerns me is (that)… · What I like about it
  is… · The good / bad news is (that)… · It's worth noting / mentioning that… · The key point, in my view,
  is… · On balance, … ; separar facto e opinião (The report says… — In my view…); graus de certeza (B2, 5A)
Palavras-alvo: results, margin, risk, cost, container, offer, share, half and half, on balance, testing
Reciclagem: previsões graduadas (be likely to, B2, 5A)
Pronúncia: uma pausa curta depois de "What worries me is…" e de "The good news is…"
Contexto: segunda-feira, 4 de Junho, 10:00; a sala provisória da obra; o Nuno e o Paulo preparam o que vão
  dizer à Hannah sobre os testes e o atraso da sala de controlo
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 9D; perguntas 7 e 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! A good mediator doesn't only pass on facts — they also say what the facts mean. Today: how to add your view clearly, without mixing it with the facts.

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 4 de Junho, 10:00; a sala provisória; o Nuno e o Paulo com o relatório da Elsa e um calendário na parede, com "Control room: 31 Aug?" escrito a vermelho]

**PAULO:** So, what do we tell Hannah?
**NUNO:** Facts first. Elsa's pre-tests are good: the network works, the backup works with a small delay, and two sensors need fixing.
**PAULO:** The good news is that the two sensors are a ten-minute job. Celso can do it today.
**NUNO:** Great. Then the control room. Sapalo says the end of August.
**PAULO:** What worries me is the testing. If we wait for the real control room, we'll only test for three months before the plant opens.
**NUNO:** That's tight, but it's what we planned. What concerns me more is that we'd have no margin. If anything goes wrong in September, there's no time to fix it.
**PAULO:** Exactly. That's why the container is important.
**NUNO:** OK. It's worth noting that the container costs about six thousand dollars. Femi will ask who pays.
**PAULO:** Kuvango should pay. It's their delay.
**NUNO:** Partly. They're likely to say the cement was the supplier's fault. On balance, I think we should offer to share it — half and half. A fight about six thousand dollars could cost us much more.
**PAULO:** Hmm. I don't love it, but you may be right.
**NUNO:** So: the key point, in my view, is that the opening date isn't at risk — as long as the container works. What worries me is the margin, not the date.
**PAULO:** That's the message. Clear and honest.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Expressões para avaliar**

[ECRÃ:]

| Para | Língua |
|---|---|
| uma preocupação | **What worries me is** the testing. · **What concerns me more is that** we'd have no margin. |
| uma boa notícia | **The good news is that** the sensors are a ten-minute job. |
| uma má notícia | **The bad news is that** the room will be late. |
| um pormenor importante | **It's worth noting that** the container costs about six thousand dollars. |
| a conclusão | **On balance,** … · **The key point, in my view, is that** … |

**PROF:** These expressions put the important idea at the end of the sentence, after a little pause — so the listener is ready for it. Learn them as fixed blocks.
[PAUSA 6 s]

**Separar facto e opinião**

[ECRÃ: Facto: **The report says** the network works. · **Sapalo says** the end of August. — Opinião: **In my view,** the date isn't at risk. · **What worries me is** the margin.]

**PROF:** In a briefing, the listener must always know: is this a fact, or your opinion? Give the facts first, then your view — and mark it.
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ What worries me it's the testing. · ✔ What worries me is the testing. · ✘ The good new is… · ✔ The good news is… (news é sempre singular)]
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Pause after the block, and stress what comes next. Listen: What worries me is… [pausa] the margin. The good news is… [pausa] it's a ten-minute job.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat both.
[PAUSA 8 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Add your view with the expression in brackets.
[ECRÃ: Facto: o projecto está dentro do orçamento, mas a equipa está cansada. (The good news… What worries me…)] [PAUSA 8 s] [ECRÃ: The good news is that we're within budget. What worries me is that the team is tired.]
[ECRÃ: Facto: o novo escritório fica longe do centro. (It's worth noting…)] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: It's worth noting that the new office is far from the centre.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Pass on a piece of news from your work, your studies or your town — facts first — and then add your view: one good thing, one thing that worries you, and your conclusion "on balance". About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Our company announced on Monday that we're moving to a new office in Talatona in September. It's bigger, and it has parking. The good news is that we'll finally have meeting rooms. What worries me is the traffic: for half of the team, the journey will be longer. It's worth noting that the rent is the same. On balance, I think it's a good move — if the company helps with transport.]

**PROF (opinião):** When you pass on bad news, is it better to start with the good news or with the bad? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B2, 5A):** How sure is Nuno that Kuvango will blame the supplier? Find his words.
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: "They're likely to say the cement was the supplier's fault." — quite sure.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Facts first, then your view — with "What worries me is", "The good news is", "It's worth noting that", "On balance". Next lesson: the words of reports and minutes.

## 9E — Reports and minutes

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9E — Reports and minutes        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar o vocabulário de relatórios e actas (minutes, agenda,
  action points, findings, outcome, attendees), os verbos de relato com os padrões certos, reconhecer a
  passiva de relato (It is said that…), usar a entoação de resumo, e evitar os falsos amigos advise e
  reunion.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 2
Gramática-alvo: colocações (take the minutes, circulate the minutes, raise an issue, reach a decision,
  agree on, follow up on an action point); verbos de relato agrupados por padrão; a passiva de relato só
  para reconhecer (It is said / reported / expected that…; The builders are said to be behind.)
Palavras-alvo: agenda, minutes, attendees, apologies, item, action point, outcome, findings, deadline,
  owner (of an action), AOB (any other business), circulate
Reciclagem: verbos de relato (9A, 9B); síntese (9C)
Pronúncia: entoação de resumo: descida no fim de cada ponto de uma lista de decisões

  meeting; reunion = reencontro)
Contexto: ecrãs com uma acta em inglês e exemplos da história
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 9E; perguntas 9 e 10 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Meetings produce documents: agendas before, minutes after. Today: their words — and two false friends that can cause serious misunderstandings at work.

### 2. Actas e reuniões (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: uma acta curta, em inglês: "Minutes — Tômbwa coordination meeting — 5 June 2029. Attendees: H. Becker (chair), B. Sapalo, Kgosi M., P. Mendes. Apologies: E. Chilombo. Item 1: Control room. Outcome: new date to be confirmed on 6 June. Action point: P. Mendes to send the container quote by 6 June. AOB: none."]

| Palavra | Sentido |
|---|---|
| agenda | ordem de trabalhos |
| minutes | acta |
| attendees | presentes |
| apologies | ausências justificadas |
| item | ponto da ordem de trabalhos |
| outcome | resultado, decisão |
| action point | tarefa com responsável e prazo |
| AOB (any other business) | outros assuntos |

**PROF:** Repeat: agenda. Minutes. Attendees. Item. Outcome. Action point.
[PAUSA 8 s]

[ECRÃ: **take** the minutes (fazer a acta) · **circulate** the minutes (enviar a todos) · **raise** an issue · **reach** a decision · **follow up on** an action point]
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 3. Os verbos de relato, por padrão (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ:]

| Padrão | Verbos |
|---|---|
| + -ing / + that | suggest, recommend, admit, deny |
| + on + -ing / + that | insist |
| + pessoa + to | warn, advise, urge, remind, persuade, encourage, tell, ask |
| + to | agree, refuse, offer, promise |

**PROF:** When you write or speak minutes, these verbs make them precise. "The builders denied causing the delay" says much more than "the builders talked about the delay".
[PAUSA 6 s]

**A passiva de relato — só para reconhecer**

[ECRÃ: **It is said that** the builders are behind. · **It is reported that** two workers felt sick. · The builders **are said to be** behind. · The plant **is expected to open** in December.]

**PROF:** In formal reports and in the news, you'll see this: "It is said that…", "is expected to…". It means "people say" or "the expectation is". You don't need to use it yet — but you need to understand it.
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 4. Pronúncia: a entoação de resumo (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** When you read out the decisions of a meeting, each one ends with the voice going down. Listen: Item one: the control room. [descida] Outcome: a new date tomorrow. [descida] Action point: Paulo sends the container quote. [descida]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat.
[PAUSA 9 s]

### 5. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: **advise** ≠ avisar]

**PROF:** "Advise" means to give advice: "Ruth advised us to take breaks."
(PT: "Avisar" é "warn" (de um perigo) ou "let someone know" (informar): "Avisa-me quando chegares" → "Let me know when you arrive". "Avisei-o do risco" → "I warned him about the risk".)
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: **reunion** ≠ reunião]

**PROF:** A "reunion" is a meeting of people who haven't seen each other for a long time: "a family reunion", "a school reunion".
(PT: Uma "reunião" de trabalho é "a meeting": "a reunião de obra" → "the site meeting".)
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 6. Prática e fecho (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Complete.
[ECRÃ: Who's going to … the minutes today?] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: take]
[ECRÃ: "Avisa-me se houver problemas." → Let me … if there are problems.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: know]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Report the minutes of a meeting you attended — at work, at school, in your church or your building: the attendees, two items, the outcome of each, and two action points with an owner and a deadline. Use at least two reporting verbs. About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last Thursday we had the monthly meeting of our building. Twelve people attended. Item one: the water tank. The administrator admitted that the pump was old, and he recommended replacing it. Outcome: we agreed to pay half each month. Item two: the parking. Mrs Dias insisted on painting numbers on the spaces. Action points: the administrator gets three quotes by the fifteenth; Mrs Dias organises the painting.]

**PROF (opinião):** Are minutes useful, or does nobody read them? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF:** Well done! Next lesson: a real site meeting — four people, four positions.

## 9F — Real English: The site meeting

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9F — The site meeting        Tipo: Real English

Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 2
Língua: A1, A2, B1, B2.1, as unidades 6 a 8 e a unidade 9 até à 9E, sem estruturas novas

  vento e um gerador ao fundo

```

### 1. Apresentação (30 s)

**PROF:** On Tuesday morning, the weekly coordination meeting at Tômbwa is in English, because Kgosi and Hannah don't speak Portuguese. Nuno is in Luanda, so he'll read the minutes later. Listen for what each person proposes, refuses, admits or insists on.

### 2. Primeira escuta: a reunião (5 min)

[ECRÃ: terça-feira, 5 de Junho, 8:00; a sala provisória da obra; a Hannah à cabeceira; o Eng. Sapalo, o Kgosi e o Paulo; o vento a bater na janela]

**PROF:** First: who proposes the container, and who refuses to work at night?

**HANNAH:** Good morning. One main item today: the control room. Mr Sapalo, the floor is yours.
**SAPALO:** Thank you. I'll be direct. The control room will be ready on the thirty-first of August. Not before.
**HANNAH:** That's a month late.
**SAPALO:** I know. But please understand: the cement arrived three weeks late, and that wasn't our fault. And we lost two days to rain in May.
**HANNAH:** And the roof? Your team started it late.
**SAPALO:** That's true. I admit it — we had the team on the road. That's maybe a week of the delay. Not a month.
**KGOSI:** Can I come in? My problem is different. My engineers are free in June, but they're booked on another project in July and August. If we can't test in June, we lose them.
**PAULO:** Then here's an idea. We put a temporary control room in a container — with air conditioning and a generator — next to the masts. Elsa's team can test from there from the eighteenth.
**HANNAH:** How much would that cost?
**PAULO:** About six thousand dollars, with the air conditioning. I'll send a quote by tomorrow.
**SAPALO:** Kuvango won't pay for it alone. I want to be clear about that.
**KGOSI:** And what about working at night on the control room? You'd finish faster.
**SAPALO:** No. Absolutely not. Night work on a roof, in this wind? I'm not putting my people at risk. Saturdays, maybe. Nights, never.
**HANNAH:** I agree with Mr Sapalo on that. Ruth would never accept night work at height.
**KGOSI:** Fair enough. Saturdays, then.
**HANNAH:** OK. So: Paulo sends the container quote by tomorrow; Mr Sapalo checks if his team can work on Saturdays; and we decide everything at tomorrow's site meeting with Helena Teixeira. I'll circulate the minutes this afternoon.

[ECRÃ: Sapalo — admitiu ter começado o telhado tarde; negou que o atraso seja todo culpa da Kuvango; recusou trabalhar à noite; recusou pagar o contentor sozinho · Paulo — propôs um contentor climatizado; ofereceu-se para enviar o orçamento · Kgosi — propôs trabalho nocturno; aceitou os sábados · Hannah — concordou com o Sapalo sobre a noite; marcou a decisão para quarta]

### 3. Segunda escuta: os pormenores (3 min)

1. When will the control room be ready, according to Mr Sapalo? (on the thirty-first of August ✓ / at the end of July / in September)
2. What does Mr Sapalo admit? (His team started the roof late. ✓ / The cement was their fault. / It rained for a month.)
3. Why does Kgosi need to test in June? (His engineers are booked on another project in July and August. ✓ / The client insists. / The container is ready.)
4. How much would the container cost? (about six thousand dollars ✓ / about sixty thousand dollars / about six hundred dollars)
5. Why does Mr Sapalo refuse night work? (It's dangerous on a roof in the wind. ✓ / It's too expensive. / His team is on the road.)
6. When will the final decision be made? (at tomorrow's site meeting ✓ / today / on Saturday)

*(Cada resposta repete-se; depois aparecem as perguntas, com 8 s para responder.)*

### 4. Terceira escuta: como disseram (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Listen and repeat.
**PROF:** That's true. I admit it. [PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Kuvango won't pay for it alone. I want to be clear about that. [PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** Saturdays, maybe. Nights, never. [PAUSA 4 s]

**PROF:** Did you notice? Nobody says "I suggest" or "I deny" — people speak naturally. The reporting verbs come later, when you report the meeting: "Sapalo admitted starting the roof late", "he refused to work at night". That's mediation: turning a conversation into clear reported facts.

[PAUSA 60 s]

Paulo suggested using a container as a temporary control room. Kgosi proposed working at night, but Sapalo refused to put his people at risk. Hannah agreed to decide everything at tomorrow's site meeting.]

### 2. Situação (6 min)

[ECRÃ: quarta-feira, 6 de Junho; a acta da reunião de obra, em português: "Acta n.º 14 — Reunião de obra — Central do Tômbwa — 06/06/2029. Presentes: Eng.ª Helena Teixeira (Morro Azul), Eng. [ECRÃ: o que o Nuno fez]
