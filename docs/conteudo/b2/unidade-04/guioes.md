# B2.1 · Unidade 4 — Client care · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções: as dos [guiões da unidade 1 do B2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2, do B1 e das unidades 1 a 3 do B2. Novo: a passiva em todos os tempos — contínua (*is being repaired, was being tested*), no *present perfect* (*has been ordered*), no *past perfect* (*had been switched off*), com modais no presente (*must be signed, can't be changed*) e no passado (*should have been checked, could have been avoided*); *have / get something done* (*We'll have the disk replaced. I got my windscreen fixed.*); linguagem de empatia (*I completely understand why you're upset. I'd feel the same.*).

**Não usar nesta unidade:** *be bound to*, *be likely to* e o *future perfect continuous* (unidade 5); *needn't have*, *was supposed to* (unidade 6); *whereas, admittedly, even if, nevertheless* (unidade 7); verbos de relato como *suggest, insist, deny, warn* (unidade 9); *wish / if only*, *would* para hábitos do passado e os condicionais mistos (unidade 10).

**Nomes de empresas e lugares:** a Kalandula Tech, a Mopane Systems, a Morro Azul Energia e a central do Tômbwa são fictícias, como a Lumo Electronics (lojas de electrónica em Luanda) e a Litoral Telecom (operadora de internet da 4E).

**Personagens:** **Nuno**; **Rosa** (do apoio ao cliente; só é referida, na 4A); **Sílvia**; **Paulo Mendes**; **Lerato**; **Hannah** (Morro Azul, Namibe); **Femi** (Morro Azul). Novos e fictícios: **Kwame Mensah** (ganês, director-geral da Lumo Electronics, cliente da Kalandula Tech desde 2027, com três lojas em Luanda: Talatona, Maianga e Kilamba); **Joel Cassule** (técnico da Kalandula Tech; escreve o relatório da 4F, em português); na 4E, quatro clientes que telefonam para a Litoral Telecom e **Benedita**, a operadora.

**A história:** Novembro de 2028. O contrato do Tômbwa vai ser assinado até 30 de Novembro (B2 3F). Na segunda, 13, o Nuno substitui um colega no apoio ao cliente e responde a pedidos de ponto de situação (4A). Na quarta, 15, descobre-se que a versão do contrato enviada ao Femi tinha o preço antigo; a Sílvia muda as regras (4B). A assinatura fica marcada para terça, 28. Na sexta, 17, o Nuno e o Paulo falam do que têm de mandar arranjar antes da primeira viagem ao Tômbwa (4C). Na segunda, 20, o Kwame Mensah, da Lumo Electronics, telefona furioso: as câmaras da loja de Talatona não gravaram nada no fim-de-semana, e houve uma tentativa de assalto (4F). A 4E é uma reportagem de rádio sobre um centro de apoio ao cliente.

**O incidente da Lumo (igual em todas as aulas em que aparece):**
- Na sexta, 10 de Novembro, o *software* do gravador das câmaras de Talatona foi actualizado pela Kalandula; durante a actualização, a função que apaga as gravações antigas foi desligada e não voltou a ser ligada.
- O disco encheu-se no sábado, 18, por volta das 14:00; a partir daí, nada foi gravado.
- O alerta de "disco cheio" foi enviado para um endereço antigo da Lumo, que já não era usado desde Setembro.
- No domingo, 19, por volta das 23:00, houve uma tentativa de assalto na loja de Talatona: a porta das traseiras foi forçada, mas o alarme tocou e nada foi levado.
- A solução: o disco foi substituído na segunda de manhã e a função foi ligada outra vez; os alertas vão passar a ser enviados para três endereços e testados todos os meses; as outras duas lojas foram verificadas.
- A compensação: um mês de serviço grátis e três meses de cópia de segurança das gravações na *cloud*, sem custo.

## 4A — It's being fixed

```
Curso: B2.1        Unidade: 4        Aula: 4A — It's being fixed        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dar o ponto de situação de um pedido com a passiva contínua e
  no present perfect (The part has been ordered. It's being shipped. It'll be installed on Friday.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: passiva contínua (is / are being + particípio; was / were being + particípio); passiva no
  present perfect (has / have been + particípio); revisão da passiva simples (B1, 5A e 5B) e com will
  (will be installed); by só quando importa quem faz
Palavras-alvo: request, status, update, order, ship, clear customs, repair, review, approve, deliver,
  install, reference number
Reciclagem: passiva simples no presente e no passado (B1, 5A e 5B); future perfect (B2, 2C)
Pronúncia: being dito com duas sílabas claras; been curto e fraco no meio da frase
Contexto: segunda-feira, 13 de Novembro de 2028, 9:00 a 10:00; o Nuno substitui a Rosa no apoio ao
  cliente; três chamadas
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 4A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 4
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! This unit is about client care: updates, mistakes, services and angry clients. Today: how to say what's happening to a request — "It's being fixed", "It has been sent".

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 13 de Novembro, 9:00; a secretária do apoio ao cliente; o Nuno com auscultadores; um ecrã com uma lista de pedidos]

**NUNO:** Kalandula Tech, client support, Nuno speaking.
**HANNAH:** Nuno? It's Hannah. Are you on the help desk now?
**NUNO:** Just for today — Rosa's ill. How can I help?
**HANNAH:** The radio we sent back last month. Any news?
**NUNO:** Let me check… Yes. The old radio has been repaired, and it's being tested in our lab this morning. It'll be sent to Namibe on Wednesday.
**HANNAH:** Perfect. Thanks.
[ECRÃ: 9:20; segunda chamada]
**NUNO:** Kalandula Tech, client support, Nuno speaking.
**FEMI:** Good morning, Nuno. Femi here. Where are we with the final contract?
**NUNO:** Good morning, Femi. The final text has been prepared, and it's being reviewed by our lawyers today. Once it's been approved by Mr Baptista, it'll be sent to you — by Wednesday at the latest.
**FEMI:** Good. Has the training for the six technicians been added?
**NUNO:** Yes, it has been added as a new clause.
**FEMI:** Excellent. Speak soon.
[ECRÃ: 9:45; terceira chamada; um número desconhecido]
**NUNO:** Kalandula Tech, client support, Nuno speaking.
**CALLER:** Hello, I ordered two network switches two weeks ago. Reference K-4471. Nothing has arrived.
**NUNO:** I'm sorry about that. Let me look… OK. Your switches were ordered from Johannesburg on the first. They've been shipped, and they're being cleared at customs now. They should be delivered on Thursday.
**CALLER:** Customs again!
**NUNO:** I know. I'll call you as soon as they've been delivered to our warehouse.
**CALLER:** Thank you.
[ECRÃ: o Nuno tira os auscultadores; a Lerato passa com um café]
**LERATO:** How's the help desk?
**NUNO:** Three calls, and everything is "being done" or "has been done". I've used more passives this morning than in my whole life!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**A passiva contínua e no *present perfect***

[ECRÃ:]

| Tempo | Activa | Passiva |
|---|---|---|
| presente contínuo | Our lab **is testing** it. | It**'s being tested**. |
| passado contínuo | They **were testing** it at ten. | It **was being tested** at ten. |
| present perfect | We**'ve repaired** it. | It**'s been repaired**. |
| futuro | We**'ll send** it on Wednesday. | It**'ll be sent** on Wednesday. |

**PROF:** For the passive, use "be" in the right tense, plus the past participle. "Is being tested" — now, in progress. "Has been repaired" — finished, with a result now. The client doesn't care who does it; they care what's happening.
[PAUSA 6 s]

***By* — só quando importa**

[ECRÃ: It's being reviewed **by our lawyers**. (importa quem) · They've been shipped. (não importa quem)]

**PROF:** Add "by" only when the person is important. "By our lawyers" tells Femi why it's taking time.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ It's been repairing. · ✘ It's being repair. · ✔ It's being repaired. · ✔ It's been repaired.]

**PROF:** The most common mistake: forgetting the past participle — "being repair" — or using "-ing" — "been repairing". In the passive, the last verb is always the participle.
[PAUSA 6 s]

**Perguntas na passiva**

[ECRÃ: **Has** the training **been added**? — Yes, it has. · **Is** it **being tested**? — Yes, it is.]
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "Being" has two clear syllables: be-ing. "Been" is short and weak in a sentence. Listen: It's being tested. It's been repaired.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: The radio has been repaired, and it's being tested this morning.
[PAUSA 7 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Change to the passive.
[ECRÃ: They're repairing the road.] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: The road is being repaired.]
[ECRÃ: Someone has paid the invoice.] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: The invoice has been paid.]
[ECRÃ: They were cleaning the office when I arrived.] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: The office was being cleaned when I arrived.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. A client calls to ask about an order, a repair or a document. Give a full update: what has been done, what is being done now, and what will be done next — with a day. About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Thank you for calling. Your laptop was received on Monday, and it's been checked by our technician. The screen has been ordered from Johannesburg, and it's being shipped now. Once it arrives, it'll be installed the same day, and your laptop will be delivered to your office by Friday. I'll call you as soon as it's been tested.]

**PROF (opinião):** When you wait for something — a repair, a document, a delivery — what's worse: no news, or bad news? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 5B):** Say it in the passive: "Kalandula Tech installed the satellite in November 2027."
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: The satellite was installed in November 2027.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! "Is being done" for now, "has been done" for finished, "will be done" for next — and "by" only when it matters. Next lesson: rules and mistakes — "It should have been checked".

## 4B — It should have been checked

```
Curso: B2.1        Unidade: 4        Aula: 4B — It should have been checked        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue falar de regras e de responsabilidades com a passiva com
  modais, no presente (must be signed, can't be changed) e no passado (should have been checked, could
  have been avoided), e usar a passiva no past perfect para explicar um erro.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: modal + be + particípio (must be approved, should be sent, can't be changed, will be
  checked); modal + have been + particípio (should have been checked, shouldn't have been sent, could have
  been avoided); passiva no past perfect (had been changed, had been sent)
Palavras-alvo: draft, version, approve, check, mistake, rule, procedure, sign off, signature, embarrassing,
  from now on
Reciclagem: should have (B1, 10B); 3.º condicional (B1, 10A)
Pronúncia: should have been dito curto (should've been) e o acento no particípio
Contexto: quarta-feira, 15 de Novembro, 11:00; o gabinete da Sílvia; a Sílvia e o Nuno; uma chamada do
  Femi acabou de revelar um erro
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 4B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Mistakes happen. Professionals talk about them clearly — what should have been done, and what must be done from now on. Today: the passive with modal verbs.

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: quarta-feira, 15 de Novembro, 11:00; o gabinete da Sílvia; o Nuno entra depressa]

**NUNO:** Sílvia, Femi just called. The contract he received says four hundred and twelve thousand, not three hundred and eighty-five.
**SÍLVIA:** What? That's the old price! How did that happen?
**NUNO:** I've just checked. The price had been changed in the final version, but an old draft was sent by mistake. Both files had the same name.
**SÍLVIA:** Did Femi get angry?
**NUNO:** No, he laughed. He said, "Nice try." But it's embarrassing.
**SÍLVIA:** It is. The final version should have been checked before it was sent. And it shouldn't have been sent without my signature.
**NUNO:** You're right. It could have been avoided with a simple check. I'm sorry — I was the one who sent it.
**SÍLVIA:** Thank you for saying so. Now, let's fix it. The correct version must be sent to Femi today, and it has to be signed by Mr Baptista first.
**NUNO:** I'll take it to him now. It can be sent before lunch.
**SÍLVIA:** Good. And from now on, two rules. One: every contract must be checked by two people before it goes out. Two: prices can't be changed in a contract after I've signed it off.
**NUNO:** And the file names. Each version should be saved with the date in the name.
**SÍLVIA:** Good idea — write that down as rule three. If that had been done this time, the wrong file wouldn't have been sent.
**NUNO:** I'll send the rules to the whole team this afternoon.
**SÍLVIA:** And call Femi back. Tell him the correct contract will be with him by one o'clock.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Regras: modal + *be* + particípio**

[ECRÃ: Every contract **must be checked** by two people. · Prices **can't be changed** after I've signed it off. · Each version **should be saved** with the date. · The correct version **will be sent** today.]

**PROF:** For rules and procedures, the passive is perfect: what matters is the action, not the person. Modal, then "be", then the participle.
[PAUSA 6 s]

**O passado: modal + *have been* + particípio**

[ECRÃ: The final version **should have been checked**. (devia ter sido verificada — não foi) · It **shouldn't have been sent** without my signature. (foi — mal) · It **could have been avoided**. (podia ter sido evitado)]

**PROF:** In B1, you learned "We should have checked". Now, without the person: "It should have been checked". This is very useful when you want to talk about a mistake without blaming anyone.
[PAUSA 6 s]

***Past perfect* passivo: o que aconteceu antes**

[ECRÃ: The price **had been changed** in the final version, but an old draft **was sent**. (primeiro mudou-se o preço; depois enviou-se o ficheiro errado)]

**PROF:** "Had been" plus the participle: something was done before another moment in the past.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ It should been checked. · ✘ It should have checked. (= devia ter verificado, activa) · ✔ It should have been checked.]
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "Should have been" is said fast and short — should've been — and the stress goes on the participle. Listen: It should've been checked.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: The final version should have been checked before it was sent.
[PAUSA 7 s]
**PROF:** It could have been avoided.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Say what should have been done.
[ECRÃ: Ninguém testou o sistema antes da entrega.] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: The system should have been tested before the delivery.]
[ECRÃ: Enviaram a factura ao cliente errado.] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: The invoice shouldn't have been sent to that client.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Think of a mistake at work, at school or in your community — a lost document, a late delivery, a wrong price. Explain what happened (with "had been"), what should have been done, and two new rules (with "must be" and "can't be"). About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last month, our school's exam results were published with the wrong names. The list had been changed, but an old file was printed. The list should have been checked by a second teacher, and it shouldn't have been published on a Friday, when nobody could fix it. From now on, results must be checked by two people, and they can't be published without the director's signature.]

**PROF (opinião):** When something goes wrong at work, is it better to say who made the mistake, or to talk only about what should have been done? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 10A):** Finish Sílvia's sentence: "If the file names had had the date…"
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: …the wrong version wouldn't have been sent.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! "Must be done" for rules, "should have been done" for mistakes, "had been done" for the earlier past. Next lesson: things you don't do yourself — "I had my car repaired".

## 4C — We'll have it replaced

```
Curso: B2.1        Unidade: 4        Aula: 4C — We'll have it replaced        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer que um serviço é feito por outra pessoa com have / get
  something done (I had my windscreen replaced. We'll get the masts painted.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: have + objecto + particípio (I had my car serviced. We'll have the disk replaced.); get +
  objecto + particípio (mais informal: I need to get my phone fixed.); em todos os tempos (I'm having…,
  I've had…, I'll have…, I need to have…); contraste com a activa (I fixed it = fui eu)
Palavras-alvo: service (o carro), windscreen, tyre, crack, screen, repair, fix, replace, paint, cut,
  check, print, deliver
Reciclagem: passiva (4A e 4B); going to (A2)
Pronúncia: had it fixed — ligação entre as palavras (ha-di-fixed)
Contexto: sexta-feira, 17 de Novembro, 13:00; a cantina; o Nuno e o Paulo almoçam e falam do que têm de
  tratar antes da primeira viagem ao Tômbwa, em Dezembro
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 4C; conversa guiada "What's being done?"; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! When a mechanic repairs your car, you don't say "I repaired my car". Today: how to talk about services — "I had my car repaired".

### 2. Situação (3 min)

[ECRÃ: sexta-feira, 17 de Novembro, 13:00; a cantina; o Nuno e o Paulo com dois pratos de muamba; o telemóvel do Nuno, com o ecrã partido, em cima da mesa]

**PAULO:** What happened to your phone?
**NUNO:** I dropped it in the car park. I'm getting the screen fixed tomorrow — there's a shop in Mutamba that does it in an hour.
**PAULO:** Get it done before Tômbwa. You'll need that phone on the road.
**NUNO:** I know. And the car needs work too. I'm having it serviced on Monday, and I'll have the two front tyres replaced.
**PAULO:** Good. Over a thousand kilometres, and the last part through the desert, is no joke. Did you have the windscreen checked? There was a crack last month.
**NUNO:** I had it replaced last week. It cost me a fortune.
**PAULO:** Ha! Welcome to adult life. What about work things?
**NUNO:** Well, the contract's being signed on the twenty-eighth, so we're having the site plans printed in A1, for the meeting with the builders.
**PAULO:** And the masts. I'd like to have them painted white before they leave Johannesburg — the sun in Tômbwa is brutal.
**NUNO:** Can Mopane do that?
**PAULO:** No, but Thandi knows a company. We'll get them painted there and delivered with the antennas.
**NUNO:** Perfect. Oh — and I need to get my hair cut. My mother says I look like a student.
**PAULO:** Your mother is right!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Have something done***

[ECRÃ:
I**'m having** the car **serviced** on Monday. (o mecânico faz)
I **had** the windscreen **replaced** last week.
We**'ll have** the site plans **printed**.
I'd like to **have** the masts **painted**.]

**PROF:** "Have" plus the object plus the past participle: somebody else does it for you, usually because you pay them. "Have" changes tense; the participle never changes.
[PAUSA 6 s]

**Atenção à ordem**

[ECRÃ: ✘ I had replaced the windscreen. (= fui eu que substituí, no past perfect) · ✔ I had the windscreen replaced. (alguém substituiu por mim)]

**PROF:** The order changes everything. "I had replaced the windscreen" means you did it yourself, before another moment. "I had the windscreen replaced" means you paid someone.
[PAUSA 6 s]

***Get something done* — mais informal**

[ECRÃ: I'm **getting** the screen **fixed** tomorrow. · I need to **get** my hair **cut**. · We'll **get** them **painted** there.]

**PROF:** "Get" means the same, but it's more informal — perfect for conversation with colleagues and friends.
[PAUSA 4 s]

[ECRÃ: Em português: "Vou cortar o cabelo." → ✔ I'm going to get my hair cut. · ✘ I'm going to cut my hair. (= vou cortá-lo eu)]

**PROF:** In Portuguese, we say "vou cortar o cabelo" even when a barber cuts it. In English, that would mean you cut it yourself!
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In fast speech, the words join: had it fixed — ha-di-fixed. Listen: I had it replaced last week.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I had it replaced last week.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** I'm getting the screen fixed tomorrow.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Say it with "have" or "get".
[ECRÃ: Um mecânico mudou o óleo do meu carro ontem.] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: I had the oil changed yesterday. / I got the oil changed yesterday.]
[ECRÃ: Uma empresa vai pintar o nosso escritório em Janeiro.] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: We're having our office painted in January.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Talk about the services in your life: two things you've had done recently, one thing you're having done soon, and one thing you need to get done but keep putting off. About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last month, I had my car serviced, and I got my glasses changed — the old ones were scratched. Next week, I'm having the kitchen painted, so the house will be a mess. And I really need to get my passport renewed: it expires in March, and I keep putting it off.]

**PROF (opinião):** What do you prefer to do yourself, and what do you prefer to pay someone to do? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — 4A):** Give an update on Nuno's phone: the shop has fixed the screen.
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: The screen has been fixed.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! "Have" or "get", the object, the participle — and the order matters. Now try the tutor's conversation "What's being done?". Next lesson: the words of complaints and service.

## 4D — Complaints and service

```
Curso: B2.1        Unidade: 4        Aula: 4D — Complaints and service        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar o vocabulário de reclamações e serviço (compensation,
  goodwill gesture, escalate, follow up, service level), a linguagem de empatia, o tom de voz certo, e
  evitar os falsos amigos claim e requirement.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3, 4
Gramática-alvo: linguagem de empatia (I completely understand why you're upset. I'd feel exactly the same.
  That must have been very stressful.); assumir (That's our responsibility. You're right to be
  disappointed.); colocações (make a complaint, deal with a complaint, offer compensation, escalate a
  problem, follow up on a request, meet a service level)
Palavras-alvo: complaint, compensation, goodwill gesture, refund, replacement (A2), escalate, follow up,
  service level, response time, unacceptable, frustrated, disappointed, reassure, apologise
Reciclagem: refund, replacement, receipt (A2, 9D); I'm afraid (A2, 9A)
Pronúncia: tom — a mesma frase dita com tons diferentes (sincero, impaciente, frio)
Falsos amigos: claim (≠ reclamar → complain; claim = afirmar, reivindicar, pedir uma indemnização);
  requirement (≠ requerimento → application, request; requirement = requisito)
Contexto: ecrãs com expressões e exemplos da história
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 4D; perguntas 7 e 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! In A2, you learned "refund" and "replacement". Today: the words of professional client care — and two false friends that appear in every complaint.

### 2. Reclamações e serviço (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ:]

| Palavra | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| complaint | We received a complaint from Lumo this morning. (reclamação) |
| compensation | We'll offer one month free as compensation. (compensação, indemnização) |
| goodwill gesture | The free backup is a goodwill gesture. (gesto de boa vontade) |
| escalate | If it isn't solved today, I'll escalate it to the director. (passar ao nível superior) |
| follow up (on) | I'll follow up on your request tomorrow. (dar seguimento) |
| service level | Our service level is a response in two hours. (nível de serviço) |
| response time | The response time was too long. (tempo de resposta) |
| reassure | I'd like to reassure you that it won't happen again. (tranquilizar) |

**PROF:** Repeat: complaint. Compensation. Goodwill gesture. Escalate. Follow up. Reassure.
[PAUSA 8 s]

[ECRÃ: **make** a complaint · **deal with** / **handle** a complaint · **offer** compensation · **escalate** a problem · **follow up on** a request · **meet** a service level]
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 3. Linguagem de empatia (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ:]

| Para | Língua |
|---|---|
| mostrar que ouviu | I completely understand why you're upset. · That must have been very stressful. |
| validar | You're right to be disappointed. · I'd feel exactly the same. |
| assumir | That's our responsibility, and I apologise. |
| tranquilizar | I'd like to reassure you that… · Let me tell you exactly what we're doing. |

**PROF:** Empathy is not just "sorry". It's showing that you understand the feeling — and the problem. But be careful: empathy without action sounds empty. Always follow it with what's being done.
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ Calm down. · ✘ It's not my fault. · ✘ That's impossible, the system never fails. · ✔ I understand. Let me check exactly what happened.]

**PROF:** Never say "calm down" to an angry client — it has the opposite effect.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia: o tom (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: "I understand." — sincero (calmo, a descer) · impaciente (rápido, seco) · frio (sem melodia)]

**PROF:** The same words can sound kind or rude. Listen: I understand. [sincero, calmo] … I understand. [impaciente, rápido] … I understand. [frio, sem melodia]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Only the first one works with a client. Repeat it: I completely understand why you're upset.
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 5. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: **claim** ≠ reclamar]

**PROF:** "Claim" means to say something is true — "He claims the cameras never worked" — or to ask officially for money or a right: "an insurance claim".
(PT: "Reclamar" é "complain" ou "make a complaint": "o cliente reclamou" → "the client complained".)
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: **requirement** ≠ requerimento]

**PROF:** "Requirement" means something that is necessary: "A helmet is a requirement on site."
(PT: "Requerimento" — um pedido formal por escrito — é "application" ou "formal request": "entregar um requerimento" → "submit an application".)
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 6. Prática e fecho (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Choose the right word.
[ECRÃ: The client wants to … about the delay. (complain / claim)] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: complain]
[ECRÃ: Two years' experience is a … for this job. (requirement / request)] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: requirement]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Tell me about a time you complained — in a shop, a bank, a hotel or with a service at home. How was your complaint handled, and what should have been done? About one and a half minutes.
[PAUSA 90 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last year, my internet stopped working for a week. I called the company five times, and every time I was told it was being fixed. Nobody followed up, and my complaint was never escalated. In the end, a technician came and fixed it in ten minutes. It should have been escalated on the first day, and some compensation should have been offered. I changed companies the next month.]

**PROF (opinião):** Is it better to complain by phone, by email or in person? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]
