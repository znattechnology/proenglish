# B2.2 · Unidade 10 — Choices · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções: as dos [guiões da unidade 1 do B2](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2, do B1 e do B2 (unidades 1 a 9). Novo: *wish / if only* + *past simple* (desejos sobre o presente) e + *past perfect* (arrependimentos sobre o passado); *would* para hábitos do passado, ao lado de *used to* (que serve também para estados); o *past perfect continuous* (*I'd been working there for six years when…*); os condicionais mistos (*If I hadn't moved to Luanda, I wouldn't be here now. / If I were braver, I'd have said yes.*).

**Não usar nesta unidade:** estruturas do C1 — inversão (*Had I known…, Never have I…*), orações clivadas como estrutura de ênfase variada (*It was Lerato who…*), *wish* + *would* para queixas (*I wish you would stop…*, só para reconhecer, se aparecer).

**Nomes de empresas e lugares:** a Kalandula Tech, a Mopane Systems e a Morro Azul Energia são fictícias; o *podcast* "Second Acts" e a sua convidada são fictícios. O Lubango, a Serra da Leba e Joanesburgo existem.

**Personagens:** **Nuno** (nasceu no Lubango em 2003; três irmãs; o pai tem uma pequena loja; licenciou-se em Julho de 2026 e entrou na Kalandula Tech em Outubro de 2026 — A1, A2, B1); **Paulo Mendes**; **Lerato** (sul-africana, da Mopane, em Luanda desde Fevereiro de 2027 — A2 7); **Thandi** (Mopane); **Mei Chen** (de Lusaca; conheceu o Nuno na formação de Joanesburgo, em Julho de 2027 — B1 1; agora chefe de equipa); **Sr. Baptista** (só por escrito). Novos e fictícios: **Teresa** (irmã mais velha do Nuno, professora no Lubango); na 10F, **Sam Okeke** (apresentador nigeriano do *podcast*) e **Amélia Chissola** (angolana, ex-gestora bancária que se tornou cozinheira aos 40 anos).

**A história:** Junho de 2029, o fim do B2. A central do Tômbwa abre no fim do ano. Na segunda, 11, a Thandi diz ao Nuno, em segredo, que a Mopane lhe vai fazer uma proposta para trabalhar em Joanesburgo; nessa noite, no Tômbwa, ele fala com o Paulo (10A). Na quarta, 13, no Lubango, para o aniversário do pai, fala da infância com a irmã Teresa (10B). Na sexta, 15, a Lerato conta-lhe como saiu de Joanesburgo para Luanda (10C). Na segunda, 18, a Mei liga-lhe de Lusaca (10D). Na quarta, 20, o Nuno ouve um *podcast* sobre mudanças de carreira (10F). Na sexta, 22, com as duas propostas na mão, conversa com a Lerato e decide (10G).

**As duas propostas (iguais em todas as aulas em que aparecem):**
- **Mopane (em inglês):** Project Lead, Southern Africa, em Joanesburgo; uma equipa de oito pessoas; começo a 1 de Outubro de 2029; salário cerca de 60% acima do actual; mudança paga; dois voos por ano para Angola.
- **Kalandula (em português, carta do Sr. Baptista):** promoção a Gestor de Projectos a partir de 1 de Agosto; aumento de 25%; viatura da empresa; curso de inglês de nível C1 pago pela empresa; responsável pela manutenção do Tômbwa em 2030.
- **A decisão (10G):** o Nuno aceita a Mopane, mas pede para começar a 7 de Janeiro de 2030, depois da abertura do Tômbwa; a Lerato fala com a Thandi, e a Mopane aceita. O Nuno agradece a proposta da Kalandula e recusa-a.

## 10A — I wish I had…

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10A — I wish I had…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue falar de desejos sobre o presente (I wish I knew…) e de
  arrependimentos sobre o passado (I wish I had studied…) com wish e if only.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: wish / if only + past simple (o presente que gostaríamos que fosse diferente: I wish I knew.
  If only I spoke Zulu.); wish + could (I wish I could stay.); wish / if only + past perfect (o passado:
  I wish I had asked. If only I hadn't said yes.); were (I wish I were…, mais formal; was, na conversa)
Palavras-alvo: offer, decision, secret, family, abroad, heart, flights, cook, regret, travel
Reciclagem: 2.º e 3.º condicional (B1); even if (B2, 7B)
Pronúncia: I wish I'd dito curto ("I wish-aid"), e o acento em "wish"
Contexto: segunda-feira, 11 de Junho de 2029, 21:00; o alojamento da obra do Tômbwa; o Nuno e o Paulo, depois
  do jantar, cá fora, a olhar para o mar
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 10A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 10
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! This is the last unit of B2. It's about choices: what we wish, what we used to do, what we regret — and how the past changes the present. Today: "I wish".

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 11 de Junho, 21:00; a obra do Tômbwa à noite; o Nuno e o Paulo sentados em cadeiras de plástico, com o mar ao fundo]

**PAULO:** You've been quiet all day. What's going on?
**NUNO:** Can you keep a secret? Thandi called me this morning. Mopane is going to offer me a job. In Johannesburg.
**PAULO:** No way! That's huge. Congratulations!
**NUNO:** Thanks. But I wish she hadn't told me now. I can't think about anything else.
**PAULO:** Ha! I wish someone would call me with news like that.
**NUNO:** The problem is, I don't know what I want. I wish I knew.
**PAULO:** What does your heart say?
**NUNO:** One day it says yes, the next day no. If only I had more time to decide.
**PAULO:** And your family?
**NUNO:** That's the hard part. My father isn't getting younger. I wish Lubango were closer to Johannesburg.
**PAULO:** There are flights from Luanda every day. Not the end of the world.
**NUNO:** I know. And honestly, there's another thing. I wish I'd learned to cook. In Joburg I'd be eating pizza every night!
**PAULO:** Ha! If only you'd listened to your mother.
**NUNO:** Don't you start. Do you ever regret staying in Luanda?
**PAULO:** Sometimes I wish I'd travelled more when I was younger. But I'm happy. Even if I could go back, I'd probably choose the same.
**NUNO:** That's what I'm afraid of — choosing, and then wishing I hadn't.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Wish* sobre o presente: um passo atrás no tempo**

[ECRÃ:]

| Realidade (presente) | Desejo |
|---|---|
| I don't know what I want. | I wish I **knew**. |
| I don't have more time. | If only I **had** more time. |
| Lubango is far from Joburg. | I wish Lubango **were** closer. |
| I can't stay and go. | I wish I **could** do both. |

**PROF:** To wish about the present, go one step back in time: present becomes past. "I don't know" — "I wish I knew". It's like the second conditional.
[PAUSA 6 s]

***Wish* sobre o passado: arrependimentos**

[ECRÃ:]

| Realidade (passado) | Arrependimento |
|---|---|
| She told me now. | I wish she **hadn't told** me now. |
| I didn't learn to cook. | I wish I**'d learned** to cook. |
| You didn't listen to your mother. | If only you**'d listened** to your mother. |

**PROF:** For the past, use the past perfect: "I wish I had learned". "If only" is the same as "I wish", but stronger and more emotional.
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ I wish I know. · ✘ I wish I would have learned. · ✔ I wish I knew. · ✔ I wish I had learned.]

**PROF:** Two common mistakes: "I wish I know" — it's "knew". And "I wish I would have" — it's "I had".
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: Em português: "Quem me dera saber." → I wish I knew. · "Quem me dera ter aprendido." → I wish I had learned. — o português também recua no tempo.]
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "I wish I had" becomes "I wish I'd" in speech — very short. Stress "wish". Listen: I wish I'd learned to cook.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: I wish I'd learned to cook.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** If only I had more time.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Make a wish.
[ECRÃ: I can't swim.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: I wish I could swim.]
[ECRÃ: I live far from my family.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: I wish I lived closer to my family.]
[ECRÃ: I didn't go to my friend's wedding.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: I wish I'd gone to my friend's wedding.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Tell me three things you wish were different now — at work, at home or in your city — and two things you wish you had done differently in the past. About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I wish I had more time for my children; I work until seven every day. I wish my office were closer to home, and I wish I could speak French — half of our clients are from Congo. About the past: I wish I'd studied harder at school, and if only I'd bought a house when prices were lower!]

**PROF (opinião):** Is it useful to think about our regrets, or is it better to forget them? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B2, 7B):** Paulo says: "Even if I could go back, I'd probably choose the same." Is "even if" here a fact or a condition?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: A condition — an imagined one. He can't go back.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! "I wish I knew" for now; "I wish I'd known" for the past — one step back in time. Next lesson: the past habits we remember — "We would play for hours".

## 10B — We would play for hours

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10B — We would play for hours        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue falar de hábitos do passado com would, ao lado de used to, e
  saber quando só used to serve (estados).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: would + infinitivo para acções repetidas no passado (We would play football for hours.);
  used to para acções e estados (We used to live above the shop. I used to be shy.); ✘ would com estados
  (✘ We would live above the shop.); o past simple para acções únicas
Palavras-alvo: childhood, mango tree, climb, comics, shop, customers, sweets, spoiled, curves, scared,
  nostalgic
Reciclagem: used to (B1, 2); wish (10A)
Pronúncia: would reduzido a 'd (we'd play) e used to dito "yoosta"
Contexto: quarta-feira, 13 de Junho, 18:00; o Lubango; o quintal da casa da família; o Nuno e a irmã Teresa
  preparam o aniversário do pai
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 10B; conversa guiada "If you could go back…"; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! When we tell stories about our childhood, English has a special way to describe what happened again and again: "We would play for hours." Today: "would" and "used to".

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: quarta-feira, 13 de Junho, 18:00; o Lubango; um quintal com uma mangueira; ao fundo, a Serra da Leba; o Nuno e a Teresa a pendurar luzes]

**TERESA:** Do you remember this tree? You used to climb it every day after school.
**NUNO:** Of course! I would sit up there and read comics until Mum shouted for dinner.
**TERESA:** And I would stand down here and tell her where you were. I used to be terrible!
**NUNO:** You still are. Remember the shop? Every Saturday, Dad would wake us up at six to help him.
**TERESA:** Yes! I would count the money, and you would carry the boxes. And the customers would always give you sweets because you were the youngest.
**NUNO:** I used to be quite spoiled, didn't I?
**TERESA:** Very. And on Sundays, we would drive up to the Serra da Leba with the whole family and look at the road from the top.
**NUNO:** I used to be scared of that road. All those curves.
**TERESA:** And now you want to move to Johannesburg. Mum told me.
**NUNO:** Mum knows? I told her one thing on the phone…
**TERESA:** Nuno, she knows everything. So? Are you going?
**NUNO:** I don't know yet. I wish things were as simple as when we used to sell sweets in Dad's shop.
**TERESA:** They weren't simple, little brother. You just didn't have to decide anything.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Would* e *used to*: hábitos do passado**

[ECRÃ: You **used to climb** it every day. · I **would sit** up there and read. · Dad **would wake** us up at six. · We **would drive** up to the Serra da Leba.]

**PROF:** Both "would" and "used to" describe things that happened again and again in the past. "Would" is very common when you tell a story — it makes it sound warm and nostalgic.
[PAUSA 6 s]

**Estados: só *used to***

[ECRÃ: I **used to be** terrible. · I **used to be** scared of that road. · We **used to live** above the shop. · ✘ I would be scared. · ✘ We would live above the shop.]

**PROF:** For states — be, have, live, like, know — only "used to" works. "Would" is only for repeated actions.
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ:]

| | used to | would |
|---|---|---|
| acções repetidas | ✔ We used to play. | ✔ We would play. |
| estados | ✔ I used to be shy. | ✘ I would be shy. |
| uma só vez | ✘ | ✘ → past simple: We played on Sunday. |

[ECRÃ: Atenção: "would" também é o condicional. "We'd play every Saturday" (hábito do passado) ≠ "We'd play if it stopped raining" (condicional). O contexto mostra qual é.]
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "Would" is often short: we'd, I'd, she'd. And "used to" sounds like "yoosta". Listen: We'd play for hours. I used to be scared.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: Every Saturday, Dad would wake us up at six.
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** I used to be quite spoiled.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** "Would" or "used to"? Sometimes both are correct.
[ECRÃ: We … live in Huambo.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: used to (estado)]
[ECRÃ: Every summer, my grandmother … make mango jam.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: would / used to]
[ECRÃ: I … have long hair.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: used to (estado)]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Describe your childhood — a place, a person, a typical weekend. Use "would" for at least three repeated actions and "used to" for at least two states. About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I grew up in Malanje, in a house with a big yard. I used to be very shy, and I used to have a dog called Kito. Every Sunday, my grandfather would take us to the river. We would swim for hours, and he would tell us stories about the old days. In the evening, my mother would make funge, and we would all eat outside.]

**PROF (opinião):** Was childhood better in the past than it is today? Why, or why not?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — 10A):** Nuno says: "Things aren't simple." Make it a wish.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I wish things were simple.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! "We would play" for repeated actions; "I used to be" for states. Try the tutor's conversation "If you could go back…". Next lesson: what had been happening before a big change.

## 10C — I'd been working there for years

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10C — I'd been working there for years        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue falar de acções que duravam até um momento do passado com o
  past perfect continuous (I'd been working at Mopane for seven years when they asked me…), e distingui-lo
  do past perfect simples.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: had been + -ing + for / since (duração até um momento do passado); contraste com o past
  perfect simples (acção terminada, resultado: I'd sold my car); com o past simple (o momento: when they
  asked me); verbos de estado no past perfect simples (I'd known her for years, não ✘ I'd been knowing)
Palavras-alvo: move, abroad, team, bored, scared, suitcase, homesick, friends, turning point, regret, ready
Reciclagem: present perfect continuous (B1, 2B); past perfect (B1, 6)
Pronúncia: I'd been dito curto ("aid-bin")
Contexto: sexta-feira, 15 de Junho, 13:00; um restaurante perto do escritório; a Lerato conta ao Nuno como
  saiu de Joanesburgo para Luanda, em 2027
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 10C; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! When we tell the story of a big change, we often need to say what had been happening for a long time before it. Today: "I'd been working there for years when…".

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: sexta-feira, 15 de Junho, 13:00; um restaurante pequeno; a Lerato e o Nuno a almoçar]

**NUNO:** Lerato, can I ask you something personal? How did you decide to come to Luanda?
**LERATO:** Ha! Thandi told you about the offer, didn't she?
**NUNO:** She did. I'm trying to decide.
**LERATO:** OK. Well, in 2026 I'd been working at Mopane in Johannesburg for seven years. I'd been leading a small team for two, and honestly, I'd been feeling a bit bored for months.
**NUNO:** And then?
**LERATO:** Then, in December, my boss asked me to go to Luanda for the joint team. I'd never been to Angola. I didn't speak a word of Portuguese.
**NUNO:** Were you scared?
**LERATO:** Terrified. My mother had been asking me to move back to Durban for years, and now I was moving even further away! But I'd been waiting for a change, so I said yes.
**NUNO:** And when you arrived?
**LERATO:** My suitcase had stayed in Johannesburg — you remember! I'd been travelling since five in the morning, and I had nothing to wear.
**NUNO:** Ha! I remember the call.
**LERATO:** The first months were hard. I was homesick. But by the end of the year, I'd made friends, I'd learned some Portuguese, and I'd stopped thinking about going back.
**NUNO:** Do you ever regret it?
**LERATO:** Never. It was a turning point. Nuno, the question isn't "Am I ready?" Nobody's ever ready. The question is "Will I regret not trying?"
**NUNO:** That's a good question. A very good question.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**O *past perfect continuous***

[ECRÃ: In 2026, I**'d been working** at Mopane **for** seven years. · I**'d been feeling** bored **for** months. · My mother **had been asking** me to move back **for** years.]

**PROF:** "Had been" plus "-ing": an action that was going on for some time up to a moment in the past. It's the past version of "I've been working here for…".
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ: linha do tempo: 2019 ——— (working at Mopane) ——→ Dezembro de 2026: "they asked me"]

***Past perfect continuous* ou simples?**

[ECRÃ:]

| had been + -ing | had + particípio |
|---|---|
| I**'d been travelling** for eleven hours. (a duração, o cansaço) | My suitcase **had stayed** in Johannesburg. (o resultado) |
| I**'d been learning** Portuguese for months. (processo) | By the end of the year, I**'d learned** some Portuguese. (resultado) |

**PROF:** The continuous focuses on the activity and how long it lasted. The simple focuses on the result — what was done.
[PAUSA 6 s]

**Verbos de estado**

[ECRÃ: ✘ I'd been knowing her for years. · ✔ I'd known her for years. · ✔ I'd never been to Angola.]

**PROF:** As with the present perfect, state verbs — know, be, have, like — use the simple form.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "I had been" becomes "I'd been" — very short. Listen: I'd been working at Mopane for seven years.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** I'd been travelling for eleven hours.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Join with the past perfect continuous.
[ECRÃ: Ela trabalhava no banco há dez anos. Depois, mudou de carreira.] [PAUSA 7 s] [ECRÃ: She'd been working at the bank for ten years when she changed careers.]
[ECRÃ: Estávamos à espera há duas horas. Depois, o autocarro chegou.] [PAUSA 7 s] [ECRÃ: We'd been waiting for two hours when the bus arrived.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Tell me the story of a big change in your life or in the life of someone you know — a new job, a move, a new course. What had been happening before it, how long, and what changed? About one minute.
[PAUSA 60 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — In 2022, my brother had been working as a taxi driver in Luanda for eight years. He'd been saving money for a long time, and he'd been thinking about opening a small business. One day, a friend offered him a shop in Benguela. He'd never run a business before, but he took the risk. Now he has two shops and five employees.]

**PROF (opinião):** Lerato says: "Nobody's ever ready." Do you agree? Why?
[PAUSA 25 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 2B):** Say it now, in the present: Nuno started at Kalandula in October 2026.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: He's been working at Kalandula since October 2026 — for almost three years.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! "I'd been working there for seven years when…" — the activity before the moment. Next lesson: how the past changes the present — mixed conditionals.

## 10D — If I hadn't…, I wouldn't be…

```
Curso: B2.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10D — If I hadn't…, I wouldn't be…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue ligar o passado e o presente com condicionais mistos (If I
  hadn't gone to Johannesburg, I wouldn't know you. / If I were braver, I'd have said yes.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: passado imaginado → resultado no presente: If + past perfect, would + infinitivo (If I
  hadn't gone to the training, we wouldn't be friends now.); presente imaginado → resultado no passado:
  If + past simple, would have + particípio (If I were braver, I'd have said yes already.); revisão do 2.º
  e do 3.º condicional (B1)
Palavras-alvo: training, quiet, mistake, app, payday, team leader, offer, brave, careful, coward
Reciclagem: 2.º e 3.º condicional (B1, 4A e 10A); wish (10A)
Pronúncia: as formas curtas: I'd be, I'd have, wouldn't be
Contexto: segunda-feira, 18 de Junho, 19:00 em Luanda (20:00 em Lusaca); videochamada; a Mei liga ao Nuno
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 10D; perguntas 7 e 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Some past decisions still shape our lives today. English has a special conditional for that: the mixed conditional. Today, Nuno talks to an old friend — the person he met at the start of B1.

### 2. Situação (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 18 de Junho, 19:00 em Luanda (20:00 em Lusaca); videochamada; o Nuno em casa; a Mei no escritório, em Lusaca]

**MEI:** Nuno! Lerato told me your news. Johannesburg! Are you going?
**NUNO:** Hi, Mei! I don't know yet. Everyone's giving me advice.
**MEI:** Then here's mine. Think about it: if you hadn't gone to that training in Johannesburg two years ago, we wouldn't know each other now.
**NUNO:** True. And if I hadn't been so nervous that week, you wouldn't laugh at me every time we talk!
**MEI:** Ha! You were so quiet. Look at you now.
**NUNO:** I know. If Lerato hadn't asked me to lead the Tômbwa proposal, I wouldn't be getting this offer.
**MEI:** Exactly. And me — if our app hadn't crashed on that payday, I wouldn't be a team leader today. My boss only noticed me because I fixed it.
**NUNO:** That's a nice way to see a mistake.
**MEI:** So, what's stopping you?
**NUNO:** My family, mainly. And Tômbwa — the plant opens in December. If I were a braver person, I'd have said yes on the first day.
**MEI:** You're not a coward. You're careful. If you weren't careful, you'd have made a lot more mistakes in the last two years.
**NUNO:** Maybe. If only I knew what I'll feel in a year.
**MEI:** Nobody knows that. But you'll find a way. You always do.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Passado → presente**

[ECRÃ: **If** you **hadn't gone** to the training, we **wouldn't know** each other **now**. · **If** Thandi **hadn't pushed** me, I **wouldn't be getting** this offer. · **If** I **hadn't made** that mistake, I **wouldn't be** a team leader **today**.]

**PROF:** The first part is the past — the third conditional: "If you hadn't gone". The second part is the present — the second conditional: "we wouldn't know". A past action, a present result.
[PAUSA 6 s]

**Presente → passado**

[ECRÃ: **If** I **were** braver, I**'d have said** yes on the first day. · **If** you **weren't** careful, you**'d have made** more mistakes.]

**PROF:** The other mix: a present quality — "if I were braver" — explains a past action — "I'd have said yes". "Braver" is about who I am, always; "said yes" is about the past.
[PAUSA 6 s]

**Os quatro condicionais e os mistos**

[ECRÃ:]

| | if… | resultado |
|---|---|---|
| 2.º (presente imaginado) | If I were braver, | I'd say yes. |
| 3.º (passado imaginado) | If I'd gone, | I'd have met her. |
