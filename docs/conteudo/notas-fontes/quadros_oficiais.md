## Q1. What each framework is, what it has per level, and which one best gives a level-by-level list of speaking functions and grammar for A1–C1

### Takeaway
The **British Council–EAQUALS Core Inventory for General English (2nd ed., 2015)** is the only free official document that sets out, on one page, the **functions, grammar, discourse markers, vocabulary areas and topics for A1→C1**. It is the most practical backbone for the scope and sequence. Use the **CEFR Companion Volume (2020)** for the can-do outcomes (spoken production, interaction, mediation, phonology), and use **Oxford 3000/5000 + Phrase List** and the **Cambridge A2/B1 word lists** to calibrate vocabulary. The **GSE Teacher Toolkit** adds the finest-grained speaking objectives, including a "Professional English" set that suits working adults.

- **Speaking test formats** **[from prior knowledge; the official handbook pages 404'd; third-party summaries disagree on exact minutes]**:
  - **A2 Key:** 8–10 min, in pairs, 2 parts: (1) interview with personal questions; (2) discussion from a visual prompt, then follow-up questions.
  - **B1 Preliminary:** 12–17 min, 4 parts: (1) interview; (2) individual photo description of about 1 minute; (3) collaborative task; (4) discussion.
  - **B2 First:** about 14 min, 4 parts: (1) interview; (2) long turn comparing 2 photos for about 1 minute, plus a ~30-second response to the partner's photos; (3) collaborative task of about 2 minutes discussion plus 1 minute to reach a decision; (4) discussion — [test-english.com](https://test-english.com/exams/b2-first/b2-first-fce-exam-1-speaking/); [live-english.net](https://live-english.net/first-certificate-in-english-exam-fce-speaking-test/)

  - These task types (personal interview → describe/compare a picture → negotiate a decision with a partner → discuss abstract issues) map directly onto voice-tutor activities.

### Inferences
- For "speaking functions + grammar per level", the ranking is: Core Inventory (compact, level-tagged, A1–C1, free PDF) > GSE Toolkit (granular, filterable by Adult/Professional, but behind a web tool) > CEFR CV (outcomes, not language content) > Cambridge handbooks (exam-shaped language specs, only for A2/B1 in detail).

- Professional adults in Angola: the GSE "Professional English" objectives and the CV "Formal discussion (meetings)", "Goal-oriented cooperation" and mediation scales are the best sources for work-related speaking (meetings, phone calls, presenting).

## Q2. What can legally be reused in a commercial platform (copied lists vs. used as reference)

### Takeaway
None of these frameworks grants an open licence (e.g. Creative Commons) for commercial reproduction. All are copyright-protected (Council of Europe, British Council/EAQUALS, UCLES/CUPA, Pearson, OUP). The safe model is **"reference, not reproduction"**: map our own originally written objectives and content to CEFR levels and cite the frameworks. Do not copy descriptor scales, word lists or GSE objectives verbatim into the product. Request permission for any verbatim embedding (e.g. showing CEFR can-do statements in the app).

### Cited Findings
- **CEFR / Council of Europe:** CoE publications of the same period state that "The reproduction of extracts (up to 500 words) is authorised, except for commercial purposes", with attribution "© Council of Europe, [year]". All other reproduction or translation requests go to the Directorate of Communications — [CoE Guide 2023](https://rm.coe.int/a-guide-to-action-oriented-plurilingual-and-intercultural-education/1680af9496). An older CoE scales document says: "The copyright of the descriptive scales and the illustrative scales (in all languages) … belongs to the Council of Europe. Publishers should ask permission prior to using these instruments, and they must mention the copyright." — [CoE document](https://rm.coe.int/168045b15e). The CV's own notice is believed to follow the 500-word / non-commercial / "© Council of Europe" formula **[not verified on the CV PDF itself — 403]**.
- **Core Inventory:** "Copyright British Council/EAQUALS … except where otherwise stated". It is offered as a free download and as a purchasable hard copy. ### Inferences
- Facts and ideas (e.g. "present perfect is typically taught at A2", "describing photos is a B1 speaking task") are not copyrightable. Using the frameworks to decide *what* to teach and *when*, then writing original scripts, exercises and our own can-do statements, is the standard industry practice and low-risk.
- Individual words are not protectable, but a curated, levelled list as a whole can be (selection and arrangement, and the EU database right for OUP/Cambridge, which are UK/EU entities). Do not ship a full copied "Oxford 3000" or "A2 Key list" with CEFR tags inside the app. Building our own vocabulary set, informed by several sources and our own frequency judgment, is safer.
- Labels such as "CEFR A2" are freely usable for describing levels. Avoid implying endorsement (e.g. "Cambridge-approved", "English Profile informed", "GSE-certified") without permission.
- If verbatim CEFR descriptors are wanted in the UI (e.g. a level page), ask publishing@coe.int (CoE Directorate of Communications) for permission, or paraphrase.
- This is not legal advice; for a commercial launch, a short review by a lawyer familiar with Angolan, EU and UK copyright is advisable.

## Q3. Compact converged inventory per level (functions, grammar, topics)

### Takeaway
All sources follow the same broad progression. **A1** is survival and personal info in the present. **A2** is routines, the past, plans and simple transactions. **B1** is narrating experiences, opinions, managing conversation and hypotheticals. **B2** is argument, speculation, passives and reported speech, and abstract topics. **C1** is nuance, hedging, persuasion, idiom and register. The summary below follows the Core Inventory, which is the backbone, enriched with CV speaking outcomes and Cambridge task types (those items are partly unverified, see Q1).

### Inferences
- Cross-source agreement is strongest on grammar sequencing: past simple at A1/A2, present perfect at A2/B1, 2nd/3rd conditionals at B1, passives and reported speech at B1→B2, mixed conditionals at B2/C1, inversion at C1.
- The Core Inventory has no explicit "Work/professional" functions above A2. For working adults, add GSE Professional objectives and CV scales (meetings, phone/online calls, presentations, negotiation, mediation PT→EN) at B1–C1.
- For PT speakers, the C1 item "eliminating false friends" should be brought forward and treated as an ongoing thread from A2.
