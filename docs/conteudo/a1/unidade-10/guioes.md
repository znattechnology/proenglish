# A1.2 · Unidade 10 — Last week, next week (7 aulas) · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções e personagens: ver os [guiões da unidade 1](../unidade-01/guioes.md); famílias, rotinas e tempos livres: unidades [4](../unidade-04/guioes.md), [5](../unidade-05/guioes.md) e [9](../unidade-09/guioes.md).

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar** (unidades 1–10): tudo das unidades 1–9. Novo: *was / were*; *past simple* regular e irregular (+, −, ? com *did*); expressões de tempo passado (*yesterday, last week, two days ago*); *going to* (+, −, ?); expressões de futuro (*tomorrow, next week, this Friday*); os meses, os números ordinais e as datas; os anos.

**Não usar nesta unidade** (são do A2): o *present perfect* (*I have visited*); o *past continuous* (*I was working*); *will*.

## 10A — Where were you?

```
Curso: A1.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10A — Where were you?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer onde esteve e como foi, com was / were (I was in
  Benguela last weekend. It was great. The roads were bad.), e perguntar e responder (Where were
  you yesterday? Were you at work? — No, I wasn't.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: was (I / he / she / it) e were (you / we / they); wasn't / weren't; Was he…? —
  Yes, he was. / No, he wasn't.; Where were you…?; yesterday, last night / week / weekend /
  Friday, two days ago
Palavras-alvo: yesterday, last, ago, weekend, wedding, trip, great, terrible, tired, busy, sunny,
  hot, the roads, full
Reciclagem: be no presente (2B); dias da semana (5A); adjectivos (8C)
Pronúncia: was e were fracos nas frases (/wəz/, /wə/); fortes nas respostas curtas (/wɒz/, /wɜː/)
Contexto: segunda-feira de manhã; a Ana pergunta ao Paulo pelo fim-de-semana
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 10A (exercícios 1–10); perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 10
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Welcome to the last unit of A1. Today we go back in time: yesterday, last week.
(PT: Bem-vindo à última unidade do A1! Hoje voltamos atrás no tempo: ontem, a semana passada.)

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a recepção, segunda-feira, 8h00; o Paulo chega com ar cansado]

**ANA:** Good morning, Paulo! Where were you on Friday afternoon? You weren't in the office.
**PAULO:** I was in Benguela. My friend's wedding was on Saturday.
**ANA:** Oh, nice! Was it a good wedding?
**PAULO:** Yes, it was great! There were three hundred people. The food was delicious.
**ANA:** And the trip?
**PAULO:** The trip was terrible. The roads were very bad, and the bus was full. I'm so tired!
**ANA:** Were your parents there too?
**PAULO:** No, they weren't. They were in Viana.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Was* e *were***

[ECRÃ: tabela: o presente a cinzento, o passado a azul]

| Presente | Passado |
|---|---|
| I **am** | I **was** |
| he / she / it **is** | he / she / it **was** |
| you / we / they **are** | you / we / they **were** |

**PROF:** Am and is become was. Are becomes were. I was in Benguela. The roads were bad.
(PT: "Am" e "is" passam a "was"; "are" passa a "were". "Era / estava / foi" em português.)
[PAUSA 2 s depois de cada linha]

**Negativa e perguntas**

[ECRÃ: I **wasn't** at work. · They **weren't** there. · **Was** it good? — Yes, it **was**. / No, it **wasn't**. · **Were** you at home? — Yes, I **was**.]

**PROF:** Not: wasn't, weren't. Questions: Was it good? Were you at home? Short answers: Yes, it was. No, I wasn't.
[PAUSA 3 s depois de cada frase]

***There was / there were***

[ECRÃ: **There was** a party. · **There were** three hundred people.]

**PROF:** Remember "there is, there are"? In the past: there was, there were.
(PT: "Havia / houve": "there was" (uma coisa) e "there were" (várias).)

**Quando**

[ECRÃ: **yesterday** — ontem · **last night** — ontem à noite · **last week / last weekend / last Friday** — a semana passada / o fim-de-semana passado / na sexta passada · **two days ago** — há dois dias]

**PROF:** Yesterday. Last night. Last week. Last Friday — no "the", no "on". Two days ago — "ago" comes after.
(PT: "Last Friday", sem "the" nem "on". "Two days ago": o "ago" vem depois, ao contrário de "há dois dias".)

[ECRÃ: ✔ last Friday ✘ the last Friday ✘ on last Friday · ✔ two days ago ✘ ago two days]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In sentences, was and were are weak: I was /wəz/ in Benguela. They were /wə/ in Viana.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** In short answers, strong: Yes, it was /wɒz/. Yes, we were /wɜː/.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min)

**PROF:** Answer about you.
**PROF:** Where were you yesterday at eight p.m.?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I was at home. / I was at a restaurant.]
**PROF:** Were you at work last Saturday?
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.]
**PROF:** How was your weekend? Say one sentence.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: It was great. / It was quiet. / It was very busy.]

**PROF (reciclagem — 7A):** There was a wedding. Were there many people?
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, there were three hundred people.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! Was, were. Next lesson: what did you do? Verbs in the past.
(PT: Muito bem! A seguir: o que fez? Os verbos no passado.)

## 10B — What did you do?

```
Curso: A1.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10B — What did you do?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue contar o que fez com verbos regulares no past simple
  (I visited my parents. We watched a film.) e fazer negativas e perguntas com did (I didn't work.
  Did you call the client? — Yes, I did. What did you do?).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: past simple regular: -ed (worked, watched), -d (lived, danced), -ied (studied),
  consoante dobrada (stopped, planned); didn't + verbo simples; Did you…? — Yes, I did. / No, I
  didn't.; What / Where / When did you…?; o mesmo para todas as pessoas (she worked)
Palavras-alvo: visit, call, watch, cook, clean, play, stay, work, finish, start, travel, study,
  plan, stop, rain
Reciclagem: was/were e expressões de tempo (10A); rotinas (5E); present simple com do/does (3B, 5C)
Pronúncia: -ed /t/, /d/, /ɪd/ (em detalhe na 10E)
Contexto: a Sílvia conta o seu fim-de-semana ao Mateus
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 10B (exercícios 1–10); perguntas 3, 4 e 5 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: what did you do? With most verbs, the past is easy: add -ed.
(PT: Hoje: o que fez? Na maioria dos verbos, o passado é fácil: acrescenta-se "-ed".)

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a copa; a Sílvia e o Mateus com café]

**MATEUS:** Hi Sílvia! How was your weekend? What did you do?
**SÍLVIA:** It was good, thanks. On Saturday I cleaned the house and I cooked for my family. In the evening we watched a film.
**MATEUS:** Did you go dancing?
**SÍLVIA:** No, I didn't! It rained a lot on Saturday night. We stayed at home.
**MATEUS:** And on Sunday?
**SÍLVIA:** We visited my mother in Cazenga. My children played with their cousins all afternoon.
**MATEUS:** Did you work on Sunday?
**SÍLVIA:** No, I didn't work at all. And you? What did you do?

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Verbos regulares: *-ed***

[ECRÃ: tabela]

| Regra | Exemplos |
|---|---|
| + ed | work → worked · watch → watched · play → played · stay → stayed |
| -e + d | live → lived · dance → danced · arrive → arrived |
| consoante + y → ied | study → studied · carry → carried |
| consoante dobrada | stop → stopped · plan → planned |

**PROF:** Add -ed: worked, watched, played. Verbs with -e: add -d. Lived, danced. Study: studied. Stop: stopped.
(PT: Regras de escrita parecidas com as de "-s" e "-ing". Atenção: "play" tem vogal antes do "y": "played", não "plaied".)

**PROF:** And the past is the same for all people: I worked, she worked, they worked. No "s"!
(PT: O passado é igual para todas as pessoas: "she worked", sem "s".)

[ECRÃ: ✔ She worked. ✘ She workeds.]

**Negativa: *didn't* + verbo simples**

[ECRÃ: I **didn't work** on Sunday. · She **didn't go** dancing. · ✘ I didn't worked.]

**PROF:** Negative: didn't + the simple verb. I didn't work. Not "I didn't worked".
(PT: Na negativa, o passado está no "didn't"; o verbo fica simples: "I didn't work", nunca "I didn't worked".)

**Perguntas: *Did…?***

[ECRÃ: **Did** you **call** the client? — Yes, I **did**. / No, I **didn't**. · **What did** you **do**? · **Where did** you **go**? · **When did** she **arrive**?]

**PROF:** Questions: Did you call the client? Yes, I did. No, I didn't. What did you do? Where did you go?
(PT: Na pergunta, "did" no início e o verbo simples — como "do/does" no presente.)
[PAUSA 3 s depois de cada pergunta]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Did you: fast, /dɪdʒə/. Did you call the client? What did you do?
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** I didn't work. — /ˈdɪdənt/. [PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min)

**PROF:** Say it in the past.
[ECRÃ: "I watch TV every night." → yesterday]
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I watched TV last night.]
[ECRÃ: "She studies English." → last year]
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: She studied English last year.]
**PROF:** Answer about you. Did you work last Sunday?
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.]
**PROF:** Did you cook yesterday?
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Say two things you did last weekend. Use regular verbs: visited, watched, played, cleaned, stayed.
[PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: I visited my family and I watched a football match.]

**PROF (reciclagem — 9D):** What do you usually do on Sundays? And what did you do last Sunday?
[PAUSA 8 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! Next lesson: the irregular verbs — went, had, saw…
(PT: A seguir: os verbos irregulares.)

## 10C — I went to Benguela

```
Curso: A1.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10C — I went to Benguela        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue contar uma pequena história no passado com os verbos
  irregulares mais comuns (I went to Benguela. I took the bus. I saw my friends. We had lunch on
  the beach.) e usar did / didn't com eles (Did you see the client? I didn't buy anything.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: irregulares: go → went, have → had, do → did, see → saw, make → made, get → got,
  come → came, buy → bought, eat → ate, take → took, drive → drove, meet → met, say → said, give → gave,
  write → wrote; didn't / Did… + verbo simples (didn't go, did you see?); conectores first, then,
  after that, finally
Palavras-alvo: os verbos acima; first, then, after that, finally, trip, present
Reciclagem: past simple regular (10B); was / were (10A); transportes (5C)
Pronúncia: bought /bɔːt/ e saw /sɔː/; said /sed/ (não "seid")
Contexto: o Paulo conta à Sílvia a viagem a Benguela
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 10C (exercícios 1–10); perguntas 6 e 7 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Some verbs don't use -ed. They're irregular: go — went, have — had. Today you learn the most important ones.
(PT: Alguns verbos não usam "-ed": são irregulares. Hoje aprende os mais importantes.)

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a esplanada; o Paulo mostra à Sílvia fotografias de Benguela no telemóvel]

**SÍLVIA:** So, tell me about Benguela!
**PAULO:** First, I took the bus on Friday at six a.m. It was a long trip — eight hours!
**SÍLVIA:** Wow. And then?
**PAULO:** Then I met my friends at the hotel. In the evening we went to the beach and we ate fish.
**SÍLVIA:** And the wedding?
**PAULO:** The wedding was on Saturday. I saw a lot of old friends. We had a great time.
**SÍLVIA:** Did you buy a present?
**PAULO:** Yes, I did. I bought a nice lamp. And I gave a speech — in Portuguese, not English!
**SÍLVIA:** Ha! And on Sunday?
**PAULO:** Finally, on Sunday I came back to Luanda. I got home at ten p.m.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Os irregulares mais importantes**

[ECRÃ: tabela com três colunas: verbo, passado, tradução]

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| go | **went** | ir |
| have | **had** | ter |
| do | **did** | fazer |
| see | **saw** | ver |
| make | **made** | fazer (produzir) |
| get | **got** | obter, chegar |
| come | **came** | vir |
| buy | **bought** | comprar |
| eat | **ate** | comer |
| take | **took** | apanhar, levar |
| drive | **drove** | conduzir |
| meet | **met** | encontrar, conhecer |
| say | **said** | dizer |
| give | **gave** | dar |
| write | **wrote** | escrever |

**PROF:** Listen and repeat. Go, went. [PAUSA 2 s] Have, had. [PAUSA 2 s]
*(O professor diz cada par com 2 s de pausa.)*

**PROF:** You need to learn these by heart. Don't worry: you practise them in the Lab every day.
(PT: Estes aprendem-se de cor. O laboratório e a revisão diária ajudam a fixá-los.)

**Na negativa e na pergunta: o verbo simples**

[ECRÃ: I **went** → I **didn't go** · You **saw** → **Did** you **see**…? · ✘ I didn't went. ✘ Did you saw…?]

**PROF:** With didn't and did, the verb is simple: I didn't go. Did you see the client?
(PT: Com "did" e "didn't" o verbo fica sempre simples, também nos irregulares: "Did you go?", nunca "Did you went?".)

**Contar por ordem**

[ECRÃ: **First,** … **Then,** … **After that,** … **Finally,** …]

**PROF:** To tell a story: first, then, after that, finally.
(PT: Para contar uma história por ordem: primeiro, depois, a seguir, por fim.)

**Ir e voltar**

[ECRÃ: **go to** Benguela · **come back** to Luanda · **get home** — chegar a casa]

**PROF:** I went to Benguela. I came back to Luanda. I got home at ten. "Get home" — no "to".
(PT: "Get home" e "go home" não levam "to".)

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Bought: /bɔːt/ — no "g" sound. [PAUSA 2 s] Saw /sɔː/. [PAUSA 2 s] Said /sed/ — like "red". [PAUSA 2 s] Ate /eɪt/ or /et/. [PAUSA 2 s]
(PT: Em "bought" o "gh" não se ouve. "Said" diz-se "séd".)

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min)

**PROF:** Say the past.
[ECRÃ: go] [PAUSA 2 s] **PROF:** went.
[ECRÃ: buy] [PAUSA 2 s] **PROF:** bought.
[ECRÃ: see] [PAUSA 2 s] **PROF:** saw.
[ECRÃ: take] [PAUSA 2 s] **PROF:** took.

**PROF:** Tell me about your last trip or a special day. Use first, then, finally.
[PAUSA 15 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — First, I went to … Then, I saw … Finally, I came back …]

**PROF (reciclagem — 5C):** How did you go to work yesterday? By bus, by car, on foot?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I took the bus. / I drove. / I went on foot.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Great! Went, had, saw, bought… Next lesson: plans with "going to".
(PT: Óptimo! A seguir: planos com "going to".)

## 10D — Next week

```
Curso: A1.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10D — Next week        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer o que vai fazer com going to (I'm going to visit a
  client next Tuesday. We aren't going to work on Friday.) e perguntar (What are you going to do
  at the weekend? Are you going to travel? — Yes, I am.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: am / is / are + going to + verbo simples (+, −, ?); respostas curtas; tomorrow,
  next week / month / Friday, this weekend, on the 25th; going to go → going to the beach
  (forma natural)
Palavras-alvo: plan, tomorrow, next, this weekend, holiday, visit, finish, start, prepare, send
Reciclagem: present continuous com be (9C); past simple (10B, 10C); dias e datas (5A)
Pronúncia: going to /ˈɡəʊɪŋ tə/; "gonna" na fala informal (só para perceber)
Contexto: sexta-feira à tarde; a equipa fala dos planos para o fim-de-semana e para a semana seguinte
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 10D (exercícios 1–10); perguntas 8 e 9 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Last week, yesterday… and now the future: plans. I'm going to…
(PT: Agora o futuro: os planos. "Vou…")

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: sexta-feira, 16h30; a Sílvia, o Paulo e o Mateus a arrumar as secretárias]

**MATEUS:** What are you going to do this weekend, Sílvia?
**SÍLVIA:** I'm going to take my children to the beach on Saturday. And on Sunday we're going to visit my mother.
**MATEUS:** Nice! Paulo, are you going to play football?
**PAULO:** Yes, I am. And on Sunday I'm not going to do anything. I'm going to sleep!
**SÍLVIA:** And you, Mateus?
**MATEUS:** I'm going to cook for Luísa's parents. And next week I'm going to travel to Cabinda.
**PAULO:** Again? When are you going to come back?
**MATEUS:** On Thursday. And on Monday we're going to have the team meeting, right?

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Going to***

[ECRÃ: tabela]

| | |
|---|---|
| I**'m going to** visit a client. | I**'m not going to** work on Sunday. |
| She**'s going to** travel to Cabinda. | He **isn't going to** play. |
| We**'re going to** have a meeting. | They **aren't going to** come. |

**PROF:** For plans: be + going to + verb. I'm going to visit a client. She's going to travel.
(PT: Para planos: "be + going to + verbo". É como "vou visitar", "ela vai viajar".)
[PAUSA 2 s depois de cada frase]

[ECRÃ: ✔ I'm going to visit. ✘ I going to visit. ✘ I'm going to visiting.]

**PROF:** Don't forget "be", and the verb is simple: I'm going to visit. Not "I going to", not "going to visiting".
(PT: Não esqueça o "be", e o verbo fica simples.)

**Perguntas**

[ECRÃ: **What are you going to do** this weekend? · **Are you going to** travel? — Yes, I **am**. / No, I'**m not**. · **When is she going to** come back?]

**PROF:** What are you going to do this weekend? Are you going to travel? Yes, I am. No, I'm not.
[PAUSA 3 s depois de cada pergunta]

**Ir: *going to go***

[ECRÃ: I'm going to go to the beach. = I'm going to the beach.]

**PROF:** With "go", we usually say: I'm going to the beach. Shorter!
(PT: Com o verbo "go", é mais natural dizer "I'm going to the beach" do que "I'm going to go to the beach".)

**Quando**

[ECRÃ: **tomorrow** · **this weekend** · **next week / next month / next Friday** — sem "the" · **on the 25th**]

**PROF:** Tomorrow. This weekend. Next week. Next Friday — no "the".
(PT: "Next Friday", sem "the", como "last Friday".)

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Going to: /ˈɡəʊɪŋ tə/. "To" is short. I'm going to visit a client.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** In informal English, you hear "gonna": I'm gonna visit… Don't write it — just understand it.
(PT: Na fala informal ouve-se "gonna" em vez de "going to". Perceba-o, mas não o escreva no trabalho.)

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min)

**PROF:** Answer about you.
**PROF:** What are you going to do this weekend?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: I'm going to visit my family. / I'm going to the beach.]
**PROF:** Are you going to work tomorrow?
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.]
**PROF:** Say one thing you're not going to do this week.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I'm not going to eat fast food. / I'm not going to work late.]

**PROF (reciclagem — 10B):** And last weekend? What did you do?
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! Did, going to. Past and future. Next: dates, months — and the sound of -ed.
(PT: Muito bem! A seguir: datas, meses e o som de "-ed".)

## 10E — Dates and weekends

```
Curso: A1.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10E — Dates and weekends        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer os meses, os números ordinais e as datas (the 11th of
  November; 4 February 2026 — the fourth of February, twenty twenty-six) e pronunciar o -ed nas
  três formas (/t/ worked, /d/ played, /ɪd/ visited).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2, 3
Palavras-alvo: January … December; first … thirty-first; date, birthday, holiday, public holiday,
  Independence Day, year; mais verbos irregulares: swim → swam, sing → sang,
  leave → left, sleep → slept, put → put, read → read /red/, think → thought, tell → told
Reciclagem: números (2C, 6C); first / second (7C); past simple (10B, 10C)
Pronúncia: -ed /t/ (worked, watched, stopped), /d/ (played, called, lived), /ɪd/ (visited, started,
  needed); os ordinais com /θ/ (fourth, fifth, twelfth)
Contexto: sem situação; um calendário de 2026 com os feriados de Angola
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 10E (exercícios 1–9); pergunta 10 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Today: months, dates and the sound of -ed.
(PT: Hoje: meses, datas e o som do "-ed".)

### 2. Os meses e os ordinais (3 min)

[ECRÃ: os doze meses, com maiúscula]
**PROF:** January [PAUSA 2 s] · February [PAUSA 2 s] · March [PAUSA 2 s] · April [PAUSA 2 s] · May [PAUSA 2 s] · June [PAUSA 2 s] · July [PAUSA 2 s] · August [PAUSA 2 s] · September [PAUSA 2 s] · October [PAUSA 2 s] · November [PAUSA 2 s] · December [PAUSA 2 s]
(PT: Os meses, como os dias, escrevem-se com maiúscula.)

[ECRÃ: 1st first · 2nd second · 3rd third · 4th fourth · 5th fifth · 6th sixth · 7th seventh · 8th eighth · 9th ninth · 10th tenth · 11th eleventh · 12th twelfth · 20th twentieth · 21st twenty-first · 22nd twenty-second · 30th thirtieth · 31st thirty-first]
**PROF:** First, second, third. Then -th: fourth, fifth, sixth…
*(O professor diz cada ordinal com 2 s de pausa.)*
**PROF:** Careful: fifth, twelfth — the spelling changes.
(PT: Atenção à escrita: "fifth" (não "fiveth"), "twelfth", "ninth" (sem "e").)

### 3. As datas (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: um calendário de 2026 com os feriados de Angola assinalados]

(PT: Escreve-se "11 November"; diz-se "the eleventh of November".)
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Independence Day in Angola is on the eleventh of November.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** The Day of Peace is on the fourth of April. [PAUSA 3 s]

(PT: Os anos dizem-se em dois blocos: "twenty twenty-six", "nineteen seventy-five".)
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** When's your birthday? — It's on the … of …
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: It's on the fifteenth of March.]

**PROF:** With dates: on. On the 11th of November. With months: in. In November.
(PT: "On" com datas; "in" com meses e anos: "in November", "in 2026".)

### 4. O som do -ed (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: três colunas, as cores do plural e da 3.ª pessoa]

| /t/ | /d/ | /ɪd/ |
|---|---|---|
| worked | played | visited |
| watched | called | started |
| stopped | lived | needed |
| danced | stayed | wanted |

**PROF:** /t/: worked, watched, stopped. [PAUSA 2 s depois de cada]
**PROF:** /d/: played, called, lived. [PAUSA 2 s depois de cada]
**PROF:** /ɪd/ — one more syllable, only after "t" or "d": visited, started, needed. [PAUSA 2 s depois de cada]
(PT: Só os verbos que acabam em "t" ou "d" ganham uma sílaba: "visit-ed". Nos outros, o "-ed" não é sílaba: "worked" diz-se numa só sílaba, não "work-ed".)

[ECRÃ: ✔ worked /wɜːkt/ — uma sílaba ✘ "work-ed"]

**Mais verbos irregulares**

[ECRÃ: swim → swam · sing → sang · leave → left · sleep → slept · think → thought · tell → told · read → read (diz-se /red/)]
**PROF:** More irregular verbs. Swim, swam. Sleep, slept. Read — read: the same spelling, but /red/.
*(2 s de pausa por par.)*

[PAUSA 6 s]

## 10F — Real English: What did you do at the weekend?

```
Curso: A1.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10F — What did you do at the weekend?        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue identificar, em três relatos de fim-de-semana a velocidade
  quase natural, onde cada pessoa foi, o que fez e o que vai fazer a seguir.

Língua: unidades 1–10, sem estruturas novas
Vozes: Isabel (angolana), Tom (britânico), Graça (angolana, a mãe do Paulo)
Velocidade: quase natural
Duração: 9 min
Verificação: Lab 10F (exercícios 1–8); pergunta 11 do questionário
```

### 1. Apresentação (30 s)

**PROF:** Three people talk about the Independence Day holiday. Listen two times.

### 2. Primeira escuta: a ideia geral (2 min 30 s)

**PROF:** First: who stayed in Luanda?

**GRAÇA:** I didn't go anywhere. My children came to my house in Viana on the holiday. I cooked funge and muamba for twelve people! Paulo didn't help in the kitchen… but he washed the dishes.

3. Where did Tom watch the celebrations? (on the Marginal ✓ / in Mussulo / at home)

### 5. A sua vez (1 min)

## 10G — Work & Life: Weekly check-in

```
Curso: A1.2        Unidade: 10        Aula: 10G — Weekly check-in        Tipo: Work & Life
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dar à chefia um ponto simples da semana: o que fez
  (Last week I installed the new laptops and I visited Mussulo Energy.), o que não fez e porquê
  (I didn't finish the report because the client was late.), e o que vai fazer (This week I'm going
  to call Mr Okafor and finish the report on Wednesday.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 4
Gramática-alvo: toda a unidade; because; datas e dias
Palavras-alvo: check-in, report, task, finish, send, install, update, deadline, on time, late,
  problem, because, Any questions?, Good job
Reciclagem: horas e dias (5A); can (8B); telefone (9G)
Pronúncia: ritmo de um relatório: frases curtas, o tempo verbal claro, pausas entre tarefas
Contexto: segunda-feira, 9h00; reunião semanal do Sr. Baptista com a equipa
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 10G (exercícios 1–9); conversa livre "Monday meeting"; pergunta 12 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Every Monday, Mr Baptista has a check-in with the team: what did you do last week, and what are you going to do this week? Let's listen.
(PT: Todas as segundas, o Sr. Baptista faz um ponto de situação com a equipa: o que fizeram e o que vão fazer.)

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: a sala de reuniões; o Sr. Baptista, o Paulo, a Sílvia e o Mateus]

**SR. BAPTISTA:** Good morning, everyone. Let's start. Paulo, what did you do last week?
**PAULO:** Last week I installed the new laptops for the team. On Wednesday I visited Mussulo Energy and I updated their system.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Good job. Any problems?
**PAULO:** Yes, one. I didn't finish the report for Mussulo Energy, because their manager was sick on Thursday.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** OK. When are you going to finish it?
**PAULO:** I'm going to finish it on Wednesday and send it to you on Thursday morning.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Perfect. And this week?
**PAULO:** This week I'm going to call Mr Okafor about the new phones. And on Friday I'm going to train Nuno.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Great. Thank you, Paulo. Sílvia?

### 3. Língua (2 min 30 s)

**A estrutura de um ponto da semana**

[ECRÃ: três blocos com cores]

| 1. O que fiz | 2. Problemas | 3. O que vou fazer |
|---|---|---|
| Last week I installed… · On Wednesday I visited… | I didn't finish… because… | This week I'm going to… · On Friday I'm going to… |

**PROF:** Three parts: what you did — past simple. Problems — didn't, because. What you're going to do — going to.
(PT: Três partes: o que fez (passado), os problemas (didn't … because …) e os planos (going to).)

***Because***

[ECRÃ: I didn't finish the report **because** the manager was sick.]

**PROF:** Because gives the reason. I didn't finish the report because the manager was sick.
(PT: "Because" — porque — dá a razão.)

**Palavras do trabalho**

[ECRÃ: a report (relatório) · a task (tarefa) · a deadline (prazo) · on time (a horas) · late (atrasado) · update (actualizar) · train (formar)]

**PROF:** Report. Task. Deadline. On time. Late.
[PAUSA 2 s depois de cada palavra]

**PROF:** The boss says: Good job! — Bom trabalho! Any questions? — Alguma pergunta?
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Make the tense clear. Last week I instALLED… This week I'm GOing to call…
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Pause between tasks. Last week I installed the new laptops. … On Wednesday I visited Mussulo Energy.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada: o seu ponto da semana (3 min)

**PROF:** Now it's your check-in. I'm your boss. Look at your notes.
(PT: Agora é o seu ponto da semana. Eu sou a sua chefia. Olhe para as suas notas.)

[ECRÃ: as suas notas: Last week ✔ called three clients · ✔ wrote the monthly report · ✘ meeting with the bank — the client was late · This week → visit the bank on Tuesday · → send the budget on Friday]

[PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: Last week I called three clients and I wrote the monthly report.]
**PROF:** Any problems?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes. I didn't have the meeting with the bank, because the client was late.]
**PROF:** And this week?
[PAUSA 10 s]
[ECRÃ: This week I'm going to visit the bank on Tuesday and send the budget on Friday.]
**PROF:** Great. Good job!

**PROF (reciclagem — 5G):** Can we meet on Thursday to talk about the budget? — Answer with a time.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, of course. How about Thursday at ten?]
