## R2A — Review: past, present and plans

```
Curso: A1.2        Unidade: Revisão 2        Aula: R2A — Review: past, present and plans        Tipo: Revisão
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue falar durante 2 a 3 minutos sobre o trabalho, os tempos
  livres, o fim-de-semana passado e os planos, escolhendo o tempo certo (present simple, present
  continuous, past simple, going to) a partir das palavras de tempo (usually, now, last, next).
Objectivos de unidade que revê: U6–U10, todos
Gramática revista: os quatro tempos do A1 lado a lado; there is / are; can (pedidos e capacidade);
  comparativos; some / any; like + -ing; was / were; did / didn't; going to
Situação nova: três meses depois, o Mateus faz ao Nuno a conversa de fim de estágio
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: laboratórios R2-1 e R2-2; conversa "A day in town"; questionário da revisão
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! You finished ten units — all of A1! Today: no new grammar. We put the four times together: usually, now, last week, next week.
(PT: Olá! Terminou as dez unidades do A1. Hoje não há gramática nova: juntamos os quatro tempos — o hábito, o agora, o passado e os planos.)

### 2. Situação: o fim do estágio (3 min)

[ECRÃ: o gabinete do Mateus; o Nuno entra com um portátil. Título no ecrã: "Nuno — three months later"]

**MATEUS:** Come in, Nuno. Sit down. So, it's your last week as an intern. How was it?
**NUNO:** It was great! I learned a lot.
**MATEUS:** What do you usually do every day?
**NUNO:** I usually help the team with the computers. I answer the phone and I visit clients with Paulo.
**MATEUS:** And what are you doing this week?
**NUNO:** This week I'm installing the new system at Mussulo Energy. It's a big job!
**MATEUS:** What was your best day here?
**NUNO:** Let me think… The trip to Benguela last month. We went to a new client and I spoke English all day! I was very nervous, but it was fine.
**MATEUS:** Your English is much better now. Can you understand the clients on the phone?
**NUNO:** Yes, I can — well, most of the time! I can't understand fast American English!
**MATEUS:** Ha! And what are you going to do next?
**NUNO:** I'm going to finish my degree in Lubango. And next year I'm going to apply for a job here — if there's a place for me!
**MATEUS:** There's always a place for you, Nuno. Good job.

### 3. Os quatro tempos lado a lado (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: uma linha do tempo; quatro caixas a cores, cada uma com a palavra-chave e uma frase do Nuno]

| Palavra-chave | Tempo | Frase do Nuno |
|---|---|---|
| **usually, every day, on Saturdays** | *present simple* | I **usually help** the team. |
| **now, this week, today** | *present continuous* | This week I**'m installing** the new system. |
| **last month, yesterday, ago** | *past simple* | We **went** to a new client last month. |
| **next year, tomorrow, this weekend** | *going to* | I**'m going to finish** my degree. |

**PROF:** Look at the time words. They tell you the tense. Usually — I help. This week — I'm installing. Last month — we went. Next year — I'm going to finish.
(PT: As palavras de tempo dizem qual o tempo verbal a usar.)
[PAUSA 3 s depois de cada frase]

**PROF:** Now you. Complete with your life.
[ECRÃ: I usually … on Saturdays.] [PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Today I'm … ] [PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Last weekend I … ] [PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Next weekend I'm going to … ] [PAUSA 6 s]

### 4. Os erros mais comuns do A1.2 (2 min)

(PT: Estes são os erros mais comuns das unidades 6 a 10. Veja a frase errada e diga a certa.)

[ECRÃ: um erro de cada vez; a frase certa aparece depois da pausa]

| Errado | Certo | Unidade |

| ~~It has a bank near here.~~ | There's a bank near here. | 7 |
| ~~Can I to pay by card?~~ | Can I pay by card? | 8 |
| ~~more cheaper~~ | cheaper | 8 |
| ~~I like dance.~~ | I like dancing. | 9 |
| ~~I working now.~~ | I'm working now. | 9 |
| ~~I didn't went.~~ | I didn't go. | 10 |

*(Para cada linha: o professor lê a frase errada com tom de dúvida, [PAUSA 4 s], e diz a certa.)*

### 5. A sua vez: a sua conversa de fim de mês (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Now I'm your manager. Answer in full sentences.
(PT: Agora sou a sua chefia. Responda com frases completas.)

**PROF:** What do you usually do at work?
[PAUSA 8 s]
**PROF:** What are you working on this week?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: This week I'm working on … / I'm preparing …]
**PROF:** What did you do last weekend?
[PAUSA 8 s]
**PROF:** What can you do well? And what can't you do?
[PAUSA 8 s]
**PROF:** What are you going to do next month?
[PAUSA 8 s]

**PROF:** Now ask me two questions — one about the past, one about plans.
(PT: Agora faça-me duas perguntas: uma sobre o passado e outra sobre os planos.)
[PAUSA 12 s]
[ECRÃ: modelos — What did you do last weekend? · Where did you go on holiday? · What are you going to do this weekend? · Are you going to travel next month?]
**PROF:** Good questions! Remember: did for the past; are you going to for plans.
(PT: Lembre-se: "did" no passado; "are you going to" para os planos.)

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Fantastic! You can talk about your life — past, present and future — in English. Next lesson: getting ready for the A1.2 speaking exam.
(PT: Fantástico! Já fala da sua vida no passado, no presente e no futuro. A seguir: preparar o exame oral do A1.2.)

## R2B — Getting ready for the A1.2 speaking exam

```
Curso: A1.2        Unidade: Revisão 2        Aula: R2B — Getting ready for the speaking exam        Tipo: Revisão
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue descrever as três partes do exame oral do A1.2, sabe o que é
  avaliado e usa as estratégias de exame, incluindo explicar uma palavra que não sabe (I don't know
  the word in English. It's a kind of…).
Objectivos de unidade que revê: U6–U10, todos
Gramática revista: toda a do A1
Língua nova (blocos fixos): I don't know the word in English. · It's a kind of…
Situação: a Lurdes (do exame de demonstração do A1.1) faz o exame de demonstração do A1.2
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: conversa "A day in town" (ensaio); exame oral do A1.2
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Soon you take your A1.2 speaking exam — the last step of A1. Today you see how it works, and you practise every part.
(PT: Em breve faz o exame oral do A1.2, o último passo do A1. Hoje vê como funciona e pratica cada parte.)

### 2. Como é o exame (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: três blocos]

| Parte | O quê | Tempo |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Interview | O seu trabalho e os seus tempos livres: o que faz, do que gosta, o que sabe fazer | cerca de 1 a 2 minutos |
| 2. Past and plans | O fim-de-semana passado (ou umas férias) e os planos para a próxima semana | cerca de 2 minutos |
| 3. Situation | Uma situação sorteada: num restaurante, numa loja, na farmácia, a dar direcções ou ao telefone | cerca de 2 minutos |

**PROF:** Like the A1.1 exam, it's a conversation with the examiner. It's a little longer: about six minutes.
(PT: Como no A1.1, é uma conversa com o examinador. É um pouco mais longa: cerca de seis minutos.)

**PROF:** The examiner doesn't correct you and doesn't help you. Just speak!
(PT: O examinador não corrige nem ajuda durante o exame. Fale à vontade.)

**PROF:** The rules are the same: the recording is your choice, the exam opens when you finish most of the A1.2 lessons, and if you don't pass, you can try again a week later.
(PT: As regras são as mesmas do A1.1: a gravação é opcional (com ela, a pronúncia conta), o exame abre com 80% das aulas do A1.2 feitas, e pode repetir uma semana depois.)

### 3. O que é avaliado (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: cinco ícones]

| Competência | No A1.2, quer dizer… |
|---|---|
| Task — a tarefa | Responde a todas as perguntas, conta o passado, diz os planos e resolve a situação. |
| Grammar — a gramática | Os quatro tempos: *present simple*, *present continuous*, *past simple*, *going to*; *there is / are*; *can*. |
| Vocabulary — o vocabulário | As palavras das unidades 6 a 10: comida, preços, a cidade, compras, tempos livres, datas. |
| Fluency — a fluência | Fala em frases ligadas (*and, but, because, then*), sem parar muito tempo. |
| Pronunciation — a pronúncia | Os outros percebem-no. (Só com a gravação.) |

**PROF:** In A1.1 you answered in short sentences. In A1.2, connect them: and, but, because, then.
(PT: No A1.1 respondia com frases curtas; no A1.2, ligue-as com "and", "but", "because", "then".)

### 4. As estratégias (2 min)

[ECRÃ: **Can you repeat that, please?** · **I don't understand.** · **Let me think.** — já conhece · **I don't know the word in English. It's a kind of…** — nova]

**PROF:** You know three strategies: Can you repeat that, please? I don't understand. Let me think.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Here's a new one. You don't know a word? Say: I don't know the word in English. It's a kind of… and explain.
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: "candongueiro" → I don't know the word in English. It's a kind of bus — a blue and white minibus. · "gindungo" → It's a kind of sauce. It's very hot!]

**PROF:** Candongueiro? I don't know the word in English. It's a kind of bus — a blue and white minibus.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** Gindungo? It's a kind of sauce. It's very hot!
[PAUSA 4 s]
(PT: Explicar uma palavra por outras é uma competência de comunicação, e o exame valoriza-a.)

### 5. Um exame de demonstração (3 min)

**PROF:** Now watch Lurdes again. She's at the end of A1.2. Listen to how she answers.
(PT: Agora veja outra vez a Lurdes, já no fim do A1.2. Repare em como responde.)

[ECRÃ: "Practice exam A1.2 — Lurdes"; ícone do examinador]

**EXAMINADOR:** Hello, Lurdes. Welcome to your A1.2 speaking exam. Tell me about your job. What do you do every day?
**LURDES:** I'm an accountant at a bank. Every day I check the accounts and I answer emails. This month I'm preparing the annual report, so I'm very busy.
**EXAMINADOR:** What do you like doing in your free time?
**LURDES:** I love cooking and I like walking on the Marginal. I can't swim, but I want to learn.
**EXAMINADOR:** Now tell me about last weekend. What did you do?
**LURDES:** On Saturday I went to my sister's house in Talatona. We cooked muamba and we watched a film. On Sunday I didn't go out, because it rained.
**EXAMINADOR:** And next week? What are you going to do?
**LURDES:** Next Friday is my daughter's birthday — the twentieth. We're going to have a party at home.
**EXAMINADOR:** Thank you. Now a situation. I'm a visitor in Luanda. I'm in front of your office. Excuse me, is there a pharmacy near here?
**LURDES:** Yes, there is. Go straight on and turn left at the traffic lights. The pharmacy is opposite the bank.
**EXAMINADOR:** Sorry, can you repeat that, please?
**LURDES:** Of course. Go straight on. Turn left at the traffic lights. It's opposite the bank, on the right.
**EXAMINADOR:** Is it far?
**LURDES:** No, it isn't. It's five minutes on foot.
**EXAMINADOR:** Thank you very much.
**LURDES:** You're welcome.
**EXAMINADOR:** Thank you, Lurdes. The exam is finished. You can press the finish button now.

**PROF:** Look at what Lurdes does well.
[ECRÃ: lista com vistos]
**PROF:** She uses all four tenses: every day I check, this month I'm preparing, I went, we're going to have. She connects sentences: so, but, because. She says a date. And when the examiner asks her to repeat, she gives the directions again, in short parts.
(PT: Usa os quatro tempos, liga as frases com "so", "but" e "because", diz uma data e, quando o examinador pede para repetir, volta a dar as direcções por partes.)

### 6. Pratique cada parte (1 min)

**PROF:** Your turn. Part one: What do you like doing in your free time?
[PAUSA 8 s]
**PROF:** Part two: What did you do last weekend? And what are you going to do next weekend?
[PAUSA 12 s]
[ECRÃ: Last weekend I … and I … Next weekend I'm going to …]
**PROF:** Part three: I'm a waiter. Good afternoon! Are you ready to order?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: Yes, I'd like … , please. / Can I have … ?]

### 7. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** You're ready! First, practise with your tutor: "A day in town". Then, when you're ready, start your exam. Good luck — and congratulations on finishing A1!
(PT: Está preparado! Primeiro treine com o tutor na conversa "A day in town". Depois, quando se sentir pronto, comece o exame. Boa sorte, e parabéns por ter chegado ao fim do A1!)
