## 5A — What time is it?

```
Curso: A1.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5A — What time is it?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue perguntar e dizer as horas de duas formas (It's nine
  thirty. / It's half past nine.) e dizer os dias da semana e as partes do dia.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: What time is it? — It's…; o'clock, half past, quarter past, quarter to,
  ten past, twenty to; a.m. / p.m.; os dias da semana; in the morning / afternoon / evening, at night
Palavras-alvo: time, o'clock, half, quarter, past, to, a.m., p.m., Monday … Sunday, today,
  tomorrow, morning, afternoon, evening, night, midday, midnight
Reciclagem: números até 100 (2C); cumprimentos conforme a hora (1A)
Pronúncia: thirteen/thirty (2C); o acento nos dias (MONday, TUESday, WEDnesday /ˈwenzdeɪ/)
Contexto: o Paulo chega à recepção; o relógio da parede está parado
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 5A (exercícios 1–10); perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 5
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Good morning! Today you learn to tell the time and the days of the week. Very useful at work!
(PT: Bom dia! Hoje aprende a dizer as horas e os dias da semana. Muito útil no trabalho!)

### 2. Situação (1 min)

[ECRÃ: recepção da Kalandula Tech; um relógio de parede marca 6:00]

**PAULO:** Good morning, Ana. What time is it? Is it six o'clock?
**ANA:** No! The clock is old. It's seven forty-five. It's quarter to eight.
**PAULO:** Quarter to eight? Great, I'm early today!
**ANA:** Yes! And it's Monday. Meeting at nine o'clock.

### 3. Língua (4 min 30 s)

**Perguntar as horas**

[ECRÃ: **What time is it?** — Que horas são? · **It's** nine o'clock. — São nove horas.]

**PROF:** To ask: What time is it? To answer: It's… It's nine o'clock.
(PT: Para perguntar: "What time is it?". A resposta começa sempre por "It's…".)

**Duas formas de dizer as horas**

[ECRÃ: relógio digital e relógio de ponteiros lado a lado]

| Hora | Forma digital (mais fácil) | Forma tradicional |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00 | nine o'clock | nine o'clock |
| 9:10 | nine ten | ten past nine |
| 9:15 | nine fifteen | (a) quarter past nine |
| 9:30 | nine thirty | half past nine |
| 9:40 | nine forty | twenty to ten |
| 9:45 | nine forty-five | (a) quarter to ten |

**PROF:** The easy way: say the numbers. Nine fifteen. Nine thirty. Nine forty-five.
(PT: A forma mais fácil: diga os números como no relógio digital. Está sempre certa.)

**PROF:** You also hear the traditional way: quarter past nine, half past nine, quarter to ten.
(PT: Também vai ouvir a forma tradicional: "past" é depois da hora, "to" é antes da hora seguinte. Precisa de a perceber; para falar, pode usar a forma digital.)

**PROF:** Half past nine is 9:30 — like "nove e meia". Quarter to ten is 9:45 — like "um quarto para as dez".
(PT: A lógica é a mesma do português: "past" é "e" (depois de), "to" é "para" (antes de). "Half past nine" = nove e meia; "quarter to ten" = um quarto para as dez.)

[ECRÃ: ✔ half past nine = 9:30]

**Manhã e tarde**

[ECRÃ: **a.m.** = da meia-noite ao meio-dia · **p.m.** = do meio-dia à meia-noite · 15:00 = **3 p.m.** = three o'clock in the afternoon]

**PROF:** In English, we usually say 3 p.m., not fifteen hundred. 3 p.m. — three o'clock in the afternoon.
(PT: Em inglês, fala-se com 12 horas, não com 24: as 15h00 são "3 p.m.". "Midday" é meio-dia e "midnight" é meia-noite.)

**Os dias da semana**

[ECRÃ: Monday · Tuesday · Wednesday · Thursday · Friday · Saturday · Sunday — com a inicial maiúscula a cor]

**PROF:** Listen and repeat. Monday. [PAUSA 2 s] Tuesday. [PAUSA 2 s] Wednesday. [PAUSA 2 s] Thursday. [PAUSA 2 s] Friday. [PAUSA 2 s] Saturday. [PAUSA 2 s] Sunday. [PAUSA 2 s]

**PROF:** In English, days always have a capital letter: Monday.
(PT: Em inglês, os dias da semana escrevem-se sempre com maiúscula.)

**PROF:** And: today — hoje. Tomorrow — amanhã.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Wednesday: we don't say the first "d". /ˈwenzdeɪ/. WENZ-day.
(PT: Em "Wednesday" o primeiro "d" não se pronuncia: "uénz-dei".)
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Thursday — tongue between the teeth: /θ/. Thursday. [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Remember: thirTEEN, THIRty. It's two thirty. It's two thirteen. [PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min)

**PROF:** What time is it? Say it the easy way.
[ECRÃ: 8:00] [PAUSA 3 s] **PROF:** It's eight o'clock.
[ECRÃ: 10:30] [PAUSA 3 s] **PROF:** It's ten thirty. Or: half past ten.
[ECRÃ: 4:15 p.m.] [PAUSA 3 s] **PROF:** It's four fifteen. Or: quarter past four.
[ECRÃ: 6:45] [PAUSA 3 s] **PROF:** It's six forty-five. Or: quarter to seven.

**PROF:** What day is it today?
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: It's …day.]

**PROF (reciclagem — 1A):** It's eight p.m. You arrive at a dinner. What do you say?
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: Good evening!]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! Now you can tell the time and say the days. Next lesson: Sílvia's day.
(PT: Muito bem! Já diz as horas e os dias. A seguir: o dia da Sílvia.)

## 5B — Her day

```
Curso: A1.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5B — Her day        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue descrever a rotina de outra pessoa com o present simple
  na 3.ª pessoa (She gets up at five thirty. He goes to work by bus.), incluindo a ortografia
  (-s, -es, -ies) e os irregulares has, does, goes.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: present simple, 3.ª pessoa afirmativa: he/she/it + verbo-s; -es (watches, goes,
  does, finishes); consoante + y → -ies (studies); have → has; at + hora
Palavras-alvo: get up, have breakfast, take (the children) to school, go to work, start work,
  have lunch, finish work, go home, cook, have dinner, watch TV, go to bed, study
Reciclagem: present simple I/you (3B); has (4B); horas (5A)
Pronúncia: o -s da 3.ª pessoa em /s/ (works), /z/ (goes) e /ɪz/ (watches) — em detalhe na 5E
Contexto: a Sílvia conta a um colega como é o seu dia
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 5B (exercícios 1–10); perguntas 3, 4 e 5 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! In Unit 3 you said "I work". Today: he works, she works. A very important lesson!
(PT: Na unidade 3 disse "I work". Hoje: "he works", "she works". Uma aula muito importante!)

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: animação com o dia da Sílvia: relógio a avançar, casa, carro, escola, escritório]

**PROF:** This is Sílvia's day. Listen.
(PT: Este é o dia da Sílvia. Ouça.)

**NARRADORA:** Sílvia gets up at five thirty. She has breakfast with her family. At six thirty, she takes her children to school by car. The traffic is terrible! She starts work at seven thirty. She has lunch at twelve. She finishes work at four thirty. In the evening, she cooks dinner and helps the children with their homework. She goes to bed at ten.

[ECRÃ: o texto, com os verbos da 3.ª pessoa a cor]

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**A regra: *he/she* + verbo com -s**

[ECRÃ: tabela]

| I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
|---|---|
| I work | she work**s** |
| I start | she start**s** |
| I get up | she get**s** up |
| I live | he live**s** |

**PROF:** With he, she and it, the verb has "s": she works, he lives, it starts.
(PT: Com "he", "she" e "it", o verbo leva "s" no fim. É a regra mais importante do nível A1 — e a mais esquecida!)

[ECRÃ: ✔ She works in Luanda. ✘ She work in Luanda.]

**PROF:** Remember: you know this already! He has. She has. "Has" is the "s" form of "have".
(PT: Já conhece um caso: "has". É o "have" com "he/she".)

**A ortografia**

[ECRÃ: tabela]

| Regra | Exemplos |
|---|---|
| quase todos: + s | work → works · start → starts · live → lives |
| -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: + es | watch → watches · finish → finishes · go → **goes** · do → **does** |
| consoante + y: y → ies | study → studies · carry → carries |
| vogal + y: + s | play → **plays** · say → says |
| irregular | have → **has** |

**PROF:** Go — goes. Do — does. Watch — watches. Study — studies. But: play — plays.
(PT: Atenção: "play" tem uma vogal antes do "y", por isso só leva "s": "plays", nunca "plaies".)
[PAUSA 2 s depois de cada par]

**Horas com *at***

[ECRÃ: She gets up **at** five thirty. · She starts work **at** seven thirty. · She goes to bed **at** ten.]

**PROF:** With a time: at. At five thirty. At ten o'clock.
(PT: Com as horas usa-se "at": "at five thirty" — às cinco e meia.)

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Listen to the "s": she works /s/. She goes /z/. She watches /ɪz/ — one more syllable.
(PT: O "s" tem os mesmos três sons do plural da unidade 4.)
**PROF:** works [PAUSA 2 s] goes [PAUSA 2 s] watches [PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** Does: /dʌz/. Not "dous". Does. [PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min)

**PROF:** Look at Paulo's day. Say the sentence with "he".
[ECRÃ: 6:00 — get up]
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: He gets up at six o'clock.]
[ECRÃ: 8:00 — start work]
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: He starts work at eight.]
[ECRÃ: 17:00 — finish work]
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: He finishes work at five p.m.]
[ECRÃ: Saturday — play football]
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: He plays football on Saturdays.]

**PROF:** Now say one thing a person in your family does every day.
(PT: Agora diga uma coisa que uma pessoa da sua família faz todos os dias.)
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: My mother gets up at five. / My brother goes to school.]

**PROF (reciclagem — 4A):** Who's Sílvia's husband? What's his job?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: His name is Carlos. He's a mechanic.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! He works, she goes, he has. Next lesson: questions and negatives with "does".
(PT: Excelente! A seguir: perguntas e negativas com "does".)

## 5C — Does he…?

```
Curso: A1.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5C — Does he…?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue fazer perguntas e negativas sobre outra pessoa com
  does/doesn't (Does he drive to work? — No, he doesn't. What time does she start work? She doesn't
  work on Sundays.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: Does he/she…? — Yes, he does. / No, she doesn't.; he/she doesn't + verbo (sem -s);
  What time does…? Where does…? What does… do?
Palavras-alvo: does, doesn't, drive, take the bus, walk, by car, by bus, on foot
Reciclagem: do/don't (3B); 3.ª pessoa afirmativa (5B); a família (4A)
Pronúncia: does /dʌz/, doesn't /ˈdʌzənt/; Does he…? com a voz a subir
Contexto: o Paulo e a Ana falam do Sr. Baptista, que chega sempre muito cedo
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 5C (exercícios 1–10); perguntas 6 e 7 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello again! Today: questions and negatives about other people. With "does".
(PT: Hoje: perguntas e negativas sobre outras pessoas, com "does".)

### 2. Situação (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: recepção, 7h05; o carro do Sr. Baptista lá fora]

**PAULO:** Ana, what time does Mr Baptista start work?
**ANA:** He starts at seven. He's always here first!
**PAULO:** Does he drive to work?
**ANA:** Yes, he does. He lives in Talatona.
**PAULO:** Does he have lunch at twelve?
**ANA:** No, he doesn't. He has meetings all morning. He doesn't have lunch before two!
**PAULO:** Wow. And does he work on Saturdays?
**ANA:** No, he doesn't. On Saturdays he plays golf.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Negativa: *doesn't* + verbo sem -s**

[ECRÃ: tabela]

| I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
|---|---|
| I **don't** work on Sundays. | He **doesn't work** on Sundays. |
| They **don't** drive. | She **doesn't drive**. |

**PROF:** With he and she: doesn't. And the verb has NO "s": he doesn't work. Not "he doesn't works".
(PT: Com "he/she": "doesn't". O "s" já está no "doesn't", por isso o verbo fica sem "s".)

[ECRÃ: ✔ He doesn't work. ✘ He doesn't works. ✘ He don't work.]

**Perguntas: *Does…?***

[ECRÃ: **Does** he drive to work? — Yes, he **does**. / No, he **doesn't**.]

**PROF:** Question: Does he drive to work? Short answer: Yes, he does. No, he doesn't.
(PT: Na pergunta: "Does" + he/she + verbo sem "s". Respostas curtas com "does / doesn't".)

[ECRÃ: ✔ Does she work here? ✘ Does she works here?]

**Perguntas com *what, where, what time***

[ECRÃ: **What time does** she start work? · **Where does** he live? · **What does** she **do**? (= What's her job?)]

**PROF:** What time does she start work? Where does he live? What does she do?
[PAUSA 3 s depois de cada pergunta]

**PROF:** "What does she do?" asks the job, like "What do you do?" in Unit 3.
(PT: "What does she do?" pergunta a profissão, como "What do you do?" na unidade 3.)

**Como vai para o trabalho**

[ECRÃ: **by** car · **by** bus · **by** taxi · **by** candongueiro · **on** foot (a pé) · He **drives** to work. · She **walks** to work. · I **take** the bus.]

**PROF:** How do you go to work? By car. By bus. On foot. I take the bus. She walks.
(PT: "By" + transporte, sem artigo: "by car". A pé: "on foot".)

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Does — /dʌz/. Doesn't — /ˈdʌzənt/.
**PROF:** Does he drive? ↗ [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** No, he doesn't. ↘ [PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** What time does she start work? ↘ — "does" is short: /dəz/. [PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min)

[ECRÃ: ficha do Sr. Baptista: starts 7:00 · drives · lunch 14:00 · Saturday: golf]

**PROF:** Does Mr Baptista take the bus?
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: No, he doesn't. He drives.]
**PROF:** What time does he start work?
[PAUSA 4 s]
[ECRÃ: He starts at seven.]
**PROF:** Now you ask me a question about Sílvia. Start with "Does she…?"
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Does she have breakfast at home? / Does she drive?]
**PROF:** Make a negative sentence about a person in your family.
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: My father doesn't work on Sundays. / My sister doesn't drive.]

**PROF (reciclagem — 3C):** How do you go to work?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I go by bus. / I take a candongueiro. / I drive.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Great work! Does, doesn't — and no "s" on the verb. Next lesson: how often.
(PT: Óptimo! "Does", "doesn't" — e o verbo sem "s". A seguir: com que frequência.)

## 5D — How often?

```
Curso: A1.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5D — How often?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer com que frequência faz alguma coisa com always,
  usually, often, sometimes e never, na posição certa (I'm never late. She usually takes the bus.),
  e perguntar "How often…?".
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: advérbios de frequência: antes do verbo principal (I usually walk), depois de be
  (I'm never late); How often…? — every day, once a week, twice a week, on Mondays
Palavras-alvo: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, once, twice, late, early,
  on time
Reciclagem: 3.ª pessoa (5B, 5C); be (2B); horas e dias (5A)
Pronúncia: o acento em USually, SOMEtimes, NEVer, OFten /ˈɒfən/ (o "t" é mudo)
Contexto: o Paulo responde a um inquérito interno sobre o trabalho e a pontualidade
Duração: 9 min
Verificação: Lab 5D (exercícios 1–9); pergunta 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: always, usually, sometimes, never. How often do you do things?
(PT: Hoje: sempre, normalmente, às vezes, nunca. Com que frequência faz as coisas?)

### 2. Situação (1 min)

[ECRÃ: o Paulo ao computador, com um inquérito: "Work habits survey"]

**ANA:** Paulo, it's the work survey. How often are you late?
**PAULO:** I'm never late! Well… I'm sometimes late on Mondays. The traffic in Viana is terrible.
**ANA:** Do you usually have lunch in the office?
**PAULO:** Yes, I usually have lunch here. I sometimes go to a restaurant on Fridays.
**ANA:** And how often do you work at the weekend?
**PAULO:** Once a month. Not often!

### 3. Língua (3 min 30 s)

**Os advérbios**

[ECRÃ: uma escala de 100% a 0%]

| | |
|---|---|
| always | 100% — sempre |
| usually | 80% — normalmente |
| often | 60% — muitas vezes |
| sometimes | 40% — às vezes |
| never | 0% — nunca |

**PROF:** Always. Usually. Often. Sometimes. Never.
[PAUSA 2 s depois de cada palavra]

**A posição**

[ECRÃ: I **usually** walk to work. · She **always** has breakfast. · I'm **never** late. · He's **often** busy.]

**PROF:** The adverb goes before the main verb: I usually walk. She always has breakfast.
(PT: O advérbio fica antes do verbo principal: "I usually walk".)

**PROF:** But after "be": I'm never late. He's often busy.
(PT: Mas depois de "be": "I'm never late" — nunca chego atrasado.)

[ECRÃ: ✔ I'm never late. ✘ I never am late. · ✔ She always works. ✘ She works always.]

**PROF:** "Never" is already negative. Don't use "don't" with "never".
(PT: "Never" já é negativo: "I never drive", não "I don't never drive".)

***How often…?***

[ECRÃ: **How often** do you…? — every day · every Monday · once a week (uma vez por semana) · twice a week (duas vezes) · three times a week · once a month (uma vez por mês)]

**PROF:** How often do you go to the gym? Twice a week. How often does she travel? Once a month.
[PAUSA 3 s depois de cada resposta]

**Pontualidade**

[ECRÃ: **early** — cedo · **on time** — a horas · **late** — atrasado]

**PROF:** At work: I'm usually early. The meeting starts on time. Don't be late!
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** USually /ˈjuːʒuəli/. SOMEtimes. NEVer. And often: the "t" is silent. /ˈɒfən/.
(PT: Em "often" o "t" normalmente não se pronuncia: "ófen".)
**PROF:** usually [PAUSA 2 s] sometimes [PAUSA 2 s] never [PAUSA 2 s] often [PAUSA 2 s]
**PROF:** I'm never late. [PAUSA 3 s] She usually takes the bus. [PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (2 min)

**PROF:** Answer with always, usually, sometimes or never.
**PROF:** How often are you late for work?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I'm never / sometimes late.]
**PROF:** How often do you have breakfast?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: I always / usually / never have breakfast.]
**PROF:** How often do you speak English at work?
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Say one thing a colleague or a person in your family always does.
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: My boss always starts meetings on time. / My mother always gets up early.]

**PROF (reciclagem — 4B):** Do you have a car? Do you usually drive to work?
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! You're never late for English, right? Next: words for your routine and transport.
(PT: Muito bem! A seguir: palavras para a rotina e para os transportes.)

## 5E — Routines and transport

```
Curso: A1.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5E — Routines and transport        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue nomear as acções de um dia normal e os meios de transporte,
  e pronunciar o -s da 3.ª pessoa nas suas três formas (works /s/, lives /z/, watches /ɪz/).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Palavras-alvo: wake up, get up, take a shower, get dressed, have breakfast, leave home, take the
  bus, catch a candongueiro, arrive, start work, have a break, have lunch, finish work, go
  shopping, go to the gym, cook, have dinner, watch TV, read, go to bed; car, bus, taxi,
  candongueiro, motorbike, plane, boat, traffic, traffic jam
Reciclagem: 3.ª pessoa (5B); plurais em /s/ /z/ /ɪz/ (4D)
Pronúncia: -s da 3.ª pessoa: /s/ (gets, works, takes), /z/ (goes, lives, reads, plays),
  /ɪz/ (watches, finishes, uses)
Contexto: sem situação; aula com uma linha do tempo de um dia e fotografias de transportes em Luanda
Duração: 9 min
Verificação: Lab 5E (exercícios 1–9); pergunta 9 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Today: a full day in words, from morning to night. And transport in Luanda.
(PT: Hoje: um dia completo em palavras, da manhã à noite. E os transportes em Luanda.)

### 2. Um dia (3 min)

[ECRÃ: linha do tempo de 5h00 às 22h00; cada acção acende com uma imagem]

**PROF:** wake up [PAUSA 2 s] · get up [PAUSA 2 s] · take a shower [PAUSA 2 s] · get dressed [PAUSA 2 s] · have breakfast [PAUSA 2 s] · leave home [PAUSA 2 s] · arrive at work [PAUSA 2 s] · start work [PAUSA 2 s] · have a break [PAUSA 2 s] · have lunch [PAUSA 2 s] · finish work [PAUSA 2 s] · go shopping [PAUSA 2 s] · go to the gym [PAUSA 2 s] · cook [PAUSA 2 s] · have dinner [PAUSA 2 s] · watch TV [PAUSA 2 s] · read [PAUSA 2 s] · go to bed [PAUSA 2 s]

**PROF:** "Wake up" is when you open your eyes. "Get up" is when you leave the bed.
(PT: "Wake up" é acordar; "get up" é levantar-se.)

**PROF:** Many things use "have": have breakfast, have lunch, have a shower, have a break.
(PT: Muitas acções do dia usam "have": "have breakfast" — tomar o pequeno-almoço.)

## 5G — Work & Life: Setting up a meeting

```
Curso: A1.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5G — Setting up a meeting        Tipo: Work & Life
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue combinar o dia e a hora de uma reunião: propor
  (Can we meet on Tuesday at ten?), recusar com uma razão (Sorry, I'm busy on Tuesday.), propor
  outra hora (How about Wednesday at two?) e confirmar (OK, see you on Wednesday at two.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 4
Gramática-alvo: in / on / at de tempo (in the morning, on Tuesday, at ten); Can we meet…? How about…?
  Is … OK for you? I'm busy / free. That's fine.
Palavras-alvo: meeting, meet, busy, free, available, How about…?, OK, fine, perfect, see you,
  next week, this week, calendar
Reciclagem: horas e dias (5A); does/doesn't (5C); frequência (5D)
Pronúncia: ritmo e entoação de propostas: "How about WEDnesday at TWO?" ↗
Contexto: o Paulo precisa de uma reunião com o Sr. Baptista, que está sempre ocupado
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 5G (exercícios 1–9); conversa livre "Let's meet"; perguntas 11 e 12 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Paulo needs a meeting with Mr Baptista. But Mr Baptista is always busy! Learn to find a day and a time.
(PT: O Paulo precisa de uma reunião com o Sr. Baptista, que está sempre ocupado. Aprenda a combinar um dia e uma hora.)

### 2. Situação (2 min)

[ECRÃ: o gabinete do Sr. Baptista; no ecrã do computador, a agenda da semana]

**PAULO:** Mr Baptista, can we meet this week? It's about the new computers.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Of course. When?
**PAULO:** Can we meet on Tuesday at ten?
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Sorry, I'm busy on Tuesday morning. I have a meeting with a client.
**PAULO:** How about Wednesday?
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Wednesday… I'm free in the afternoon. Is two o'clock OK for you?
**PAULO:** Yes, that's fine. Wednesday at two.
**SR. BAPTISTA:** Perfect. See you on Wednesday at two, in my office.
**PAULO:** Thank you, Mr Baptista. See you on Wednesday.

### 3. Língua (3 min)

***In, on, at***

[ECRÃ: tabela com cores]

| | Usa-se com | Exemplos |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | horas | at ten · at two thirty · at night |
| **on** | dias | on Tuesday · on Wednesday morning · on Saturdays |
| **in** | partes do dia | in the morning · in the afternoon · in the evening |

**PROF:** At + time: at ten. On + day: on Tuesday. In + part of the day: in the morning.
(PT: "at" com horas, "on" com dias, "in" com partes do dia. Excepções úteis: "at night" e "at the weekend".)

[ECRÃ: ✔ on Tuesday ✘ in Tuesday · ✔ at ten ✘ in ten · ✔ on Tuesday morning]

**Propor, recusar, confirmar**

[ECRÃ: três colunas]

| Propor | Recusar | Aceitar e confirmar |
|---|---|---|
| Can we meet on…? | Sorry, I'm busy on… | Yes, that's fine. |
| How about…? | Sorry, I'm not free on… | Perfect. |
| Is … OK for you? | I have a meeting at… | See you on … at … |

**PROF:** To propose: Can we meet on Tuesday? How about Wednesday? Is two o'clock OK for you?
[PAUSA 3 s depois de cada frase]
**PROF:** To say no, politely: Sorry, I'm busy on Tuesday. Then give a reason.
(PT: Para recusar com educação: "Sorry, I'm busy…" e uma razão curta.)
**PROF:** To confirm: say the day and the time again. See you on Wednesday at two.
(PT: Para confirmar, repita o dia e a hora. Evita enganos!)

**Livre e ocupado**

[ECRÃ: **free** / **available** — livre, disponível · **busy** — ocupado]

**PROF:** I'm free on Thursday. I'm busy on Monday.
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

[PAUSA 5 s]

[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: I'm free on Thursday at three. / How about Thursday at three?]
**PROF:** Thursday at three is perfect. Now confirm.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: Great. See you on Thursday at three.]
