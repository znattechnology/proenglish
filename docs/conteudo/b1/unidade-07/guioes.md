# B1.2 · Unidade 7 — Calls and video calls · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções: as dos [guiões da unidade 1 do B1](../unidade-01/guioes.md): tudo em inglês, e o português só nos falsos amigos e na mediação.

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2, do B1.1 e da unidade 6. Novo: o discurso indirecto — afirmações com *say / tell* e a mudança de tempos (*is → was, will → would, can → could, has done → had done*); perguntas (*asked if / asked where, when, how many…*, com a ordem da afirmação); pedidos e ordens (*asked / told + pessoa + to / not to*); três verbos de relato (*explain that, promise to, agree to*); a linguagem do telefone e das videochamadas.

**Não usar nesta unidade:** outros verbos de relato com padrões difíceis (*suggest, recommend, insist, deny*), que ficam para o B2; o 3.º condicional, *should have* e *could have*; *must / might / can't have* de dedução; o *future continuous*; *wish*; a passiva com modais ou noutros tempos.

**Nomes de empresas e lugares:** a Mopane Systems, a Morro Azul Energia e a Tsodilo Sensors (de Gaborone) são fictícias.

**Personagens:** **Nuno**; **Lerato** (coordenadora); **Sílvia** (directora financeira); **Paulo**; **Sr. Baptista** (director da Kalandula Tech; só por escrito, na 7G). Da unidade 6: **Pieter** (director do terminal do Lobito), **Ingrid** (directora de operações da Morro Azul), **Thandi**. Novos e fictícios: **Kagiso Sebina** (do Botsuana, engenheiro comercial da Tsodilo Sensors) e **Lesego** (recepcionista da Tsodilo Sensors).

**A história:** Fevereiro de 2028. Depois do incidente do Lobito (B1 6G), a Morro Azul quer sensores de água em todas as salas de servidores: duas no terminal do Lobito, duas na central do Namibe e duas na sede, em Luanda — doze sensores, dois por sala, com um programa de alertas. A Mopane não tem este equipamento, por isso o Nuno procura um fornecedor: a Tsodilo Sensors, de Gaborone. Na segunda, 7, o Nuno relata à Lerato as chamadas de sexta (7A); na terça, 8, relata à Sílvia as perguntas do fornecedor (7B); na quarta, 9, explica ao Paulo o que lhe pediram (7C); na quinta, 10, liga para a Tsodilo e deixa um recado (7D); na segunda, 14, há uma videochamada com problemas de ligação (7F); na quarta, 16, o Nuno negoceia as condições e relata o resultado à equipa (7G).

## 7A — She said it was ready

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 7        Aula: 7A — She said it was ready        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue relatar o que outra pessoa disse, com say / tell e a mudança
  de tempos (She said she was happy with the report. He said he would send the price.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: say (sem pessoa) / tell + pessoa; that (pode sair); is → was, are → were, will → would,
  can → could, present continuous → past continuous, present perfect / past simple → past perfect;
  pronomes (I → he / she, my → his / her, we → they)
Palavras-alvo: claim, insurance, accept, price, quote, supplier, sensor, alert, by Wednesday, report
  (verbo e nome)
Reciclagem: past perfect (B1, 6A); passiva no passado (B1, 5B)
Pronúncia: said (com "e" curto, como em "red"); that fraco
Contexto: segunda-feira, 7 de Fevereiro de 2028, 9:00; o escritório da equipa conjunta, em Luanda; a
  Lerato pergunta ao Nuno pelas chamadas de sexta-feira
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 7A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 7
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! This unit is about calls and video calls — and about telling other people what someone said. Today: how to report what people said.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 7 de Fevereiro, 9:00; o escritório; a Lerato com uma chávena de café; o Nuno com o portátil aberto e um bloco de notas]

**LERATO:** Morning, Nuno. You had a lot of calls on Friday. Any news?
**NUNO:** Yes, good news, mostly. First, Pieter called. He said the insurance company had accepted the claim. They're paying for the switches and the UPS.
**LERATO:** Excellent. And Ingrid?
**NUNO:** She called after lunch. She said she was very happy with the report. And she told me she wanted water sensors in every server room — in Lobito, in Namibe and at their head office here.
**LERATO:** That's six rooms. Can Mopane supply them?
**NUNO:** No. I called Thandi. She said they didn't sell that kind of sensor, but she told me about a company in Gaborone: Tsodilo Sensors.
**LERATO:** Never heard of them. Did you call them?
**NUNO:** Yes. I spoke to Kagiso, their sales engineer. He said they'd installed sensors at three mines in Botswana last year. And he said he would send us a price by Wednesday.
**LERATO:** Good. Did he say anything about the delivery time?
**NUNO:** He said it was usually three weeks, but he couldn't promise anything before he saw the numbers.
**LERATO:** OK. Tell Sílvia, too — she'll want to see the price.
**NUNO:** I've already told her. She said she was free on Wednesday afternoon.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Do directo ao indirecto**

[ECRÃ:
Ingrid: "I**'m** very happy with the report." → She said she **was** very happy with the report.
Kagiso: "I**'ll** send you a price by Wednesday." → He said he **would** send us a price by Wednesday.
Kagiso: "I **can't** promise anything." → He said he **couldn't** promise anything.
Kagiso: "We **installed** sensors at three mines." → He said they**'d installed** sensors at three mines.]

**PROF:** When we report what someone said, the verb usually goes one step back into the past: "am" becomes "was", "will" becomes "would", "can" becomes "could", and the past becomes the past perfect.
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ: "I" → he / she · "my" → his / her · "we" → they · "you" → me / us]

**PROF:** And the people change too: "I" becomes "he" or "she"; "you" becomes "me" or "us".
[PAUSA 4 s]

***Say* ou *tell*?**

[ECRÃ: She **said** (that) she was happy. · She **told me** (that) she was happy.
✘ She said me… · ✘ She told that…]

**PROF:** "Tell" needs a person: told me, told Lerato. "Say" doesn't: she said. "That" can be left out.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: "Ela disse-me que…" → ✔ She told me that… · ✔ She said that… · ✘ She said me that…]
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Quando não muda**

[ECRÃ: Kagiso (sexta): "Delivery is usually three weeks." → He said delivery **was** usually three weeks. / He said delivery **is** usually three weeks. (ainda é verdade)]

**PROF:** If something is still true now, you can keep the present. Both are correct. But when you're not sure, go one step back.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "Said" sounds like "red", with a short "e". Listen: said.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: She said she was happy with the report.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** He said he would send a price by Wednesday.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Report it.
[ECRÃ: Paulo: "I'm tired." → Paulo said…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Paulo said he was tired.]
[ECRÃ: Sílvia: "I'll check the budget." → Sílvia said…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Sílvia said she would check the budget.]
[ECRÃ: Thandi: "We don't sell sensors." → Thandi told me…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Thandi told me they didn't sell sensors.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Think of a conversation you had last week — with a colleague, a friend, someone in your family. Report three things the person said. About forty seconds.
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last week I had lunch with my old colleague Ana. She said she'd changed jobs in January, and she was much happier now. She told me her new office was in Talatona. And she said she would call me when her company needed an accountant.]

**PROF (opinião):** Do you prefer to call people or to send messages? Why?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 5B):** Who were the sensors installed by, at the three mines?
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: They were installed by Tsodilo Sensors.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! "Said" and "told me", and one step back in time. Next lesson: reporting questions.

## 7B — He asked if we could…

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 7        Aula: 7B — He asked if we could…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue relatar perguntas (He asked how many sensors we needed. He
  asked if we could pay in advance.) com a ordem da afirmação.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: asked + if / whether (perguntas de sim / não); asked + palavra interrogativa (what, when,
  how many…); a ordem da afirmação, sem do / does / did; a mudança de tempos
Palavras-alvo: in advance, deposit, invoice, payment terms, quantity, how many, whether, warranty
Reciclagem: perguntas indirectas (B1, 1B): a mesma ordem; 7A
Pronúncia: asked (com som de "t" no fim); a entoação de um relato, a descer
Contexto: terça-feira, 8 de Fevereiro, 15:00; o gabinete da Sílvia; o Nuno conta-lhe as perguntas do
  fornecedor
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 7B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Yesterday: reporting what people said. Today: reporting what people asked.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: terça-feira, 8 de Fevereiro, 15:00; o gabinete da Sílvia; o Nuno com o bloco de notas]

**SÍLVIA:** So, Nuno, the supplier from Botswana called you again?
**NUNO:** Yes, this morning. Kagiso had a lot of questions.
**SÍLVIA:** What did he ask?
**NUNO:** First, he asked how many sensors we needed. I said twelve — two in each room. Then he asked if we wanted the alert software too.
**SÍLVIA:** And do we?
**NUNO:** Yes. Ingrid wants an alert on her phone. Then he asked where the rooms were, because their engineer needs to visit them.
**SÍLVIA:** Did he ask anything about money?
**NUNO:** He did. He asked if we could pay fifty percent in advance.
**SÍLVIA:** Fifty percent! What did you say?
**NUNO:** I said I didn't know, and that the finance director would decide. He asked when you could give him an answer.
**SÍLVIA:** Good answer. And did he ask when we need them?
**NUNO:** Yes — he asked whether the end of February was OK. I said it was a bit late, but possible.
**SÍLVIA:** OK. I'll look at the price on Wednesday. And fifty percent in advance is too much — we normally pay thirty days after delivery.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Perguntas de sim / não → *if* ou *whether***

[ECRÃ: Kagiso: "**Can** you pay fifty percent in advance?" → He asked **if** we **could** pay fifty percent in advance.
Kagiso: "**Do** you want the software?" → He asked **if** we **wanted** the software.]

**PROF:** For a yes-or-no question, use "asked if" — or "asked whether", a bit more formal. Then the normal order: subject, then verb.
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Perguntas com palavra interrogativa**

[ECRÃ: Kagiso: "How many sensors **do you need**?" → He asked how many sensors **we needed**.
Kagiso: "Where **are** the rooms?" → He asked where the rooms **were**.
Kagiso: "When **can you** give me an answer?" → He asked when **you could** give him an answer.]

**PROF:** For "wh-" questions, keep the question word, and then use the normal order: subject, then verb. No "do", "does" or "did".
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ He asked how many sensors did we need. · ✘ He asked where were the rooms. · ✔ He asked where the rooms were.]

**PROF:** It's the same order as the indirect questions of unit one: "Could you tell me where the rooms are?"
[PAUSA 4 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ He asked me that if we could pay. · ✔ He asked (me) if we could pay.]

**PROF:** And no "that" with "asked".
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "Asked" has a "t" sound at the end — say it quickly. Listen: asked. He asked if we could pay.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: He asked how many sensors we needed.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** He asked if we could pay fifty percent in advance.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Report the question.
[ECRÃ: Lerato: "Are you free on Friday?" → Lerato asked me…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Lerato asked me if I was free on Friday.]
[ECRÃ: Pieter: "When will the sensors arrive?" → Pieter asked…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Pieter asked when the sensors would arrive.]
[ECRÃ: Kagiso: "What's your email address?" → Kagiso asked…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Kagiso asked what my email address was.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Think of a job interview, a visit to a bank, or a call with a client. Report three or four questions the person asked you. About forty seconds.
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — When I opened my bank account, the manager asked me a lot of questions. She asked where I worked and how much I earned. She asked if I had another account, and whether I wanted a credit card. In the end, she asked when I could bring my documents.]

**PROF (opinião):** In your opinion, is it fair for a supplier to ask for fifty percent in advance? Why?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 1B):** Ask Kagiso about the delivery time, politely, with "Could you tell me…".
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: Could you tell me how long the delivery takes?]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! "Asked if" for yes-or-no questions, and the normal order after "how many", "where", "when". Next lesson: reporting requests and orders.

## 7C — They told us to wait

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 7        Aula: 7C — They told us to wait        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue relatar pedidos e ordens (She asked us to send weekly reports.
  He told me not to pay.) e usar três verbos de relato simples: explain that, promise to, agree to.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: ask + pessoa + to / not to (pedido); tell + pessoa + to / not to (ordem); explain (that) +
  frase; promise to + verbo; agree to + verbo
Palavras-alvo: contract, sign, discount, include, weekly, quote (orçamento), in writing, deadline
Reciclagem: 7A e 7B; going to (A2)
Pronúncia: to fraco ("asked us to send" — "to" quase sem vogal)
Contexto: quarta-feira, 9 de Fevereiro, 17:00; o escritório; o Nuno explica ao Paulo o que ficou
  decidido na reunião com a Sílvia e a Lerato, e o que o Kagiso prometeu
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 7C; conversa guiada "Take a message"; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: how to report what people asked you to do, or told you to do — and three useful reporting verbs.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: quarta-feira, 9 de Fevereiro, 17:00; o escritório; o Paulo chega de uma visita a um cliente; o Nuno ao computador]

**PAULO:** How was the meeting with Sílvia and Lerato?
**NUNO:** Busy. Kagiso called this morning with the price: one hundred and eighty dollars per sensor.
**PAULO:** Is that OK?
**NUNO:** Sílvia thinks so. But she told me not to pay anything in advance before we sign the contract. And she asked me to get the price in writing, with the delivery date.
**PAULO:** Makes sense. And Lerato?
**NUNO:** Lerato told me to call Kagiso again tomorrow, and she asked us to send Ingrid a weekly report about the sensors, every Friday.
**PAULO:** Us? So, me too?
**NUNO:** You too! She asked you to do the visit in Namibe with their engineer.
**PAULO:** OK, fine. And did Kagiso answer your questions about the software?
**NUNO:** Yes. He explained that the software was included for the first year. After that, it's three hundred dollars a year.
**PAULO:** Not bad.
**NUNO:** And he promised to send a sample sensor by courier, so we can test it. And he agreed to give us a five percent discount if we order before the fifteenth.
**PAULO:** Then let's not wait!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Pedidos e ordens: *ask / tell + pessoa + to***

[ECRÃ:
Sílvia: "**Please get** the price in writing." → She **asked me to get** the price in writing. (pedido)
Lerato: "**Call** Kagiso again tomorrow." → She **told me to call** Kagiso again. (ordem)
Sílvia: "**Don't pay** anything in advance." → She **told me not to pay** anything in advance.]

**PROF:** For requests, use "asked" plus a person plus "to". For orders or instructions, "told" plus a person plus "to". For "don't", use "not to".
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ: "Pediu-me para ligar." → ✘ She asked me for call. · ✘ She asked me call. · ✔ She **asked me to call**.
"Disse-me para não pagar." → ✘ She told me don't pay. · ✔ She **told me not to pay**.]

**PROF:** Careful: after "asked me" and "told me", always "to" plus the verb.
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Três verbos de relato**

[ECRÃ:
**explain** (that) + frase: He **explained that** the software **was** included.
**promise to** + verbo: He **promised to send** a sample.
**agree to** + verbo: He **agreed to give** us a discount.]

**PROF:** "Said" is always correct, but these verbs give more information. "Explained that", "promised to", "agreed to".
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ He explained me that… · ✔ He explained (to me) that…]

**PROF:** And notice: "explain" is not like "tell". You can't say "explained me". Say "explained to me", or just "explained".
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "To" is very weak in these sentences. Listen: She asked me to get the price.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: She told me not to pay anything in advance.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** He promised to send a sample.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Report it.
[ECRÃ: Lerato to Paulo: "Please do the visit in Namibe." → Lerato asked Paulo…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Lerato asked Paulo to do the visit in Namibe.]
[ECRÃ: Doctor to me: "Don't work this weekend." → The doctor told me…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: The doctor told me not to work this weekend.]
[ECRÃ: Kagiso: "OK, I'll give you five percent." → Kagiso agreed…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Kagiso agreed to give us five percent.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Your manager gave you instructions this week, or a teacher, or a doctor. Report two things they asked you to do, one thing they told you not to do, and one promise. About forty seconds.
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — On Monday my manager asked me to prepare the budget for March, and she told me to send it to her by Thursday. She told me not to include the new office, because it isn't approved yet. And she promised to give me a day off next week!]

**PROF (opinião):** Do you prefer instructions in writing or by phone? Why?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — 7A):** What did Nuno say about the price? Report it.
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: He said the price was one hundred and eighty dollars per sensor.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! "Asked me to", "told me not to", and "explained", "promised", "agreed". Now talk to the tutor: "Take a message". Next lesson: phone calls.

## 7D — Can I take a message?

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 7        Aula: 7D — Can I take a message?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue fazer e receber uma chamada de trabalho: identificar-se, pedir
  para falar com alguém, deixar e anotar um recado completo, soletrar e confirmar números e e-mails.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: This is… (não "I am…"); Could I speak to…? / Is … available?; I'm afraid he's not
  available. / He's in a meeting.; Can I take a message? / Could you ask him to call me back?; Could you
  spell that? / Let me read that back to you.
Palavras-alvo: speak to, available, take a message, leave a message, call back, extension, spell, read
  back, at (@), dot, underscore, hyphen
Reciclagem: pedidos com ask + to (7C): Could you ask him to call me?; A1, unidade 9 (o recado simples)
Pronúncia: soletrar (as letras difíceis para quem fala português: a, e, i, g, j, h, y, w); números em grupos
Contexto: quinta-feira, 10 de Fevereiro, 10:00; o Nuno liga para a Tsodilo Sensors, em Gaborone; atende a
  Lesego, a recepcionista
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 7D; conversa guiada "Take a message"; pergunta 7 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! You learned to leave a short message in A1. Today: a complete business call — who you are, who you want, the message, and checking every detail.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: quinta-feira, 10 de Fevereiro, 10:00; o Nuno ao telefone, em Luanda; do outro lado, a recepção da Tsodilo Sensors, em Gaborone]

**LESEGO:** Good morning, Tsodilo Sensors, Lesego speaking. How can I help you?
**NUNO:** Good morning. This is Nuno Kapinga, from Kalandula Tech in Luanda. Could I speak to Kagiso Sebina, please?
**LESEGO:** I'm afraid Kagiso's in a meeting at the moment. Can I take a message?
**NUNO:** Yes, please. Could you ask him to call me back today? It's about our order for the water sensors.
**LESEGO:** Of course. Could you spell your surname, please?
**NUNO:** Sure. It's K-A-P-I-N-G-A. Kapinga.
**LESEGO:** K-A-P-I-N-G-A. And your number?
**NUNO:** It's plus two four four, nine two three, four five six, seven one eight.
**LESEGO:** Let me read that back to you: plus two four four, nine two three, four five six, seven one eight.
**NUNO:** That's right. And could you ask him to send the price in writing, with the delivery date? My email is nuno dot kapinga at kalandula tech dot co dot ao. "Kalandula tech" is one word.
**LESEGO:** Nuno dot kapinga at kalandulatech, one word, dot co dot ao. Got it.
**NUNO:** Perfect. And one more thing: the discount is only until the fifteenth, so it's quite urgent.
**LESEGO:** So, just to check: you'd like Kagiso to call you back today about the order, and to send you the price in writing with the delivery date, before the fifteenth. Is that right?
**NUNO:** Exactly. Thank you, Lesego.
**LESEGO:** You're welcome. I'll give him the message as soon as he's free. Goodbye!

[ECRÃ: o recado da Lesego: "For Kagiso — Nuno Kapinga, Kalandula Tech (Luanda), +244 923 456 718, nuno.kapinga@kalandulatech.co.ao. Please call back today (order, water sensors). Send price in writing + delivery date. Urgent: discount until 15 Feb."]

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Começar a chamada**

[ECRÃ: atender — **Good morning, [empresa], [nome] speaking.** · ligar — **This is Nuno Kapinga, from Kalandula Tech.** · **Could I speak to…, please?** · **Is … available?**]

**PROF:** On the phone, say "This is…", not "I am…". And to ask for someone: "Could I speak to…?"
[PAUSA 4 s]

[ECRÃ: "Fala o Nuno." → ✘ I am Nuno. / ✘ Here is Nuno. · ✔ **This is Nuno.** / **Nuno speaking.**]
[PAUSA 3 s]

**Quando a pessoa não está**

[ECRÃ: I'm afraid he's **in a meeting** / **out of the office** / **on another call**. · **Can I take a message?** · **Would you like to leave a message?** · **Could you ask him to call me back?**]

**PROF:** "Take a message" is what the receptionist does; "leave a message" is what the caller does.
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Confirmar**

[ECRÃ: **Could you spell that, please?** · **Let me read that back to you.** · **So, just to check: …** · **Is that right?**]

**PROF:** On the phone, always read back names, numbers and emails. It's not rude — it's professional.
[PAUSA 4 s]

**E-mails ao telefone**

[ECRÃ: @ = **at** · . = **dot** · _ = **underscore** · - = **hyphen** ("dash") · tudo junto = **one word**]
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia: soletrar e números (1 min)

[ECRÃ: as letras que mais confundem: **A** (ei) · **E** (ii) · **I** (ai) · **G** (dji) · **J** (djei) · **H** (eitch) · **Y** (uai) · **W** (dâbliu)]

**PROF:** These letters sound very different in English. Listen and repeat: A, E, I.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** G, J. H. Y, W.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** And numbers in groups of three, with a short pause: nine two three, four five six, seven one eight.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Answer the phone, or ask for someone.
[ECRÃ: Atende o telefone na sua empresa.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Good morning, [your company], [your name] speaking. How can I help you?]

### 3. Como se relata uma chamada (2 min 30 s)
