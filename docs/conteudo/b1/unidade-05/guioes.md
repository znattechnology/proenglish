# B1.1 · Unidade 5 — How it works · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções: as dos [guiões da unidade 1 do B1](../unidade-01/guioes.md): tudo em inglês, e o português só nos falsos amigos e na mediação.

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2 e das unidades 1 a 4 do B1. Novo: a voz passiva no presente e no passado simples (*The panels are cleaned every month. The plant was built in 2025.*), com *by* só quando importa; orações relativas de definição com *who, which, that, where* e sem pronome (*the report I sent you*); sequenciadores de processo (*First… Then… After that… Once… Finally…*); regras com *must / have to / mustn't / don't have to* (revistas do A2).

**Não usar nesta unidade:** a passiva com modais, no *present perfect* ou contínua (nem "must be worn", "has been installed", "is being cleaned": diga *You must wear…*); orações relativas explicativas, entre vírgulas (*Hannah, who…*), que ficam para o B2; o *past perfect*; o 3.º condicional e *should have*; *wish*; o discurso indirecto no passado; o *future continuous*; *must / can't* de dedução (*must* só de obrigação); *be / get used to*. As orações relativas só aparecem a partir da 5C.

**Nomes de empresas e lugares:** a Mopane Systems e a Morro Azul Energia são fictícias. Os números da central (25 megawatts, cinquenta mil painéis) são fictícios.

**Personagens:** **Nuno** e **Paulo** (no Namibe para a instalação); **Hannah** (engenheira de projecto da Morro Azul); **Sr. Domingos** (chefe de manutenção; só fala português, B1 3G); **Kofi Asante** (ganês, engenheiro de redes numa empresa de energia solar de Acra; B1 1C e 1E) e a sua colega **Wanjiru** (queniana); **Femi** (director financeiro da Morro Azul; B1 3F) e **Sipho** (Mopane Systems; B1 1A e 3C).

**A história:** Novembro de 2027. O despachante tirou as antenas da alfândega a tempo (B1 4F). O Nuno e o Paulo chegam à central do Namibe no domingo, 14. Na segunda, 15, a Hannah mostra-lhes como funciona a central (5A); na terça, 16, ao jantar, conta a história da central (5B); na quarta, 17, a ligação por satélite fica pronta e a equipa confere o equipamento (5C); na quinta, 18, a Hannah faz uma visita guiada ao Kofi e à Wanjiru, que vêm ver a solução de reserva (5E); na sexta, 19, antes de a grua começar, o Nuno faz o briefing de segurança ao Femi e ao Sipho (5F).

## 5A — It's made in…

```
Curso: B1.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5A — It's made in…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue descrever um processo e factos com a voz passiva no presente
  (The panels are cleaned every month. The data is sent to the control room.), com by só quando importa.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: am / is / are + particípio passado; negativas e perguntas (Are they cleaned by hand?);
  by + quem faz, só quando é informação útil; particípios irregulares (made, sent, built, done, taken)
Palavras-alvo: solar panel, sunlight, electricity, inverter, substation, grid, sensor, control room,
  clean, collect, send, change into, megawatt
Reciclagem: present simple para processos (A2); probabilidade (B1, 4C); question tags (B1, 1A)
Pronúncia: are e is fracos na passiva; o -ed dos particípios (cleaned, collected, changed)
Contexto: segunda-feira, 15 de Novembro, 8:00; a central solar do Namibe; a Hannah mostra a central ao
  Nuno e ao Paulo
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 5A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 5
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! This unit is about how things work — processes, history, and safety. Today: how to describe a process when the action is more important than the person.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 15 de Novembro, 8:00; a central solar do Namibe; filas de painéis até ao horizonte; a Hannah, o Nuno e o Paulo de capacete]

**HANNAH:** Welcome to Morro Azul, both of you! So, this is the plant. Twenty-five megawatts — about fifty thousand panels.
**PAULO:** Fifty thousand! How does it work, exactly?
**HANNAH:** It's simple, really. First, sunlight is changed into electricity by the panels. But it's the wrong kind of electricity for the grid, so it's sent to the inverters — those white boxes over there.
**NUNO:** And then?
**HANNAH:** Then it's changed into the right kind, and after that, it's sent to the substation. From there, it goes into the national grid.
**PAULO:** And the panels? With all this sand, they get really dirty, don't they?
**HANNAH:** Very dirty! They're cleaned every two weeks. In the windy months, every week.
**NUNO:** Are they cleaned by hand?
**HANNAH:** No, a small machine is used — it's driven along each row. And no water is used: it's a dry system.
**NUNO:** And the data from the sensors? That's our part.
**HANNAH:** Exactly. The data is collected every minute and sent to the control room by the radio link. When the link goes down, nothing is received — and we're blind.
**NUNO:** Not after this week. With the satellite, the data will probably arrive all the time.
**HANNAH:** I hope so! Come on — I'll show you the control room.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**A passiva no presente**

[ECRÃ: Activa: The panels **change** sunlight into electricity.
Passiva: Sunlight **is changed** into electricity (by the panels).
is / are + particípio passado]

**PROF:** In the passive, the important thing is the action, or the thing — not the person. "Is" or "are", plus the past participle: is changed, are cleaned, is sent.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: The panels **are cleaned** every two weeks. · The data **is collected** every minute. · **Are** they **cleaned** by hand? · No water **is used**.]

**PROF:** Singular: "is". Plural: "are". For questions, put "is" or "are" first: "Are they cleaned by hand?"
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Particípios irregulares**

[ECRÃ: make → **made** · send → **sent** · build → **built** · do → **done** · take → **taken** · drive → **driven** · use → **used** (regular)]

**PROF:** Many past participles are irregular. Learn them with the passive: it's made, it's sent, it's done.
[PAUSA 4 s]

***By* — só quando importa**

[ECRÃ: ✔ The panels are cleaned every two weeks. (quem limpa? não importa)
✔ Sunlight is changed into electricity **by the panels**. (importa: são os painéis)
⚠ The panels are cleaned every two weeks by people. (informação inútil)]

**PROF:** Use "by" only when the person or the thing is useful information. Most passive sentences don't have "by".
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: "Os painéis limpam-se de duas em duas semanas." / "Fala-se inglês." → The panels **are cleaned** every two weeks. · English **is spoken** here.]

**PROF:** In Portuguese, you often say "limpam-se" or "fala-se". In English, that's usually the passive.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In the passive, "is" and "are" are weak and short. The participle is strong. Listen: They're cleaned every two weeks.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: The data is collected every minute.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Sunlight is changed into electricity.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Make it passive.
[ECRÃ: They send the data to the control room. → The data …] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: The data is sent to the control room.]
[ECRÃ: People make these panels in China. → These panels …] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: These panels are made in China.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Describe how something is made or done: coffee, bread, a passport, or something at your work. Use at least three passive sentences. About forty seconds.
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — In my bank, a new account is opened in three steps. First, the documents are checked at the front desk. Then the client's information is entered in the system. After that, the card is sent to the branch, and the client is called. It usually takes a week.]

**PROF (opinião):** Do you think solar energy is a good idea for Angola? Why?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 4C):** Will the panels be clean tomorrow? There's a sandstorm tonight. Answer with "probably".
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: They probably won't be clean.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! "Is" or "are" plus the participle — the action is the star. Next lesson: the passive in the past, for the history of a place.

## 5B — It was built in 2025

```
Curso: B1.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5B — It was built in 2025        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue contar a história de um lugar ou de um projecto com a voz
  passiva no passado (The plant was built in 2025. The panels were made in China.).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: was / were + particípio passado; perguntas (When was it built? Where were they made?);
  activa ou passiva conforme o que é importante
Palavras-alvo: build, open, design, choose, buy, land, construction, project, company, engineer,
  investor, history
Reciclagem: past simple (A2); 2.º condicional (B1, 4A)
Pronúncia: was e were fracos (it was built, they were made)
Contexto: terça-feira, 16 de Novembro, 20:00; um restaurante em Moçâmedes, junto ao mar; a Hannah, o Nuno
  e o Paulo ao jantar
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 5B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! When we tell the history of a building or a project, the passive is very useful. Today: the passive in the past.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: terça-feira, 16 de Novembro, 20:00; um restaurante em Moçâmedes, junto ao mar; peixe grelhado na mesa]

**PAULO:** Hannah, how old is the plant? It looks very new.
**HANNAH:** It is. It was built in 2025, and it was opened in October that year.
**NUNO:** And when was the project started?
**HANNAH:** Much earlier. The land was chosen in 2022, because there's sun almost every day here. Then the land was bought, and the plant was designed by a team in Lisbon.
**PAULO:** Where were the panels made?
**HANNAH:** In China. They were sent by ship to the port here in Moçâmedes. The inverters were made in Germany.
**NUNO:** And the radio link? When was that installed?
**HANNAH:** In 2025, during the construction — by a small company from Luanda. It was the cheapest option.
**NUNO:** Ah. That explains a lot.
**HANNAH:** Ha! Yes. If we chose again today, we'd choose differently. But that's why you're here.
**PAULO:** How many people were employed during the construction?
**HANNAH:** About four hundred. Most of them were hired here in Namibe. Now we're only thirty-five.
**NUNO:** It's an amazing place. And the fish is fantastic.
**HANNAH:** It was caught this morning. Enjoy!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**A passiva no passado**

[ECRÃ: The plant **was built** in 2025. · The panels **were made** in China. · was / were + particípio passado]

**PROF:** For the past: "was" or "were", plus the past participle. Singular "was", plural "were".
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Perguntas**

[ECRÃ: **When was** the plant **built**? · **Where were** the panels **made**? · **How many** people **were employed**?]

**PROF:** For questions, the question word, then "was" or "were", then the subject, then the participle.
[PAUSA 4 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ When the plant was built? · ✘ When was built the plant? · ✔ When **was** the plant **built**?]
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Activa ou passiva?**

[ECRÃ: A team in Lisbon **designed** the plant. (o importante: a equipa) · The plant **was designed** by a team in Lisbon. (o importante: a central)]

**PROF:** Both are correct. Choose the passive when the thing is your topic: Hannah is talking about the plant, so: "The plant was designed by a team in Lisbon."
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: "Nasci em Lubango." → ✔ I **was born** in Lubango. · ✘ I born in Lubango. · ✘ I am born in Lubango.]

**PROF:** You already know one past passive: "I was born". Born is always passive in English.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "Was" and "were" are weak in the passive. Listen: It was built in 2025.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: It was built in 2025.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** They were made in China.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Ask the question.
[ECRÃ: the bridge / build / ? → (1972)] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: When was the bridge built?]
[ECRÃ: these phones / make / ? → (South Korea)] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Where were these phones made?]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Tell me the history of a building, a company or a project. Say when it was started, built or opened, and give two more facts. Use the passive. About forty seconds.
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — My company was started in 2010 by two engineers from Benguela. The first office was opened in Lobito, and our head office was built in Luanda in 2019. Last year, a new branch was opened in Huambo, and twenty people were hired.]

**PROF (opinião):** In your opinion, is it better to hire local people for a big project, or experienced people from other places? Why?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 4A):** Hannah says: "If we chose again today, we'd choose differently." What would you change in your city, if you were the mayor?
[PAUSA 15 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — If I were the mayor, I'd build more bus lanes.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! "Was" or "were" plus the participle for the past. Next lesson: how to say exactly which person, thing or place — "the engineer who…", "the room where…".

## 5C — The engineer who designed it

```
Curso: B1.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5C — The engineer who designed it        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue definir pessoas, coisas e lugares com orações relativas (the
  engineer who…, the cable that…, the room where…) e omitir o pronome quando ele é objecto (the report
  I sent you).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: who (pessoas), which / that (coisas), where (lugares); sem pronome quando a oração tem
  outro sujeito (the cable [that] Paulo needs); sem repetir o pronome pessoal (✘ the cable that I need it)
Palavras-alvo: cable, router, box, dish, label, list, check, connect, the one, equipment
Reciclagem: passiva no presente (5A); question tags (B1, 1A)
Pronúncia: who, which, that, where fracos e ligados ao nome
Contexto: quarta-feira, 17 de Novembro, 16:00; a sala de controlo da central; o Nuno, o Paulo e a Hannah
  conferem o equipamento do satélite antes do teste
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 5C; conversa guiada "How is it done?"; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Sometimes "the cable" isn't enough — there are twenty cables! Today: how to say exactly which person, which thing, or which place.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: quarta-feira, 17 de Novembro, 16:00; a sala de controlo; uma mesa com cabos e caixas; no ecrã, "Satellite link: TEST"]

**HANNAH:** OK, before the test — everything's connected, isn't it?
**PAULO:** Almost. Nuno, can you pass me the cable that goes to the router?
**NUNO:** This one?
**PAULO:** No, the one with the blue label. The one you had this morning.
**NUNO:** Ah, here. Sorry.
**HANNAH:** And the box which controls the dish — is it on?
**NUNO:** Yes, it's on. Hannah, who's the person who checks the system at night?
**HANNAH:** That's Lourenço. He's the technician who works the night shift. I'll introduce you.
**PAULO:** And where's the room where the old radio equipment is?
**HANNAH:** Next door. Why?
**PAULO:** Because the satellite router has to be next to it. That's the place where the two systems meet.
**NUNO:** Right. And Hannah, did you get the report I sent you yesterday?
**HANNAH:** The one with the test plan? Yes, I read it last night. It's very clear.
**PAULO:** OK — everything's connected. Ready for the test?
**HANNAH:** Ready. Let's turn off the radio link… and… We're still receiving data!
**NUNO:** The satellite works!
**HANNAH:** Fantastic. That's the moment I've been waiting for since August!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Who, which / that, where***

[ECRÃ:
the technician **who** works at night (pessoa)
the box **which** controls the dish / the box **that** controls the dish (coisa)
the room **where** the old equipment is (lugar)]

**PROF:** To say exactly which one: "who" for people, "which" or "that" for things, "where" for places. "That" is very common in conversation — you can also use it for people.
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Sem pronome**

[ECRÃ: the report **(that) I sent you** · the cable **(that) you had** this morning · the moment **(that) I've been waiting for**]

**PROF:** When there's another subject after the pronoun — "I", "you", "Paulo" — you can leave out "that", "which" or "who". "The report I sent you." This is very common.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: the cable **that goes** to the router → "that" é o sujeito: não sai · ✘ Can you pass me the cable goes to the router?]

**PROF:** But when "that" is the subject of the verb, you need it: "the cable that goes to the router".
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Não repetir**

[ECRÃ: "O relatório que te enviei" → ✘ the report that I sent **it** to you · ✔ the report (that) I sent you
"O técnico que trabalha de noite" → ✘ the technician who **he** works at night · ✔ the technician who works at night]

**PROF:** Careful: the pronoun replaces the word. Don't add "it" or "he" again.
[PAUSA 5 s]

***The one…***

[ECRÃ: the one with the blue label · the one you had this morning · the one with the test plan]

**PROF:** "The one" is very useful when you point or choose: "Not this cable — the one with the blue label."
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "Who", "that" and "where" are short, and joined to the noun. Listen: the technician who works at night.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: the cable that goes to the router.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** the report I sent you.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Join the two sentences.
[ECRÃ: Lourenço is a technician. He works at night.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Lourenço is the technician who works at night.]
[ECRÃ: This is the room. The old equipment is in this room.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: This is the room where the old equipment is.]
[ECRÃ: Did you read the report? I sent it yesterday.] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Did you read the report I sent yesterday?]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Describe your workplace — or your school — to a new colleague: two people, one thing and one place. Use "who", "that" or "which", and "where". About forty seconds.
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Ana is the person who answers the phone, and João is the manager who signs the contracts. The printer that works best is on the second floor. And the kitchen is the place where everyone meets at eleven for coffee.]

**PROF (opinião):** What's the most important thing a new colleague needs to know on the first day? Why?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — 5A):** How is the data sent to the control room now? Use the passive.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: It's sent by the radio link — and by the satellite when the link goes down.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! "Who", "which" or "that", "where" — and no pronoun when there's another subject. Now talk to the tutor: "How is it done?". Next lesson: the words of energy and safety.

## 5D — Energy and safety

```
Curso: B1.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5D — Energy and safety        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar palavras de energia e de segurança (renewable, fuel,
  power plant, hazard, protective equipment), dizer regras com must / have to / mustn't / don't have to,
  acentuar bem palavras técnicas (engineer, renewable, electrical) e evitar os falsos amigos petrol e
  fabric.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: must / have to (obrigação); mustn't (proibição); don't have to (não é preciso) — revistos
  do A2 5
Palavras-alvo: energy, renewable, solar, wind power, fuel, oil, gas, pipeline, power plant, generator,
  grid; safety, hazard, protective equipment, helmet, safety boots, gloves, high-visibility vest,
  emergency exit, assembly point, first aid, fire extinguisher
Reciclagem: passiva (5A e 5B); relativas (5C)
Pronúncia: acento de palavra em termos técnicos (engineer, renewable, electrical, electricity,
  equipment, emergency, inverter)
Falsos amigos: petrol (≠ petróleo → oil, crude oil; petrol = gasolina); fabric (≠ fábrica → factory,
  plant; fabric = tecido)
Contexto: ecrãs com expressões e exemplos da história
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 5D; perguntas 7 e 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: the words of energy and safety, the rules of a work site, and how to stress long technical words.

### 2. Energia e segurança (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ:]

| Energia | Segurança |
|---|---|
| renewable energy (solar, wind power) | a hazard (um perigo) |
| fuel: oil, gas, diesel | protective equipment: a helmet, safety boots, gloves, a high-visibility vest |
| a pipeline | the emergency exit |
| a power plant | the assembly point (ponto de encontro) |
| a generator | first aid · a first-aid kit |
| the grid (a rede eléctrica) | a fire extinguisher |

**PROF:** Repeat: renewable. Fuel. Pipeline. Power plant. Hazard. Helmet. Assembly point.
[PAUSA 8 s]

[ECRÃ: Solar energy is renewable: the sun doesn't run out. · Oil and gas aren't renewable. · A lot of Angola's electricity is produced by dams on the Kwanza river. (dam = barragem)]
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 3. Regras (2 min)

[ECRÃ:
You **must** wear a helmet. / You **have to** wear a helmet. (obrigatório)
You **mustn't** touch the inverters. (proibido)
You **don't have to** wear gloves in the office. (não é preciso)]

**PROF:** You know these from A2. "Must" and "have to": it's necessary. "Mustn't": it's prohibited. "Don't have to": it isn't necessary — you can, but you don't need to.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ You don't have to touch the inverters. (= não é preciso) · ✔ You **mustn't** touch the inverters. (= é proibido)]

**PROF:** Careful: "mustn't" and "don't have to" are very different. For danger, always "mustn't".
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia: acento de palavra (2 min)

[ECRÃ: engi**NEER** · re**NEW**able · e**LEC**trical · elec**TRI**city · e**QUIP**ment · e**MER**gency · in**VER**ter]

**PROF:** Long technical words have one strong part. In Portuguese, "engenheiro" has the stress on "nhei"; in English, "engineer" has it at the end. Listen: engineer.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: engineer. Renewable. Electrical. Electricity.
[PAUSA 8 s]
**PROF:** Equipment. Emergency. Inverter.
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** Now a sentence: The electrical engineer checks the equipment.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: **petrol** ≠ petróleo]

**PROF:** "Petrol" is the fuel for cars: "I need petrol for the car." In American English, it's "gas".
(PT: "Petróleo" diz-se "oil" ou "crude oil": "Angola produz petróleo" → "Angola produces oil". Uma petrolífera é "an oil company".)
[PAUSA 4 s]

[ECRÃ: **fabric** ≠ fábrica]

**PROF:** "Fabric" is cloth — the material of a shirt.
(PT: "Fábrica" diz-se "factory": "a fábrica de cimento" → "the cement factory". Para energia, diz-se "plant": "a central solar" → "the solar plant".)
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 6. Prática e fecho (1 min 30 s)

**PROF:** Must, mustn't, or don't have to?
[ECRÃ: In the office, you … wear a helmet.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: don't have to]
[ECRÃ: You … smoke near the fuel.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: mustn't]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. What are the safety rules at your work, or in a place you know — a factory, a hospital, a school? Say two things people must do, one thing they mustn't do, and one thing they don't have to do. About thirty-five seconds.
[PAUSA 35 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — In the hospital where I work, you must wash your hands before you see a patient, and you have to wear your ID card all the time. You mustn't use your phone in the operating rooms. But visitors don't have to wear a uniform.]

**PROF (opinião):** In your opinion, which energy is best for Angola in the future: oil, gas, or renewable energy? Why?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF:** Well done! Next lesson: a guided tour of the plant, with two visitors from Ghana and Kenya.

## 5E — Real English: A tour of the plant

```
Curso: B1.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5E — A tour of the plant        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue seguir uma visita guiada e identificar os passos de um
  processo e as regras de segurança.
```

## 5F — Work & Life: Safety briefing

```
Curso: B1.1        Unidade: 5        Aula: 5F — Safety briefing        Tipo: Work & Life
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue explicar a visitantes estrangeiros, por passos, o procedimento
  de segurança de um local de trabalho, responder a perguntas e reformular quando não percebem; e explicar
  em inglês um aviso de segurança escrito em português.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3, 4 (e 1, 2)
Gramática-alvo: toda a da unidade: passiva; relativas; sequenciadores (First… Then… After that…
  Once… Finally…); must / have to / mustn't / don't have to
Palavras-alvo: briefing, visitor, sign in, badge, crane, zone, lift (verbo), alarm, assembly point,
  supervisor, in other words, what I mean is
Reciclagem: verificar a compreensão (B1, 3C): Is that clear? / So, …?; 1.º condicional (A2, 8)
Pronúncia: dar instruções com pausas curtas e a voz a descer no fim de cada passo
Mediação: o Nuno explica em inglês o aviso de segurança, em português, que está na vedação da zona
  da grua
Contexto: sexta-feira, 19 de Novembro, 7:30; o portão da central; o Nuno com dois visitantes: o Femi
  (Morro Azul, de Luanda; primeira visita à central) e o Sipho (Mopane Systems; veio para ajudar com as
  antenas)
Duração: 12 min

```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today the crane starts work, and Nuno has two visitors. Hannah is busy, so Nuno gives the safety briefing. Watch how he explains the steps, answers questions, and says things again in a different way.

### 2. Situação (4 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: sexta-feira, 19 de Novembro, 7:30; o portão da central; atrás, a grua; um aviso em português na vedação; o Nuno com uma prancheta; o Femi e o Sipho com capacete na mão]

**NUNO:** Good morning, Femi. Good morning, Sipho. Welcome to the plant. Hannah's with the crane team, so I'm going to do the safety briefing. It takes five minutes.
**FEMI:** Thank you, Nuno. It's my first time here.
**NUNO:** OK. First, you sign in at the gate, and you get a visitor badge. You have to wear it all the time. Then, put on your helmet, your vest and your safety boots. The boots are in the box by the door.
**SIPHO:** Do we have to wear gloves?
**NUNO:** No, you don't have to wear gloves. You mustn't touch the equipment — only the engineers who work on it can do that.
**FEMI:** Fine by me!
**NUNO:** After that, we walk on the yellow path. Today there's one more rule. You see that sign on the fence? It's in Portuguese, so let me explain.

[ECRÃ: o aviso, em português: "ATENÇÃO — Zona de elevação de cargas. Proibida a entrada a pessoas não autorizadas. Durante a operação da grua, é obrigatório manter uma distância mínima de 20 metros. Em caso de alarme, dirija-se ao ponto de encontro (portão principal). Responsável: Sr. Domingos, chefe de manutenção."]

**NUNO:** It says this is the zone where the crane lifts heavy things. Only people with permission can go in. When the crane is working, you have to stay twenty metres away — at least. If you hear the alarm, you go to the assembly point, at the main gate. And the person who's responsible for the zone is Mr Domingos, the maintenance manager.
**FEMI:** Sorry — what's an "assembly point"?
**NUNO:** It's the place where everyone meets in an emergency. In other words, if there's a problem, we all go there, and the supervisor counts the people. It's the green sign at the main gate.
**FEMI:** Ah, I see. Like a fire drill in an office.
**NUNO:** Exactly. And once the crane stops, you can come closer — but only with me or Hannah.
**SIPHO:** How will we know the crane has stopped?
**NUNO:** Good question. The red light on the crane goes off, and Mr Domingos blows a whistle twice.
**SIPHO:** Got it.
**NUNO:** Finally, the emergency phone number is on the back of your badge. So, is everything clear? Any questions?
**FEMI:** Just one. Twenty metres — that's about the length of… that building?
**NUNO:** Yes, more or less — the length of the control room. What I mean is: always stay behind the red and white tape.
**FEMI:** Clear. Thank you, Nuno. That was very professional.
**NUNO:** Thank you. OK — let's go and watch the first antenna go up!

### 3. Como se faz um briefing de segurança (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o que o Nuno fez]

| Momento | Língua | Exemplo do Nuno |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Dizer o que vai acontecer | I'm going to… It takes… | I'm going to do the safety briefing. It takes five minutes. |
| 2. Os passos, por ordem | First… Then… After that… Once… Finally… | First, you sign in at the gate. Then, put on your helmet. |
| 3. As regras | have to / don't have to / mustn't | You don't have to wear gloves… You mustn't touch the equipment. |
| 4. Explicar um aviso | It says… · the zone where… · the person who… | It says this is the zone where the crane lifts heavy things. |
| 5. Reformular | In other words… · What I mean is… | It's the place where everyone meets. In other words… |
| 6. Verificar | Is everything clear? Any questions? | So, is everything clear? Any questions? |

**PROF:** Nuno gives one step at a time, with a sequence word. When Femi doesn't understand "assembly point", Nuno doesn't just repeat it louder: he explains it with simpler words — "the place where everyone meets" — and then with an example.
[PAUSA 5 s]

**A mediação**

**PROF:** The sign is in Portuguese, and it's very formal: "proibida a entrada a pessoas não autorizadas". Nuno says it in simple English: "only people with permission can go in". He keeps all the facts: the zone, the distance, the alarm, the person.
(PT: Num aviso de segurança, não deixe nenhuma informação de fora: números, lugares e nomes contam. Mas pode simplificar a forma: "é obrigatório manter uma distância mínima de 20 metros" → "you have to stay twenty metres away — at least".)
