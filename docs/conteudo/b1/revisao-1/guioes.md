# B1.1 · Revisão 1 (unidades 1 a 5) · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções: as dos [guiões da unidade 1 do B1](../unidade-01/guioes.md).

**Língua que a revisão pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2 e das unidades 1 a 5 do B1, e nada de novo, com duas excepções, que são estratégias de exame: organizar um turno longo (*There are two reasons. Firstly… Secondly… So, in short…*) e reformular (*What I mean is… In other words…*, vistos na 5F). As estratégias do A2 mantêm-se (*Can you repeat that, please? Let me think. Do you mean…? Sorry, I mean…*).

**Não usar:** o *past perfect*; o 3.º condicional e *should have*; *wish*; o discurso indirecto no passado; o *future continuous*; *must / can't* de dedução; a passiva com modais ou noutros tempos; orações relativas explicativas, entre vírgulas.

**Personagens:** **Nuno** e **Chanda Mwale** (zambiano, de Lusaca, engenheiro de manutenção numa empresa de logística do Lobito; personagem nova, fictícia; a empresa não tem nome) (R1A); **Lurdes Cassoma**, a aluna-modelo dos exames (contabilista num banco, de Benguela), agora no exame do B1.1 (R1B).

**Quando:** sábado, 27 de Novembro de 2027, no voo do Namibe para Luanda, no dia seguinte ao fim da instalação (B1 5F).

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

### 2. Situação: no avião (3 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: sábado, 27 de Novembro, 10:00; um avião pequeno, a descolar do Namibe; o Nuno à janela; ao lado, o Chanda, com um capacete de obra no colo]

**CHANDA:** Sorry — is this your bag? Can I move it a little?
**NUNO:** Of course, sorry. You work on a site too, don't you? I saw the helmet.
**CHANDA:** Ha, yes. I'm Chanda, from Lusaka. I'm a maintenance engineer for a logistics company in Lobito. We move goods between the port and Zambia, by train and by lorry.
**NUNO:** Nice to meet you. I'm Nuno. I work in IT support. We've just finished a project at the solar plant near Namibe.
**CHANDA:** The one with the sandstorms? I've heard about it. Do you know if the new system works?
**NUNO:** It does! We've been working on it since August, and yesterday the last antenna went up.
**CHANDA:** Congratulations. Have you always worked in energy?
**NUNO:** No. I used to fix printers and laptops in an office. Now I talk to clients in three countries. And you? Did you use to work in Angola?
**CHANDA:** No, I used to work in the mines in the Copperbelt, in Zambia. I've been living in Lobito for two years now. At first, the language was frustrating, but my Portuguese has improved a lot.
**NUNO:** So, what do you think of Lobito? Is your work busy?
**CHANDA:** Very busy. In my view, the port is really important for the whole region. But we don't have enough engineers. If we had ten more, we'd work much faster.
**NUNO:** I see your point. It's the same in our team — two engineers for fourteen antennas! If I were your boss, I'd train local technicians.
**CHANDA:** We do — a group of twenty was hired last year. They're trained in Lobito by a team from Lusaka.
**NUNO:** That's a good idea. Do you think the company will hire more?
**CHANDA:** Probably. I doubt it'll happen this year, but next year, maybe.
**NUNO:** Could you tell me who I should talk to? Our company might be interested in a project there.
**CHANDA:** Sure. Here's my card. Call me next week, and I'll introduce you to the person who manages the IT.
**NUNO:** Thank you! Let's keep in touch.

### 3. O B1.1 numa conversa (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o que o Nuno e o Chanda usaram, unidade a unidade]

| Unidade | Língua | Frase da conversa |

## R1B — Getting ready for the B1.1 speaking exam

```
Curso: B1.1        Unidade: Revisão 1        Aula: R1B — Getting ready for the B1.1 speaking exam        Tipo: Revisão
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue descrever as quatro partes do exame oral do B1.1, sabe o que é
  avaliado e usa duas estratégias: organizar um turno longo (There are two reasons. Firstly… Secondly…
  So, in short…) e reformular (What I mean is… In other words…).
Objectivos de unidade que revê: U1–U5, todos
Gramática revista: toda a do B1.1
Língua nova (bloco fixo): There are two reasons. · Firstly… Secondly… · So, in short…
Situação: a Lurdes faz o exame de demonstração do B1.1
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: conversa de ensaio; exame oral do B1.1
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Soon you take your B1.1 speaking exam. Today you see how it works, you learn two strategies, and you watch a practice exam.

### 2. Como é o exame (2 min)

[ECRÃ: quatro blocos]

| Parte | O quê | Tempo |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Interview | Como mudou o seu trabalho, o que tem andado a fazer, o que costumava fazer (*How has your job changed? How long have you been…? What did you use to do?*) | cerca de 2 minutos |
| 2. Long turn | Explicar, sozinho, um processo ou um procedimento do seu trabalho ou dos seus estudos; depois, duas perguntas do examinador | cerca de 2 minutos |
| 3. Situation | Uma situação sorteada de quatro: conhecer alguém num evento; uma reunião com desacordo e uma decisão; um problema com soluções; explicar a segurança a um visitante | cerca de 3 minutos |
| 4. Discussion | Uma pergunta de opinião ligada à situação; o examinador dá outra opinião, e o aluno responde | cerca de 1 minuto |

**PROF:** The exam is a conversation with the examiner, about eight or nine minutes. In part two, you talk alone for about one minute. In part four, the examiner disagrees with you — on purpose. Stay calm, and answer the argument.
(PT: É uma conversa de oito ou nove minutos. Na parte 2, fala sozinho cerca de um minuto. Na parte 4, o examinador discorda de propósito: responda ao argumento com calma.)

**PROF:** The rules are the same as in A2: the recording is your choice, the exam opens when you finish most of the B1.1 lessons, and when you don't pass, you can try again a week later.
(PT: A gravação é opcional (com ela, a pronúncia conta), o exame abre com 80% das aulas do B1.1 feitas, e pode repetir uma semana depois.)

### 3. O que é avaliado (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: cinco ícones]

| Competência | No B1.1, quer dizer… |
|---|---|
| Task — a tarefa | Responde com detalhe e exemplos, explica o processo por ordem, resolve a situação e responde ao argumento do examinador. |
| Grammar — a gramática | Escolhe entre vários tempos (*used to*, *present perfect continuous*), usa o 2.º condicional e a passiva. |
| Vocabulary — o vocabulário | As palavras das unidades 1 a 5, colocações (*make a decision, solve a problem*) e explica por outras palavras o que não sabe. |
| Fluency — a fluência | Turnos longos e ligados; gere a conversa: pergunta, verifica, retoma. |
| Pronunciation — a pronúncia | Percebe-se sempre; o acento e a entoação ajudam o sentido. (Só com a gravação.) |

**PROF:** In A2, you explained and solved problems. In B1, you also give opinions, disagree, and talk alone for longer.
(PT: No A2, explicava e resolvia problemas. No B1, também dá opiniões, discorda e fala sozinho durante mais tempo.)

### 4. Duas estratégias (2 min)

**Organizar um turno longo**

[ECRÃ: **There are two reasons.** **Firstly,** … **Secondly,** … **So, in short,** …]

**PROF:** In a long turn, or when you give an opinion, say how many points you have — then give them in order. The listener can follow you easily.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: "I think meetings should be shorter. There are two reasons. Firstly, people stop listening after thirty minutes. Secondly, we have too many meetings, so we don't have enough time for our real work. So, in short, shorter meetings mean better work."]
**PROF:** I think meetings should be shorter. There are two reasons. Firstly, people stop listening after thirty minutes. Secondly, we have too many meetings. So, in short, shorter meetings mean better work.
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Reformular**

[ECRÃ: já conhece — **Can you repeat that, please?** · **Let me think.** · **Do you mean…?** · **Sorry, I mean…** · **What I mean is…** · **In other words…**]

**PROF:** You know "What I mean is" and "In other words" from unit five. In the exam, use them when you can't find a word, or when the examiner doesn't understand you. It doesn't lower your score — it shows control.
[PAUSA 3 s]
[ECRÃ: "We need a… I don't know the word. What I mean is, a machine that cleans the panels without water."]
(PT: Reformular não baixa a nota. Quando lhe falta uma palavra, explique-a com outras.)
[PAUSA 3 s]

### 5. Um exame de demonstração (4 min)

**PROF:** Now watch Lurdes — the accountant from Benguela. Now she's at the end of B1.1.

[ECRÃ: "Practice exam B1.1 — Lurdes"; ícone do examinador]

**EXAMINADOR:** Hello, Lurdes. Welcome to your B1.1 speaking exam. How has your work changed in the last few years?
**LURDES:** A lot. I used to work at the front desk of the bank, with clients all day. Now I'm in the accounts department, so I work mostly with numbers. I prefer it, but I miss the clients sometimes.
**EXAMINADOR:** What have you been doing recently?
**LURDES:** I've been preparing the bank's budget for next year. I've been working on it since October, and I've finished about half.
**EXAMINADOR:** Thank you. Now please explain a process or a procedure at your work, step by step.
**LURDES:** OK. I'll explain how a small business loan is approved. First, the client's documents are checked at the branch. Then the application is sent to our department, and we look at the numbers — the client's income and their other loans. After that, the application is discussed in a meeting every Thursday. Once it's approved, the client is called, and the money is usually in the account in a week. So, in short, it takes about three weeks.
**EXAMINADOR:** Which part is the most difficult?
**LURDES:** The numbers. Some clients don't have good records, so we can't see their income clearly.
**EXAMINADOR:** If you could change one thing in that process, what would you change?
**LURDES:** I'd put the documents online. If clients sent them by app, we'd save a week.
**EXAMINADOR:** Now a situation. I'm your manager. Our team's printer broke this morning, and we have forty contracts to print for tomorrow. What should we do?
**LURDES:** Oh no. Can I ask a few questions first? Is there another printer in the building?
**EXAMINADOR:** Yes, on the third floor, but it's very slow.
**LURDES:** Then why don't we print half there, and send the other half to the copy shop near the bank? It'll probably cost about twenty thousand kwanzas, but we'll finish today.
**EXAMINADOR:** Hmm. The copy shop? Those contracts are confidential.
**LURDES:** That's a good point. In that case, I'd use the printer on the third floor and stay one hour later. It isn't as fast, but it's safe. And I'll call the technician for our printer now.
**EXAMINADOR:** OK. Now a question for discussion. Should companies pay for their staff's English lessons?
**LURDES:** In my view, yes. There are two reasons. Firstly, staff who speak English can work with foreign clients, so the company earns more. Secondly, people feel valued, and they work better.
**EXAMINADOR:** But some people say that staff leave the company after it pays for their lessons.
**LURDES:** I see your point. Some people do leave. But what I mean is: if the company is a good place to work, most people stay. And the company can ask them to stay for a year after the course. So, in short, it's a good investment.
**EXAMINADOR:** Thank you, Lurdes. The exam is finished. You can press the finish button now.

**PROF:** Look at what Lurdes does well.
[ECRÃ: lista com vistos]
**PROF:** In part one, she compares before and now with "used to", and uses "I've been preparing… since October". In the long turn, she uses the passive and sequence words, and finishes with "So, in short". In the situation, she asks questions first, then proposes a solution — and when the examiner says "confidential", she changes her solution. In the discussion, she answers the counter-argument with "I see your point… but what I mean is…".
(PT: Na entrevista, compara o antes e o agora; no turno longo, usa a passiva e sequenciadores; na situação, pergunta primeiro e muda de solução perante a dificuldade; na discussão, responde ao argumento contrário.)
[PAUSA 4 s]
