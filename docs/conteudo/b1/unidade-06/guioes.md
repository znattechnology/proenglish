# B1.2 · Unidade 6 — What happened? · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções: as dos [guiões da unidade 1 do B1](../unidade-01/guioes.md): tudo em inglês, e o português só nos falsos amigos e na mediação.

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2 e do B1.1 (unidades 1 a 5). Novo: o *past perfect* (*had* + particípio) com *already, just, before, by the time, when*; os três tempos narrativos juntos (*past simple, past continuous, past perfect*); causa e consequência (*because, because of, due to, so, as a result, that's why*); sequenciadores de narração (*at first, after that, eventually, in the end*); reagir a uma história (*No way! Really? What did you do then?*).

**Não usar nesta unidade:** o discurso indirecto (nem "he told me that…", "she said it had…": diga o que aconteceu, ou cite em estilo directo: *He says…* no presente); o *past perfect* contínuo e a passiva no *past perfect* ("had been damaged": diga *the water had damaged…*); o 3.º condicional, *should have* e *could have*; *must / might / can't have* de dedução; o *future continuous*; *would* para hábitos do passado; *wish*.

**Nomes de empresas e lugares:** a Mopane Systems, a Morro Azul Energia e o seu terminal de combustíveis no Lobito são fictícios. O empreiteiro de electricidade não tem nome.

**Personagens:** **Nuno** (lidera o seu primeiro projecto, como queria na avaliação anual, B1 2F); **Paulo**; **Thandi** (por videochamada, de Joanesburgo); **Lerato** (só mencionada). Novos e fictícios: **Pieter van Wyk** (sul-africano, director do terminal da Morro Azul no Lobito); **Sr. Teodoro** (vigilante do terminal; só fala português; o seu relato escrito é a mediação da 6G); **Ingrid Solberg** (norueguesa, directora de operações da Morro Azul, em Luanda). Na 6F voltam **Carlos** (Maputo), **Neo** (Gaborone) e **Mei** (Lusaca), da formação em Joanesburgo (B1 1E).

**A história:** Janeiro de 2028. Desde segunda, 10, o Nuno lidera a instalação da rede e das câmaras do terminal de combustíveis da Morro Azul no Lobito, com o Paulo e um empreiteiro de electricidade local. Na sexta, 14, o equipamento fica na nova sala dos servidores. Na noite de segunda, 17, para terça, 18, chove muito: a água entra pelo telhado, porque um ralo estava entupido, e desce por uma conduta de cabos que o empreiteiro ainda não tinha vedado. Às 4:30 há um curto-circuito; às 4:35 o detector de fumo dispara; o Sr. Teodoro dá o alarme, desliga a corrente e apaga um pequeno fogo com o extintor. Ninguém fica ferido; dois *switches* e uma UPS ficam estragados; as câmaras ficam sem imagem durante nove horas. A distribuição de combustível não pára (tem um sistema separado). Na terça, 18, o Nuno chega e sabe o que aconteceu (6A); à tarde, reconstrói a noite com o Paulo e o Pieter (6B); na quarta, 19, encontram a causa (6C) e o Nuno conta tudo à Thandi (6D); na quinta, 20, os colegas da formação respondem com as suas histórias (6F); na sexta, 21, o Nuno faz o relato à directora de operações (6G).

## 6A — It had already started

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 6        Aula: 6A — It had already started        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar o past perfect para dizer o que tinha acontecido antes de
  outro momento do passado (When I arrived, the fire team had already left.), com already, just, before,
  by the time e when.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: had ('d) + particípio passado; negativas (hadn't) e perguntas (Had you…?); already / just /
  before / by the time / when; past simple para a ordem normal, past perfect só para "antes"
Palavras-alvo: terminal, server room, fire team, alarm, go off, smoke, power, turn off, put out, guard,
  shift, by the time
Reciclagem: passiva no passado (B1, 5B): the fire was put out
Pronúncia: 'd do past perfect (he'd already…, they'd left) e hadn't
Contexto: terça-feira, 18 de Janeiro de 2028, 7:45; o portão do terminal da Morro Azul no Lobito; o Pieter
  espera o Nuno, que chega do hotel
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 6A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 6
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Welcome to B1.2. This unit is about telling stories — what happened, in what order, and why. Today: how to talk about something that happened before another moment in the past.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: terça-feira, 18 de Janeiro, 7:45; o portão do terminal de combustíveis no Lobito; chão molhado; o Pieter à espera, com um café; o Nuno sai de um táxi]

**PIETER:** Morning, Nuno. I tried to call you at five, but your phone was off.
**NUNO:** Sorry, Pieter. I'd turned it off for the night. What's wrong?
**PIETER:** There was a short circuit in the new server room, at about half past four. The smoke alarm went off at four thirty-five.
**NUNO:** What? Is everyone OK?
**PIETER:** Everyone's fine. Teodoro, the night guard, was very quick. By the time I arrived, he'd already turned off the power and put out a small fire with the extinguisher.
**NUNO:** Thank goodness. And the fire team?
**PIETER:** They'd just left when I got here. They checked everything — it's safe now.
**NUNO:** But how did it happen? Everything was working on Friday when we left.
**PIETER:** I know. It rained all night — really heavily. When I opened the server room, there was water on the floor. It had come through the roof.
**NUNO:** Through the roof? Nobody had told us about a problem with the roof.
**PIETER:** Nobody knew. It had never rained like that before. And the cameras have been off since four thirty — so we've had no images for three hours.
**NUNO:** OK. I'll call Paulo now. Can I see the room?
**PIETER:** Of course. Put on your boots — it's still wet.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**O *past perfect***

[ECRÃ: linha do tempo: 4:40 Teodoro turns off the power · 5:15 the fire team leaves · 5:20 Pieter arrives
By the time I arrived, he**'d already turned off** the power. (primeiro ele desligou; depois eu cheguei)]

**PROF:** When we tell a story in the past, sometimes we need to go back further — to something that happened before. For that, use "had" plus the past participle: the past perfect.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: They**'d just left** when I got here. · It **had come** through the roof. · **Nobody had told** us. · **Had** you **turned** your phone off? — Yes, I **had**.]

**PROF:** "Had" is often short: I'd, he'd, they'd. The negative is "hadn't". The question starts with "had".
[PAUSA 4 s]

***Already, just, before, by the time, when***

[ECRÃ: **already** — he'd already turned off the power · **just** — they'd just left · **never… before** — it had never rained like that before · **by the time** — by the time I arrived, … · **when** — when I opened the door, the water had come in]

**PROF:** These words often go with the past perfect. "By the time" means "when, or before".
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Só quando é preciso**

[ECRÃ: ✔ The alarm went off, Teodoro turned off the power, and he called the fire team. (por ordem: past simple)
✔ When I arrived, he'd already called the fire team. (para trás: past perfect)]

**PROF:** If you tell the story in order, the past simple is enough. Use the past perfect only when you go back in time.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: "Quando cheguei, ele já tinha saído." → ✔ When I arrived, he **had** already **left**. · ⚠ When I arrived, he already left. (no inglês americano informal ouve-se, mas a ordem fica confusa) · ✘ When I arrived, he has already left.]

**PROF:** The Portuguese "tinha saído" is very close to the English: "had left".
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** The "'d" is very short, but you need it. Listen: he turned off the power — he'd turned off the power.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: He'd already turned off the power.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** They'd just left when I got here.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Nobody had told us. We hadn't heard about it.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Join the two actions with the past perfect.
[ECRÃ: 1. The meeting started. 2. I arrived. → When I arrived, …] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: When I arrived, the meeting had already started.]
[ECRÃ: 1. I sent the report. 2. My boss called. → By the time my boss called, …] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: By the time my boss called, I'd already sent the report.]
[ECRÃ: 1. I never saw snow. 2. I went to Lesotho last winter. → I'd never …] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: I'd never seen snow before I went to Lesotho last winter.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Think of a day when you arrived late — at work, at school, at an airport. What had already happened when you arrived? About forty seconds.
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last year I was late for a job interview because of the traffic. When I arrived, the other candidates had already finished their tests, and the manager had gone to lunch. I'd never felt so embarrassed! In the end, they gave me the test in the afternoon.]

**PROF (opinião):** In your opinion, what's the best thing to do when you arrive late for something important?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 5B):** What happened to the fire? Use the passive.
[PAUSA 5 s]
[ECRÃ: It was put out by Teodoro, with the extinguisher.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! "Had" plus the participle, to go back in time. Next lesson: all three past tenses together, to tell a full story.

## 6B — While I was checking…

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 6        Aula: 6B — While I was checking…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue contar uma história com os três tempos narrativos: o past
  simple para as acções principais, o past continuous para o fundo e as acções interrompidas, e o past
  perfect para o que tinha acontecido antes.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: past simple / past continuous / past perfect juntos; while / when / as soon as
Palavras-alvo: round (a ronda do vigilante), log, recording, timeline, flash, smell, drip, run, stop
  working, go dark
Reciclagem: past continuous (A2, 2); 6A
Pronúncia: was / were fracos no past continuous (he was doing, they were working)
Contexto: terça-feira, 18 de Janeiro, 14:00; a sala de controlo do terminal; o Nuno, o Paulo e o Pieter
  vêem as gravações das câmaras e os registos do sistema
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 6B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! A good story has three kinds of past: the main events, the background, and what had happened before. Today: all three together.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: terça-feira, 18 de Janeiro, 14:00; a sala de controlo; no ecrã, a gravação de uma câmara com a hora: 04:21; o Paulo toma notas]

**PAULO:** OK, the cameras are back. Let's make a timeline. Pieter, what was happening at four?
**PIETER:** It was raining very hard, and Teodoro was doing his round of the tanks.
**NUNO:** Look at this recording — four twenty-one. The water was already dripping from the ceiling, next to the cable duct.
**PAULO:** And the system was still working. The log shows no errors until four twenty-nine.
**NUNO:** Then, at four thirty, while the water was running down the cables, the main switch stopped working. That was the short circuit.
**PIETER:** And at four thirty-five, while Teodoro was walking back to the gate, the smoke alarm went off.
**PAULO:** What did he do?
**PIETER:** He ran to the control building. As soon as he opened the door, he saw a small fire behind the switch. He'd done the fire training in December, so he knew what to do.
**NUNO:** He turned off the main power first, didn't he?
**PIETER:** Yes, and then he used the extinguisher. By four forty, the fire was out.
**PAULO:** Great work. And the cameras went dark at four thirty — when the switch stopped working.
**NUNO:** OK. So now we know what happened. Tomorrow we need to find out why the water came in.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Os três passados**

[ECRÃ:
**past continuous** — o fundo, o que estava a acontecer: It **was raining**. Teodoro **was doing** his round.
**past simple** — as acções principais, por ordem: The alarm **went off**. He **ran** to the building. He **turned off** the power.
**past perfect** — o que tinha acontecido antes: He**'d done** the fire training in December.]

**PROF:** Think of a film. The past continuous is the background — the rain, what people were doing. The past simple moves the story forward. The past perfect goes back in time.
[PAUSA 6 s]

**Uma acção interrompe outra**

[ECRÃ: **While** Teodoro **was walking** back to the gate, the smoke alarm **went off**.
Teodoro **was walking** back **when** the alarm **went off**.]

**PROF:** A long action in the past continuous, and a short action in the past simple that interrupts it. "While" goes with the long action; "when" usually goes with the short one.
[PAUSA 5 s]

***As soon as***

[ECRÃ: **As soon as** he opened the door, he saw a small fire. (logo que)]

**PROF:** "As soon as" means "immediately after".
[PAUSA 3 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ While the alarm went off, he was walking. · ✔ While he was walking, the alarm went off.]
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In the past continuous, "was" and "were" are weak. Listen: It was raining very hard.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: Teodoro was doing his round.
[PAUSA 4 s]
**PROF:** While he was walking back, the alarm went off.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Past simple, past continuous, or past perfect?
[ECRÃ: I … (drive) to work when my phone … (ring).] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: I was driving to work when my phone rang.]
[ECRÃ: When I … (get) to the station, the train … (already / leave).] [PAUSA 6 s] [ECRÃ: When I got to the station, the train had already left.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Tell me about a surprise or a small problem in your life: what was happening, what happened, and what had happened before. About forty-five seconds.
[PAUSA 45 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last month I was working at home when the power went off. I was writing an important report, and I hadn't saved it for an hour! At first I was really worried. But as soon as the power came back, I opened the file, and the program had saved a copy. What a relief!]

**PROF (opinião):** Do you think every company needs a fire training for all its staff? Why?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — 6A):** When the fire team arrived, what had Teodoro already done?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: He'd already turned off the power and put out the fire.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! The background, the events, and what happened before. Next lesson: why did it happen? Causes and consequences.

## 6C — Because of the rain

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 6        Aula: 6C — Because of the rain        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue explicar causas e consequências com because / because of / due
  to / so / as a result / that's why.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: because + frase; because of / due to + nome; so / as a result / that's why + consequência
Palavras-alvo: cause, reason, result, drain, blocked, gutter, duct, seal, contractor, leaves, check,
  maintenance
Reciclagem: past perfect (6A); 2.º condicional (B1, 4A): If we sealed every duct, …
Pronúncia: due to e because of ligados (du-tu, bi-cos-ov), sem pausa antes do nome
Contexto: quarta-feira, 19 de Janeiro, 10:00; o telhado do edifício de controlo; o Nuno e o Pieter
  procuram a causa; depois, a conduta de cabos na sala dos servidores
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 6C; conversa guiada "Tell me what happened"; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! After "what happened", the next question is always "why?". Today: how to explain causes and consequences.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: quarta-feira, 19 de Janeiro, 10:00; o telhado plano do edifício de controlo; folhas e lama à volta de um ralo; o Pieter aponta]

**PIETER:** Look at this. The roof drain is completely blocked — leaves, sand, a plastic bag.
**NUNO:** So the water couldn't go down the drain.
**PIETER:** Exactly. Because of the rain, the water stayed on the roof, and it found another way in.
**NUNO:** Through that crack, next to the wall?
**PIETER:** Yes. As a result, it came into the ceiling, just above the server room.
**NUNO:** But a little water on the ceiling isn't enough for a short circuit. Let's look at the duct.

[ECRÃ: a sala dos servidores; uma conduta de cabos aberta no tecto]

**NUNO:** Here's the real problem. The cable duct is open at the top. The contractor hadn't sealed it.
**PIETER:** Why not?
**NUNO:** He was waiting for a special material, and it only arrived yesterday. That's why the duct was still open on Monday night.
**PIETER:** So the water came down the duct, directly onto the switch.
**NUNO:** Right. The short circuit was due to two things: the blocked drain and the open duct.
**PIETER:** OK. So, two actions: we clean the drains every month, not every year — and your contractor seals the duct today.
**NUNO:** Today, definitely. I'll call him now.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**A causa**

[ECRÃ: **because** + frase: The water stayed on the roof **because** the drain was blocked.
**because of** + nome: **Because of** the rain, the water stayed on the roof.
**due to** + nome: The short circuit was **due to** two things.]

**PROF:** "Because" comes before a full sentence, with a verb. "Because of" and "due to" come before a noun. "Due to" is more formal — good for reports.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ because of the drain was blocked · ✔ because the drain was blocked · ✔ because of the blocked drain]

**PROF:** Careful: "because of" never comes before a verb.
[PAUSA 4 s]

**A consequência**

[ECRÃ: **so** — The drain was blocked, **so** the water stayed on the roof.
**as a result** — The water came in. **As a result**, the switch stopped working.
**that's why** — The material hadn't arrived. **That's why** the duct was open.]

**PROF:** For the result: "so" in the same sentence; "as a result" and "that's why" usually start a new sentence.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: "Devido à chuva" → **due to** the rain / **because of** the rain · "Por isso" → **so** / **that's why**]

**PROF:** "Devido a" is "due to" or "because of". "Por isso" is "so" or "that's why".
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Say "because of" and "due to" quickly, joined to the noun. Listen: because of the rain. Due to two things.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: Because of the rain, the water stayed on the roof.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** The short circuit was due to the open duct.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Because or because of?
[ECRÃ: The flight was late … the weather.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: because of]
[ECRÃ: The flight was late … there was a storm.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: because]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Think of a problem in your city or at your work: a traffic jam, a power cut, a delay. Explain the cause and the consequences. Use "because of" or "due to", and "so" or "as a result". About forty seconds.
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last week there was a big traffic jam on the main road to my office. It was due to an accident between two lorries. As a result, I arrived an hour late, so I missed the morning meeting. That's why I now leave home at six thirty!]

**PROF (opinião):** In your opinion, are most accidents at work due to bad luck or to bad planning? Why?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 4A):** What would happen if every company cleaned its roof drains every month?
[PAUSA 8 s]
[ECRÃ: If every company cleaned its drains every month, there'd be fewer problems in the rainy season.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! "Because" with a sentence, "because of" and "due to" with a noun, and "so", "as a result", "that's why" for the result. Now talk to the tutor: "Tell me what happened". Next lesson: telling a story so people want to listen.

## 6D — Then what happened?

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 6        Aula: 6D — Then what happened?        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue contar uma história com sequenciadores (at first, after that,
  eventually, in the end) e reagir à história de outra pessoa (No way! Really? What did you do then?).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 3
Gramática-alvo: at first / then / after that / eventually / in the end / finally; reacções e perguntas de
  seguimento; adjectivos em -ed / -ing (revistos) para dizer como se sentiu
Palavras-alvo: scared, shocked, relieved, proud, lucky, stressful, embarrassing, What a relief!, No way!,
  You're joking!, Poor you!, Thank goodness
Reciclagem: tempos narrativos (6B); question tags (B1, 1A)
Pronúncia: reacções com entoação viva (No way! Really?); pausas entre as partes de uma história
Contexto: quarta-feira, 19 de Janeiro, 19:00; videochamada; o Nuno no hotel, no Lobito; a Thandi em
  Joanesburgo
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 6D; perguntas 7 e 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! A story is a conversation: one person tells it, the other reacts. Today: how to tell a story in order, and how to be a good listener.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: quarta-feira, 19 de Janeiro, 19:00; videochamada; o Nuno, cansado, num quarto de hotel; a Thandi em casa]

**THANDI:** Nuno! I heard about the problem in Lobito from Lerato. What happened?
**NUNO:** You won't believe it. My first project — and in the second week, there was a fire in the server room.
**THANDI:** No way! Was anyone hurt?
**NUNO:** No, thank goodness. It happened at half past four in the morning. At first, it was just water — it was raining really hard, and the water came through the roof.
**THANDI:** Through the roof? And then?
**NUNO:** Then it ran down a cable duct, onto the main switch, and there was a short circuit. After that, there was a small fire.
**THANDI:** You're joking! What did you do?
**NUNO:** I was asleep! My phone was off. The night guard, Teodoro, did everything. He'd done a fire training in December, so he turned off the power and put out the fire. Eventually, the fire team came and checked everything.
**THANDI:** What a hero! How did you feel when you heard?
**NUNO:** Honestly? I was shocked — and a bit scared. It was my first project. But in the end, nobody was hurt, and we lost only two switches and a UPS.
**THANDI:** That's lucky. And you've found the cause, haven't you?
**NUNO:** Yes — a blocked drain and an open duct. We've fixed both. I'm really relieved.
**THANDI:** Well, you handled it well. Poor you, though — what a stressful week!
**NUNO:** Thanks, Thandi. Now I have to explain it all to Morro Azul's director of operations on Friday.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Contar por ordem**

[ECRÃ: **At first**, it was just water. → **Then** it ran down a duct. → **After that**, there was a small fire. → **Eventually**, the fire team came. → **In the end**, nobody was hurt.]

**PROF:** "At first" is the beginning. "Then" and "after that" move the story forward. "Eventually" means "after a long time, or after many things". "In the end" gives the final result.
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ: **eventually** ≠ eventualmente → "eventualmente" = possibly / maybe · eventually = finalmente, por fim]

**PROF:** Careful: "eventually" doesn't mean "maybe". It means "in the end, after some time".
(PT: "Eventualmente vamos mudar" — no sentido de "talvez" — diz-se "We might move" ou "Maybe we'll move".)
[PAUSA 4 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ At the end, nobody was hurt. · ✔ **In the end**, nobody was hurt. (o resultado) · ✔ **at the end of** the week (o fim de um período)]
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Ouvir e reagir**

[ECRÃ: surpresa — **No way! Really? You're joking!** · alívio — **Thank goodness. What a relief!** · simpatia — **Poor you! That's terrible.** · para continuar — **And then? What did you do? How did you feel?**]

**PROF:** A good listener reacts and asks for more. Thandi says "No way!", "You're joking!", and asks "What did you do?", "How did you feel?".
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Como se sentiu**

[ECRÃ: scared · shocked · relieved · proud · lucky · stressful / stressed · embarrassing / embarrassed]

**PROF:** Say how you felt — it makes the story real. Remember: "-ed" for the person, "-ing" for the situation.
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** Reactions need energy: the voice goes up. Listen: No way! Really?
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: No way! You're joking! What did you do then?
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** Thank goodness. What a relief!
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** React to my story. I'll stop after each part.
**PROF:** Last Friday I lost my wallet in a taxi.
[PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: Oh no! What did you do?]
**PROF:** I called the taxi company, and eventually they found it.

## 6E — Incidents


[ECRÃ: **raise** the alarm · **put out** a fire · **evacuate** a building · **cause** damage · **file** a report / **write** a report · **give** first aid]

[ECRÃ: The water caused a lot of … .] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: damage]
