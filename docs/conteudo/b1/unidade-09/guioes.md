# B1.2 · Unidade 9 — Media and opinions · Guiões das vídeo-aulas

Guiões para o professor de IA. Conteúdo original da ProEnglish. Convenções: as dos [guiões da unidade 1 do B1](../unidade-01/guioes.md): tudo em inglês, e o português só nos falsos amigos e na mediação.

**Língua que esta unidade pode usar:** tudo do A1, do A2, do B1.1 e das unidades 6 a 8. Novo: conectores de contraste (*although, even though, however, despite, in spite of*); de finalidade (*to, in order to, so that*); de consequência (*as a result, therefore*), revendo os de causa da unidade 6; a linguagem de resumir e citar (*According to…, The article says that…, The main point is…, The writer thinks…*); recomendar (*It's worth watching. I'd recommend it.*).

**Não usar nesta unidade:** o 3.º condicional, *should have* e *could have*; *wish*; a dedução sobre o passado (*must have / can't have*); a passiva com modais ou noutros tempos; outros verbos de relato além dos da unidade 7 (*suggest, claim, argue, deny*) — para citar, usa-se *says*, *thinks*, *according to*.

**Textos e nomes:** o artigo da 9C e da 9F, as notícias em português da 9F, o programa de rádio da 9E, a série da 9A, o filme da 9A e o livro da 9D **foram escritos por nós e são fictícios**; a Sumos do Planalto (9F) também é fictícia; nenhum é um texto real de um jornal, de uma revista ou de um sítio da internet. A Morro Azul Energia, a Mopane Systems e a Tsodilo Sensors são fictícias. O 4 de Abril (Dia da Paz) é feriado nacional em Angola.

**Personagens:** **Nuno**; **Paulo**; **Sílvia**; **Lerato**; **Thandi** (por videochamada, na 9F). Na 9E, vozes novas e fictícias: **Bella** (apresentadora, angolana), **Mr Daniel Okoro** (nigeriano, director de recursos humanos), **Inês** (angolana, 24 anos, programadora) e **Dr Grace Phiri** (zambiana, psicóloga do trabalho).

**A história:** Abril de 2028. O sistema de sensores da Tsodilo funciona nas seis salas da Morro Azul desde Março (B1 7G, 8C). Na segunda, 3, véspera do feriado do Dia da Paz, o Nuno e o Paulo falam de uma série (9A); na quarta, 5, a Sílvia apresenta as novas regras de custos (9B); na quinta, 6, o Nuno lê um artigo sobre centros de dados em África e conta-o ao Paulo (9C); na sexta, 7, faz um *briefing* à equipa sobre o artigo e sobre uma notícia em português: a Morro Azul vai construir uma segunda central solar (9F).

## 9A — Although it was late…

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9A — Although it was late…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue contrastar ideias com although / even though / however /
  despite / in spite of, e recomendar uma série, um filme ou um livro.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2, 4
Gramática-alvo: although / even though + frase; however no início de uma frase nova (com vírgula);
  despite / in spite of + nome ou -ing (não + frase)
Palavras-alvo: series, season, episode, plot, character, ending, slow, gripping, worth watching,
  recommend, holiday, binge-watch (só para reconhecer)
Reciclagem: conectores de causa (B1, 6C): because / because of; future continuous (B1, 8C)
Pronúncia: entoação de contraste: a voz sobe na primeira parte e desce na segunda (Although it's long, | it's
  really good.)
Contexto: segunda-feira, 3 de Abril de 2028, 13:00; a cantina; o Nuno e o Paulo ao almoço; amanhã é
  feriado (Dia da Paz)
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 9A; perguntas 1 e 2 do questionário da unidade 9
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! This unit is about the news, articles, films and opinions. Today: how to show a contrast between two ideas — "although", "however", "despite" — and how to recommend a series or a film.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: segunda-feira, 3 de Abril, 13:00; a cantina; o Nuno e o Paulo com os tabuleiros; o Paulo com ar cansado]

**NUNO:** You look tired, Paulo. Did you go out last night?
**PAULO:** No — I stayed at home and watched four episodes of a series. Although it was late, I couldn't stop!
**NUNO:** Four episodes! What is it?
**PAULO:** It's called *Port of Daughters*. It's about a family business at a port in West Africa. The father wants to sell the company, but his daughter wants to keep it.
**NUNO:** Is it good?
**PAULO:** Really good. The first episode is a bit slow. However, after that it's gripping. Every episode ends with a surprise.
**NUNO:** I don't usually like family dramas.
**PAULO:** Neither do I, but this one is different. Even though it's a drama, it's quite funny. And the main character, the daughter, is brilliant.
**NUNO:** How many seasons are there?
**PAULO:** Two. Despite the long episodes — about an hour each — I finished the first season in a week.
**NUNO:** OK, you've convinced me. Tomorrow's a holiday, so I'll be watching it all day!
**PAULO:** It's worth watching, I promise. But even though it's so good, don't watch four episodes on a Sunday night. Look at me!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

***Although / even though* + frase**

[ECRÃ: **Although** it was late, I couldn't stop. · **Even though** it's a drama, it's quite funny. · I couldn't stop, **although** it was late.]

**PROF:** "Although" introduces a contrast — a fact that makes the other part surprising. "Even though" is the same, but stronger. Both come before a full sentence, with a subject and a verb.
[PAUSA 5 s]

***However* — numa frase nova**

[ECRÃ: The first episode is a bit slow. **However,** after that it's gripping.]

**PROF:** "However" usually starts a new sentence, with a comma after it. It's a bit more formal — very common in writing and in presentations.
[PAUSA 4 s]

***Despite / in spite of* + nome ou -ing**

[ECRÃ: **Despite** the long episodes, I finished the season in a week. · **In spite of** the rain, we went out. · **Despite** being tired, he went to work.
✘ Despite it was late, … · ✔ Although it was late, … · ✔ Despite the late hour, …
✘ Despite of the rain · ✔ Despite the rain / In spite of the rain]

**PROF:** "Despite" and "in spite of" are followed by a noun or an "-ing" form — never by a full sentence. And careful: "despite", without "of"; "in spite of", with "of".
[PAUSA 6 s]

[ECRÃ: "Apesar de estar cansado, foi trabalhar." → ✔ **Although** he was tired, he went to work. / ✔ **Despite being** tired, he went to work.]
[PAUSA 4 s]

**Recomendar**

[ECRÃ: It's **worth watching** / **worth reading**. · I'd **recommend** it. · You should watch it. · It isn't worth it.]

**PROF:** To recommend: "It's worth watching" — it's good enough to spend your time on it. "I'd recommend it."
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In a contrast, the voice goes up at the end of the first part, and down at the end of the second. Listen: Although it's long, [voz a subir] it's really good. [voz a descer]
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: Although it was late, I couldn't stop.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Despite the long episodes, I finished it in a week.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Join the ideas.
[ECRÃ: It rained. We played football. → Although…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Although it rained, we played football.]
[ECRÃ: the rain / we played football → Despite…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: Despite the rain, we played football.]
[ECRÃ: The film is old. It's still fantastic. → … However, …] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: The film is old. However, it's still fantastic.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Recommend a series, a film or a book to me. What's it about? Give one weak point and one strong point, with "although" or "despite", and say why it's worth watching or reading. About forty seconds.
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I'd recommend a film called *Kianda Road*. It's about two brothers who drive a lorry from Luanda to Lubango. Although the story is simple, the characters are wonderful, and it's very funny. Despite the sad ending, I loved it. It's really worth watching.]

**PROF (opinião):** Do you prefer series or films? Why?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 8C):** It's a holiday tomorrow. What will you be doing at this time tomorrow?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: At this time tomorrow, I'll be having lunch with my family. / I'll be watching a series!]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Very good! "Although" with a sentence, "despite" with a noun, "however" in a new sentence. Next lesson: saying why we do things — "to", "in order to", "so that".

## 9B — In order to save money

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9B — In order to save money        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue dizer a finalidade com to / in order to / so that, e a
  consequência com as a result / therefore.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2
Gramática-alvo: to / in order to + verbo; so that + frase (com can / will / could / would); in order not
  to; as a result / therefore (consequência), revendo because / so (B1, 6C)
Palavras-alvo: policy, rule, approve, book, apartment, monthly, exchange rate, risk, avoid, protect,
  reduce, save
Reciclagem: números e tendências (B1, 8B); future continuous (B1, 8C)
Pronúncia: to e so that fracos e ligados (in order to save — "in-or-der-tu"; so that we can — "sou-dat")
Contexto: quarta-feira, 5 de Abril, 9:30; a reunião semanal; a Sílvia apresenta as novas regras de custos,
  depois do desvio do projecto do Lobito (B1 8F)
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 9B; perguntas 3 e 4 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: how to say why we do something — the purpose — and what happens as a result.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: quarta-feira, 5 de Abril, 9:30; a sala de reuniões; a Sílvia ao lado de um ecrã: "New cost rules — April 2028"]

**SÍLVIA:** Good morning, everyone. I hope you enjoyed the holiday. As you know, the Lobito project went fifteen percent over budget. Therefore, from this month, we have three new rules.
**PAULO:** Only three? That's not bad.
**SÍLVIA:** First: we pay suppliers in dollars as soon as we sign the contract, in order to avoid problems with the exchange rate.
**LERATO:** That's what Nuno proposed to David, isn't it?
**SÍLVIA:** Exactly. Second: for any stay longer than ten days, we book an apartment at a monthly rate, so that we spend about thirty percent less on accommodation.
**NUNO:** And the third?
**SÍLVIA:** Third: every budget has a contingency of five percent. We do that so that small problems don't become big ones. And in order not to lose clients, we don't show the contingency as a separate line in our price.
**PAULO:** Clever.
**SÍLVIA:** Thank you. We did a test with the numbers from Lobito. With these three rules, the project would cost about five and a half million kwanzas less. As a result, we'd be only about five percent over budget, not fifteen — and the contingency would cover almost all of that.
**LERATO:** Good. And to make it easy for everyone, Sílvia's team will check every new budget before we send it.
**SÍLVIA:** That's right. So please send me your budgets a week before the deadline, so that we have time to check them.

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**A finalidade: *to / in order to***

[ECRÃ: We pay in dollars **to avoid** problems. · We pay in dollars **in order to avoid** problems. (mais formal) · **in order not to** lose clients]

**PROF:** To say why — the purpose — use "to" plus a verb. "In order to" is the same, a bit more formal. The negative is "in order not to".
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: "Para poupar dinheiro" → ✔ **to save** money · ✘ for save money · ✘ for to save money]

**PROF:** Careful! "Para poupar" is "to save", not "for save" or "for to save".
[PAUSA 5 s]

***So that* + frase**

[ECRÃ: We book an apartment **so that we spend** less. · Send me your budgets early **so that we have** time to check them. · …**so that** small problems **don't become** big ones.]

**PROF:** "So that" is also for purpose, but it's followed by a full sentence — usually with a new subject: "so that we have time". It's very common with "can", "will" or "could": "so that we can check them".
[PAUSA 5 s]

**A consequência: *as a result / therefore***

[ECRÃ: The project went fifteen percent over budget. **Therefore,** we have three new rules. · We'd spend four million less. **As a result,** we'd be only eight percent over.]

**PROF:** "Therefore" and "as a result" introduce a consequence. "Therefore" is quite formal — good for reports and presentations. You know "because", "so" and "that's why" from unit six.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: causa — **because** · consequência — **so / therefore / as a result** · finalidade — **to / in order to / so that**]
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "To" and "so that" are weak and joined to the next word. Listen: in order to save money. So that we can check them.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: We pay in dollars in order to avoid problems.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** Send me your budgets early, so that we have time to check them.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Say the purpose.
[ECRÃ: I study English. Purpose: talk to clients. → I study English to…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: I study English to talk to clients.]
[ECRÃ: I leave home at six. Purpose: the traffic — not get stuck. → … in order not to…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: I leave home at six in order not to get stuck in the traffic.]
[ECRÃ: I'll send the slides today. Purpose: you can read them. → … so that…] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: I'll send the slides today so that you can read them.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Explain three things you do at work or at home, and why. Use "to", "in order to" and "so that". About forty seconds.
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I get up at five thirty in order to avoid the traffic. I keep a list of all my tasks on my phone so that I don't forget anything. And every Sunday I cook for the whole week, to save time and money. As a result, my evenings are much calmer.]

**PROF (opinião):** Do you think companies should have strict rules about money, or should they trust their teams? Why?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 8B):** With the new rules, how much less would the Lobito project cost, and how much over budget would it be?
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: About five and a half million kwanzas less — only about five percent over budget, not fifteen.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Excellent! "To" and "in order to" with a verb, "so that" with a sentence, and "therefore" for the result. Next lesson: how to summarise an article.

## 9C — The article says…

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9C — The article says…        Tipo: Língua
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue resumir um artigo em 4 a 6 frases (o quê, quem, onde, porquê, o
  que vem a seguir) e citar a fonte (According to…, The article says that…, The main point is…).
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1
Gramática-alvo: The article says (that)… / According to the article,… / The writer thinks… / The main point
  is… / It also mentions… (no presente); separar facto e opinião (It says… · In my opinion…)
Palavras-alvo: article, headline, writer, source, according to, main point, data centre, dust, heat,
  humidity, survey, damage, cost, mention
Reciclagem: discurso indirecto (B1, 7A) — aqui no presente, porque o texto continua a dizê-lo;
  percentagens (B1, 8B)
Pronúncia: according to (a-COR-ding-tu), com "to" fraco
Contexto: quinta-feira, 6 de Abril, 11:00; o escritório; o Nuno leu um artigo na internet e conta-o ao Paulo
Duração: 11 min
Verificação: Lab 9C; conversa guiada "Have you seen it?"; perguntas 5 e 6 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! At work, we often read something and then tell our colleagues about it. Today: how to summarise an article in a few sentences, and how to say where the information comes from.

### 2. Situação (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: quinta-feira, 6 de Abril, 11:00; no portátil do Nuno, um artigo: "Heat, dust and water: the hidden enemies of Africa's data rooms"]

**NUNO:** Paulo, have you got five minutes? I've just read an interesting article. It's about our work.
**PAULO:** Sure. What's it about?
**NUNO:** The headline is "Heat, dust and water: the hidden enemies of Africa's data rooms". The main point is that most problems in server rooms in Africa don't come from the equipment, but from the room.
**PAULO:** That's exactly what happened in Lobito.
**NUNO:** Exactly. According to the article, a survey of two hundred companies in eight countries found that almost half of them had a problem with heat or water last year.
**PAULO:** Almost half! Who did the survey?
**NUNO:** A university in Nairobi. The article says that the biggest problem is heat, then dust, and then water from roofs and pipes.
**PAULO:** And does it say what companies should do?
**NUNO:** Yes. It mentions three things: sensors, regular checks of the roof and the air conditioning, and training for the staff. The writer thinks that most companies spend money on equipment but forget the room.
**PAULO:** I agree with the writer.
**NUNO:** So do I. And here's the interesting part: the article says that companies are going to spend about twenty percent more on monitoring in the next two years.
**PAULO:** That's good news for us. Send me the link!

### 3. Língua (4 min)

**Resumir: as perguntas-chave**

[ECRÃ: **o quê?** problems in server rooms · **quem?** a survey of 200 companies · **onde?** 8 countries in Africa · **porquê?** heat, dust, water · **e a seguir?** 20% more on monitoring]

**PROF:** A good summary answers five questions: what, who, where, why — and what next. Four to six sentences are enough.
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Citar a fonte**

[ECRÃ: **The article says (that)…** · **According to** the article, … · **The main point is (that)…** · **It also mentions…** · **The writer thinks (that)…**]

**PROF:** When you summarise, say where the information comes from. Notice: "the article says" is in the present — the article still says it today.
[PAUSA 5 s]

[ECRÃ: ✘ According to me, … · ✔ In my opinion, … / I think…]

**PROF:** Careful: "according to" is for other people or sources — "according to the article", "according to Lerato". For yourself, say "In my opinion".
[PAUSA 5 s]

**Facto ou opinião?**

[ECRÃ: facto — **The article says** almost half of the companies had a problem. · opinião do escritor — **The writer thinks** companies forget the room. · a sua opinião — **I agree with the writer. / In my view, …**]

**PROF:** Keep three things separate: what the article reports, what the writer thinks, and what you think.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** "According to" has the stress on "cord", and "to" is weak. Listen: according to the article.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: According to the article, almost half of the companies had a problem.
[PAUSA 6 s]
**PROF:** The main point is that the room is the problem.
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 5. Prática guiada (3 min)

**PROF:** Fact, the writer's opinion, or your opinion? Say it with the right expression.
[ECRÃ: Heat is the biggest problem. (from the survey)] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: According to the survey, heat is the biggest problem.]
[ECRÃ: Companies forget the room. (the writer's view)] [PAUSA 5 s] [ECRÃ: The writer thinks that companies forget the room.]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Think of a piece of news you read or heard this week. Summarise it in four or five sentences — what, who, where, why, what next — and then give your opinion. About forty-five seconds.
[PAUSA 45 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — I read an article about a new bus line in Luanda. According to the article, it's going to connect Viana and the city centre. The main point is that people will spend less time in traffic. The buses start in June. In my opinion, it's a good idea, but the city needs ten lines like that.]

**PROF (opinião):** Where do you usually get your news: newspapers, television, radio or social media? Why?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF (reciclagem — B1, 7A):** What did Paulo say about the article? Report it.
[PAUSA 6 s]
[ECRÃ: He said he agreed with the writer, and that it was good news for them.]

### 6. Fecho (30 s)

**PROF:** Well done! What, who, where, why, what next — and "according to", "the article says", "the main point is". Now talk to the tutor: "Have you seen it?". Next lesson: the words of news, films and books.

## 9D — News, films and books

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9D — News, films and books        Tipo: Vocabulário e pronúncia
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue usar palavras e colocações de notícias, redes sociais, filmes,
  livros e séries, usar a entoação de lista e de contraste, e evitar os falsos amigos propaganda e novel.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 4
Gramática-alvo: colocações (break the news, go viral, post a comment, read a review, make a film); adjectivos
  de opinião (gripping, moving, boring, predictable, well written)
Palavras-alvo: headline, source, fake news, reliable, review, plot, character, ending, series, episode,
  season, documentary, novel, author, advertising, social media, post, share, go viral
Reciclagem: contraste (9A); finalidade (9B)
Pronúncia: entoação de lista (sobe, sobe, desce: news, films and books) e de contraste
Falsos amigos: propaganda (≠ propaganda no sentido de publicidade → advertising; propaganda = só a
  política); novel (≠ novela → soap opera; novel = romance)
Contexto: ecrãs com expressões e exemplos
Duração: 10 min
Verificação: Lab 9D; perguntas 7 e 8 do questionário
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Today: the words of news, social media, films and books — and how to say a list and a contrast with the right intonation.

### 2. Notícias e redes sociais (2 min)

[ECRÃ:]

| Palavra | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| headline | The headline was very dramatic. (título da notícia) |
| source | What's the source of this news? (fonte) |
| reliable | Is this website reliable? (de confiança) |
| fake news | That story is fake news. |
| go viral | The video went viral in two days. |
| post / share | She posted a photo, and thousands of people shared it. |
| break the news | Lerato broke the news to the team. (dar a notícia) |

**PROF:** Repeat: headline. Source. Reliable. Fake news. Go viral.
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 3. Filmes, livros e séries (2 min)

[ECRÃ:]

| Palavra | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| plot | The plot is simple, but the characters are great. (enredo) |
| character | The main character is a young doctor. |
| ending | I didn't like the ending. |
| review | The reviews were excellent. (crítica) |
| documentary | a documentary about the Namib desert |
| novel / author | a novel by an Angolan author (romance / autor) |
| gripping · moving · predictable · well written | It's gripping — you can't stop. · It's predictable — you know the ending. |

**PROF:** Repeat: plot. Character. Ending. Review. Gripping. Moving. Predictable.
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 4. Pronúncia: listas e contraste (2 min)

[ECRÃ: news ↗, films ↗ and books ↘ · Although it's long ↗, it's really good ↘]

**PROF:** In a list, the voice goes up on each item, and down on the last one. Listen: news, films and books.
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Repeat: The article mentions heat, dust and water.
[PAUSA 5 s]
**PROF:** In a contrast, up on the first part, down on the second. Repeat: Although the plot is simple, the characters are great.
[PAUSA 6 s]

### 5. Falsos amigos (1 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: **propaganda** ≠ propaganda (publicidade)]

**PROF:** In English, "propaganda" is only political — false or one-sided information to change people's opinions.
(PT: "Propaganda" no sentido de publicidade diz-se "advertising" ou "an advert": "Vi a propaganda na televisão" → "I saw the advert on TV".)
[PAUSA 4 s]

[ECRÃ: **novel** ≠ novela]

**PROF:** A "novel" is a long book that tells a story.
(PT: Um romance é "a novel". A "novela" da televisão diz-se "a soap opera", ou, na América Latina, "a telenovela".)
[PAUSA 4 s]

### 6. Prática e fecho (2 min)

**PROF:** Complete.
[ECRÃ: The video … viral in two days.] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: went]
[ECRÃ: You can't stop reading it. It's … .] [PAUSA 4 s] [ECRÃ: gripping]

**PROF:** Now a longer turn. Tell me about a book, a series or a documentary you liked: what's it about, what's good about it, and who would enjoy it? About forty seconds.
[PAUSA 40 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — Last year I read *The Last Boat to Baía Farta*, a novel about a family of fishermen in the south of Angola. The plot is simple, but the characters are very real, and the ending is really moving. I'd recommend it to anyone who likes stories about families.]

**PROF (opinião):** In your opinion, how can we know if a piece of news is fake?
[PAUSA 20 s]

**PROF:** Well done! Next lesson: a radio debate — phones at work: yes or no?

## 9E — Real English: What do you think about it?

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9E — What do you think about it?        Tipo: Real English
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue identificar a opinião e os argumentos de cada pessoa num debate
  de rádio sobre o uso do telemóvel no trabalho.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 2, 3
```

### 1. Apresentação (30 s)

**PROF:** "Talking Point" is a radio debate programme. Today's question: should companies ban phones at work? Three guests answer. Listen for each person's opinion, and their arguments.

### 2. Primeira escuta: a ideia geral (4 min)

**PROF:** First: who is for a ban, who is against, and who is in the middle?

**BELLA:** Good evening, and welcome to Talking Point. Tonight: phones at work. Some companies in Luanda have banned personal phones in the office. Is that a good idea? Mr Okoro, you're a human resources director. What's your view?
**OKORO:** Thank you, Bella. I'm for a ban — during working hours, at least. According to a study by our company, our staff checked their phones about eighty times a day. As a result, they lost almost two hours of work. So last year we asked everyone to leave their phones in a locker, and productivity went up by fifteen percent.
**BELLA:** Inês, you're a software developer. Do you agree?
**INÊS:** Not at all. Although I understand the problem, a ban treats adults like children. I use my phone to test our apps, to talk to clients on WhatsApp, and so that my family can call me if there's an emergency. In my view, the problem isn't the phone — it's bad management.
**OKORO:** Sorry, can I come in here? In our company, there's a phone on every desk for emergencies.
**INÊS:** That's fine for an office. But in spite of that, young people work in a different way. If you take our phones away, the best developers will go to other companies.
**BELLA:** Dr Phiri, you're a work psychologist. Who's right?
**PHIRI:** Both of them, in a way. On the one hand, the research is clear: every interruption costs us about twenty minutes of concentration. On the other hand, a total ban creates stress, especially for parents. Therefore, I'd recommend a middle way: phones away during meetings and focus time, for example two hours in the morning, and free use at other times.
**BELLA:** So, not a ban, but rules.
**PHIRI:** Exactly. Rules that the team makes together, so that people accept them.
**BELLA:** Thank you, all three. And now, a short break.

[ECRÃ: Okoro — a favor da proibição (80 consultas por dia, quase 2 horas perdidas; produtividade +15%) · Inês — contra (o telemóvel é uma ferramenta de trabalho; os melhores vão-se embora) · Dr Phiri — a meio (regras feitas pela equipa: sem telemóvel nas reuniões e em períodos de concentração)]

## 9F — Work & Life: Team briefing

```
Curso: B1.2        Unidade: 9        Aula: 9F — Team briefing        Tipo: Work & Life
Objectivo da aula: No fim, o aluno consegue resumir à equipa, em cerca de 2 minutos, um artigo sobre o
  sector e dizer o que significa para a empresa; e resumir em inglês uma notícia em português.
Objectivo da unidade a que serve: 1, 3 (e 2)
Gramática-alvo: toda a da unidade: resumir e citar; contraste, finalidade e consequência
Palavras-alvo: briefing, opportunity, market, client, investment, plant, monitoring, offer, propose,
  what this means for us
Reciclagem: future continuous (B1, 8C); 2.º condicional (B1, 4A); números (B1, 8B)
Pronúncia: um resumo com pausas entre as partes: o artigo, a notícia, o que significa para nós
Mediação: o Nuno resume à equipa, em inglês, uma notícia em português (escrita por nós, ao estilo de um
  jornal angolano)
Contexto: sexta-feira, 7 de Abril, 9:00; a sala de reuniões; a Lerato, a Sílvia e o Paulo; a Thandi por
  videochamada
Duração: 12 min
Verificação: Lab 9F; conversa livre "Hot topic"
```

### 1. Abertura (30 s)

**PROF:** Hello! Every Friday, someone in Lerato's team gives a short briefing about the sector. Today it's Nuno. Watch how he summarises an article and a piece of news, and says what they mean for the company.

### 2. Situação (4 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: sexta-feira, 7 de Abril, 9:00; a sala de reuniões; a Thandi no ecrã; no portátil do Nuno, o artigo da 9C e uma notícia em português]

**LERATO:** OK, Nuno, the floor is yours. Five minutes, please.
**NUNO:** Thank you. I have two things: an article and a piece of news. And then, what they mean for us.
**NUNO:** First, the article. It's called "Heat, dust and water: the hidden enemies of Africa's data rooms". According to a survey by a university in Nairobi, almost half of two hundred companies in eight African countries had a problem with heat or water in their server rooms last year. The main point is that companies spend money on equipment but forget the room. The article also says that spending on monitoring is going to grow by about twenty percent in the next two years.
**THANDI:** That's a big number.
**NUNO:** It is. Second, the news. It's in Portuguese, from this week, so let me summarise it.

[ECRÃ: a notícia, em português (texto fictício, escrito pela ProEnglish): "Morro Azul Energia investe numa segunda central solar no Namibe. A Morro Azul Energia anunciou ontem um investimento de 40 milhões de dólares numa nova central solar perto do Tômbwa, na província do Namibe. As obras começam em Outubro e a central deverá estar pronta no fim de 2029. Segundo a directora de operações, Ingrid Solberg, a nova central vai produzir energia para cerca de 30 mil casas. A empresa quer também instalar sistemas de monitorização modernos, depois dos problemas causados pelas tempestades de areia na primeira central."]

**NUNO:** Morro Azul announced yesterday that it's investing forty million dollars in a second solar plant, near Tômbwa, also in Namibe province. And the most important part for us: the company wants modern monitoring systems, because of the problems with sandstorms at the first plant.
**PAULO:** That's us!
**NUNO:** Exactly. So, what does this mean for us? Two things. First, the market for monitoring is growing, and we already have the experience: the satellite backup in Namibe, and the water sensors with Tsodilo. Second, we have a client who's going to need it. **SÍLVIA:** I agree. However, we must be careful with the budget this time.
**NUNO:** Of course — with the new rules: payment in dollars when we sign, an apartment for long stays, and a five percent contingency.
**LERATO:** Good. Although October is six months away, these offers take time. Nuno, can you lead the offer?
**NUNO:** I'd love to.
**THANDI:** And Mopane will be helping with the satellite part. Count us in.
**LERATO:** Great. Thank you, Nuno — a clear briefing.

### 3. Como se faz um *briefing* (2 min 30 s)

[ECRÃ: o que o Nuno fez]

| Momento | Língua | Exemplo do Nuno |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Anunciar o plano | I have two things… And then… | I have two things: an article and a piece of news. And then, what they mean for us. |
| 2. Resumir com a fonte | According to… · The main point is… · The article also says… | According to a survey by a university in Nairobi… |
| 3. Mediação da notícia | announced that… · According to Ingrid… | Morro Azul announced yesterday that it's investing forty million dollars… |
| 4. O que significa para nós | So, what does this mean for us? · Therefore, I propose… | Therefore, I propose that we prepare an offer… |
| 5. Responder a uma objecção | Of course — with… | Of course — with the new rules. |

**PROF:** Nuno doesn't just repeat the news. He finishes with the most important question for his team: "What does this mean for us?" — and he proposes an action.
[PAUSA 5 s]

**A mediação**

**PROF:** The news is in Portuguese, and it has many details. Nuno keeps the important facts — who, how much, where, when — and the detail that matters most for his team: the monitoring systems. He doesn't translate every sentence.
(PT: Ao resumir uma notícia para a equipa, escolha os factos que interessam a quem ouve. "Segundo a directora de operações" → "According to the director of operations". Os números redondos ditos em voz alta: "30 mil casas" → "about thirty thousand homes".)
[PAUSA 5 s]

### 4. Pronúncia (1 min)

**PROF:** In a briefing, make clear pauses between the parts, and stress the words that start each part. Listen.
**PROF:** First, the article. [pausa] Second, the news. [pausa] So, what does this mean for us?
[PAUSA 3 s]
**PROF:** Now you. Say the three parts.
[PAUSA 8 s]

### 5. A sua vez (3 min)

**PROF:** It's your turn to give the Friday briefing. Summarise an article or a piece of news about your sector — real or invented — in four or five sentences, and then say what it means for your company. About forty-five seconds.
[PAUSA 45 s]

The main point is that young clients don't want to go to a branch. So, what does this mean for us? I propose that we train our staff to help clients with the app.]

[PAUSA 35 s]
[ECRÃ: modelo — It's about a new juice factory in Caála, in Huambo province. A company called Sumos do Planalto is investing twelve million dollars, and the factory will create three hundred jobs. According to the director, production starts in September, and twenty percent of the juice will be sold in Namibia.]

**PROF (opinião):** In your opinion, why is it important to follow the news about your sector?
